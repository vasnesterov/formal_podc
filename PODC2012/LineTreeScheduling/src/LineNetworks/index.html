A demand `a` has a window `[rt a, dl a]`, a processing time `ρ a ≥ 1`, a profit, a height and a set `acc a` of accessible resources. Its demand instances are the pairs `(q, s)` with `q ∈ acc a` and `rt a ≤ s`, `s + ρ a - 1 ≤ dl a`; such an instance occupies the timeslots `[s, s + ρ a - 1]` of resource `q`.
