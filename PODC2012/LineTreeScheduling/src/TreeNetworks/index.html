A demand instance is a pair `(a, q)` with `q ∈ acc a`; it uses the edges of the path between `src a` and `dst a` in `T q` (edges of different networks are different: `E = Q × Sym2 V`). The per-tree layered decompositions of Lemma 4.3 (`Δ = 6`, length `≤ 2⌊log₂ n⌋ + 1`) combine to a layered decomposition of all instances (`treeLayered`), and the generic results give: * `theorem_5_3`: unit heights, `(1-ε) · p(Opt) ≤ 7 · p(S)`;
