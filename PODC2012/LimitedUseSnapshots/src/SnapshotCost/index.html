import Papers.PODC2012.LimitedUseSnapshots.SnapshotDefs import Mathlib.Data.Nat.Log /-! # Lemma 4.3 of Aspnes–Attiya–Censor-Hillel–Ellen: step complexity of Algorithm 3 Step complexity is counted as a *weighted* number of base-object accesses: an access to a base object costs what one operation on its implementation costs (`Costs`: `MaxUpdate`, `MaxScan`, `WriteMax`, `ReadMax`; register accesses cost `1`). With the max arrays and the max register as atomic base objects, `Costs` with all weights `1` counts the accesses. * `lemma_4_3_scan`: a `Scan` costs at most `ReadMax + 1`; * `lemma_4_3_update`: an `Update` of process `p` costs at most `depth(leaf_p) · (MaxUpdate + MaxScan + 3) + WriteMax + 1`: one iteration per ancestor of the leaf, each with one `MaxUpdate`, one `MaxScan` and three register accesses, plus the leaf write and the final `WriteMax`; * `depth_balanced_le`: for the balanced tree `balanced l` on `n` leaves every leaf has depth at most `⌈log₂ n⌉` (`Nat.clog 2 n`); * `lemma_4_3_asymptotic`: with the costs of Section 3 (`MaxUpdate`, `MaxScan` = `O(log² b)`, `WriteMax`, `ReadMax` = `O(log b)`) this gives the paper's `O(log b)` for `Scan` and `O(log² b · log n)` for `Update`. The erratum of the paper raises the cost of `MaxUpdate₁` of the 2-component max array from `O(log b)` to `O(log k log h) = O(log² b)`, the same order as `MaxScan`, so the bound `O(log² b log n)` survives. The general cost lemma `Impl.tauCost_add_le` (a potential on local states that drops by at least the cost of every access bounds the total cost of a process's accesses) does not depend on Algorithm 3.
