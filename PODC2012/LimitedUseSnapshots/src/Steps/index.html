import Papers.PODC2012.LimitedUseSnapshots.Algorithm import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.SharedMemory.Potential import Mathlib.Data.Nat.Log /-! # Step complexity of Algorithm 2 (Lemma 3.5 and its erratum) Aspnes, Attiya, Censor-Hillel, Ellen, *Limited-Use Atomic Snapshots with Polylogarithmic Step Complexity* [AACE15], Lemma 3.5: with balanced trees, `MaxUpdate0` takes `O(log k)` steps, `MaxUpdate1` takes `O(log h)` steps and `MaxScan` takes `O(log k log h)` steps. The authors' erratum corrects the step complexity of `MaxUpdate1` to `O(log k log h)`: with the correction (line 9.5) it performs an embedded `MaxScan`. In `alg P T fix` the `MaxReg_h` objects `second` are atomic base objects (`Obj.sec a`); an access to one of them costs `cm` steps (for the balanced max register of Aspnes et al., `cm = ⌈log₂ h⌉`), an access to a switch (`Obj.sw a`, a 1-bit register) costs `cs` steps (`cs = 1`). We count the accesses of one operation of a process `p` with these weights (`Obj.cost cs cm`). ## Main results * `opCost` — the weighted cost of the base-object accesses that `p` performs after invoking `o` (at the end of `β`) during the continuation `γ`; `accCost` replays `γ` from a configuration. Generic over implementations (CSLib candidates), with `accCost_le_of_potential`: a potential on local states that drops by at least `c i` at every access to base object `i` bounds the cost. * `opCost_le` — per-operation bound in terms of `D = T.depth` (exact for every tree): `MaxUpdate0`: `cs·D`; `MaxScan`: `(cs + 3·cm)·D + cm`; `MaxUpdate1`: `cm` (Algorithm 2), `cm + (cs + 3·cm)·D + cm` (corrected algorithm). * Specializations: `switchAccesses_le`, `maxRegAccesses_le` (switch accesses / max-register accesses separately: `MaxUpdate0` reads or writes exactly one switch per level and never touches a max register; `MaxScan` reads one switch and accesses at most 3 max registers per level, plus one at the leaf) and `tauSteps_le` (all accesses, unweighted, as a count of `.tau p` labels). * `Shape.depth_balanced`: the balanced tree with `k` leaves has depth `⌈log₂ k⌉`. * `lemma_3_5`, `lemma_3_5_erratum`: the paper's conclusions for the balanced tree. -/ namespace
