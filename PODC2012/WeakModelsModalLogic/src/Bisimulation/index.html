import Papers.PODC2012.WeakModelsModalLogic.Model /-! # Kripke models `K_{a,b}(G, p)`, bisimulation, and Corollary 3 (§4 of Hella et al.) For a port numbering `p` of `G` the paper defines accessibility relations `R(i,j) = {(u, v) : p((v, j)) = (u, i)}` (`u` receives from `v` on in-port `i`, sent by `v` to its out-port `j`), `R(i,*) = ⋃ⱼ R(i,j)` and `R(*,*) = ⋃ R(i,j) = E`, and the valuation `τ(qᵢ) = {v : deg v = i}`. We only need bisimulations of a model with itself: * `IsBisimPP P Z`: `Z` is a bisimulation of `K_{+,+}(G, p)`; * `IsBisimPM P Z`: `Z` is a bisimulation of `K_{+,-}(G, p)`; * `IsBisimMM G Z`: `Z` is a bisimulation of `K_{-,-}(G, p)` (independent of `p`). Condition (B1) (agreement on all `qᵢ`, `i ∈ [Δ]`) is stated as `deg v = deg v'`; for graphs in `F(Δ)` the two are equivalent. Instead of going through the modal-logic characterisation (Theorem 2) and Fact 1, we prove directly that bisimilar nodes run through identical states for every algorithm of the corresponding class
