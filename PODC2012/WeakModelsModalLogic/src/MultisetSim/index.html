The execution of `A.sim` on `(G, p₀)` reproduces, up to time `T`, the execution of `A` on the port numbering `p*` obtained from `p₀` by permuting the in-ports of every node so that they are sorted by the message history up to time `T` (`PortNumbering.permIn`, `simPort`).
