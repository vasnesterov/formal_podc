Vaidya, Tseng, Liang — Iterative Approximate Byzantine Consensus in Arbitrary Directed Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. Tight characterization (Theorems 1–3) and Section 6 hold; the necessity proof misuses validity, repaired by a short local-validity lemma.

176

Holzer, Wattenhofer — Optimal Distributed All Pairs Shortest Paths and Applications

⚠️ Weakened. O(n) APSP and the diameter lower bounds hold. Algorithm 2 for S-SP computes wrong distances, so Theorem 5 is false and the (1+ε) approximations built on it are unproved.

105

Feinerman, Korman, Lotker, Sereni — Collaborative Search on the Plane without Communication

🔧 Easy fix. All bounds hold: uniform O(log^{1+ε} k) search, no O(log k), advice bound, harmonic algorithm. Proofs of Theorems 3.3, 4.1, 5.1 need local patches; degenerate formulas and Theorem 5.1's wording are slips.

84

Clement, Junqueira, Kate, Rodrigues — On the (Limited) Power of Non-Equivocation

⚠️ Weakened. The n > 3f lower bounds hold for n ≥ 3. The generic crash-to-Byzantine translation is unsound: a Byzantine process can replay messages addressed to others, and no repaired compiler is proved.

48

Faleiro, Rajamani, Rajan, Ramalingam, Vaswani — Generalized Lattice Agreement

❌ Intent lost. Liveness Theorem 5.15 is false: with one crash among three processes a received value is never learnt, so O(N) falls; no repair proved. Safety holds; Algorithm 1 fixed by one guard.

45

Feigenbaum, Godfrey, Panda, Schapira, Shenker, Singla — Brief Announcement: On the Resilience of Routing Tables

⚠️ Weakened. 1-resilient patterns exist and perfect resilience is impossible, by new proofs. Lemmas 4.1 and 4.2 are false. The paper's algorithm is ill-defined, so extending arbitrary tables in polynomial time stays unproved.

44

Kesselheim — Dynamic Packet Scheduling in Wireless Networks

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 1 is false: Algorithm 1 can fail with probability above 0.98, leaving the results through it unestablished. The local-clock bound holds for m ≥ 4; Section 4's stable protocol was not checked.

43

Jaffe, Moscibroda, Sen — On the Price of Equivocation in Byzantine Agreement

🛠️ Hard fix. Tight bounds Tₙ(h) = Θ(n³/h²) hold, but the upper bound's expander proof needed a new counting argument; the exact formula for Uₙ(h) lacks a ceiling. The link to Byzantine agreement was not checked.

39

Bushkov, Guerraoui, Kapałka — On the Liveness of Transactional Memory

🛠️ Hard fix. The impossibility holds, but the literal framework admits a silent TM, so it needs a step-based model, and the crash-free case needs a new schedule. Theorem 3 was not checked.

38

Giakkoupis, Woelfel — On the Time and Space Complexity of Randomized Test-And-Set

🔧 Easy fix. The Ω(log n) space lower bound holds after local proof repairs (the coin-fixing step is dropped). The randomized time upper bounds were not checked.

22

Hirvonen, Suomela — Distributed Maximal Matching: Greedy is Optimal

🔧 Easy fix. The k - 1 lower bound and Ω(Δ) hold; the proof omits the passage from infinite colour systems to finite graphs, closed by a short 2^k-copies construction.

19

Ellen, Fatourou, Kosmas, Milani, Travers — Universal Constructions that Ensure Disjoint-Access Parallelism and Wait-Freedom

✏️ Slip. Theorem 5's impossibility holds; its statement only leaves implied assumptions unstated, and Assumption 3 needs a per-instance reading. The DAP-UC construction and Theorem 6 are unchecked.

14

Hella, Järvisalo, Kuusisto, Laurinharju, Lempiäinen, Luosto, Suomela, Virtema — Weak Models of Distributed Computing, with Connections to Modal Logic

🔧 Easy fix. Both linear orders hold; the simulations of Theorems 4, 8 and 9 overlook stopped neighbours and are fixed locally with the claimed times; the modal-logic characterisation is unchecked.

9

Gramoli, Kuznetsov, Ravi — From Sequential to Concurrent: Correctness and Relative Efficiency

❌ Intent lost. The optimistic list I_RM is not concurrency-optimal: it rejects an observable schedule, so Theorem 3 is false and Corollary 4 loses its proof. A lookahead reader–writer-lock implementation in P accepts the schedule that Theorem 2(2) says no pessimistic implementation accepts. Compositionality and the SM half of Theorem 2(1) hold; the correctness of I_RM was not checked.

7

Liang, Vaidya — Byzantine Broadcast in Point-to-Point Networks Using Local Linear Coding

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 1 (Equality Check) holds; Appendix C.3's argument is wrong but easily replaced, and Lemma 1 needs large fields. Theorem 2 and Phases 1 and 3 were not checked, so the capacity fraction is conditional.

5

Aspnes, Attiya, Censor-Hillel, Ellen — Faster than Optimal Snapshots (for a While)

🛠️ Hard fix. Algorithm 2's max array is not linearizable as published. The erratum's fix works, with a new proof; Theorem 4.2's linearization points are repaired, and the snapshot bounds stand. Section 6 is unchecked.

3

Chakaravarthy, Roy, Sabharwal — Distributed Algorithms for Scheduling on Line and Tree Networks

🔧 Easy fix. All approximation ratios of v2 hold; the depth bounds of the tree decompositions, the BuildIdealTD case analysis and the line-network length groups need local fixes.

2

Taubenfeld — A Closer Look at Fault Tolerance

🛠️ Hard fix. Almost-wait-free election with ⌈log n⌉+2 registers and Lemma 6.1 with Theorems 6.2–6.3 hold, but election safety needed a new credit invariant. The test-and-set bit has two winners as printed, fixed by one condition. Renaming, Common2 and TAS liveness not checked.

2

Casteigts, Flocchini, Godard, Santoro, Yamashita — Brief Announcement: Waiting in Dynamic Networks

🛠️ Hard fix. All three main results hold: no waiting gives all computable languages, waiting gives exactly the regular ones, bounded waiting changes nothing. The regularity proof rests on a false powerset-wqo lemma and invalid steps; it is redone via Higman's lemma on walks.

1

Denysyuk, Rodrigues — Brief Announcement: Order-Preserving Renaming in Synchronous Message Passing Systems with Byzantine Faults

⚠️ Weakened. In the BA-era full version (arXiv v1/v2), the constant-time Algorithm 1 for N > 3t² (namespace N² + Nt) is correct. The O(log N) Algorithm 2 for the optimal resilience N > 3t is refuted: Lemma 2 (Skew ≤ 3t²) fails for N = 7, t = 2, so the correct ids 10 < 11 get the names 7 > 6, and for N = 4, t = 1 a correct process gets a name 9 > 2N.

Raynal, Stainer — Brief Announcement: Increasing the Power of the Iterated Immediate Snapshot Model with Failure Detectors

⚠️ Weakened. The generic simulation of Figure 3 fails for Sigma, with no repair proved; the simulations for Omega_k and ◇P and the consensus algorithm hold; Theorem 1, the converse simulation, was not checked.
