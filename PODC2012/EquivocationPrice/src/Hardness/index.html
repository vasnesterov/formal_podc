import Papers.PODC2012.EquivocationPrice.Equivalence /-! # Theorem 1: the reduction from balanced bipartite independent set **Theorem 1** of [JMS12]: deciding whether a 3-uniform hypergraph is `h`-disjoint (equivalently, by Theorem 2, whether Byzantine agreement is possible) is co-NP-complete. Membership in co-NP is immediate (a violating colouring is a certificate). Hardness is by a reduction from balanced bipartite independent set (BBIS), NP-complete by Alon, Duke, Lefmann, Rödl, Yuster. We verify the combinatorial core: the correctness of the reduction (`theorem_1_reduction`). Given a bipartite graph `G` between `X` and `Y` and `t ≥ 1`, the hypergraph `redE G` on `X ⊕ Y ⊕ Z` with `|Z| = |X| + |Y| − t` has the hyperedges (Section 6) * all triples inside `Z`; `(u, v, w)` for `u, v ∈ Z`, `w ∈ X ∪ Y`; * `(u, v, w)` for `u, v ∈ X`, `w ∈ Z`, and for `u, v ∈ Y`, `w ∈ Z`; * `(u, v, w)` for every edge `(u, v)` of `G` and `w ∈ Z`; and `G` has an independent pair `A ⊆ X`, `B ⊆ Y` with `|A| = |B| = t` iff `redE G` is not `t`-disjoint. The paper assumes `|X| = |Y|`; the reduction is correct for `t ≤ |X|, |Y|`. The size of `redE G` is polynomial; the complexity-theoretic wrapper (NP-completeness of BBIS, polynomial-time computability) is not formalized. Through Theorem 2 the same instance decides `f`-tolerance with `n' = 2f + h`, `f = |X| + |Y| − t`, `h = t ∈ [1..f]`, as the paper's statement of Theorem 1 in the introduction requires
