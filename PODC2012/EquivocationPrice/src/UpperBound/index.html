import Papers.PODC2012.EquivocationPrice.Defs /-! # Upper bound `Tₙ(h) = O(n³/h²)` (Theorem 7), by counting Jaffe, Moscibroda, Sen, *On the Price of Equivocation in Byzantine Agreement*, PODC 2012, Section 4. The paper proves Theorem 7 with lifted hypergraphs of Ramanujan graphs of degree `Θ(n/h)` (see `Expander.lean` for the conditional part of that argument) and remarks that a simple probabilistic argument also works. We formalize the latter, derandomized as a greedy hitting-set argument: * every valid colouring `(A, B, C)` has `|A|·|B|·|C| ≥ h²·n/3` crossing triples (`ab ≥ h(a + b - h)` twice, `card_crossing_ge`); * there are at most `4ⁿ` colourings (maps `V → Fin 4`); * greedily choosing a triple hitting the largest number of not-yet-hit colourings, `m` triples leave at most `4ⁿ (1 - K/N)ᵐ ≤ 4ⁿ e^{-Km/N}` colourings unhit, `N = C(n, 3)` (`exists_hitting`); this is `< 1` for `m = ⌊n³/h²⌋`. Main results: `theorem_7` (an `h`-disjoint 3-uniform hypergraph with `|E|·h² ≤ n³` exists for `1 ≤ h ≤ n/3`) and `T_le` (`Tₙ(h)·h² ≤ n³`). -/ namespace Podc.EquivocationPrice open Finset section
