For `n = 3k` and `h ≤ k`, fix a `k`-set `C`. Every bisection `(S, U \ S)` of `U = V \ C` together with `C` is a valid colouring (all parts have `k ≥ h` vertices and the sizes sum to `n ≥ (n + 3h)/2`), so `h`-disjointness gives a rainbow hyperedge, whose projection by `C` crosses the bisection. By Lemma 4 the projection has at least `|U|/2 = k` edges. Summing over all `C(n, k)` sets `C` and using that every hyperedge is counted at most `3 · C(n-3, k-1)` times (`sum_card_proj_le`) gives `k · C(n, k) ≤ 3 · C(n-3, k-1) · |E|`, i.e. `|E| ≥ (n-1)(n-2)/(4n/3 - 2)` (`theorem_3`, `theorem_3_nat`, `theorem_3'`). The paper silently assumes `3 ∣ n`.
