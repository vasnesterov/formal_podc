LockStep round `r` (counting from `0`) is the paper's Round `r + 1`. A process receives its own broadcast through the self-loop (link `n` in the paper); here it adds its own message to its inbox (`Function.update`). * Round 1: broadcast `⟨ID, my id⟩`; `Ids` := ids received. * Round 2: broadcast `⟨Echo, id⟩` for `id ∈ Ids`; the ids echoed by at least `n - t` links are "considered for the following round" (text of §6.1.1; see `differencies.md` for the pseudocode, which keeps the Round-1 ids in `Ids`). * Round 3: broadcast `⟨Ready, id⟩` for these ids; `timely` := ids with `≥ n - t` Ready messages, `accepted` := ids with `≥ n - 2t`; `ranks[id] := rank(accepted, id) · δ`, `δ = 1 + 1/(3(n+t))`. * Rounds `≥ 4`: broadcast `⟨AA, ranks⟩` (the domain of `ranks` is `accepted`); keep the votes `R` with `isValid(timely, R)` (both ids of every pair `id < id'` of `timely` are in `R` and `R[id'] - R[id] ≥ δ`), and run `approximate`: `votes[id]` = multiset of the valid votes for `id`; `accepted := {id ∈ accepted : |votes[id]| ≥ n - t}`; for these ids fill `votes[id]` up to `n` with the own value, drop the `t` largest and `t` smallest, and average `select_t` of the rest. * After `L` rounds (paper: `L = 2⌈log t⌉ + 8`; §6.2: `L = 7`) return `round(ranks[my id])`. The choice function `select_t` is a parameter `sel`; `selPaper` is the formula of §6.1.2 (elements `it + 1`, `0 ≤ i < ⌊|set|/t⌋`), `selDLPSW` the one of Dolev et al.
