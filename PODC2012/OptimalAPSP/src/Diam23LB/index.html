Wattenhofer, *Optimal Distributed All Pairs Shortest Paths and Applications*, PODC 2012. ## The lower-bound graphs `G_{a,b}` (proof of Theorem 8) For `k ≥ 1` and inputs `a b : Fin k × Fin k → Bool` (the `k²` bits of `disj_{k²}`): * Alice's side: `l_ν` (`ν < 2k`; the *upper* half `ν < k` is `Vtx.l false i`, the *lower* half `l_{k+j}` is `Vtx.l true j`) and `c_L`. Edges: `c_L – l_ν` for all `ν`; a clique on each half; `l_i – l_{k+j}` iff `a(i, j) = 0`. * Bob's side: the same with `r_ν`, `c_R` and `b`, plus `f` fill-up nodes `w_j` adjacent to all of `R ∪ {c_R}`. * Cut (`2k + 1` edges): `l_ν – r_ν` for all `ν`, and `c_L – c_R`.
