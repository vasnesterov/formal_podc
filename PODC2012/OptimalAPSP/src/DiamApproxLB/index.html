Bob symmetric (`ru, rl, tb, c₂, c₃, wb`), with the paths at the *lower* nodes `r_μ`. Edges: `l_ν l_μ` iff `a (ν, μ) = 0`, `r_ν r_μ` iff `b (ν, μ) = 0`, `c₀ – l_upper`, `c₁ – l_lower`, `c₀ c₁`, `w^a – {l_upper, c₀, c₁}`, and symmetrically for Bob; cut: `l_i r_i` (`i < 2k`) and `c₀ c₂`.
