import Papers.PODC2012.GeneralizedLatticeAgreement.Model /-! # Executions: correct processes, fairness, liveness, concrete runs * `Action.actor`, `Action.sender`: the process taking a step and the sender of the message it consumes. A process is identified by its role: `P ⊕ A ⊕ Ln`. * `Exec.Fair corr`: weak fairness for correct processes. A non-input action of a correct process that consumes no message, or a message sent by a correct process, and that is enabled from some time on, is eventually taken. For message-consuming actions this is the paper's liveness assumption (d) "all messages between correct processes are eventually delivered"; messages sent by faulty processes may be lost (the paper's failure model includes message losses and crashes in the middle of a step). * `Exec.CrashStop corr`: a faulty process eventually takes no more steps. * `LivenessAssumptions corr`: the paper's assumptions (a)–(c): a majority (quorum) of acceptors, at least one proposer and at least one learner are correct. * `Exec.GLALiveness corr`: Liveness of generalized lattice agreement (Section 4, item 4): every value received by a correct proposer is eventually below the `learntValue` of every correct learner. * `run`: executing a finite list of actions (used to check concrete executions by `decide`); `Quiescent`: no action of a correct process on a message of a correct process is enabled; `Exec.ofRun`: a finite run followed by stuttering, which is fair if its last state is quiescent. -/
