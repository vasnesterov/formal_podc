Signatures are CSLib's abstract unforgeable tokens (`Cslib.Byzantine.Auth`): the algorithm is `Unforging` (correct processes only emit their own or received tokens) and the adversary is `AuthValid` (Byzantine messages only carry Byzantine-signed tokens or tokens already emitted by correct processes).
