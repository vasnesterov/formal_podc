import Papers.PODC2012.TVGWaitingExpressivity.NoWait /-! # Bounded waiting: `𝓛_wait[d] = 𝓛_nowait` (Theorem 5.1) The paper proves only that every language `L` is `L_wait[d](G_{2,d})` for the time-dilated TVG `G_{2,d}` (here `G2d_LwaitAtMost`). The equality of the two *families* needs two simulations, which we give for arbitrary TVGs (they keep the node set finite and preserve computability of `ρ` and `ζ`): * `dilate d A`: time stretched by `d + 1`, edges present only at multiples of `d + 1`; a pause of at most `d` cannot reach the next multiple, so `L_wait[d](dilate d A) = L_nowait(A)`; * `withPause d A`: nodes `V × Fin (d + 1)`; being in `(u, j)` means "leave `u` after a pause of `j`", so direct journeys of `withPause d A` simulate `d`-bounded journeys of `A`: `L_nowait(withPause d A) = L_wait[d](A)`. -/ namespace Podc.TVGWaitingExpressivity open TVG TVGAutomaton variable {V α : Type*} /-- Time dilation by `d + 1`. -/ def dilate (d : ℕ) (A : TVGAutomaton V α) : TVGAutomaton V α where present e t := (d + 1) ∣ t ∧ A.present e (t / (d + 1)) latency e t := (d + 1) * A.latency e (t / (d + 1)) init := A.init accept := A.accept start := (d + 1) * A.start theorem dilate_journey_iff (d : ℕ) (A : TVGAutomaton V α) (u : V) (t : ℕ) (w : List α) (v : V) (t'' : ℕ) : (dilate d A).Journey (waitAtMost d) u ((d + 1) * t) w v t'' ↔ ∃ t', t'' = (d + 1) * t' ∧ A.Journey noWait u t w v t' := by induction w generalizing u t with | nil => simp only [journey_nil_iff] constructor · rintro ⟨rfl, rfl⟩;
