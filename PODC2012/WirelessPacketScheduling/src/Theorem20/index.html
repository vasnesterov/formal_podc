The paper shows that with local clocks no acknowledgement-based protocol is stable for `λ ≥ ln m / m`, and concludes that no such protocol is `m / (2 ln m)`-competitive. * `theorem_20`: for every `m ≥ 4` there is `λ < ln m / m` at which no protocol with local clocks is stable on the network of Figure 1; with the TDMA rate `1/2` this gives competitive ratio `> (1/2) / (ln m / m) = m / (2 ln m)`.
