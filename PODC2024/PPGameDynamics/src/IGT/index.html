drawing an (initiator, responder) pair `ω` uniformly from a finite set in which every GTFT initiator has `cAD` AD partners and `cN` non-AD partners, the count process is *exactly* the `(k, a, b, m)`-Ehrenfest process with `a = w cN m`, `b = w cAD m` (`w` = probability of a pair). * `replKernel` (initiator and responder drawn independently and uniformly from all `n` agents): `a = γ(1-β)`, `b = γβ` — the identification claimed in Section 2.4; `theorem_2_7_stationary`: the count process is the Ehrenfest process and its unique stationary distribution is multinomial with `p_j ∝ (1/β - 1)^(j-1)`
