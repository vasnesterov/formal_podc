import Papers.PODC2024.OnlineLocalGrids.BValue import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.Local.OnlineLocalLocality /-! # Theorem 1.3: `Ω(√n)` for 3-colouring cylindrical and toroidal grids (§3.3) Chang–Mishra–Nguyen–Yang–Yeh, PODC 2024. Host graphs: `cylinder a b = P_a □ C_b` (rows `Fin a` form a path, every row is a cycle of length `b`) and `torus a b = C_a □ C_b`. The adversary reveals two whole rows `r₁ = T` and `r₂ = 3T + 2` first. Their radius-`T` neighbourhoods (rows `[0, 2T]` and `[2T+2, 4T+2]`) are disjoint and non-adjacent, so reflecting the columns (`j ↦ -j`) of all rows `≥ 2T + 2` is invisible to the algorithm. In both runs the row sums (`b`-values of the row cycles) of `r₁` and `r₂` are equal (`rowSum_eq_of_reachable`); the reflection fixes row `r₁` and negates the row sum of `r₂` (`rowSum_reflect`), so the row sum is `0`, contradicting its oddness (`rowSum_odd`, `b` odd). For the torus the two bands must also be separated across the wrap-around, which needs one more row: `4T + 4 ≤ a` (instead of `4T + 3 ≤ a` for the cylinder). -/ namespace
