Verdict

12

Civit, Gilbert, Guerraoui, Komatovic, Paramonov, Vidigueira — All Byzantine Agreement Problems Are Expensive

🔧 Easy fix. The Ω(t²) lower bound holds for all t after rounding Lemma 1's parameters. Lemma 10 and unauthenticated Theorem 4 fail at n = 2, t = 1; one added condition corrects them.

11

Yu, Losa, Wang — TetraBFT: Reducing Latency of Unauthenticated, Responsive BFT Consensus

⚠️ Weakened. Termination is false: view change deadlocks after GST. It is proved only with a repaired echo rule on reliable channels, not in the paper's lossy model. Agreement and validity hold; latency and multi-shot unchecked.

11

Jayanti, Jayanti, Jayanti — MemSnap: A Fast Adaptive Snapshot Algorithm for RMWable Shared-Memory

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 7.3 holds. Lemma 7.2's printed Q lacks x_μ = X, which the prose states; I₁ of Lemma 7.1 is not inductive and needs a local strengthening.

10

Tonkikh, Freitas — Swiper: a new paradigm for efficient weighted distributed protocols

🛠️ Hard fix. The weight restriction and qualification bounds hold. The weight separation bound and Swiper's construction for it are false; a different linear bound, with a new rounding offset, is proved. Protocol transformations unchecked.

9

Manoharan, Ramachandran — Computing Minimum Weight Cycle in the CONGEST Model

🔧 Easy fix. Both (2 − ε) lower bounds hold after a hub or sink and a parity step; the algorithms, the paper's other main part, and the larger-factor lower bounds were not checked.

9

D'Amato, Losa, Zanolini — Asynchrony-Resilient Sleepy Total-Order Broadcast Protocols

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 1 as printed is unsafe even in synchronous runs; a one-word fix from [MMR] saves it, and Theorem 2 and Theorem 1's safety are proved. Liveness and healing unchecked.

5

Attiya, Castañeda, Enea — Strong Linearizability using Primitives with Consensus Number 2

⚠️ Weakened. Theorems 1, 2, 5, 6, 9 hold; Algorithm 2's set (Theorem 10) is refuted, and Lemma 12's gap leaves the stack impossibility unestablished; the queue impossibility was not checked.

4

Attiya, Bender, Farach-Colton, Oshman, Schiller — History-Independent Concurrent Objects

🛠️ Hard fix. All impossibilities and algorithms hold for at least two processes; the broken proof of the universal construction (Theorem 32) needed a new proof, done for one fixed interleaving of its parallel blocks.

4

Balliu, Brandt, Kuhn, Nowicki, Olivetti, Rotenberg, Suomela — Brief Announcement: Local Advice and Local Decompression

🛠️ Hard fix. 3-colouring with 1 bit of advice holds. Order-invariance (Theorem 5) holds, but its proof ignores that nodes know n and needed a new Ramsey argument. The other main results were not checked.

3

Chang, Mishra, Nguyen, Yang, Yeh — A Tight Lower Bound for 3-Coloring Grids in the Online-LOCAL Model

✏️ Slip. Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 are proved with explicit constants; Theorem 1.3 omits that √n must be odd, as its proof assumes. The k-partite results were not checked.

2

Ovens — Determining Recoverable Consensus Numbers

🔧 Easy fix. Robustness and the T_{n,n′} separation both hold: false steps in the proofs of Lemmas 15 and 16 have local repairs, and Observation 5 needs an implied hypothesis.

2

Alistarh, Chatterjee, Karrabi, Lazarsfeld — Game Dynamics and Equilibrium Computation in the Population Protocol Model

⚠️ Weakened. Stationary laws and Ω(km) mixing lower bound hold; Theorem 2.5's upper bound is false for small a + b; the mixing and equilibrium claims of the k-IGT dynamics are unchecked.

1

Fuchs, Kuhn — Simpler and More General Distributed Coloring Based on Simple List Defective Coloring Algorithms

🔧 Easy fix. Theorems 1.1 and 1.4 hold; proofs of Lemmas 3.1, 4.5 and A.1 need local repairs, plus a few slips. Round bounds of the (Δ+1)-colouring applications were not checked.

1

Delporte-Gallet, Fauconnier, Fraigniaud, Rajsbaum, Travers — The Computational Power of Distributed Shared-Memory Models with Bounded-Size Registers

🔧 Easy fix. Non-universality for n/2 < t < n and 3-bit universality for two processes hold (the latter given BMZ); Algorithm 2's parameters and some proof cases needed local fixes. Theorems 1.3–1.5 unchecked.

0

Altisen, Cournier, Defalque, Devismes — On Self-stabilizing Leader Election in Directed Networks

🔧 Easy fix. Unison algorithm U(K) is not self-stabilizing as printed; a one-line change of E's input repairs it with the claimed bound. Leader election, the impossibility and both characterizations hold.

0

Frei, Gelles, Ghazy, Nolin — Content-Oblivious Leader Election on Rings

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithms and message counts hold as claimed. The lower bound's lockstep step needed a local repair and the bound is worst-case over IDs only; IDs must be positive.

0

Alves Ferreira, Atre, Sherry, Sobrinho — Impossibility Results for Data-Center Routing with Congestion Control and Unsplittable Flows

🔧 Easy fix. All three impossibility results hold. Theorem 5.4's tightness is proved with 1/(n-1) for odd n but only 1/(n-2) for even n; two proof gaps close locally.

0

Bousquet, Feuilloley, Zeitoun — Brief Announcement: Global certification via perfect hashing

✏️ Slip. Theorems 3 and 4 hold and Conjecture 1 is false as claimed; only the implied M(n) ≥ n, degenerate cases and the wording "has size" need correcting.

0

Charron-Bost, Lambein-Monette — Brief Announcement: Know Your Audience

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 4.1 fails only on one- and two-agent networks; the corrected characterization (vector length ≥2, or ≥3 for symmetric links) is proved. Section 5 on dynamic networks unchecked.

0

Losa, Gafni — Brief Announcement: Understanding Read-Write Wait-Free Coverings in the Fully-Anonymous Shared-Memory Model

🛠️ Hard fix. Snapshot, stable-view DAG and renaming hold with at least N registers, but Theorem 4.8 needs that bound and its proof and the safety proof needed new arguments; consensus unchecked.

0

Gay, Mostéfaoui, Perrin — Brief Announcement: No Broadcast Abstraction Characterizes k-Set-Agreement in Message-Passing Systems

⚠️ Weakened. Lemma 9 is false; Theorem 1 is reproved by a new adversary, without content-neutrality, but only for k-set-agreement algorithms using the broadcast alone, not the point-to-point channels the paper allows.
