Consequences: * `computable_of_frequencyBased` — **Theorem 4.1, (i) ⇒ (ii)** (and Corollary 4.2, (i) ⇒ (ii)). * `computable_of_frequencyBasedFrom_two`, `computable_of_frequencyBasedFrom_three_symmetric` — the converses of the corrected impossibility results: with an extra test for `n = 1` (indegree `1`), resp. for `n ∈ {1, 2}` (symmetric communications), the corrected classes are computable. Together with `Impossibility.lean` this gives the exact characterisation. * `computable_sizeEq_of_multisetBased` — Corollary 4.3, (i) ⇒ (ii): knowing `n`, the multiset of inputs is recovered from `u` (`recover`). * `computable_leader_of_multisetBased` — Corollary 4.4, (i) ⇒ (ii): with one leader, `n` is recovered from the frequency of the leader's input. -/ namespace Podc.KnowYourAudience open
