We use depth-indexed views: * `Msg I t`: a depth-`t` view: a tag (the sender's outdegree, or `0` in the simple broadcast model), an input value, and (for `t > 0`) the multiset of depth-`(t-1)` views of the in-neighbours. * `msg N x dd t j`: the depth-`t` view of agent `j` in network `N` with inputs `x` (tag `dd (outdeg j)`). * `Msg.trunc`: truncation; `trunc_msg`: views of depth `t + 1` determine views of depth `t`. * `eventually_stable`: the partition of the agents by their depth-`t` views stabilises (colour refinement on a finite set). * `desc p K m`: the depth-`p` views of the nodes of a
