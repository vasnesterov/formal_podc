import Papers.PODC2024.KnowYourAudience.Rings /-! # The impossibility half of Theorem 4.1, corrected The paper's argument (Section 4.1): two vectors `v`, `w` with the same frequencies are lifted along the coverings `R_n → R_p ← R_m` of rings, `p = lcm` of the denominators of the frequencies. This needs the ring `R_p` to be a network of the class, i.e. `p ≥ 3` (for `p ∈ {1, 2}` the quotient is a multigraph). We use instead the coverings `R_{n'n} → R_n` and `R_{nn'} → R_{n'}` (`n = |v|`, `n' = |w|`), together with Lemma 3.3 on vectors of length `nn'`. This needs `n, n' ≥ 3` for bidirectional rings (symmetric communications) and `n, n' ≥ 2` for directed cycles (the other models). These bounds are optimal (see `Counterexample.lean`). * `eq_of_freq_eq_of_covers`: the abstract argument for any family of networks with coverings `i ↦ i mod n`. * `frequencyBasedFrom_two_of_computable` (outdegree awareness, output port awareness, and also simple broadcast): computable ⇒ frequency-based on vectors of length `≥ 2`. * `frequencyBasedFrom_three_of_computable_symmetric`: with symmetric communications, computable ⇒ frequency-based on vectors of length `≥ 3`. * The same for the classes `C_{≤B}` for every `B` (Corollary
