Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Self-stabilizing Leader Election in Directed Networks — PODC 2024.

Paper: Altisen, Cournier, Defalque, Devismes, On Self-stabilizing Leader Election in Directed Networks, PODC 2024. Formalized from the full version hal-04434345v4. Each item has a classification.

Errors in the paper

  1. Theorem 5 (and Lemmas 16, 19, Corollary 6) — false statement. The unison algorithm U(K) = U-DAG(K) ∘ PEL relies on the claim "in a terminal configuration of PEL, p.E.d = ‖ℓ, p‖, and ℓ is the only process with E.d = 0". The paper attributes this claim to Theorem 1 (§5.3, proof of Lemma 16 Claim 3, Remark 3, proof of Lemma 19). It is false for I_E(p) = (¬p.A.M, p.MI.M). In fact p.E.d = Mind_E(p), and Mind_E(q) = 0 for every q with I_E(q) = (0, mid). This includes all of the source component and every process all of whose ancestors have identifiers ≥ mid. Each such process increments its clock on its own.

    • Lean: U.paper_ed_eq_zero shows that E.d = 0 holds at every process whose ancestors all have identifiers ≥ mid (U.LowAnc).

    • U.paper_unison_not_selfStab_gen: U(K) is not self-stabilizing (for any set of legitimate configurations) as soon as two distinct processes satisfy LowAnc. This holds for all K ≥ 2 and all α ≥ MAD.

    • Corollaries:

      • U.paper_unison_not_selfStab: the source component has ≥ 2 processes (any ι).

      • U.paper_unison_not_selfStab_of_min: the globally smallest identifier lies in the source component, so it fails on every network with n ≥ 2 for such ι.

    • Concrete instances: twoCycle_paper_unison_fails (2-cycle) and dipath_paper_unison_fails (dipath 0 → 1, whose source component is a single process).

    • Figures 5(c) and 6 are inconsistent with each other. Six processes have E-input (0,4), yet Figure 6 shows distinct E.d values for them.

    • Repair: I_E(p) = (¬p.A.M, p.Id) (fixed = true in PEL.next). Leader election is unaffected (PEL.lemma15 and PEL.theorem3 are proved for both inputs). For unison we prove Theorem 5 with the paper's bound 3α + 4MAD + 6 (U.theorem5, U.theorem5_time). This requires unique identifiers, used to show that ℓ alone has input (0, mid).

  2. Lemma 10 — typo. The induction claim says "If MinI(p) < i"; it should be Mind(p) < i.

  3. Lemmas 5, 8, 9 — typos. Lemma 5's proof has q ∈ F_{i+1}(q) for F_{i+1}(p). Lemma 8 has q ∉ F_j(q) and q ∈ F_j(q) for F_j(p). Lemma 9 has "Mdf_{R(α+1)} q(p)".

  4. Lemmas 9, 10 — imprecise indexing. R(i) (end of the i-th round) is undefined when the finite execution ends earlier. Harmless: a terminal configuration is legitimate. In Lean, the Ext∞ form makes every index meaningful.

  5. Theorem 4 — proof by reference only. Not an error: the paper cites [ACDD23], and the cited proof was not examined. We give a direct proof for arbitrary deterministic local algorithms with identifiers (theorem4). It is true for every period K ≥ 2; the hypothesis K ≥ 2 is needed, since K = 1 is trivially solvable.

Modelling choices

  1. Atomic-state model, deterministic algorithms. An algorithm is a function next : (V → Q) → V → Q, i.e. one action per process with the statement "assign next γ p". The paper allows several actions and a nondeterministic choice among the enabled ones. This loses nothing:

    • C-MAI has a single action.

    • In the compositions E ∘ A ∘ MI and U-DAG ∘ PEL, at most one action per process is enabled.

    • Lemma 12/13 in the paper are stated for deterministic algorithms. Trivial actions are excluded, as in the paper: a process is enabled iff next γ p ≠ γ p.

  2. Executions in Ext∞ form. IsUnfairRun next e S describes infinite sequences in which a nonempty set S t of enabled processes moves at every non-terminal configuration, and terminal configurations repeat. These are exactly the paper's infinite extensions of maximal executions under the distributed unfair daemon. Lemma 11 (SP_L(e) ≡ SP_L(Ext∞(e))) is therefore built in. Silence = every execution reaches a terminal configuration.

  3. Rounds. The paper defines rounds with neutralization. RoundDone e S s s' says: "every process enabled at s is activated at some u ∈ [s, s') or is disabled at some u + 1 ≤ s'". This is equivalent to "executes an action or is neutralized during [s, s']", because a process that becomes disabled without being activated is neutralized at that step.

    • Upper bounds hold for every sequence of boundaries in which each interval contains a complete round (RoundBounds). This includes the paper's minimal rounds (IsLeastRoundBounds, which exist for eventually terminal runs, IsUnfairRun.exists_isLeastRoundBounds).

    • Under the synchronous daemon, r = id.

  4. MAD. MADLe G α says that every ancestor is at distance ≤ α, i.e. α ≥ MAD. mad G is the least such value. Time bounds are proved for every m with MAD ≤ m ≤ α (in particular m = MAD); PEL.theorem3_time is stated with mad G.

  5. Identifiers and α.

    • Identifiers are an input assignment ι : V → ID (ID totally ordered), not a component of the state. The algorithm is a family indexed by ι, local by IsLocal.

    • "Insensitive to its inputs" becomes "self-stabilizing for every injective ι".

    • α is a fixed natural number known to the algorithm. The impossibility does not use α.

    • |IDSET| > |V| is an injection Option V ↪ ID. Lemma 13 uses only the existence of an unused identifier (exists_fresh).

    • idset_card_needed shows that Corollary 2 is false with |IDSET| = |V|. This is not an error, since the paper assumes |IDSET| > |V|.

  6. Leader output. L(p) may depend on p's identifier and state only (LEAlg.local_out), as in the paper ("a local function").

  7. Domains of variables. A C-MAI state is a key with d ≤ α (CState D α), so arbitrary initial configurations respect the variable domains, as in the paper. The input I_A is a Bool; B × IDSET with <_C is Bool ×ₗ ID.

  8. Source components. We use the paper's definition:

    • the induced subgraph is strongly connected and maximal,

    • the component is nonempty,

    • there are no predecessors outside. We add nonemptiness so that the empty digraph has no source component. The paper assumes n ≥ 1 anyway, and the necessity results assume V nonempty where needed. Digraph.isSourceComponent_iff relates this to "S = Anc(r) with r upstream of all its ancestors".

  9. Self-loops are allowed in Digraph and do not affect any result. The counterexample twoCycle has none.

Generalisations

  1. Lemma 4 is proved for runs with eventually constant, time-dependent inputs and arbitrary activation sets (CMAI.Run.eventually_const), not only for executions of C-MAI. This is what makes PEL's termination direct.

    • Proof: well-founded induction over the finitely many reachable keys ("freeze all keys ≤ k").

    • The paper's proof uses limit inferiors. Its argument is correct; it is replaced, not repaired.

  2. Lemmas 5–9 are proved for fake keys at all processes, not only at ancestors of a fixed p. The restriction to Anc(p) is unnecessary.

  3. Theorem 3 does not need injectivity of the identifiers for the paper's PEL (the spec then still holds literally). The fixed variant needs injectivity only for the unison (U.fixed_ed).

  4. Theorem 5 (repaired) holds for every K ≥ 1. The paper's K > 1 matters only for Claim 3 of Lemma 16 in its formulation; the spec SP_SU is trivial for K = 1.

Not formalized

  1. Lemma 1 and Corollary 1 (generic hierarchical collateral composition of silent algorithms) are only used through their instances for PEL. We prove those directly: PEL.silent and PEL.terminal_within, via PEL.runMI/runA/runE.

  2. Lemma 17 (no terminal configuration of U(K)) and Remark 3 are not needed in that form (Property 1 is CMAI.target_le_succ_target).

  3. Space complexity O(log α + B) bits and O(log α + B + log K) bits is not formalized. It is evident from the state types CState, PState, UState.
