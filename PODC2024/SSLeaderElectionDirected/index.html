Self-stabilizing Leader Election in Directed Networks — PODC 2024

Karine Altisen, Alain Cournier, Geoffrey Defalque, Stéphane Devismes. On Self-stabilizing Leader Election in Directed Networks. PODC 2024. DOI 10.1145/3662158.3662778. Formalized version: the full version on HAL, hal-04434345v4 (4 March 2024); numbering follows it (v1 has the same statements and algorithms). Copy of the paper: paper.pdf.

DOI · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🔧 Easy fix. Both characterizations hold, for leader election (Corollary 3) and synchronous unison (Corollary 5), but the unison algorithm U(K) of Theorem 5 is not self-stabilizing as printed: the bug is in the input of the third instance E of PEL. A one-line change (I_E(p) = (¬p.A.M, p.Id) instead of (¬p.A.M, p.MI.M)) repairs it, and with that change the paper's proof of Theorem 5 goes through. Everything else we checked holds: C-MAI (Theorems 1 and 2, with the bound α + MAD + 2), PEL (Theorem 3, with 3α + 3MAD + 6 rounds under the distributed unfair daemon) and the impossibility (Lemma 13, Corollary 2); the space bounds were not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

🔧 Theorem 5 (Algorithm 2 on top of PEL, synchronous unison)

U(K) does not stabilize: every process of the source component gets E.d = 0 and increments its clock on its own, so e.g. on the 2-cycle 0 ⇄ 1 with K = 2 the clocks alternate (1, 0), (0, 1), … forever (twoCycle_paper_unison_fails, U.paper_unison_not_selfStab).

Impact. The printed algorithm does not work, but a one-line change (I_E(p) = (¬p.A.M, p.Id) instead of (¬p.A.M, p.MI.M)) repairs it, and the characterization of Corollary 5 survives. Leader election (PEL, Theorem 3) is unaffected.

Details

Section 5.3 and the proofs of Lemmas 16 and 19 use that in a terminal configuration of PEL, p.E.d = ‖ℓ, p‖, so the leader ℓ is "the only process satisfying E.d = 0". The paper cites Theorem 1 for this. But Theorem 1 gives p.E.d = Mind(p): the distance to the closest ancestor whose E-input equals the minimum (0, mid). That input I_E(q) = (¬q.A.M, q.MI.M) equals (0, mid) for every q with q.MI.M = mid. This includes the whole source component (Corollary 4 and Lemma 14). So all these processes have E.d = 0, and U-DAG makes each of them increment its own clock independently.

Counterexample: the 2-cycle 0 ⇄ 1 with identifiers 0, 1, α = 1 and K = 2. Start from a terminal configuration of PEL with clocks (0, 1). Under the synchronous daemon the clocks alternate (1, 0), (0, 1), … forever. They are never equal, so no choice of legitimate configurations makes U(K) self-stabilizing. The paper's own example is inconsistent in the same way. In Figure 5(c), the six processes of the two triangular components have E-input (0,4), yet Figure 6 shows E.d = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 for them.

Scope: we prove the failure for every network whose source component has two distinct processes (so on every strongly connected network with n ≥ 2), every K ≥ 2, every identifier assignment and every α ≥ MAD (U.paper_unison_not_selfStab; concrete instance twoCycle_paper_unison_fails). More generally, it fails as soon as two processes have only ancestors with identifiers ≥ mid (U.paper_unison_not_selfStab_gen). So if the smallest identifier lies in the source component, every process has E.d = 0, and U(K) fails on every network with n ≥ 2 (U.paper_unison_not_selfStab_of_min). An example is the dipath 0 → 1, whose source component is a single process (dipath_paper_unison_fails).

Repair

With I_E(p) = (¬p.A.M, p.Id), PEL still elects mid: Lemma 15 holds for both inputs (PEL.lemma15). Moreover the leader is now the only process with E.d = 0, and E.d is a BFS distance from it (U.fixed_ed). The paper's Lemmas 16 and 19 then go through. We prove Theorem 5 for the repaired algorithm, with the claimed bound of 3α + 4MAD + 6 synchronous steps (U.theorem5, U.theorem5_time), and Corollary 5 (corollary5).

✏️ Slips

  • Lemma 10's induction claim reads "If MinI(p) < i"; it should be "Mind(p) < i".

  • Lemma 9 and Lemma 10 index rounds by R(i), which is undefined once the (finite) execution has ended. This is harmless: terminal configurations are legitimate.

Typos

  • Lemma 5's proof writes q ∈ F_{i+1}(q) for q ∈ F_{i+1}(p), and Lemma 8 has F_j(q) for F_j(p).

  • Lemma 9 has a stray q in "Mdf_{R(α+1)} q(p)".

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model (the shared daemon library Cslib/Computability/Distributed/SelfStabilization/{Defs,Daemon}.lean, namespace Cslib.SelfStabilization, and digraph reachability Cslib/Foundations/Combinatorics/Digraph/Walk.lean).

  • Atomic-state model on a finite Digraph V. G.Adj q p means p reads q, i.e. q is a predecessor of p.

  • Deterministic algorithms next : (V → Q) → V → Q. A process is enabled iff its action changes its state. The hierarchical collateral compositions E ∘ A ∘ MI and U-DAG ∘ PEL are written as one such function.

  • Distributed unfair daemon: IsUnfairRun next e S (IsUnfairExec next), where each step activates a nonempty set of enabled processes. Executions are in the paper's Ext∞ form: a terminal configuration repeats forever, so "finite" means "eventually terminal".

  • Synchronous daemon: IsSyncExec next e, i.e. e (t+1) = next (e t).

  • Rounds follow the paper's neutralization definition. RoundDone e S s s' says that every process enabled at s executes or becomes disabled in [s, s']. Time bounds hold for every sequence RoundBounds e S r of such boundaries, a family that contains the paper's minimal rounds (IsLeastRoundBounds).

  • Self-stabilization (Definition 1, SelfStab, Defs.lean: a nonempty legitimate set; equal to the library's SelfStabilizing by selfStab_iff) and silent self-stabilization (Definition 2, SilentSelfStabilizing) are as in the paper.

  • Identifiers are a separate input ι : V → ID, and algorithms are families indexed by ι. They are local: IsLocal lets a process use only the inputs and states of itself and its predecessors. So an algorithm must work for every assignment of unique identifiers ("insensitive to its inputs").

  • Ancestors are G.Reachable q p; MADLe G α (α ≥ MAD, Defs.lean) says that every ancestor is within α arcs (Relation.RelatesWithinSteps). α is a fixed parameter with MADLe G α. |IDSET| > |V| is an injection Option V ↪ ID. Source components (Digraph.IsSourceComponent) follow the paper's definition (maximal strongly connected induced subgraphs without outside predecessors).

  • The impossibilities (Lemma 13, Corollaries 2 and 3) quantify over all deterministic local algorithms, with an arbitrary state type, as in the paper.

Proof remarks. Theorem 4 is only cited in the paper (from [ACDD23]); we prove it directly (theorem4): shift the configuration of one source component by one synchronous step; the two components never hear from each other, so their clocks stay one apart forever. Lemma 4's limit-inferior argument is correct; we replace it by an elementary "freeze keys from the smallest up" induction.

Not formalized.

  • The generic composition results (Lemma 1, Corollary 1). We prove their instances for PEL directly: each layer is a C-MAI run whose inputs become constant once the lower layers have frozen.

  • Lemma 17 (no configuration of U(K) is terminal).

  • The space bounds O(log α + B) and O(log α + B + log K). They can be read off the state types: CState holds a key with d ≤ α, PState holds three of them, and UState adds a clock.

  • The proof of Theorem 4 cited from [ACDD23] was not examined; we prove the theorem directly (see Proof remarks).

Trust.

  • No sorry. All listed theorems use only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

  • Non-vacuity on the 2-cycle (Examples.lean): PEL and the repaired U(K) are proved correct there (twoCycle_PEL, twoCycle_fixed_unison).

  • idset_card_needed shows that Corollary 2 needs |IDSET| > |V|. With exactly |V| identifiers, leader election is trivially solvable on two isolated nodes.

  • The model (daemons, rounds, silent self-stabilization, locality) is the shared library Cslib/Computability/Distributed/SelfStabilization/Daemon.lean; ancestors and source components are Cslib/Foundations/Combinatorics/Digraph/Walk.lean (CSLib candidates). Only MAD and Definition 1 are paper-local (Defs.lean).
