The paper uses `I_E(p) = (¬ p.A.M, p.MI.M)` (`fixed = false`). We also consider `I_E(p) = (¬ p.A.M, p.Id)` (`fixed = true`), which makes the leader the *only* process with input `(0, mid)`, as required by the unison algorithm of Section 5 (see `Unison.lean`). Both variants solve leader election. Main results here: `silent` (termination, from Lemma 1 + Theorem 1 in the paper) and `terminal_within` (the stabilization time `3α + 3MAD + 6` rounds of Theorem 3, from Corollary 1 + Theorem 2). We do not formalize the generic composition results (Lemma 1, Corollary 1); we prove their instances for PEL directly: each layer is a `CMAI.Run` whose inputs become constant once the lower layers have stabilized, and every round of PEL is a round of each such layer. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false open Cslib.SelfStabilization Relation namespace
