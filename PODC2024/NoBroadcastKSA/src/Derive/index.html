Keep only * the broadcast events of the messages of a set `Ms` (the messages of selected copies), and * the internal steps of the upper layer of the *active* processes from time `ws p` on (the start of the selected copy of `p`), turned into the decisions and idle steps of `A`. The result (`derive`) is an execution of `A` on top of the broadcast abstraction. Its broadcast events are the restriction of those of `α` to `Ms`, so if `B` implements `S` and `S` is compositional, it is admitted by `S`; under the hypotheses of `derive_admissible` (the selected copy of each active process sees exactly the selected messages) it is an admissible execution of `A`, and the local history of an active process is the view of its copy (`derive_hist`). -/ namespace
