import Papers.PODC2024.NoBroadcastKSA.Spec /-! # The model `CAMP_n[k-SA]` and implementations of broadcast abstractions Section 2 of the paper: `n` asynchronous processes, any number of which may crash, reliable non-FIFO point-to-point channels (SR-Validity, SR-No-Duplication, SR-Termination), and any number of k-set agreement objects (k-SA-Validity, k-SA-Agreement, k-SA-Termination, Section 4.1). An algorithm `B` implementing a broadcast abstraction (`BAlgo`) is deterministic: the next action of process `p` is a function of its local history (`B.next p h`), which consists of its own actions, the invocations of `broadcast` by the upper layer, the receptions of point-to-point messages and the answers of the k-SA objects. Its actions are: send a message, propose a value to a k-SA object, return from the pending `broadcast`, B-deliver a message, or a local computation. An execution (`CExec`) is a sequence of steps (`none`: no step at that time): * `act p a` — a step of `B` at `p`, performing `a`; * `inv p m` — the upper layer of `p` invokes `B.broadcast(m)`; * `recv p q x s` — `p` receives the message `x` sent by `q` at time `s`; * `dec p o w` — the k-SA object `o` returns `w` to `p`; * `up p` — an internal step of the upper layer of `p` (invisible to `B`). `Adm B k C α`: `α` is an admissible execution of `B` in `CAMP_n[k-SA]` with correct processes `C`: `B`'s steps follow `B`, correct processes take infinitely many steps of `B`, faulty processes take finitely many steps (they crash), the channel and k-SA properties hold, and the upper layer is well-formed (it invokes `broadcast` only after the previous invocation returned, with a fresh message). `Implements B k S`: the broadcast events of every admissible execution form an execution admitted by `S`. -/ namespace Podc.NoBroadcastKSA.Camp set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false /-- Actions of
