A feasible allocation of `MS_n` is exactly a *fractional matching* of `G^MS`: non-negative weights on the flows with total weight at most `1` at every source and at every destination. The folklore Lemma 3.2 of Ferreira–Atre–Sherry–Sobrinho (PODC 2024) states that the `0/1` allocation of a maximum matching is a maximum-throughput allocation. The non-trivial direction is that the fractional matching number of a bipartite multigraph equals its matching number; we prove it (`exists_matching_sum_le`) for an arbitrary finite bipartite multigraph via König's theorem in the deficiency form of Hall's theorem: for the set `X'` of sources maximizing the deficiency `δ = |X'| - |N(X')|`, every flow has its source outside `X'` or its destination in `N(X')`, so the weight is at most `|X| - δ`;
