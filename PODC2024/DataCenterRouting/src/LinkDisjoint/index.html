import Papers.PODC2024.DataCenterRouting.Model import Cslib.Foundations.Combinatorics.Matrix.Birkhoff /-! # Link-disjoint routing of a matching (Lemma 5.2, footnote 5) A matching `S` of the macro-switch graph `G^MS` (flows with pairwise distinct sources and pairwise distinct destinations) can be routed link-disjointly in the Clos network `C_n`: consider the `2n × 2n` matrix `A i o = #{f ∈ S : I(s_f) = I_i, O(t_f) = O_o}`. Its row and column sums are at most `n` (each input/output switch has `n` servers), so by König's line-colouring theorem (`Matrix.exists_split_parts` with `D = 1`) it splits into `n` matrices `P m` with row and column sums at most `1`. Sending exactly `P m i o` of the flows of `S` from `I_i` to `O_o` through the middle switch `M_m` uses every link `I_i M_m` and `M_m O_o` at most once. ## Main statements *
