Hence the hypothesis `p ≠ p'` / `c ≠ r` of Proposition 14, Theorem 17, Theorem 20, … cannot be dropped (the paper assumes `n ≥ 2` processes implicitly). -/ namespace Cslib.SharedMemory open List
