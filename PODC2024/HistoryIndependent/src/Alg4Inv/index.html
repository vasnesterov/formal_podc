It formalizes, as forward invariants: * the shape of `A` during a `Write` (`memA`, `tagA`), and the values of writes (`lastv`, `pendv`); * the flag protocol (Lemmas 35, 36): every `1` in `B` is either "covered" by the reader (it will be cleared by the pending `Read` in Line 8) or belongs to the writer's window between Lines 13 and 15 (`bprot`); * Lemma 11 (`b11`): a `1` in `B` seen by a `Read` was written by a `Write` that had not returned when the `Read` was invoked; * Lemma 34 / Lemma 10 (`h1`–`h4`, `l10`): if both `TryRead`s fail, some `Write` invoked after Line 1 executed Line 16 and left a `1` in `B` which stays there until the `Read` scans `B`; * the reads-from candidate `rf` of the pending `Read` (`rf`, `rfB`) and the floor `fl` given by the last completed `Read` (`fl1`–`fl5`), which yield the conditions of the reads-from criterion (no read from the future, no overwritten value, no new-old inversion). -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Cslib.SharedMemory
