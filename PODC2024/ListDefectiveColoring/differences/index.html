Differences between the formalization and [FK24] (arXiv 2405.04648v1)

Rendered from differencies.md of List defective colouring — PODC 2024.

Issues in proofs and constructions (with Lean counterexamples)

No stated theorem of the paper is refuted. The three items below are an error in a definition used by an algorithm, a gap in a proof's construction, and an error in a proof.

  1. Section 4.1, eq. (10)/(11): floor/ceiling slip in the modified defect function.

    • What the paper says. It defines d'_v(x) := ⌊(d_v(x)+1)/(7θ)⌋ − 1 and states that d' is non-negative and that ∑(d'+1) ≥ (1/7θ)∑(d+1).

    • Why it fails. Both statements are false whenever d_v(x) < 7θ − 1: then d' = −1, and the floor goes the wrong way for (11).

    • Consequence. With negative d' no colour ever satisfies (12). Every L_{v,i} and every H_i is empty, so the Section 4.1 algorithm as defined colours no node.

      • This happens for every colour with d < 7θ − 1, including every proper list colouring instance (d ≡ 0). It is not a degenerate case.

    • Counterexample (Section41Counterexample.lean). Take the triangle K₃: its maximum degree is 2, so Δ = 2 needs no convention. Take θ = 1, S = 1, d ≡ 0 and 85 colours per list. This satisfies the paper's slack 42θ log₂Δ·S and the corrected eq. (9).

      • theorem_1_4_paper_defects_false and lemma_4_2_fails: no node is ever coloured.

      • eq11_fails: ∑(d'+1) = 0 < 85/7.

      • isRun_ce: a run exists, so the statement is not vacuous.

    • Fix. d' := ⌈(d+1)/(7θ)⌉ − 1 = ⌊d/(7θ)⌋ (Sec41.dPrime). It is non-negative, as the paper intends, with ∑(d'+1) ≥ ∑(d+1)/(7θ) and 7θ d' ≤ d. Theorem 1.4 then holds (Sec41.theorem_1_4).

  2. 🔧 Lemma 4.5 (colour space reduction): gap in the proof's construction.

    • What the paper says. The subspace-choice instance gives every node all p subspaces.

    • Why it fails. A node may choose a subspace C_i with L_v ∩ C_i = ∅, because d_{v,i} = 0 is not violated when no neighbour chooses i. The node is then left with an empty list, and its sub-instance has no solution. The slack chain also divides by deg(v), which may be 0.

    • Counterexample (ColorSpace.paper_counterexample): one isolated node, L = {0}, two subspaces, ψ ≡ 1.

    • Fix. Give a node only the subspaces that meet L_v (lemma45). The slack bound ∑_i (d_{v,i}+1) > σ deg(v) survives because L_v ≠ ∅.

    • The lemma's round-time statement (17) is not refuted.

  3. 🔧 Lemma A.1: error in the proof (existence of a colouring unaffected).

    • What the paper says. H_i is the set of nodes with ≤ Δ/2 coloured neighbours. The proof claims ∑_{L'}(d'+1) > deg(v) − deg~(v) ≥ Δ − Δ/2 ≥ µ deg_{G_i}(v).

    • Why it fails. The step deg(v) − deg~(v) ≥ Δ − Δ/2 fails for nodes of degree < Δ.

    • Counterexample (SlackReduction.paperA1_counterexample): 15 nodes, Δ = 6, µ = 2, ε = 1/4.

      • Node v has degree 4, 3 coloured neighbours and 1 neighbour in its class.

      • Its residual weight is 2 = µ·deg_{G_i}(v), so the sub-instance does not have slack µ.

      • It still has slack 1, so the round-complexity argument breaks, not the existence of a colouring.

    • Fix (lemmaA1, lemmaA1_halving, two_pow_le_of_halving).

      • Test deg~(v) ≤ r(v)/2, where r(v) is the residual degree at the start of the phase.

      • Take the defective colouring relative to the residual graph.

      • Per-node residual degrees then halve in every phase, giving O(log Δ) phases.

Gaps in proofs (results correct; fixed in the formalization)

  • 🔧 Lemma 3.1, case |L_v| > p.

    • The flaw. The final pigeonhole step bounds |N_>(v)| + ∑_{x∈S_v} k_v(x) by the average over all p-subsets and then by ∑_{x∈L_v}(d_v(x)+1). That does not give (4), whose right-hand side is ∑_{x∈S_v}(d_v(x)+1).

    • Correct argument. Average the difference of the two sides of (4) over all p-subsets. Equivalently, a best subset is at least as good as the average (IsBestSubset.mul_sum_le with sum_powersetCard_sum). This also proves the Remark: the subset chosen by Algorithm 1 satisfies (4).

    • What we prove. Lemma 3.1 is true (lemma_3_1, IsBestSubset.lemma_3_1).

  • ✏️ Lemma 3.2.

    • The text "some colour x_v such that r_v(x_v) + k_v(x_v) ≤ |N_>(v)| < d_v(x_v) + 1" is garbled (and "l'_v" is a typo for S_v).

    • The correct step is a pigeonhole on sums: if every x ∈ S_v had k + r ≥ d + 1, summing would contradict (6) (exists_phaseII_color).

  • ✏️ Section 4.1, setup of Lemma 4.2. The paper writes deg~(v,i) ≤ ∑_x a_v(x,i). The proof needs the reverse inequality, which is the true one: neighbours with colours outside L_v exist.

  • ✏️ Eq. (9) omits the factor deg(v). It must read ∑(d+1) > 21θ(⌈log Δ⌉+1)S·deg(v), as in Definition 1.1 and in the theorem statement.

  • Lemma 4.2's proof needs d' ≥ 0. This holds only for the corrected d'. The pigeonhole over iterations can be replaced by summing the per-iteration bound ∑_{L_{v,i}}(d'+1) ≤ 2S deg(v).

  • Lemma 4.3 is stronger than stated.

    • Claim 4.1's argument applies directly to W_j = {u ∈ N(v) | u coloured with x in iteration j}. The auxiliary graph G_{x,j}(v) is not needed.

    • ∑_{j≤i}(2d_j+1) = 2^{i+2} − i − 3 ≤ 4d_i for i ≥ 1, so X_v ≤ 4θ d'_v(x) (Sec41.lemma_4_3_sharp). The constant 7 in (10), and the 21/42 in the slack, could be 4 and 12/24.

  • Theorem 1.4, w.l.o.g. assumption.

    • The paper's "w.l.o.g. d_v(x) ≤ Δ" (p. 18) is a hypothesis (hdΔ). The reduction to this case is not formalized.

    • The step from the slack 42θ log₂Δ·S to 21θ(⌈log₂Δ⌉+1)S is proved (clog_add_one_le_two_logb, via 2^{⌈log Δ⌉+1} ≤ Δ²).

  • Theorem 1.2, round count (remark, not formalized).

    • The paper's argument as written combines Lemma 3.5 (time O(T(4)·⌈log₄ C⌉)) with T(4) = O(log²C + log* q). That gives O(log³C + log C · log* q), not the claimed O(log³ C + log* q).

    • The reason is that the defective colouring of Lemma 3.4 depends on the current out-degrees and is recomputed at each of the ⌈log₄ C⌉ levels. We have not checked whether it can be shared across levels.

    • ✏️ For C = 1, ε = 1/(3·0) is undefined. Lemma 3.5 assumes "≥" while Theorem 1.1 gives ">" (harmless).

  • Lemma A.1, round count (remark, not formalized). As written, every phase recomputes Lemma 3.4. That would give O(log Δ · log* q) rather than +O(log* q).

  • Lemma 4.5, hypotheses. Only σ > 0 (and S ≥ 0) is used, not 1 ≤ σ.

Modelling choices and minor differences

  • Conventions of Section 2. The paper defines Δ(G) = max(2, maxdeg), so Δ ≥ 2 is a hypothesis of Sec41.theorem_1_4. For Δ = 1, ⌈log Δ⌉ = 0 and the bound d ≤ 2d_0 = 0 would fail.

    • The paper also defines β_v = max(1, outdeg), and we use this (beta).

    • ✏️ The statements of Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 say "β_v is the outdegree of v". This is inconsistent with Section 2.

    • With the literal outdegree, an isolated node with an empty list would satisfy the hypothesis of Theorem 1.2.

  • Out-neighbourhoods. They are arbitrary finite sets of G-neighbours, not necessarily an orientation.

    • The initial colouring only has to be proper along out-edges. This is strictly more general.

    • Section 1.1's list defective 3-colouring corollary needs out v = N(v), which is not an orientation.

  • Colours. Initial colours are naturals < q instead of {1,…,q}. List colours have an arbitrary type α with decidable equality instead of ℕ.

  • Algorithm 1, line 4. "Sort by d − k and take the first p" is modelled as an arbitrary best min(p,|L_v|)-subset (IsBestSubset). isBestSubset_take proves that the sorted prefix is one, so every tie-breaking of the sort is covered.

  • Distributed model. CSLib's synchronous SET-LOCAL round model, with identifiers and local inputs in the states.

    • Phase I uses rounds 0..q-1 and Phase II rounds q..2q-1, so there are exactly 2q rounds. The paper says O(q).

    • Message sizes (Lemma 3.3: lists of p colours, then one colour) are in docstrings only.

  • Theorem 1.1, ε > 0.

    • Lemma 3.4 ([Kuh09, KS18]) is not formalized. Its output, a colouring Ψ with K colours and at most ⌊ε β_v/p⌋ same-colour out-neighbours, is a hypothesis of fastTwoSweep_correct.

    • Lemma 3.4 needs α = ε/p ≤ 1, which Algorithm 2 leaves implicit.

    • "Let ε > 0 be an integer" (p. 13) is a typo: ε is real.

    • The min{q, (p/ε)² + log* q} round bound is the choice between twoSweep_correct (2q rounds) and fastTwoSweep_correct (2K rounds after Lemma 3.4). It is not formalized as a single statement.

  • β_v = max(1, outdeg) (paper's Section 2). Because of this convention, the corollary "lists of size ≥ β² + β + 1" (twoSweep_listColoring) needs β ≥ 1. For β = 0, slack (2) fails for lists of size 1.

  • "List d-defective 3-colouring in O(Δ + log* n) rounds whenever d > (2Δ − 3)/3."

    • We prove the 2q-round statement with p = 2, ε = 0 (twoSweep_threeColoring).

    • The O(Δ + log* n) bound needs an initial proper O(Δ)-colouring computed in O(Δ + log* n) rounds (e.g. [BEG18]). The paper does not say this.

    • Using Theorem 1.1 with ε > 0 would need ε = Θ(1/Δ) and give O(Δ²) rounds.

  • Section 4.1.

    • The arbdefective subroutine A is replaced by its output specification. IsRun quantifies over all outputs.

    • Sec41.exists_isRun (greedy) shows that runs exist for S ≥ 1 on finite graphs.

    • Δ in Lean is any bound on the defects. deg ≤ Δ is not needed for correctness.

  • Lemmas 4.4, 4.5, A.1. These are spec-level reductions; round complexities are in docstrings only. Lemma 4.4 also proves that a colouring exists, by induction over the classes.

  • Not formalized.

    • Theorem 1.3 and Theorem 1.5 / Lemma 4.6. These are O-notation recursions over cited black boxes (Theorem 3 and Theorem 4 of [FK23a], Lemma 3.4), and they inherit the Lemma A.1 issue above.

    • We noticed an inconsistency in Theorem 1.5's arithmetic but did not check it formally. Eq. (20) multiplies O(θ² log³Δ) by O(C^{1/4} log Δ log³ C), which gives log⁷Δ for C = O(Δ). The theorem states log⁸Δ; this only weakens the bound.
