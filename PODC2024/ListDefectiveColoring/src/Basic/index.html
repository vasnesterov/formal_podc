import Mathlib /-! # List defective colouring: basic definitions Fuchs, Kuhn. *Simpler and More General Distributed Coloring Based on Simple List Defective Coloring Algorithms* (PODC 2024, brief announcement; full version arXiv:2405.04648). Cited as [FK24]. We fix a vertex type `V` and a colour type `α` (the paper takes colours from `ℕ`). * `ListInstance V α`: a colour list `L v` and a defect function `d v : α → ℕ` for every node (Section 1, "List Defective Coloring"). Only the values of `d v` on `L v` matter. * `ListInstance.weight I v = ∑_{x ∈ L_v} (d_v(x) + 1)`: the quantity that all slack conditions of the paper bound from below. * `beta out v = max 1 |out v|`: the paper's `β_v` (Section 2: "we define `β_v` as the maximum of `1` and the outdegree of `v`"). * `IsOLDC I out col`: `col` solves the *oriented* list defective colouring instance `I` with out-neighbourhoods `out` (every node `v` gets `col v ∈ L v` and at most `d_v(col v)` out-neighbours of the same colour). * `IsListDefective G I col`: list defective colouring of the graph `G` (all neighbours count). * `IsListArbdefective G I col O`: list arbdefective colouring, i.e. a colouring together with an orientation `O` of the monochromatic edges such that `v` has at most `d_v(col v)` out-neighbours (w.r.t. `O`) of its own colour. * `HasSlack G I S` (Definition 1.1): `∑_{x ∈ L_v} (d_v(x) + 1) > S · deg(v)` for all `v`. Out-neighbourhoods are given as an arbitrary function `out : V → Finset V`; the paper's "edge orientation" is the special case where `out` comes from orienting every edge of `G` in one direction. All results of Section 3 hold for arbitrary `out` (in particular for `out v = N(v)`, which turns oriented list defective colouring into list defective colouring). -/ open Finset namespace FK24
