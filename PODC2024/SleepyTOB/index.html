Asynchrony-Resilient Sleepy TOB — PODC 2024

Francesco D'Amato, Giuliano Losa, Luca Zanolini. Asynchrony-Resilient Sleepy Total-Order Broadcast Protocols. PODC 2024. DOI 10.1145/3662158.3662779. Formalized version: arXiv 2309.05347 v2 (6 May 2024); numbering follows it.

DOI · arXiv · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 1 as printed is not the protocol the paper means, which is Malkhi–Momose–Ren's protocol [MMR] with latest unexpired votes: its input rule lets a Byzantine VRF winner erase the lock, and the printed algorithm then violates safety even with no asynchrony at all. With the one-word fix taken from [MMR] and from the paper's own proofs, all checked statements are true, namely Lemma 1 (the extended graded agreement and clique validity), Lemmas 2–3, Theorem 1's safety and the main Theorem 2 (π-asynchrony resilience for π < η), and two proofs need short repairs. Liveness (Theorem 1's second half) and healing (Theorem 3) were not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

🔧 Algorithm 1, lines 6–7 (input rule of GA_{v,1})

With the printed rule "highest-VRF proposal not conflicting with L_{v-1}", a Byzantine VRF winner can propose a strict prefix of the decided log and erase the lock, so Algorithm 1 as printed violates safety even with no asynchrony (algorithm_1_literal_unsafe, theorem_1_false_for_literal_algorithm, theorem_2_false_for_literal_algorithm).

Impact. The paper presents Algorithm 1 as [MMR]'s protocol, whose rule is "extends L_{v-1}, else input L_{v-1}", and its own proofs reason with that rule; with this one-word fix all checked statements, including Theorem 2, are true.

Details

arXiv v1 says "not conflicting" too, three times. The printed algorithm also has no fallback when no proposal qualifies. With the printed rule, the winning proposal may be a strict prefix of the decided log, and then the lock is lost.

Counterexample: 4 processes, process 3 Byzantine, everyone always awake, all messages delivered at once, η = 2. All honest processes vote [1] in view 1 and decide [1] in round 3. The Byzantine process then wins the VRF with the proposal [] (the genesis log), which does not conflict with L_1 = [1], so every honest process votes []. In view 3 an honest proposal [2] = b‖C_2 wins, and [2] is decided in round 7. So Lemma 3 fails (round-3 voters do not extend the decided [1]), Theorem 1's safety fails (with π = 0), and Theorem 2 fails ([1] ∈ D_ra for ra = 3, π = 1). All of the paper's hypotheses hold in this execution: η-sleepiness 3 > 2/3·4 in every round, Equations 4 and 5, π < η. The view-1 votes [1] come from a Byzantine view-0 proposal that wins the lottery. Shifting the run by one view gives a variant in which the Byzantine process wins only once (not formalized).

The paper defers the protocol's correctness to [MMR]'s proof, and its own proofs use the step "outputs Λ with grade 1, hence votes for an extension of Λ", which holds in odd rounds only under the "extends" rule.

Repair

We formalize the [MMR] rule (Exec.Follows), and the printed rule only for the counterexample.

✏️ Slips

  • Definition 4 (integrity): "there exists a well-behaved process that inputs the log" fails for every graded agreement with validity: one honest process with input [1] outputs [] with grade 1 (integrity_literal_false). The intended property, as in [MMR] and the proof of Lemma 1, is that some honest input extends the log. That is what we prove.

  • Algorithm 1, line 1, for v = 1 refers to GA_{0,2}, which does not exist (view 0 only proposes). We start decisions at round 3 and leave round-1 votes unconstrained.

  • In the proof of Theorem 2, |H_ra \ H_{ra+π+1}| should be |H_ra \ B_{ra+π+1}|.

  • In the proof of Lemma 2, the latest votes in M0 are "from rounds no later than ra" only up to later honest votes, which also extend Λ.

Typos

  • In the proof of Lemma 2, |S_r' ∪ P0| should be |O_r' ∪ P0|.

Proof gaps in true statements (2)Lemma 3 · Lemma 2 and Theorem 2

🔧 Lemma 3 (base case)

The proof says that once some process decides Λ, "by graded consistency" every process of H_r votes for an extension of Λ, but graded consistency only gives Λ grade ≥ 0 elsewhere (graded_consistency_insufficient).

Impact. Statement true; repaired with [MMR]'s argument (Exec.Follows.decision_lock).

Details

Bounded divergence allows a second, conflicting and longer grade-0 log, which would become L_{v-1}. Example: 7 honest processes (5 vote [1], 2 vote [2,5]) and 2 Byzantine processes voting [1] to p and [2,5] to q. Then p outputs [1] with grade 1, while q's longest output is [2,5]. In the paper's own network model, all honest receivers of a synchronous round receive the same messages, so their tallies agree and the conclusion also follows directly. Only the cited justification is insufficient.

Repair

Integrity of GA_{v-1,2} makes every output extended by an honest GA_{v-1,2} input. Those inputs are grade-1 outputs of GA_{v-1,1} and pairwise compatible by its uniqueness. So no output of GA_{v-1,2} conflicts with Λ, and the longest one extends Λ (Exec.Follows.decision_lock).

🔧 Lemma 2 and Theorem 2 (clique validity applied outside its hypotheses)

Lemma 1 requires H' ⊆ H_r ∪ H_{r+1} and concludes only for receivers in H', but the proof of Lemma 2 applies it to H' = H_ra \ B_r', which contains processes asleep at both r' and r'+1.

Impact. Statements true; a more general form of clique validity covers both uses (clique_validity).

Details

The proof of Theorem 2 needs the conclusion for every receiver of the synchronous round ra+π+1 and redoes the argument by hand.

Repair

The proof of clique validity in fact only needs H' disjoint from B_r, and works for any receiver whose initial votes contain the clique's votes. We prove this general form (clique_validity) and derive the paper's form from it (lemma_1_clique_validity).

Model.

  • n processes, synchronous rounds 0, 1, 2, … with sleepiness: O_r is awake at round r, and B_r ⊆ O_r is Byzantine with a growing adversary (B_r ⊆ B_{r+1}; Byzantine processes never sleep). H_r = O_r \ B_r, and the receivers of round r are H_{r+1}. Signatures are modelled by message senders. There is no randomness: VRF proposals are abstracted adversarially. In odd rounds an honest vote is any extension of a longest output L_{v-1}, which covers every proposal that can win the VRF lottery, so safety holds for every outcome.

  • Messages are (sender, round, log). Each q ∈ H_t (t ≥ 1) sends one vote per round. Byzantine processes send arbitrary sets of messages with arbitrary round tags, including back-dated ones. rcv p r (messages received by the end of round r) only grows. In a synchronous round, every receiver has every message sent so far. In the single asynchronous period [ra+1, ra+π] delivery is arbitrary.

  • Expiration: the GA of round r is tallied from the latest received votes with round tag in [r-η, r]. A sender whose latest votes equivocate is discarded. Grade 1 means more than 2m/3 votes for extensions, an output means more than m/3, and β = 1/3 throughout.

  • We prove that this tally is an instance of the extended GA of Figure 3, with M0 = latest votes from [r-η, r) and P0 = O_{r-η,r} (Exec.tally_eq_ga_M). This is the correspondence claimed in Section 3.3, so Lemma 1 transfers verbatim (Exec.isGA, Exec.clique).

  • Figure 3 is modelled as an abstract one-shot instance (ExtGA). Each receiver has initial votes M0 from P0. Honest senders send only their input, absent senders send nothing, Byzantine senders send anything. Round-r votes override M0, and equivocations are discarded.

Remarks. The paper states Equations 1–2 as the synchronous-round assumptions but uses η-sleepiness (Equation 3) throughout: Lemma 2's base case needs it in round ra, Lemma 3 in rounds ≤ ra, and Theorem 2 in rounds ≥ ra+π+2. This is not a mistake: Section 3.3 works in the η-sleepy model, and Equations 1 and 2 imply 3 with exactly the paper's β̃ (Exec.sleepy_of_churn_of_failure, betaTilde_identity). Footnote 7's equivalences hold (Exec.footnote_7).

Not formalized. Liveness: Theorem 1's liveness half, which is probabilistic via the VRF and deferred to [MMR], and Theorem 3 (healing), which has a two-line informal proof. Also the necessity of β̃ claimed in Figure 1's caption. Safety under the original (non-expiring) MMR protocol is not treated separately: it is the case η = 0.

Trust. No sorry. The listed theorems use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. Non-vacuity: theorem_2_hypotheses_satisfiable exhibits an execution of the modified Algorithm 1 that satisfies every hypothesis of Theorem 2 and contains a decision before the asynchronous period. The model is self-contained in this folder (Mathlib only). Tally.lean (logs, faithful tallies, graded-agreement counting) is marked as a CSLib candidate.
