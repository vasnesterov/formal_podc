Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Asynchrony-Resilient Sleepy TOB — PODC 2024.

D'Amato, Losa, Zanolini, Asynchrony-Resilient Sleepy Total-Order Broadcast Protocols, PODC 2024, arXiv:2309.05347v2.

Statement-level issues

  1. Algorithm 1, lines 6–7, input rule of GA_{v,1}: "not conflicting with L_{v-1}". With this rule Algorithm 1 is unsafe. A Byzantine VRF winner proposes a strict prefix of the decided log, all honest processes input it, and a conflicting log is decided two views later. This happens in a fully synchronous execution with 1 Byzantine process out of 4 that satisfies η-sleepiness, Equations 4–5 and π < η (algorithm_1_literal_unsafe; theorem_1_false_for_literal_algorithm with π = 0; theorem_2_false_for_literal_algorithm). So Lemma 3, Theorem 1 (safety) and Theorem 2 are false for the printed algorithm. The printed algorithm also has no fallback when no proposal qualifies. (The gaps in the line numbering, 4 and 11 in v2 and 10 in v1, are probably typesetting artefacts. They are not evidence of a dropped line.) The wording is consistent: arXiv v1 says "not conflicting with L_{v-1}" in the pseudocode, in the prose and in the proof of its Proposition 1, and v2 says it in the pseudocode and the prose. So this is not a one-off typo. Label: 🔧. The paper presents Algorithm 1 as [MMR]'s protocol ("We recall the total-order broadcast protocol proposed by [MMR]", and its correctness is delegated to [MMR]'s Lemmas 6–7). [MMR] requires the proposal to extend L_{v-1} and inputs L_{v-1} otherwise. The paper's proofs reason with that version: Lemma 2 ("outputs Λ with grade 1, and thus multi-casts a vote message for an extension of it"), Lemma 3's inductive step, and Theorem 2 ("deciding Λ' would imply multi-casting a vote for an extension of Λ'"). The fix is one word plus a missing case in pseudocode. Reading it as the intended rule rests on the paper's own proofs and on its attribution to [MMR]. The [MMR] ePrint could not be retrieved during the review, so its exact wording was not re-checked. We formalize the "extends" rule (Exec.Follows), and show that it is a special case of the printed rule (Exec.Follows.followsLiteral).

  2. Definition 4, integrity: "there exists a well-behaved process that inputs the log". Taken literally, this is false for every graded agreement with validity (integrity_literal_false), so Lemma 1's claim "satisfies the original properties" is literally false. Label: ✏️. The proof of Lemma 1 proves, and [MMR] states, "some honest input extends the log". We formalize that.

  3. Lemma 1 / clique validity: hypotheses too narrow for its uses. Stated for H' ⊆ H_r ∪ H_{r+1} and receivers in H' ∩ H_{r+1}. Lemma 2 applies it with H' = H_ra \ B_r', which may contain processes asleep in rounds r' and r'+1. Theorem 2 needs it for all receivers of round ra+π+1. Label: 🔧 (proof gap). The statement as written is true (lemma_1_clique_validity). The general form (H' disjoint from B_r, any receiver whose M0 contains the clique's votes) has the same proof (clique_validity).

  4. Lemma 3, base case. "Follows from graded consistency": insufficient, since graded consistency yields only grade ≥ 0, and a longer conflicting grade-0 output may exist (graded_consistency_insufficient). Label: 🔧 (proof gap). Repaired with [MMR]'s argument: integrity of GA_{v-1,2} plus uniqueness of GA_{v-1,1} (Exec.Follows.decision_lock). This uses the synchrony and η-sleepiness of rounds r-2, r-1. In the paper's network model (Section 2.1: in a synchronous round every awake honest receiver gets all messages sent so far, gossip included), the receivers of a synchronous round have the same messages and hence the same tally. So the conclusion also follows directly in the paper's model. graded_consistency_insufficient shows that the cited GA property alone does not justify it. That example lets Byzantine processes send different votes to different receivers, which an abstract GA allows.

Implicit assumptions made explicit

  1. Synchronous-round assumption. Section 2.3 states Equations 1 (churn) and 2 (failure ratio β̃). Section 3.3 and the proofs use η-sleepiness (Equation 3). We assume Equation 3 in every round outside [ra+1, ra+π+1] (where Equation 4 replaces it). We prove that Equations 1 and 2 imply 3 for 0 ≤ γ < β < 1 (Exec.sleepy_of_churn_of_failure), and that the paper's β̃ makes the worst-case bound exact (betaTilde_identity). The paper does not state this implication; it is consistent with Figure 1. The "must have" (necessity) of β̃ is not formalized.

  2. Lemma 2 uses validity at round ra, so round ra must be η-sleepy (assumed). We also assume ra ≥ 1: in round 0 nobody votes, and the round-1 vote is unconstrained (degenerate case).

  3. Lemma 3 needs synchrony (automatic for rounds ≤ ra) and η-sleepiness of all rounds in [r-2, ra] (assumed for all rounds ≤ ra). The hypothesis r ≤ ra is dropped (vacuous).

  4. Theorem 2 additionally needs η-sleepiness in rounds ≥ ra+π+2 for the synchronous induction after the asynchronous period ("apply the same inductive reasoning of MMR").

  5. Lemma 1, original properties: hold in synchronous rounds. The paper states this only in the proof ("for all properties other than clique validity, network synchrony is assumed").

Modelling choices

  1. Rounds and receivers. H_{r+1} (awake at the beginning of r+1) are exactly the receivers of round r, as in the paper. Received messages are kept across sleep (rcv_mono); a process that wakes up in a synchronous round receives everything sent so far.

  2. Byzantine messages. Arbitrary sets of messages with sender in B_t, sent in round t, with arbitrary round tags (back-dating allowed). The sender of every received message tagged s lies in O_s (honest) or in B_r (Byzantine), so senders of unexpired messages lie in O_{r-η,r}, as the paper uses.

  3. Equivocation. A sender whose latest unexpired messages carry different logs is discarded entirely, for the round-r messages and for M0 alike, as in Figure 3. No fallback to an older message.

  4. Proposals and VRF. Not modelled. The odd-round vote of an honest process is required only to extend some longest output L_{v-1} of the previous GA. This over-approximates every outcome of the VRF lottery, including Byzantine winners, so the safety theorems hold for all outcomes. In the counterexample the votes are realised by concrete proposals: a Byzantine view-0 proposal [1] winning view 1 (honest view-0 proposals are the genesis log), the Byzantine proposal [] (genesis, the same log honest processes propose in view 0) in round 3, and the honest proposal b‖C_2 = [2] in round 5. Shifting the run by one view (honest genesis votes in view 1, honest proposal [1] in view 2, Byzantine [] in view 3, honest [2] in view 4) needs a single Byzantine lottery win (argued on paper, not formalized).

  5. Even rounds without a grade-1 output (possible only under asynchrony): the vote is unconstrained (the paper leaves this undefined).

  6. View 1. Round 1 votes are unconstrained (any proposal extends genesis), and decisions start in round 3 (GA_{0,2} does not exist; see the typo list).

  7. Validity is stated for every common prefix of the honest inputs. It is equivalent to the paper's "the longest common prefix is output with grade 1", because grade-1 outputs are prefix-closed.

  8. Bounded divergence is formalized as "any pairwise conflicting family of output logs has at most 2 members".

  9. P0 in the bridge (Exec.ga) is taken to be O_{r-η,r}, a superset of the actual senders of M0. The quorum hypothesis of Lemma 1 then is exactly η-sleepiness, the step argued in Section 3.3.

  10. Logs are List α with the prefix order; the genesis block is the empty log [].

  11. Thresholds: β = 1/3 (decision threshold 2/3) in Sections 3.x, as in the paper; Section 2.3 results are for general real β, γ.

Typos / small slips (no effect)

  1. Proof of Theorem 2: |H_ra \ H_{ra+π+1}| should be |H_ra \ B_{ra+π+1}|.

  2. Proof of Lemma 2: |S_r' ∪ P0| should be |O_r' ∪ P0|. The claim that the latest messages in M0 are "all from rounds no later than ra" is inaccurate (later honest votes may be latest), but those votes extend Λ by the induction hypothesis, which is how we prove it.

  3. Algorithm 1, line 1 for v = 1 refers to GA_{0,2}, which does not exist.

Not formalized

  • Theorem 1, liveness half (probabilistic, VRF-based, deferred to [MMR]'s Lemma 7).

  • Theorem 3 (healing after asynchrony), informal two-line proof about liveness.

  • Necessity of β̃ (Figure 1 caption, Section 2.3 "must").
