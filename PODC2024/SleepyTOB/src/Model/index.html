import Papers.PODC2024.SleepyTOB.ExtendedGA /-! # The η-sleepy model with one bounded asynchronous period, and the modified Algorithm 1 D'Amato, Losa, Zanolini, PODC 2024, Sections 2.1, 2.3 and 3.3. **Model** (`Exec`). * `n` processes (`Fintype P`), rounds `0, 1, 2, …`. `O r`: processes awake at (the beginning of) round `r`; `B r ⊆ O r`: Byzantine processes at round `r`; the adversary is *growing* (`B r ⊆ B (r+1)`; Byzantine processes never sleep). `H r = O r \ B r`. The processes awake at the end of round `r` are those awake at the beginning of round `r + 1`, so the receivers of round `r` are `H (r+1)`. * Messages are vote messages `(sender, round tag, log)` (signed: a message with sender `q` was sent by `q`). In every round `t ≥ 1` each `q ∈ H t` multicasts exactly one vote `(q, t, vote t q)`; a Byzantine `q ∈ B t` may send any set `byz t` of messages with sender `q`, with arbitrary round tags. * `rcv p r`: messages received by `p` by the end of round `r`. Only sent messages are received, received messages are kept (`rcv_mono`), and in a *synchronous* round `r` every `p ∈ H (r+1)` has received every message sent in rounds `≤ r` (`rcv_sync`). All rounds are synchronous except the rounds `ra + 1, …, ra + π` of the single asynchronous period, in which delivery is arbitrary. * Message expiration `η`: the GA instance of round `r` is computed by `p ∈ H (r+1)` from the *latest* messages with round tag in `[r - η, r]` that `p` has received (`tally`), discarding a sender whose latest messages equivocate. **Protocol** (`Follows`, the modified Algorithm 1, with the input rule of [MMR]). View `v ≥ 1` consists of rounds `2v - 1` (GA_{v,1}) and `2v` (GA_{v,2}); the GA of round `r` is tallied at the beginning of round `r + 1`. * Odd round `r ≥ 3` (round 1 of view `v ≥ 2`): decide every log output with grade 1 by the GA of round `r - 1` (`Decides`); let `L` be a longest log output with any grade; vote for (the proposal with the highest VRF among those that) extend(s) `L`, or `L` itself. As the adversary controls the proposals that can win the VRF lottery, we only require `L <+: vote`. * Even round `r ≥ 2`: vote for the longest log output with grade 1 by the GA of round `r - 1` (if there is one; otherwise the vote is unconstrained). * Round 1 (view 1): vote for any proposal (unconstrained). The paper's Algorithm 1 says "not conflicting with `L`" instead of "extending `L`"; that version is unsafe, see `Counterexample.lean`. -/ namespace Podc.SleepyTOB open Finset noncomputable section
