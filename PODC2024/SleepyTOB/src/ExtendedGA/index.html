import Papers.PODC2024.SleepyTOB.Tally /-! # The extended graded agreement (Figure 3) and Lemma 1 D'Amato, Losa, Zanolini, PODC 2024, Section 3.2. One instance of the extended graded agreement runs in a round `r`: * `H = H_r`, `B = B_r`: honest and Byzantine processes awake at round `r` (`O_r = H ∪ B`); `Hnext = H_{r+1}`: the honest processes awake at the end of round `r` (the receivers). Since the adversary is growing (`B_r ⊆ B_{r+1}`), `Hnext` is disjoint from `B`. * Every receiver `p` starts with an initial vote assignment `M0 p` (at most one message per process, equivocations already discarded), all from senders in `P0`. * In the send phase each honest `q ∈ H` multicasts its input `input q`; `R p q` is the set of round-`r` vote messages that `p` received from `q`. Honest senders send only their input; processes not awake at round `r` send nothing; Byzantine senders send anything. * `M p` (the paper's `M_r^p`): the round-`r` vote of `q` if `p` received exactly one, nothing if `q` equivocated in round `r`, and the initial vote `M0 p q` if `p` received no round-`r` vote from `q`. * In a synchronous round every receiver gets every honest round-`r` vote (`Sync`). Lemma 1 holds under the assumption `|H_r| > 2|O_r ∪ P0|/3` (`Quorum`): the five graded-agreement properties of Definition 4 in synchronous rounds (`lemma_1`), and clique validity in every round (`lemma_1_clique_validity`, generalised in `clique_validity`). -/ namespace Podc.SleepyTOB open Finset noncomputable section open Classical variable {P α : Type*} [Fintype P] /-- One instance of the extended graded agreement of Figure 3 (round `r`). -/ structure ExtGA (P α : Type*) [Fintype P] where /-- `H_r`: honest processes awake at round `r` (the senders). -/ H : Finset P /-- `B_r`: Byzantine
