We define * `valAfter E O σ` — the value of `O` after the processes of the schedule `σ` take one step each; * `U z D α O v` — the paper's `U_v`: values of `O` after schedules starting with a team-`v` process; * `Hiding z D α O v` — `Cα` is `v`-hiding; `Recording z D α O` — `Cα` is `n`-recording; * `lam k` — the schedule `λ_k = c_k c_{k+1} … c_{n-1}`; and prove Observation 11 (`obs11`) and that an `n`-recording configuration makes the type of `O` `n`-recording (`Recording.isRecording`).
