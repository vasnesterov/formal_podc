We prove the slightly stronger bound for the registers **not** last written by `A` (this also counts registers never written); the printed form is `Exec.lemma_4_5` in `Lemmas.lean`: > If, from time `t₂` on, every read by a member of `A` returns a value last written by a member of > `A`, and some member of `A` is live, then at time `t₂` at most `|A|` registers are not last > written by a member of `A`. Proof (the paper's): take the first scan of a member of `A` that starts at or after `t₂` and completes. Until it completes, each member of `A` writes at most once (a second write would come after an earlier completed scan).
