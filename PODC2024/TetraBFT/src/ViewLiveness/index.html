This is the paper's Lemma 5 combined with Lemmas 2 and 4 and the timing budget of a view: the proof/suggest messages (sent when the nodes entered `v`, before `t0`), the proposal, and vote-1, vote-2, vote-3, vote-4: six hops, each costing one message delay `δ` plus one tick of processing in this discrete-time model (the paper's "6Δ"). It does *not* establish that such a synchronized interval ever occurs; for the view-change rule of the paper it need not (`Counterexample.lean`). -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false
