import Papers.PODC2019.StrongLinSnapshots.SnapDefs import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.SharedMemory.LinPoints /-! # Algorithm 3: the simulation invariant Linearization points (R-1, R-2 of the paper): an `SLscan` at its last `R.DRead` (line 49); an `SLupdate_p(x)` at the first linearization point of an `SLscan` that returns a vector with `x` in entry `p` while the update is pending, or at its own `R.DWrite` (line 45), whichever comes first. Updates linearized at the same step precede the scan and are ordered by process id (U-2, U-3). Invariant (with `S` atomic, the paper's "interpreted value" of `S` is its actual value): * `g1`: every entry `S[p]` equals the abstract entry `Q[p]`, unless `p` has executed line 43 of an update that is not yet linearized; `g1'`: after line 43 of `update(x)`, `S[p] = x`; `g2`: a linearized pending update `update(x)` of `p` has `Q[p] = x`. * `rf`: a scanner `q` that read `s₁` on line 47 sees `R = s₁` as long as its ABA flag is clear. * `good` (the core of Lemmas 19–22): if a scanner `q` read `s₁` on line 47 and `ℓ = s₁` on line 48 and no `R.DWrite` happened since its line 47, then for every `p`: `S[p] = ℓ[p]` unless `p` is after line 43 of an unlinearized update, and `Q[p] = ℓ[p]` unless `p` has a pending unlinearized `update(ℓ[p])`. Hence linearizing those updates at the scan's line 49 makes the abstract state equal to `ℓ`, the scan's response. -/ namespace Podc.StrongLinSnapshots.Snap open Cslib.SharedMemory
