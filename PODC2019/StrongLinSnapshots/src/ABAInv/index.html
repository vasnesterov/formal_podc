import Papers.PODC2019.StrongLinSnapshots.ABADefs /-! # Algorithm 2: the simulation invariant The linearization points are those of the paper (Q-1, Q-2): a `DWrite` at its write to `X` (line 2), a `DRead` at its last read of `X` (line 37, the one that ends the loop). The invariant relates a configuration to the abstract state `(x, F)` of the linearization (`F q` = "some `DWrite` was linearized since `q`'s last linearized `DRead`"). * `RInv` (reader `q`): while `q` is *clean* (`CleanPC`: idle, writing, about to return, or in the first iteration of a `DRead` whose announcement `A[q]` is unchanged), `F q ↔ X.tag ≠ A[q]`; within the loop, the values read so far determine the outcome. * `WInv` (writer `p`): if `X` carries a tag `(p, s')` then `s'` is the newest (or, after a new `GetSeq`, the second newest) entry of `usedQ`; a chosen but not yet written `s` is not among the older entries. * `ProtBy` (Observation 6(b) of the paper, as an invariant): if a clean reader `q` announces `A[q] = (p, s')`, then `p` will not use `s'` before reading `A[q]` again: either `(q, s') ∈ na` or `s'` is in `usedQ` at a position that keeps it there until `p` reads `A[q]` again. -/ namespace Podc.StrongLinSnapshots open Cslib.SharedMemory Cslib.SharedMemory.ABADetection
