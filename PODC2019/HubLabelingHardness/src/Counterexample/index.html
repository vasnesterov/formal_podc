import Papers.PODC2019.HubLabelingHardness.Main /-! # The claim `d_G(u, v) = d_H(u, v)` in the proof of Lemma 2.2 is false The proof of Lemma 2.2 asserts: "for any `u ∈ V_i` and `v ∈ V_j` with `i < j`, we have `dist_G(u, v) = dist_H(u, v)`", justified "from the construction of `G_{b,ℓ}` for `j = i + 1`". This fails for non-adjacent `u ∈ V_0`, `v ∈ V_1`: in `G` a walk may enter the in-tree of an upper vertex through one leaf and leave it through another leaf *without visiting that vertex*, saving up to `2b + 2 - 2` steps compared with the corresponding `H`-walk, which has to pass through the vertex. Concretely, `b = 1`, `ℓ = 2` (so `s = 2`, `A = 24`), `u = v_{0,(0,0)}`, `v = v_{1,(0,1)}`: * every `H`-walk from `u` to `v` has weight `≥ 3A + 1 = 73` (`le_wlen_cex`; it needs `≥ 3` edges by parity and some edge changes coordinate 2); * `G` has a walk of length `71 = 3A - 1`: `u → v_{1,(0,0)} → v_{2,(0,1)}`'s in-tree (leaf `0`, root, leaf `1`) `→ v_{1,(0,1)}`, turning around inside the in-tree of `v_{2,(0,1)}` (`edist_cex_le`).
