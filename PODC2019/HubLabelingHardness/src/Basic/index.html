Notation (0-indexed coordinates; the paper indexes coordinates from 1): * `s = 2^b` (`side`), `A = 3ℓs²` (`bigA`). * `Main b ℓ = Fin (2ℓ+1) × (Fin ℓ → Fin s)`: the vertex `v_{i,j}` of `H_{b,ℓ}` (level `i`, vector `j`). * `cnat ℓ i`: the coordinate that may change between levels `i` and `i+1` (`i` for `i < ℓ`, `2ℓ-1-i` for `i ≥ ℓ`; the paper's `c = i+1`, resp. `c = 2ℓ-i`). * `H b ℓ`: `v_{i,j} ~ v_{i+1,j'}` iff `j, j'` agree outside coordinate `cnat ℓ i`; weight `hw u v = A + Σ_k (u_k - v_k)²` (which is `A + (j_c - j'_c)²` on edges), `wlen` = weight of a walk. * `GV b ℓ`, `G b ℓ`: the max-degree-3 graph `G_{b,ℓ}`: every vertex `v` of `H` gets an in-tree (`tin`, leaves = lower neighbours, absent at level 0) and an out-tree (`tout`, leaves = upper neighbours, absent at level `2ℓ`), perfect binary trees with `s` leaves in heap numbering (node `n` has children `2n+1, 2n+2`;
