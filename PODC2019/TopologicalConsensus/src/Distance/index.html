That this is a topology is Lemma 4.1. No symmetry, triangle inequality or definiteness is needed. * `expDist bad = 2^{-inf {t | bad t}}` (with `2^{-∞} = 0`), the common shape of all execution distances of the paper; `expDist_lt_pow_iff`:
