import Papers.PODC2019.TopologicalConsensus.Characterization /-! # Counterexamples to the "if" directions of Theorems 5.2 and 5.3 1. `CexUniform.theorem_5_2_false`: the paper's Theorem 5.2 ("uniform consensus is solvable iff `Σ` has a `d_u`-clopen partition with validity") is false, even for lock-step synchronous full-information models (`LockstepRecall` holds). Two processes that never communicate; the view of a process is (time, own input). Executions: `α` (inputs `0,0`), `β` (inputs `1,1`) and `δ` (inputs `0,1`) in which process `0` is disobedient from time `1` on (it suffers, say, an omission failure that it cannot observe). Since `d_u` only counts processes that are obedient in *both* executions, `d_u(α, δ) = 1/2`, and `{α}`, `{β, δ}` is a `d_u`-clopen partition with validity. But process `0` decides `0` in `α`, hence also in `δ` (same view), while process `1` decides `1` in `β`, hence also in `δ`: uniform agreement fails in `δ`. (The corrected distance `d_uc` of `Characterization.lean` has `d_uc(α, δ) = 0`.) 2. `CexAsync.theorem_5_3_false_async`: in the generic model of Section 3 (weak clocks only), both theorems are false. Two non-communicating, always obedient processes whose view is (own input, number of own steps); global time is not visible. In `α` (inputs `0,0`) and `β` (inputs `1,1`) both processes step at even times, in `δ` (inputs `0,1`) at odd times. All pairwise distances are `≥ 1/2`, so `Σ` is discrete and `{α, δ}`, `{β}` is a clopen partition with validity, but consensus is impossible: process `0` sees the same views in `α` and `δ` (only
