import Papers.PODC2019.TopologicalConsensus.Broadcast import Papers.PODC2019.TopologicalConsensus.LossyLink /-! # Canonical strong broadcaster decision sets need not be closed (Theorem 7.9, Corollaries 8.2, # 8.3, Lemma 8.6, Corollary 8.7) Definition 7.8 assigns every connected component `Σ_γ` of `Σ` its *canonical* broadcaster `b(Σ_γ)`: the smallest process that is a broadcaster in every execution of the component. The strong broadcaster decision sets are `Σ^p_v = ⋃ {Σ_γ | b(Σ_γ) = p, I_p(γ) = v}`. Theorem 7.9 and Corollary 8.2 claim that consensus is solvable iff (i) every component is broadcastable and (ii) the sets `Σ^p_v` are clopen (closed); Corollary 8.3 claims that for limit-closed message adversaries they are clopen and pairwise at positive distance, and Lemma 8.6 / Corollary 8.7 claim that their `ε`-approximations coincide with them for small `ε`. Counterexample (two processes, a limit-closed message adversary): `MAex = {σ_n | n ∈ ℕ} ∪ {σ_∞}`, where `σ_∞ = (1 → 0)^ω` and `σ_n` is `σ_∞` with round `n + 1` replaced by `0 ↔ 1`. * Consensus is solvable: everybody knows the input of process `1` after round 1 (`solvable`). * Every execution `(x, σ_n)` is isolated, so `{(x, σ_n)}` is a connected component; both processes broadcast in `σ_n`, so its canonical broadcaster is `0`. * In `(y, σ_∞)` process `0` is not a broadcaster, so the canonical broadcaster of its component is `1`. * `(y, σ_∞)` is the limit of `(x, σ_n)` with `x = (0, y₁)` (process 1 hears nothing up to round `n`), so `(y, σ_∞) ∈ closure Σ^0_0 \ Σ^0_0`: `Σ^0_0` is not closed (`theorem_7_9_canonical_false`). * For every `ε > 0`, `(y, σ_∞)` lies in both `ε`-approximations `Σ^{0,ε}_0` and `Σ^{1,ε}_{y₁}` (`corollary_8_7_false`). With an existentially chosen broadcaster (here: always `1`) the decision sets are clopen (`Broadcast.dn_theorem_7_9_exists`), so only the canonical choice is at fault. -/ namespace TopoConsensus
