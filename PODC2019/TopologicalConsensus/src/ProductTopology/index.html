We prove the lemma by a chain argument instead: the open sets of `T_d` are upward closed under the reflexive-transitive closure of `d x y = 0` (`isOpen_reach`), and a `prodDist`-open set containing `γ` with radius `2^{-T}` contains every `δ` whose coordinates `≤ T` are reachable from those of `γ` (`mem_of_reach`). -/ namespace TopoConsensus open Set Topology Relation set_option
