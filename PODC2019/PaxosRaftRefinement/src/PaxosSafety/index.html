We prove it for the model of `Paxos.lean`. The proof is Lamport-style: votes are the `acceptOK` messages `m2b`; `SafeAt i b v` says that no value other than `v` can have been or can still be chosen in instance `i` at a ballot below `b`; the inductive invariant `Inv` says that every proposal is safe, that a ballot has at most one proposal per instance and at most one leader, and relates logs and `prepareOK` reports to votes. -/ namespace
