import Mathlib /-! # MultiPaxos (Figure 1 of [paper]), as an interleaving transition system [paper]: Z. Wang, C. Zhao, S. Mu, H. Chen, J. Li. *On the Parallels between Paxos and Raft, and how to Port Optimizations.* PODC 2019 (extended version with Appendices A–C). Model (as in the paper's TLA+ appendix B.1 and in its proof, Appendix C): * a finite set of servers `N`, each playing proposer, acceptor and learner; values `V`; ballots `ℕ` (initially `0`, `Phase1a` increments the ballot, as in Figure 1); * asynchronous message passing over an unreliable network: every message ever sent stays in a set (`m1a`, `m1b`, `m2a`, `m2b`); a server "receives" a message by reading it from the set, so messages may be lost (never read), duplicated (read twice) and reordered. Crash-stop failures are servers that stop taking steps; no step is ever forced; * each server keeps `ballot`, `leader` (`phase1Succeeded` in Figure 1), its log `inst : ℕ → Option (ℕ × V)` (`instances`, an entry is `(accepted ballot, value)`) and its `chosen` set (`chosenSet`); * as in Appendix C, the `unchosen` optimisation of Figure 1 is dropped: `prepareOK` carries the whole log. Repairs of Figure 1 (all recorded in `differencies.md`): * `phase1a` resets `leader` (as `IncreaseHighestBallot` in the TLA+ spec) and also produces the proposer's own (implicit) `prepareOK`; * `phase1Succeed` is guarded by `¬ leader` and needs the proposer's own report among the quorum's (both as `BecomeLeader` in the TLA+ spec); it sets the whole log to the safe entries (indices above the largest reported one become empty); * `phase2a` may not propose a second value for the same instance and ballot (Figure 1 records nothing when proposing, so as written a leader can propose two values); * `learn` only adds to `chosen` (Figure 1 also overwrites the instance with `(b, v)`); the `acceptOK` messages are visible to every learner (the paper's "notify other servers").
