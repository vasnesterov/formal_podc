We prove (`speedup2_ineq`) that for every threshold `f ≥ 0` `f^(Δ-1) · (p' − (Δ−1) c f) ≤ p`, where `p` and `p'` are the local failure probabilities of `B` and of the node algorithm. This is the inequality `p ≥ (p' − 3cf) f³` of the paper's proof. In the proof the paper's node `v'` is the origin and `v = dir (d₀, false)`, so that `v'` is the neighbour of `v` in the positive direction of dimension `d₀`. -/ namespace Podc.MinimalSymmetryBreaking
