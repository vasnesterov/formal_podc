Olivetti, J. Suomela, *Hardness of minimal symmetry breaking in distributed computing*, PODC 2019 (arXiv:1811.01643), Section 3.1. Model: deterministic LOCAL, extensional locality (`Cslib.Local.IsLocal R f`: the output of a node depends only on its radius-`R` view). The algorithm below is anonymous (it does not use the node names / identifiers) and works on arbitrary simple graphs (no finiteness or degree bound). ## Main definitions * `IsDistWeakColoring G k φ` (Definition 1): every node has a node of a different colour within distance `k`. * `IsWeakColoring G ψ`: every node has a neighbour of a different colour. * `dmin k G φ v`: the distance from `v` to the closest node of a different colour (searched within distance `k`); `parityColor k G φ v = (φ v, dmin k G φ v mod 2)`. * `greedyAlg`: the locally-iterative greedy MIS by colour classes on the subgraph of bichromatic edges; `weakTwo N G col`: its output after `N` rounds. * `lemma2Alg k c`: the composed algorithm. ## Main results * `parityColor_isWeakColoring` (first step of the proof of Lemma 2): `(φ, dmin mod 2)` is a weak `2c`-colouring, computed in `k` rounds (`parityColor_isLocal`). * `weakTwo_isWeakColoring`, `weakTwo_isLocal`: a weak colouring with values `< N` gives a weak 2-colouring in `N` rounds (greedy MIS of the bichromatic subgraph by colour classes). * `lemma_2_explicit`, `lemma_2` (Lemma 2): from a distance-`k` weak `c`-colouring a weak 2-colouring is computed
