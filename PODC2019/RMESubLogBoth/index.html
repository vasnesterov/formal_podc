Recoverable Mutex with Sub-logarithmic RMR on CC and DSM — PODC 2019

Prasad Jayanti, Siddhartha Jayanti, Anup Joshi. A Recoverable Mutex Algorithm with Sub-logarithmic RMR on Both CC and DSM. PODC 2019. DOI 10.1145/3293611.3331634. Formalized version: arXiv 1904.02124 v2 (full version, proofs in Appendices C–E); numbering follows it.

DOI · arXiv · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 9, the 39-condition invariant on which all the correctness proofs rest, is false as stated: several of its conditions fail in crash-free runs with two processes. We replace it with our own invariant and a new liveness argument, and prove that the k-ported algorithm of Figures 3–4 is correct: mutual exclusion, starvation freedom (finitely many crashes), wait-free exit, wait-free critical-section re-entry and critical-section re-entry (Lemmas 4–8), and Theorem 1 on the Signal object. The paper's main point, the sub-logarithmic RMR bounds of Theorems 2 and 3, was not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

🛠️ Lemma 9, Condition 5 (and hence the proofs of Lemmas 4 and 5)

Lemma 9 claims that all 39 conditions hold in every configuration, but Condition 5 fails as soon as one process has executed line 14 in a crash-free run (PaperInv.cond5_csSignal_false, PaperInv.cond5_nonNil_false).

Impact. The proofs of Lemmas 4 and 5 (mutual exclusion and starvation freedom) cite conditions of this invariant, so they are not established by the paper: the proof of Lemma 4 is one line from Condition 19, and the proof of Lemma 5 uses Conditions 15 and 19. The algorithm itself is correct: we prove Lemmas 4–8 from a new invariant (inv_reach).

Details

  • Condition 5 says qnode.CS_Signal = 0 ⇒ qnode.Pred ∈ {NIL, &Crash, &InCS}. Every waiting node (lines 15 and 25) has CS_Signal = 0 and Pred pointing to its predecessor. So does an exiting node at line 28, with Pred = &Exit.

  • Condition 5 also says qnode.NonNil_Signal = 0 ⇒ qnode.Pred = NIL. Between lines 14 and 15 (and between lines 18 and 23 after a crash) Pred is already set while NonNil_Signal is still 0.

  • The fixes are not evident from the paper: one would have to add node values and &Exit to the right-hand side, which makes the conjunct nearly empty. Reversing the implications gives true statements, but they merely restate Condition 5's = 1 conjuncts. The proof of Lemma 9 (Appendix E) claims that the conjunction of all conditions is preserved by every step, but never re-checks Condition 5 at line 14.

Repair

Our invariant (Inv, InvDefs.lean) is preserved by every step and crash (inv_reach); Lemmas 4–8 follow from it.

Lemma 4 (mutual_exclusion). The new invariant's core: every node has at most one successor, nobody is behind Tail, and at most one node "holds the right to the CS" (InH). A node holds it if it is in the CS, is behind a released node, or is exiting with a successor. The hard part is the repair code (lines 30–49). We characterise what the non-atomic scan of Node[] still says when the decision at line 46 is taken. It uses a ghost set of nodes that had already done their FAS when Tail was read (line 31): every later node hangs behind that Tail value. Then the FAS branch never creates a cycle. In the attach branch, the chosen predecessor has no successor and is not Tail. If headpath = NIL, nobody holds the right to the CS (ScanInv.fas_facts, ScanInv.attach_facts, ScanInv.none_noH).

Lemma 5 (starvation_freedom), true under finitely many crashes and the paper's fairness conditions. After the last crash, every recovering process gets through RLock. The repair terminates: the waits at line 35 end, and the loop over maximal paths is finite. Eventually nobody is in the recovery code or in RLock (eventually_no_rec). From then on, the unique holder of the right to the CS is an ancestor of every waiting node. A rank (10 × number of unreleased ancestors + stage of the holder) never increases and drops at every step of the holder (token_step, phase2_cs).

🛠️ Lemma 9, Condition 4 (third conjunct)

Condition 4 asks that Pred be iterated at most k times from a node to reach {NIL, &Crash, &InCS, &Exit}, which fails once two processes are queued (PaperInv.cond4_false).

Impact. The paper uses this bound to exclude cycles in the proof of Condition 23. The correctness lemmas it served are proved from our invariant (inv_reach).

Details

With k = 2 and both processes queued behind SpecialNode, the second node needs 3 hops (node → first node → SpecialNode → &Exit). The proof's Condition 4 case counts b1 + b2 ≤ k and forgets the fragment's head (SpecialNode or an exited node).

Repair

The bound should be k + 1. We did not prove the invariant with this bound; the proved repair is our invariant (Inv, inv_reach).

✏️ Slips

  • Condition 18 quantifies over the real program counter PC_π' where \hat{PC}_π' is meant (as in Condition 19(g)). Read literally it fails in the initial configuration, where every PC_π = 10.

  • Theorem 2 claims O(1) RMRs "for a process that does not crash during its passage". A passage that starts after a crash runs RLock and the repair scan of all k entries of Node[], even if no crash happens during it. The intended statement is about crash-free super-passages, and O(fk) should read O(1 + fk). Not formalized.

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Asynchronous shared memory with NVRAM and individual crash–recovery failures. A crash resets the program counter to line 10 and erases all local variables. Shared memory is unaffected. Deterministic algorithm; executions are arbitrary interleavings of steps and crashes (an adversarial scheduler).

  • One process per port (Fin k). The paper's n ≥ k processes are allowed one super-passage per port at a time. A process accesses only Node[p] of its own port, and its local state is wiped at every crash. So identifying processes with ports loses nothing for the properties proved.

  • One shared-memory access per step. Lines 11–12 form one step (allocation and Node[p] ← mynode), and lines 16–17 are merged into line 10. Lines 39–41 and the order of the loop at line 42 are nondeterministic (any maximal path, any order), and all results hold for every resolution.

  • Signal objects are atomic bits (set = one write, wait = busy-wait reads). Theorem 1 treats the implementation of Figure 2 separately.

  • RLock is an abstract lock variable: line 24 acquires it if it is free or already held by the caller (re-entry after a crash), and otherwise busy-waits. Any order of acquisition is allowed, so the safety results hold for every mutual exclusion lock with re-entry. Starvation freedom needs no fairness assumption on RLock: after the last crash only finitely many acquisitions remain.

  • Liveness: runs are infinite sequences of events. Fairness (Fair) is the paper's run condition: a process outside the remainder section later takes a step or crashes. FinCrash says that only finitely many crashes occur.

Remark on Theorem 1. It is proved for crash-free executions (Section 2 does not mention crashes) with linearization point "first completed set() or first wait() that reads Bit = 1". With crashes, a set() that crashes after line 1 leaves a spinning waiter with Bit = 1 (Signal.crash_spin, Signal.crash_rewait), so (iv) needs the interrupted set() to be re-executed. The algorithm of Figures 3–4 always does this (lines 22–23).

Not formalized. The RMR bounds of Theorem 2, Theorem 3 (arbitration tree), and the two scenarios of Appendix A against Golab–Hendler's algorithm. The composition of Figure 2's non-atomic Signal object with crashes inside Figures 3–4. Whether the paper's invariant with Conditions 4, 5 and 18 patched is inductive: we prove our own invariant instead. The informal argument of Lemma 5's proof for the case where the queue is broken ("applying the above argument inductively on these fragments …"): it cites the invariant, but no specific step was shown wrong, and we prove Lemma 5 by a different argument.

Trust. No sorry; the listed theorems use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. Before the Lean proof, explicit-state model checking (modelcheck/) confirmed mutual exclusion for k = 3 with up to 2 crashes and 5 passages (12.4M states), and starvation freedom for k = 2 with up to 2 crashes. The candidate invariant was checked on all reachable states for k ≤ 4 with bounded crashes and passages. These Python scripts (no dependencies; python3 mc.py K MAXCRASHES [live], python3 inv.py ...) are a sanity check only and use a FIFO RLock. Paper-local model; no CSLib dependency.
