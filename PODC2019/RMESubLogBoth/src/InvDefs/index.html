Central notions: * `vp q`: the *virtual predecessor* of port `q`: `mypred` while `q` is between the `FAS` and the write of `Pred` (lines 14 and 49), otherwise `Node[q].Pred`. * `Bh y z`: the node `y` of some port is behind `z` (`vp = z`). * `Rel z`: `z` is released (`Pred = &Exit` and `CS_Signal = 1`). * `InH y`: `y` holds the right to the CS: it is in the CS (`&InCS`), behind a released node, or exiting (`&Exit`, signal not yet set) with a successor (or as `Tail`). At most one node is in `InH` (`Glob.hUniq`), which gives mutual exclusion. * The repairer (the process in the CS of `RLock`) keeps facts about its partial view `(V, E)` of the queue. They mention the ghost set `EA` of *early* nodes: nodes that existed and had executed their `FAS` when the repairer read `Tail` (line 31). Nodes outside `EA` are *late*: they all hang behind the node `tail` read at line 31. -/ namespace Podc.RMESubLogBoth variable {k : ℕ} /-- `mypred` overrides `Node[q].Pred` at lines 14 and 49. -/ def
