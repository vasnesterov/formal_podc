Rosenbaum, *With Great Speed Come Small Buffers: Space-Bandwidth Tradeoffs for Routing*, PODC 2019 (arXiv 1902.08069). Theorem 5.1: for every `ρ > 1/(ℓ+1)` there is a `(ρ, 1)`-bounded adversary on a path with `n ≈ (ℓ+1) m^ℓ` nodes such that *every* forwarding protocol needs buffers of size `Ω(((ℓ+1)ρ - 1) m / (ℓ+1))`. ## Model * The network is the directed path `0 → 1 → ⋯`. A `Packet` has an injection round, a source and a destination. A finite packet family `P : ι → Packet` is an (offline) injection pattern; its count function `counts P` is used to state `(ρ, σ)`-boundedness with `Cslib.AQT.IsBounded`. * An `Execution P` is an arbitrary (offline, centralized, possibly clairvoyant) forwarding protocol: `pos t p` is the node holding `p` in round `t` (after injections, before forwarding); in each round a packet either stays or moves one hop towards its destination, and every edge carries at most one packet per round. A packet at its destination is delivered and occupies no buffer (`Execution.load`). ## The adversary (`lbAdv`) Rounds are grouped into phases of `m` rounds; the phase of round `t` is `P = t / m`, whose base-`m` digits are the paper's `t_ℓ … t_1` (`digit m P j = t_j`). The paper's sites are `v_i = ∑_{j=i}^{ℓ} ((j+1) m^j - (t_j+1) j m^{j-1})` (`site`, with `term_eq`), and `v_0 = (ℓ+1) m^ℓ` is the last node (the paper writes `n`; with `n = (ℓ+1) m^ℓ` nodes this is off by one, so the path here has `(ℓ+1) m^ℓ + 1` nodes). In every round `t < (m-1) m^ℓ` with `⌊ρ (t+1)⌋ ≠ ⌊ρ t⌋` (`jump`), one packet of each type `r = 0, …, ℓ` is injected at `v_{r+1}` with destination `v_r`; this makes precise the paper's informal "inject `ρm` packets per phase". The adversary is `(ρ, 1)`-bounded (`lbAdv_isBounded`) since the `ℓ+1` routes of a round are disjoint. ## Proof `front ℓ m t = v_1(t/m)` is the paper's `F(t)`. A packet is *fresh* in round `t` if it is at a node `≤ F(t)`. Claim 3 (`claim3`) and its consequence `site_one_lt` show Lemma 5.3 (`lemma_5_3`): a packet that has reached its destination is to the right of `F` (so fresh packets are undelivered). Lemma 5.2/5.4 (`becomeStale_pos`, `card_becomeStale_le_one`, `card_becomeStale_le_sum_load`): a packet that becomes stale at the end of round `t` lies in `[F(t+1), F(t)]` in round `t`, and if `F` does not move, at most one packet becomes stale; the paper's length of that interval, `F(t) - F(t+1) = ∑_{j=0}^{k} m^j`, is `lemma_5_4_drop` (not needed below). Instead of the dichotomy of Lemma 5.5 we use a global telescoping count (`Execution.card_injected_le_real`): with `L` the maximal load, at most `1 + 2 L (F(t) - F(t+1))` packets become stale at the end of round `t`, which sums to `T + 2 L F(0)`; the fresh packets at the end are at most `(F(T)+1) L`.
