import Mathlib /-! # The hierarchical partition of the path `⟨m^ℓ⟩` (Section 4.1 of the paper) Nodes are written in base `m` with `ℓ` digits. A packet at node `i` with destination `w > i` travels on a *segment* whose *level* `lv i w` is the most significant digit position in which `i` and `w` differ (Definition 4.2); its *intermediate destination* is `xd i w = ⌊w / m^j⌋ m^j` for `j = lv i w`. The pair `key i w = (lv i w, xd i w)` identifies the pseudo-buffer `L_{j,k}(i)` that holds the packet (Definition 4.3: `k` is the index of `xd i w` among the intermediate destinations of the level-`j` interval of `i`; we use the destination itself instead of its index). `lvl j v = ⌊v / m^(j+1)⌋ m^(j+1)` is the left endpoint of the level-`j` interval containing `v`. -/ namespace Podc.SmallBuffersAQT variable (m ℓ : ℕ) /-- The level `lv(i, w)` of the segment of a packet at `i` with destination `w`: the largest digit position `j < ℓ` (base `m`) where `i` and `w` differ. -/ def lv (i w : ℕ) : ℕ := Nat.findGreatest (fun j => i / m ^ j ≠ w / m ^ j) ℓ /-- The intermediate destination `x(i, w) = ⌊w / m^j⌋ m^j`, `j = lv(i, w)` (Definition 4.2). -/ def xd (i w : ℕ) : ℕ := w / m ^ lv m ℓ i w * m ^ lv m ℓ i w /-- The pseudo-buffer of a packet at node `i` with destination `w`: (level, intermediate destination). -/ def key (i w : ℕ) : ℕ × ℕ := (lv m ℓ i w, xd m ℓ i w) /-- Left endpoint of the level-`j` interval containing `v`. -/ def lvl (j v : ℕ) : ℕ := v / m ^ (j + 1) * m ^ (j + 1) variable {m ℓ} lemma two_le_of_lt {i w : ℕ} (hiw : i < w) (hw : w < m ^ ℓ) : 2 ≤ m ∧ 1 ≤ ℓ := by rcases Nat.lt_or_ge m 2 with h | h · interval_cases m · rcases ℓ with _ | ℓ <;> simp at hw;
