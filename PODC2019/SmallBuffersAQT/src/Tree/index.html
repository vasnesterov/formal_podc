import Mathlib import Podc.Computability.Distributed.AQT.Basic /-! # Propositions 3.5 and B.3: PPTS and PTS on directed trees (Appendix B.2) **Model.** A finite directed forest `T : DTree V` (each node has at most one parent, edges are directed towards the root(s); acyclicity is witnessed by a depth function). `T.Lt u v` (`u ≺ v`): `v` is a strict ancestor of `u`; `T.Le u v` (`u ≼ v`): ancestor-or-self. A configuration `c : V → V → ℕ` stores `c u w` packets at node `u` with destination `w` (meaningful only for `u ≺ w`). Every edge forwards at most one packet per round. A packet with source `a` and destination `w` (`a ≺ w`) has the buffer `v` in its route iff `a ≼ v ≺ w`. The adversary `A t a w` injects packets in round `t` at `a` with destination `w`; entries with `¬ a ≺ w` are ignored. Packets injected in round `t` are added before the forwarding step of round `t` (as in the paper); `exec` is the configuration after the injections of round `r`. **Algorithm 6 (PPTS on trees), closed form.** Destinations `W` with a topological numbering `idx` (`w ≺ w' → idx w < idx w'` on `W`, injective on `W`). Node `v` is activated for the destination `w ∈ W` of largest index such that `v ≺ w` and some node `u ≼ v` holds at least two packets with destination `w`; it then forwards one packet with destination `w` if it has one. `alg6` is the loop of Algorithm 6 (with `Path(u, w)` excluding `w`) and `alg6_eq_act` shows it equals this closed form; `alg6Incl_fwd_iff` shows that the paper's literal `Path(u, w)` (including `w`) forwards the same packets. With `W = {r}` this is the generalized PTS (`act_single`). **Badness.** `bad c v = Σ_{w ∈ W, v ≺ w} Σ_{u ≼ v} max(c u w - 1, 0)`. The paper (Appendix B.2) sums over *all* destinations `k ∈ ⟨d⟩`, which also counts bad packets whose destination is not above `v` (they never cross `v`); with that definition the invariant `B(v) ≤ ξ(v) + 1` fails (e.g. three packets injected at a child `u` of `v` with destination `v`: `ξ(v) = 0`, `B(v) = 2`). The restriction `v ≺ w` is the one of the path case (Definition 3.3). **Main results.** `bad_step_le` (Lemma B.5, generalized), `bad_exec_le` (invariant (3)), `prop_3_5`, `prop_3_5_depth` (Proposition
