import Papers.PODC2019.SmallBuffersAQT.Theorem41 /-! # Lemma 4.8 fails for the schedule of Algorithm 3 Lemma 4.8 claims that during one phase (one round at each level, no injections after the first round) the badness of every node with positive badness strictly decreases. Its proof uses that "levels are activated in decreasing order over the course of a phase". Algorithm 3, however, activates level `λ = t mod ℓ` in round `t`; phases start at `t ≡ 0 (mod ℓ)` (acceptance at `λ = 0`), so the levels of a phase are `0, 1, …, ℓ-1` (increasing). With the indexing used in the statement of Lemma 4.8 (phase = rounds `t+1, …, t+ℓ`, `t = (φ-1)ℓ`) they are `1, 2, …, ℓ-1, 0`. In both cases a level is processed *after* a lower level, and badness pushed down to that lower level (Case 2 of Claim 2) survives the phase. Instance: `m = 3`, `ℓ = 3` (27 nodes), node `8` holds two packets to `15`, node `9` one packet to `15`, node `10` two packets to `20` and one to `15`. Node `10` has badness `1` (a bad level-2 packet to intermediate destination `18`). In the level-2 round, node `8` forwards a packet to its level-2 intermediate destination `9`, where it becomes pre-bad at level 1; `ActivatePreBad` moves the level-1 packet of `9` to `10`, which is busy forwarding at level 2, so node `10` now has a bad level-1 pseudo-buffer: the badness of `10` stays `1`. The rounds at levels `0` and `1` do nothing.
