The pseudo-buffer `L_{j,k}(i)` of the paper is `pb c i (j, x)` where `x` is its intermediate destination. A round of HPTS at level `lam` (Algorithm 3, one value of `λ`): * `fpAct c lam`: the activation of `FormPaths` (Algorithm 4) in all level-`lam` intervals. We use the following closed form of the loop of Algorithm 4 (equivalently Algorithm 2, PPTS): node `v` activates the level-`lam` pseudo-buffer whose intermediate destination `x` is the largest among the bad (size `≥ 2`) level-`lam` pseudo-buffers at nodes `u ≤ v` with `x > v`. (In the loop, the interval `[i_k, min(i'-1, w_k-1)]` of destination `w_k` contains `v` iff `w_k` is that largest one.) * `preBadStep j`: `ActivatePreBad(A, j)` (Algorithm 5) for all level-`j` intervals `[a, b]`: if the packet that `a - 1` forwards into `a` is pre-bad at level `j` (Definition 4.6: its new pseudo-buffer `(j, x)` at `a` is nonempty) and `a` is inactive, then the pseudo-buffers `(j, x)` of the inactive prefix `[a, e]` of `[a, x - 1]` are activated.
