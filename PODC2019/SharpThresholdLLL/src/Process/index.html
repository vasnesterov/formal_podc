Every ordered pair `(t, u)` of adjacent events (an edge `{t,u}` of the dependency graph, seen from `t`) carries a value `φ t u`. Property `P*` (Definition 3.1, `PStar`) says that 1. `φ t u ≥ 0` and `φ t u + φ u t ≤ 2` on every edge, and 2. `Pr[E_v | fixed variables] ≤ p · ∏_{u ∈ N(v)} φ v u` for every event `v`. A step of the process (`Step`) fixes one unfixed variable `X_j` to a value of positive probability. It changes `φ` only on the edges inside the hyperedge `evs j` of `X_j`. It must re-establish `P*` *locally*, for the events of `evs j` and the edges among them. Every condition in `Step` involves only `X_j`, its events, and their incident edges. This is the locality claimed by the paper. * `step_pstar`: a step preserves `P*` globally. * `exists_step` (Lemma 3.2, generalised): if `P*` holds, then **every** unfixed variable `X_j` can be fixed by a step, provided the hyperedge `evs j` has the *fixing property* `FixingProperty`. The fixing property is the averaging statement of Lemma 3.9. We prove it for hyperedges of size `≤ 3` (`fixingProperty_of_card_le_three`). * `process_correct`: from the all-ones initial state, every reachable state has a step for every unfixed variable, and every reachable state with all variables fixed avoids all bad events, provided `Pr[E_v] ≤ p` and `p · 2^d < 1`.
