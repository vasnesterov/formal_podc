Let `ρ` be the root register of the tree `T₁` of `r₁`. * `caseA` — if some root-to-leaf path of `T₂` avoids `ρ`: the argument of the paper's proof of Lemma 4.2 (two reads around the first step of `W₁` that flips `ρ`) gives a contradiction; * `caseB` — if every root-to-leaf path of `T₂` reads `ρ`: the argument of the main part of the paper's proof of Theorem 4.1 (writes `W₀(a) W₁(z) W₂(b)`, Cases 1 and 2) gives a contradiction. The paper only proves (Lemma 4.2) that `T₁` and `T₂` have the same root and then runs the second argument; its proof of Lemma 4.2 needs a path of `T₂` avoiding `ρ`, whose existence it justifies incorrectly (see `Lemma42Gap.lean`).
