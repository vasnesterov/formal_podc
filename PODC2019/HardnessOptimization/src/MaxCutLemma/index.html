The paper proves Lemma 2.4 via Claims 2.9–2.12 about a maximum cut; the proof of Claim 2.9 needs the total weight of the non-`k⁴` edges to be `< k⁴`, which holds only for `k ≥ 128`. We give a direct counting proof that works for every `L ≥ 1`. For a 2-colouring `σ` let `U σ` be the number of uncut `k⁴`-edges. We show (`main_ineq`) `w(σ) + k⁴·U + 2·(uncut input edges between selected rows) + (k² + k)·(#badly placed rows) ≤ M + k³·U`, which gives at once `w(σ) ≤ M` for all cuts, that a cut of weight `≥ M` cuts all heavy edges (Claim 2.9, also for maximum cuts: `claim_2_9`), and that it then witnesses an intersection of `x` and `y`.
