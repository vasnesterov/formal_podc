1368

Yin, Malkhi, Reiter, Golan Gueta, Abraham — HotStuff: BFT Consensus with Linearity and Responsiveness

⚠️ Weakened. Doubling pacemaker fails, so liveness stays conditional on synchronized views. Basic safety needed a new proof; Chained HotStuff is unsafe as printed, fixed by a height check. Linear complexity not checked.

233

Abraham, Malkhi, Spiegelman — Asymptotically Optimal Validated Asynchronous Byzantine Agreement

⚠️ Weakened. Hard-coded 2f+1 breaks agreement for n > 3f+1, fixed by n−f; no halting rule, so total O(n²) messages unproved; Quality never proved; agreement, validity, expected O(1) views proved.

88

Abraham, Chan, Dolev, Nayak, Pass, Ren, Shi — Communication Complexity of Byzantine Agreement, Revisited

⚠️ Weakened. Both lower bounds hold with repaired proofs (Theorem 13 needs n >= C + 2); Theorem 7's proof for the subquadratic protocol has a wrong step not repaired in Lean; upper bounds otherwise unchecked.

81

Guerraoui, Kuznetsov, Monti, Pavlovič, Seredinschi — The Consensus Number of a Cryptocurrency

🛠️ Hard fix. Consensus numbers 1 and k hold, but Lemma 2's wait-freedom needed a new proof; Figure 1 needs a local fix and Figure 4 a one-term fix. Message-passing safety was not checked.

71

Brandt — An Automatic Speedup Theorem for Distributed Problems

⚠️ Weakened. The forward speedup and applications hold. The converse of Theorem 1, and with it reversibility and exact complexities, is false without edge symmetry breaking. Theorem 4 is checked only in the port-numbering model.

49

Aguilera, Ben-David, Guerraoui, Marathe, Zablotchi — The Impact of RDMA on Agreement

🔧 Easy fix. Protected Memory Paxos breaks agreement as printed, and two local fixes restore safety; the lower bound and Lemma 4.1 hold; the Byzantine algorithm and two-delay liveness were not checked.

45

Bachrach, Censor-Hillel, Dory, Efron, Leitersdorf, Paz — Hardness of Distributed Optimization

🛠️ Hard fix. Near-quadratic bounds for MDS, directed Hamiltonian path and cycle, Steiner tree and max-cut hold; Claim 2.2 needed a new proof, the others local fixes; undirected variants and Sections 3-5 unchecked.

38

Balliu, Hirvonen, Olivetti, Suomela — Hardness of Minimal Symmetry Breaking in Distributed Computing

🛠️ Hard fix. Ω(log* n) for weak 2-coloring holds after local proof repairs; Lemma 3's algorithm is wrong and was redesigned, so P* stays Θ(log n). The classification was not checked.

32

Nowak, Schmid, Winkler — Topological Characterization of Consensus under General Message Adversaries

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 5.5 and the limit form of Corollary 5.18 hold; the component and broadcastability characterizations (5.6, 5.11, 6.7) and Corollaries 6.1, 6.5 are false; Theorem 6.6 is unestablished.

31

Bodwin, Patel — A Trivial Yet Optimal Solution to Vertex Fault Tolerant Spanners

✏️ Slip. Theorem 1 and Corollary 2 hold for vertex and edge faults; Algorithm 1 leaves u, v not in F implicit, and the bound degenerates for f > n/2. Optimality was not checked.

30

Wang, Zhao, Mu, Chen, Li — On the Parallels between Paxos and Raft, and how to Port Optimizations

⚠️ Weakened. Printed specs are unsafe, but repaired Raft* provably refines repaired MultiPaxos; the porting guarantee holds only for single-step refinements, so it is not established for Raft*, the paper's actual case.

29

Khan, Naqvi, Vaidya — Exact Byzantine Consensus on Undirected Graphs under Local Broadcast Model

🔧 Easy fix. The tight connectivity and degree characterization holds for n ≥ 2. Algorithm 1 needs simple paths, and short proof repairs are needed. The hybrid model and the efficient algorithm were not checked.

24

Jayanti, Jayanti, Joshi — A Recoverable Mutex Algorithm with Sub-logarithmic RMR on Both CC and DSM

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 9's invariant is false, even in crash-free runs; a new invariant and liveness argument prove Lemmas 4-8 for the k-ported algorithm. The RMR bounds and the arbitration tree are unchecked.

22

Aghazadeh, Imbs, Raynal, Taubenfeld, Woelfel — Optimal Memory-Anonymous Symmetric Deadlock-Free Mutual Exclusion

⚠️ Weakened. Algorithm 1 livelocks at n = 4, m = 5, so the read/write characterization has only its necessity half. The read/modify/write characterization holds, with a new proof of Theorem 4.

15

Skrzypczak, Schintke, Schütt — Linearizable State Machine Replication of State-Based CRDTs without Logs

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 2 is not linearizable, and Lemma 3 and Theorems 3 and 4 are false; a one-line VOTE fix restores them all and linearizability, assuming a causal history. Termination unchecked.

13

Brandt, Maus, Uitto — A Sharp Threshold Phenomenon for the Distributed Complexity of the Lovász Local Lemma

🔧 Easy fix. Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 hold; the reduction to rank 3 and the rank-1 case needed local repairs. Round bounds and cited lower bounds were not checked.

12

Ovens, Woelfel — Strongly Linearizable Implementations of Snapshots and Other Types

🔧 Easy fix. The first-DRead specification needs one added condition; the ABA-detecting register and the snapshot on atomic base objects are then proved. The bounded-space composition and simple types were not checked.

12

Kosowski, Uznański, Viennot — Hardness of Exact Distance Queries in Sparse Graphs Through Hub Labeling

🛠️ Hard fix. The hub-labeling lower bound holds, but its key step, distance preservation from H to G, is false and needed a new potential argument. The Sum-Index and Ruzsa-Szemerédi sections were not checked.

7

Bitton, Emek, Izumi, Kutten — Message Reduction in the LOCAL Model is a Free Lunch

🔧 Easy fix. Spanner size Õ(n^{1+δ}) in Theorem 1.2 is false (counterexample); the corrected bound equals the message bound. The stretch analysis stands; the LOCAL implementation and Theorem 1.3 were not checked.

7

Kokociński, Kobus, Wojciechowski — On Mixing Eventual and Strong Consistency: Bayou Revisited

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 5 and Corollary 1 hold; its proof misapplies the stable-run hypothesis and ignores local clocks, both repaired locally. Theorems 1–4 (ANNC, AcuteBayou) were not checked.

5

Hussak, Trehan — On Termination of a Flooding Process

🔧 Easy fix. All results hold for finite connected graphs; the proof of Theorem 3.5 has a short gap and Figure 7 as drawn does not show sharpness, both fixed locally.

5

Ben-Basat, Even, Kawarabayashi, Schwartzman — Optimal Distributed Covering Algorithms

⚠️ Weakened. The (f + ε) hypergraph cover algorithm and its iteration bound hold after small corrections; the abstract's covering-ILP extension fails, both its ratio and the degree bound behind its running time.

5

Miller, Patt-Shamir, Rosenbaum — With Great Speed Come Small Buffers: Space-Bandwidth Tradeoffs for Routing

⚠️ Weakened. HPTS works only after reversing its level order; the tradeoff holds in the number of nodes n, while the abstract's upper bound in destinations d is unproved; lower bound and tree results stand.

5

Rinberg, Spiegelman, Bortnikov, Hillel, Keidar, Rhodes, Serviansky — Fast Concurrent Data Sketches

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 1 and Theorem 1 hold for the empty sketch's initial hint and a stronger relaxation; the simulation proof of Theorem 1 fails and was replaced by a new invariant proof.

4

Jayanti, Jayanti — Constant Amortized RMR Abortable Mutex for CC and DSM

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 1 and all its bounds hold; AFCFS as defined is unsatisfiable and needs one added case, f lacks a value at line 11, and the invariant of Theorem 4.1 needs one conjunct.

1

Golab — Brief Announcement: The Recoverable Consensus Hierarchy

🔧 Easy fix. Both transformations and both lower bounds hold; the proof of Theorem 3 needs a one-condition change to its failure model, and multi-register scans need a local argument. Θ(f) tightness for TAS unchecked.

0

Severson, Haley, Doty — Composable Computation in Discrete Chemical Reaction Networks

✏️ Slip. One wrong formula in §1.2; the 1D characterization and the sufficiency direction of Theorem 5.2 hold, but the necessity of its eventually-min condition (Theorem 7.1) was not checked.

0

Chaudhuri, Frank, Welch — Brief Announcement: How Fast Reads Affect Multi-Valued Register Simulations

🛠️ Hard fix. All lower bounds hold, but the proof of Lemma 4.2 rests on a false claim about decision trees, so Theorem 4.1 needed a new case split; Section 3 proofs had small gaps.
