i), σ)` goes to the proposer of `i`, with `n ≤ rnr` and `σ ⊑ s` (in particular: Lemma 1 + Corollary 1, the states in `ACK`s are below the current payload); * `ack_uniq`: two `ACK`s of `a` with the same round number have the same round id; * `rid_inv`: the current round `(rnr, rid)` was acknowledged; * `voted_inv`, `voted_ack`: a `VOTED(s)` for round `(n, i)` comes after the `ACK` for `(n, i)`, and every `ACK` with a larger round number carries a state above `s` (the Paxos-style invariant behind Lemma 4); * `snap` (repaired variant only): all states sent by `a` in `ACK`s and `VOTED`s are pairwise comparable — they are payloads that `a` held at some time. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.CRDTReplication open Cslib.SharedMemory Cslib.AsyncMP variable {P :
