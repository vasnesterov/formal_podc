Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Optimal Distributed Covering Algorithms — PODC 2019.

Paper: Ben-Basat, Even, Kawarabayashi, Schwartzman, Optimal Distributed Covering Algorithms, PODC 2019, arXiv 1902.09377v2.

Model and definitions

  1. Global-state iteration instead of messages. One iteration (steps 3a–3f) is the function step. The messages ("covered", "raise"/"stuck", deals) are replaced by reading the corresponding values. Every step only uses information of the vertices of an edge or of the edges of a vertex, so this is the paper's synchronous execution. CONGEST message sizes (Appendix B) are not modelled.

  2. Hypergraph representation. inc : E → Finset V with an arbitrary finite edge type, so parallel edges are allowed (the set-cover reading e_x = {u_i : x ∈ U_i} produces them). Edges must be nonempty: the paper implicitly assumes this, since min_{v ∈ e} in iteration 0 needs it.

  3. Weights. Positive reals instead of ℕ⁺. Assumptions (i)–(iii) of Section 3.1 (polynomial weights/degrees, Δ ≥ 3) are only needed for CONGEST and asymptotics. They are not assumed, except Δ ≥ 3 in theorem_4_9.

  4. f and Δ are bounds, not exact values. Every theorem takes f with rank ≤ f and f ≥ 1 (the parameter P.f used in β), and Δ with deg ≤ Δ. This is more general.

  5. Parameters. ε > 0 (the paper: ε ∈ (0,1]; Claim A.1: ε ∈ (0,1)) and α > 1 (the paper: α ≥ 2). α ≥ 2 is used only in iterBound_le / mwhvc_main to bound log_α 2 ≤ 1.

  6. One proof for Section 3 and Appendix C. The dual increment is δ(e) += κ·deal(e), with κ = 1 for Section 3 and κ = 1/2 for Appendix C. All invariants are proved for every κ ∈ (0,1]. The initial deal is c₀ · min_{v∈e} w(v)/|E(v)| with c₀ ∈ (0,1/2]. Section 3 uses c₀ = 1/2, Appendix B uses c₀ = β (an inconsistency in the paper; both are covered). Lemma 4.6 is stated for c₀ = 1/2 as in the paper (lemma_4_6) and in general with the bound log_α(Δ·2^{fz}/(2c₀)) (lemma_4_6_general).

  7. Step 3a lets every v ∉ C join, as written. A vertex that has already terminated with E'(v) = ∅ can never become β-tight later, so this agrees with "terminated vertices do nothing".

  8. Step 3d (while loop) is lvlUp: the least ℓ' ≥ ℓ with ∑_{E(v)} δ ≤ w(1 − 2^{-(ℓ'+1)}). If no such level exists the level is unchanged. This never happens for active vertices (lvlUp_upper), because they are not β-tight. The k halvings of a deal (one per level increment of a vertex of the edge) are applied as a single factor 2^{-k}.

  9. Covered edges. Covered edges are terminated: their δ and deal are frozen. This matches the paper's convention "δ_i(e) = δ_{j-1}(e)".

  10. Termination is "every edge is covered" (Terminated). Then every vertex has terminated as well (v ∈ C, or E'(v) = ∅).

  11. opt. Corollary 4.3 and Claim A.1 are proved against every fractional vertex cover x, i.e. w(C) ≤ (f+ε)·∑ w x. This avoids talking about the existence of an LP optimum and implies the paper's statement and the bound against every integral cover (corollary_4_3_integral).

  12. Definition 4.5. RaiseIter e i and StuckIter v i refer to iteration i ≥ 1. A v-stuck iteration requires v to be active in iteration i, i.e. to send a message.

Statements

  1. Claim 4.2, Eq. (1) is proved for vertices active in iteration i (claim_4_2). The paper states it for every v ∉ C, which is false for vertices that terminated without joining C (Eq1.claim_4_2_eq1_false). The proof only needs active vertices. Feasibility (IsPacking) is proved for all iterations i ≥ 0 (the paper: δ_{i-1} for i ≥ 1, the same thing).

  2. Lemma 4.7 / Lemma C.2 are proved in the corrected form #stuck(v, ℓ) < α/κ + 1, i.e. ≤ ⌈α/κ⌉ (lemma_4_7, lemma_4_7_ceil). The paper's ≤ α (≤ 2α) holds for integral α (lemma_4_7_of_nat, lemma_C_2_of_nat) and fails otherwise (Stuck.lemma_4_7_false, α = 29/10, three stuck iterations). The count is over iterations 1..n for every n.

  3. Lemma 4.6 is stated as a bound on the number of e-raise iterations among 1..n, for every n, in real numbers (≤ log_α(Δ·2^{fz})). The proof is the paper's. Additionally the deal is tracked exactly: deal_n(e) = deal_0(e)·α^{#raises}·2^{-∑_{v∈e} ℓ_n(v)} (deal_formula).

  4. Theorem 4.8. Explicit bound: every edge is covered by the end of iteration ⌊log_α(Δ·2^{fz}/(2c₀))⌋ + f·z·⌈α/κ⌉ + 1 (theorem_4_8). For c₀ = 1/2, α ≥ 2, this is ≤ log_α Δ + f z (α/κ + 2) + 1 (iterBound_le). Also z ≤ log₂((f+ε)/ε) + 1 (z_le). The proof's "number of e-stuck iterations" is a typo for "e-raise".

  5. Theorem 4.9. Asymptotic statements are made explicit: for every γ > 0 there is an explicit constant C49 γ such that, for every instance with Δ ≥ 3, T ≤ C49 γ · (F + log Δ/log log Δ + min{log Δ, F·(log Δ)^γ}) with F = f·z. Logarithms are natural, L = log Δ. F = f·z replaces the paper's f·log(f/ε), which is 0 for f = ε = 1 (division by zero in the definition of α). F = Θ(f log(f/ε)) for f/ε ≥ 2. The case analysis is the paper's, including the case L^{γ/2} ≤ x < 2 where Δ is bounded.

  6. Corollaries 4.10–4.12 are not formalized as round bounds. Corollary 4.10's approximation claim with ε = 1/(nW) is refuted (Cor410.corollary_4_10_false). The run is certified for α = 2. No vertex ever sends "raise" there, so the run is the same for every α > 1. The instance uses three parallel edges (to have Δ = 3); the refutation follows the definition W = max w / min w given just before Corollary 4.10, not the introduction's w : V → {1,…,W}.

Section 5

  1. Covering ILPs are indexed by finite row/column types. "Optimal value" statements are replaced by cost-preserving maps between feasible solutions in both directions (claim_5_6). This is equivalent and avoids existence of optima.

  2. Lemma 5.2. The hyperedge set is the paper's: {σᵢ \ S | i, S ⊆ [n], Aᵢ·I_S < bᵢ} as a Finset (duplicates merged). Proved: covers ↔ feasible zero-one solutions (needs A ≥ 0), nonempty edges (needs feasibility), rank ≤ f(A), degree ≤ 2^{f(A)-1}·Δ(A) < 2^{f(A)}·Δ(A) (the strict form needs f(A), Δ(A) ≥ 1). The stated rank < f(A) is refuted (lemma_5_2_rank_false).

  3. Definition 5.4 is the predicate IsM A b M ("M is the maximum of bᵢ/Aᵢⱼ over nonzero entries"). The repaired results only use bᵢ ≤ Aᵢⱼ·M for nonzero entries.

  4. Proposition 5.5 is false for non-integral M (prop_5_5_false). It is proved with the box [0, ⌈M⌉]ⁿ (prop_5_5_ceil).

  5. Claim 5.6. The statement is false for M < 1 (claim_5_6_false), and the proof's B = ⌈log₂ M⌉ bits are too few (claim_5_6_proof_false). Proved: for any B with ⌈M⌉ < 2^B the binary expansion solves the ILP (claim_5_6). B = ⌈log₂ M + 1⌉ satisfies this for M ≥ 1 (claim_5_6_bits). f(A') = f(A)·B and Δ(A') = Δ(A) (fA_binExp, ΔA_binExp).

  6. Claim 5.3 (distributed simulation over N(ILP)) and the running-time part of Claim 5.7 are not formalized. The approximation claim of Claim 5.7 with f = f(A) is refuted (ILPCex.claim_5_7_false). The true ratio of the reduction is f(A)·B + ε, which follows from Corollary 4.3 and fA_binExp. The degree parameter 2^{f(A)}·M·Δ(A) of Claim 5.7 is refuted (Degree57.claim_5_7_degree_false).

Not formalized

  1. Appendix B (CONGEST encoding), the local-Δ(e) variant of α, Corollaries 4.10–4.12 as round bounds, the final asymptotic simplification of Section 5.
