The key lemma (`no_inflation`) shows that this cannot inflate the count of `q` beyond what `q` held *simultaneously* at some time of the scan, provided `p` itself owns a register `y₀` during the scan: * while `q` is in `lock()`'s first two loops (`cas`/`read`), its own registers only grow; * while `q` erases (line 7), its own registers only shrink; * after erasing, `q` waits for an all-`⊥` scan (lines 8–10); this scan reads `y₀`, which holds `p`'s identifier during the whole scan of `p`, so `q` cannot re-enter before `p`'s scan ends.
