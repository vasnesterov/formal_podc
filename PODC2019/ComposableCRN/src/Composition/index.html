The paper composes by *renaming* the output species of `C_f` to the input species of `C_g` (Observation 2.2, for `κ = 1`; "more generally, g can take any number of inputs ..."). We use an explicit transfer reaction instead, which keeps the species of the modules disjoint; this only delays the hand-over and does not change what is computed. -/ set_option
