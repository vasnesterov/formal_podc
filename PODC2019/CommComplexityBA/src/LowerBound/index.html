import Papers.PODC2019.CommComplexityBA.Adversaries /-! # The core of the lower bound (proof of Theorem 4) `Protocol.core_bound`: fix inputs `inp`, a value `v`, a set `V` of nodes and a threshold `k` with `|V| - 1 + k ≤ f` and `|V| + k < n`. Suppose that * under `deafAdv V` all nodes outside `V` output `v`, except with probability `δ`; * every adversary breaks termination or consistency with probability at most `δ`; * every `p ∈ V`, never receiving anything, outputs `v` with probability at most `1/2`. Then under `deafAdv V` the expected number of messages sent by honest nodes is at least `(1/2 - 2δ) · |V| · (k + 1)`. The proof is the paper's, with one change: the paper conditions on the event "`p` receives at most `f/2` messages under `A`" and claims that, conditionally on it, `p` still fails to output `v` with probability `≥ 1/2`. This is unjustified (the event depends on `p`'s own randomness, which also determines its silent output). We use a union bound instead, and replace the paper's Markov-plus-averaging step by Markov's inequality for each `p ∈ V` separately; this gives `2ε · |V| · (k + 1)` for `δ = 1/4 - ε`, which is *better* than the paper's `(εf)²`. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false open MeasureTheory namespace CommComplexityBA variable {P I M Ω : Type*} [Fintype P] [DecidableEq P] {f : ℕ}
