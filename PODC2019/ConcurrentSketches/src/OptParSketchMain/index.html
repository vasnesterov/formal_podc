The paper proves it by simulating `OptParSketch` with `ParSketch` running `2N` update threads. We prove it directly (`theorem1`), by the ghost/invariant method used for Lemma 1: the strong linearization function is again `l` (updates at their invocation, queries at their snapshot), and the witness in `SeqSketch` is the visibility order `f` (accepted updates become visible when the propagator merges the batch containing them). Every update thread has at most `2b` unpropagated updates (its active buffer and the batch handed over to the propagator), which gives the relaxation `2Nb`.
