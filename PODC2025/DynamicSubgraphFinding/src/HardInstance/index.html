After the last interval, in round `T = 2qt + 1` (`t_final`), the "final" edges are inserted. A scenario is a `Spec`: at most one creation per index `i` (`cr i`), the final edges `fin`, and whether all nodes are present from the start (`full`, the edge-insertion model) or a node of `I` appears with its first edge (the node-insertion model: "nodes in `I` without incident edges are considered as not yet inserted"). -/ namespace Podc.DynamicSubgraphFinding open Classical namespace Hard
