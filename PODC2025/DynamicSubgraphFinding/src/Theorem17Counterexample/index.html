import Papers.PODC2025.DynamicSubgraphFinding.FullHist /-! # The algorithm of Theorem 1.7 is unsound Theorem 1.7 claims a one-round `List(K₃)` algorithm under node *and* edge insertions. In the case `t_uv - t_uw ≤ d` of its correctness proof, node `v` lists the triangle `{u, v, w}` "by the same argument as for node `v` in the proof of Theorem 1.4": `v` receives `R^d_edge(u) ∋ j` from its new neighbour `u` (Step 3) and `j ∈ R^d_signal(v)` because `w` sent `(Signal)` `j` rounds ago (Step 1), and infers the edge `{u, w}` "by the fact that in each round at most one topological change occurs". With node insertions this inference is wrong: the single change `j` rounds ago may be the insertion of a *new node* `x` adjacent to both `u` and `w`. Counterexample (`scenarioB`, maximum degree 2): `G⁰` has the edge `{v, w}`; in round 2 the node `x` is inserted with edges `{x, u}`, `{x, w}`; in round 3 the edge `{u, v}` is inserted. In round 3, `u` sends `R_edge(u) = {1}` to `v` and `w` had sent `(Signal)` to `v` in round 2, so `v` lists `{u, v, w}`, which is not a triangle. `alg17` is the algorithm family: messages consist of `(Signal)` (Step 1) and `R^d_edge` sent to a new neighbour (Step 3), as in the paper, plus an *arbitrary* further component `oth` (which can implement Steps 2 and 4, the bit reports and the periodic `1-DBList` updates, in any way); outputs are the triangles found by the paper's rule for `v`, plus arbitrary further triangles `extra`. `theorem_1_7_algorithm_unsound`: no member of the family solves
