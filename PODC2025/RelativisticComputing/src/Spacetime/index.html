RP3 (transmissions are physically permissible iff sent at `e` and received at `e'` with `e ≺ e'`) enters the definition of a relativistic physical execution (`IsRelPhys`). Non-vacuity: `minkowski d` is the Minkowski spacetime `ℝ × ℝᵈ` (with `c = 1`), with the inertial frames of velocity `v`, `‖v‖ < 1`; frame time is `t - ⟪v, x⟫`, which is the Lorentz time `γ_v (t - ⟪v, x⟫)` up to the positive factor `γ_v` (and the order is all that matters).
