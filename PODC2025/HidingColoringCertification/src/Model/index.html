import Mathlib /-! # Locally checkable proofs with one verification round The model of Section 2 of [MMR25] (full version arXiv:2502.13854v1), specialised to one round (`r = 1`), which is the only radius used by the results we formalize. * `PortGraph`: a finite simple graph `G` with a port numbering (`port v w < deg v`, injective at every node), an injective identifier assignment `id : V → ℕ` with `id v < N`, and the value `N` given to every node (`N = poly(n)` in the paper; see `PortGraph.Admissible`). * `View L`: the radius-1 view of a node in a labelled port graph: `N`, its own identifier, label and degree, and the multiset of the records of its neighbours (port at the centre, port at the neighbour, identifier, label and degree of the neighbour). This is at least the information of the paper's view `(G¹ᵥ, prt|N(v), Id|N(v), I|N(v))` (we add the neighbours' degrees, which only makes the extracting algorithms stronger, hence the hiding results stronger). * `AnonView L`: the same without identifiers (anonymous algorithms factor through `View.anon`). * `LCP L`: a binary decoder `D : View L → Bool` with a set `sz n` of admissible certificates on `n`-node graphs ("certificates of size `f(n)`"). Properties (all relative to the global choice `bound : ℕ → ℕ`, `N = bound n`): `Complete`, `Sound`, `StronglySound` (the paper's strong *promise* soundness), `StronglySoundAll` (accepted nodes induce a bipartite graph in every instance), `Hiding` (against every, possibly identifier-aware, extraction algorithm), `AnonHiding` (against anonymous extraction algorithms) and `Anonymous`. -/ -- CSLib candidate: one-round LCP model (port graphs with identifiers, views, LCP properties) namespace Podc.HidingColoringCertification open SimpleGraph Finset /-- A class of graphs (the promise of a promise problem). -/ abbrev GraphClass := ∀ (V : Type), SimpleGraph V → Prop /-- A finite simple graph with a port numbering, identifiers and the value `N` known to all nodes.
