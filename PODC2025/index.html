Vacus, Ziccardi — Minimalist Leader Election Under Weak Communication

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorems 2 and 3 hold. Lemmas 14 and 17 have wrong proof steps, so the O(D² log n) bound needed a new martingale proof; Lemma 19 is false for small D, fixed by 2D+3 windows.

6

Ben-David, Dzulfikar, Ellen, Gilbert — Byzantine Agreement with Predictions

⚠️ Weakened. Message bound and conditional algorithms hold; the round lower bound's proof fails for decision times and is reproved only without signatures, so tightness with signatures is weaker; an unused remark is false.

5

Boyar, Ellen, Larsen — Brief Announcement: Distributed Graph Algorithms with Predictions

✅ Correct. No mistakes: the pruning algorithm, greedy MIS, the eta + 3 bound and the line lower bound all hold; the generic compositions and framework definitions were not checked.

4

Constantinescu, Dufay, Ghinea, Wattenhofer — Byzantine Stable Matching

🔧 Easy fix. All characterizations hold for k ≥ 2 but fail for the degenerate k = 1; two necessity proofs are repaired locally. Sufficiency assumes Theorems 8, 9 and the cited broadcast protocols.

4

Chang, Mishra, Nguyen, Salim — Round and Communication Efficient Graph Coloring

✏️ Slip. Deterministic edge colouring (Theorems 2, 3) and the ZEC bound hold; only slips, such as Lemma 5.3 at Delta = 0. Vertex colouring and the final lower bound were not checked.

4

Amores-Sesar, Cachin, Villacis, Zanolini — DAG-based Consensus with Asymmetric Trust

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 3.2 and the new constant-round gather hold after local repairs; the side remark that quorum replacement needs log n - 1 rounds is false. The consensus protocol of Section 4 was not checked.

3

Attiya, Fernández Anta, Milani, Rapetti, Travers — Auditing without Leaks Despite Curiosity

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 8 (the auditable register) holds, but Lemma 7 needed a new simulation; the write bound becomes m + 2, Lemma 38 fails at w0 only; max-register linearizability and snapshots were not checked.

2

Jayanti — On Interplanetary and Relativistic Distributed Computing

🔧 Easy fix. The Central Theorem fails only for uncountably many processors; Lemma 6.3's proof wrongly assumes order type ω and two definitions need local fixes, after which Theorems 6.7 and 6.8 hold.

2

Castañeda, Rodríguez — Asynchronous Fault-Tolerant Language Decidability for Runtime Verification of Distributed Systems

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 5.2 and the impossibilities hold, but WEC_COUNT is not in WD as defined, SD ⊊ WD needs a changed language, and the proof of PSD ⊂ PWD (Lemma 6.4) is broken and unsaved.

1

Bourreau, Narayanan, Nolin — Brief Announcement: Optimal Deterministic Rendezvous in Labeled Lines

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 1.1 holds, but Lemma 3.5's time analysis fails as written and would lose optimality; we redesigned Algorithm 3's colouring. Lemma 4.5's constant becomes 8L. The cited lower bound is unchecked.

1

Mirault, Robinson — Brief Announcement: Towards Energy-Efficient Distributed Agreement

⚠️ Weakened. The binary algorithm behind Theorem 2 violates agreement (n = 5, f = 3), so O(⌈f/√n⌉) energy is unestablished. The multi-value result (Theorem 1) holds, after correcting Lemma 2 and repairing Lemma 4.

1

Augustine, Chatterjee, King, Kumar, Meir, Peleg — Brief Announcement: Distributed Download from an External Data Source in Byzantine Majority Settings

⚠️ Weakened. Rapid Download (Algorithm 7) fails for f = 2, leaving Theorem 5.16 unestablished; Theorem 3.6 and Static Download (Theorem 5.9) hold. The randomized Byzantine and asynchronous protocols were not checked.

0

Gafni, Losa, Raynal, Taubenfeld — Stranger-Free Tasks

⚠️ Weakened. Algorithm 1 is not wait-free for k >= 2, so the wait-free hierarchy survives only obstruction-free and for k = 1. The n-register and k + 1 lower bounds hold.

0

Anoprenko, Kuznetsov, Aksenov — Optimal Construction of Unique Identifiers from Bounded Registers

🔧 Easy fix. The (k - 1) 2^w construction and the k 2^w + k + 1 bound hold; the definition of poised lacks one condition, and Claim 1 misses the all-FAI case.

Ryabinin, Gotsman, Sutra — Brief Announcement: Revisiting Lower Bounds for Two-Step Consensus

🛠️ Hard fix. Both tight bounds hold, but the object protocol of Fig. 1 is not wait-free and needed rotating ballots, and Lemma 4's safety proof needed a new invariant; several lower-bound proofs had local gaps.

Nguyen, Sutra — An Exact Characterization of the Two-shot Deterministic Objects Solving Two-process Consensus

🔧 Easy fix. Characterization and all three algorithms hold after an index typo and local proof repairs. The illustrating example 'AQ is in C1.5' is false: AQ is in C2.

Hu, Toueg — You can lie but not deny: SWMR registers with signature properties in systems with Byzantine processes

✅ Correct. No mistakes found in the checked part: the impossibility for 3 <= n <= 3f holds, with Theorem 31 proved directly. The implementations for n > 3f were not checked.

Naser-Pastoriza, Chockler, Gotsman, Ryabinin — Tight Bounds on Channel Reliability via Generalized Quorum Systems

🔧 Easy fix. Lower bounds (Theorems 2, 6) hold once values are non-trivial; a remark on U_f is reworded and a proof step closed. The upper bounds were not checked.

Austin, Gadouleau, Mertzios, Trehan — Brief Announcement: Amnesiac Flooding: Easy to Break, Difficult to Escape

⚠️ Weakened. Dichotomy and single-message fragility hold, Byzantine broadcast is corrected. Link failure needs one initiator; the non-leaf Byzantine claim is false; Random Flooding's termination proof is broken. Uniqueness was not checked.

Canonne, Li, Umboh — Brief Announcement: Local Computation Algorithms for Knapsack: impossibility results, and how to avoid them

⚠️ Weakened. Impossibility results hold after new proofs; the main positive Theorem 4.1 is false, since LCA-KP answers no to every query on identical items, and no repaired algorithm was proved.

Modanese, Montealegre, Ríos-Wilson — Brief Announcement: Strong and Hiding Distributed Certification of k-Coloring

❌ Intent lost. Main lower bound Theorem 4.2 is false: anonymous O(log n)-bit and constant-size LCPs refute it. Upper bounds and Lemma 3.2 hold, but two decoders are wrong and needed other protocols.

Chang, Chen, Chen, Mishra, Yang — Brief Announcement: The Complexity Landscape of Dynamic Distributed Subgraph Finding

⚠️ Weakened. Lower bounds 1.3, 1.6 and Theorem 1.4 hold after local fixes; the Theorem 1.7 algorithm lists a non-triangle, so tightness under node insertions is unproved. Sections 6–8 unchecked.
