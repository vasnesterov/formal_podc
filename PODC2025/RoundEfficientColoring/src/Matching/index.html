The paper argues via the integrality of the bipartite fractional matching polytope; we use Hall's theorem directly (double counting: `Δ |S|` edges leave a set `S` of degree-`Δ` vertices and every vertex receives at most `Δ` of them). The lemma needs `Δ ≥ 1` (or no vertex of degree `Δ`): for `Δ = 0` a graph with one isolated vertex is a counterexample (`lemma_5_3_false_of_zero`). The matching is described by an injective map `f` from the vertices of degree `k` to their neighbours; its edges are `s(u, f u)`. Since `f u` has a neighbour of degree `k`, `f u` has degree `< k`, so the edges are pairwise disjoint. -/ namespace Podc.RoundEfficientColoring open SimpleGraph Finset variable {V : Type*} [Finite V]
