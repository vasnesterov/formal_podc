import Papers.PODC2025.RoundEfficientColoring.Plan /-! # Algorithm 2: messages, outputs and correctness Chang–Mishra–Nguyen–Salim, PODC 2025, Algorithm 2 (`Δ ≥ 8`), lines 11–23, Lemma 5.4 and Lemma 5.5, and the correctness part of the proof of Theorem 2. Palettes: one party (Alice) has the own palette `[o, o + Δ - 1)` with `o = 0`, the other (Bob) `[o', o' + Δ - 1)` with `o' = Δ - 1`; the special colour is `2Δ - 2`. Each party sends one message (`Plan.info`), computed from its own graph only: * `cov v`: `v` is covered by its matching (line 11); * `high v`: its degree at `v` is `> Δ / 2` (line 11); * `cs`, `lvl`: the greedy colour cover of its low-degree vertices (Lemma 5.4); * `used7 v j` (`j < 7`): its own palette colour `j` is used at `v` by its colouring of `R \ M` (Lemma 5.5). Hence **one** round of simultaneous messages suffices (the paper uses `O(1)` rounds: it sends the information of Lemma 5.5 in a later round, but nothing in it depends on the other party). Outputs (`Plan.color`): edges of `R \ M` get their own colour; an edge `s(u, f u)` of the matching gets the special colour if `f u` is not covered by the other party's matching or has degree `> Δ/2` there, and otherwise the colour of the other party's palette that the cover assigns to `f u` (lines 12–22); deferred edges are coloured greedily from the first seven colours of the other party's palette, avoiding the colours the other party uses at the endpoints and the colours of the own matching edges at the endpoints (line 23, Lemma 5.5). -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false
