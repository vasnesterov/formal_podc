Each party, on its own, defers a maximal set of edges joining two vertices of (current) degree `Δ` — these form a matching whose endpoints have all their `Δ` edges in that party's graph — colours them with one colour of the other party's palette, and colours the rest with its own palette of `Δ` colours by Fournier's theorem. The deferral is the sequential procedure of the paper ("each party sequentially identifies edges connecting two vertices of degree `Δ`"), where *degree* is the current degree in the remaining graph;
