import Mathlib /-! # Additive functionals along walks; cycles and faux-even cycles span closed walks Pure graph theory used by the termination dichotomy (Theorem 5.1) of Austin–Gadouleau–Mertzios–Trehan, *Amnesiac Flooding: Easy to Break, Difficult to Escape* (PODC 2025, arXiv 2502.06001). For edge weights `g` (antisymmetric) and `o` (symmetric) we consider * `walkFlow g p = Σ_k g(w_k, w_{k+1})` and * `walkSig o p = Σ_k (-1)^k o(w_k, w_{k+1})` along a walk `p = w_0 w_1 … w_ℓ`. A faux-even cycle (`FEC`, Definition 5.1 of the paper) is a pair of odd cycles which either share exactly one vertex, or are vertex-disjoint and joined by a path meeting them only at its endpoints. `FEC.walk` is the closed walk `c₁ ++ p ++ c₂ ++ p⁻¹`, which traverses the paper's "even cycle representation" `H²` (Definition 5.2). Main results: * `walkFlow_eq_zero_of_isCycle`: if `walkFlow g` vanishes on all cycles, it vanishes on all closed walks. * `walkSig_eq_zero_of_isCycle_of_fec`: if `walkSig o` vanishes on all even cycles and on all faux-even cycles, it vanishes on all closed walks of even length.
