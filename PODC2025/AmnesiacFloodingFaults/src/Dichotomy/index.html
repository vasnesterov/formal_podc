Proof: if a message survives `2|E|` rounds, follow its ancestors back to round `0`; by pigeonhole some directed edge carries an ancestor twice, so a message travelled once around a closed walk, contradicting `not_balancedOn_of_front`. * `terminates_iff_balancedAll` — Theorem 5.1 for closed walks. * `theorem_5_5_walks` — terminating ⇔ reachable from `∅` by multicasts ⇔ balanced. * `theorem_2_1` — multicast amnesiac flooding terminates (Theorem 2.1 / Lemma 3.1, from the literature;
