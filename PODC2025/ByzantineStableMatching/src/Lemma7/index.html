We prove directly, for every `k ≥ 2` and every network `G` without edges inside `L` (in particular one-sided and bipartite networks), that sSM is unsolvable when `2 tR ≥ k` (for any `tL`, even `0`). `L` is split into two nonempty groups `a`, `b`, `R` into two nonempty groups `c`, `d` of size `≤ ⌈k/2⌉`.
