Parties are `Party k = Fin k ⊕ Fin k`: `inl i` is the `i`-th party of `L`, `inr j` the `j`-th party of `R`. Outputs are `Option (Fin k)`: `some j` means "match with the `j`-th party of the other side", `none` means "match with nobody". * `bSM`: inputs are preference lists `π : Equiv.Perm (Fin k)` (`π 0` is the favourite); * `sSM`: inputs are favourites `Fin k`; * networks: `fully`, `oneSided` (all pairs except inside `L`), `bipartite` (only `L × R` pairs); * adversary structure `thresholds k tL tR`: at most `tL` Byzantine parties in `L`, `tR` in `R`. -/ namespace Papers.PODC2025.ByzantineStableMatching open Cslib.Byzantine.LockStep Sum /-- The parties: `L = range inl`, `R = range inr`. -/ abbrev
