For two leaders `u ≠ v` and a window `[t, t+L)`, `L = 2D+3`, the "good" event `Good D t u v` (some `true` coin of `u` in `[t+2, t+D+2]`, all coins of `v` in `[t, t+L)` `false`) depends only on coins of the window and has probability `Q = (1-(1-p)^{D+1})(1-p)^L`. By `elim_window`, `u` and `v` are not both leaders at time `t+L` if the right one of `Good D t u v`, `Good D t v u` happens (which one depends on the past through `N_t(u) ≥ N_t(v)`), so `P(both leaders at t+L) ≤ (1-Q)·P(both leaders at t)`. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false open MeasureTheory ProbabilityTheory unitInterval Cslib.Probability open scoped ENNReal namespace
