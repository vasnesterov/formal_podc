Minimalist Leader Election — PODC 2025

Robin Vacus, Isabella Ziccardi. Minimalist Leader Election Under Weak Communication. PODC 2025. DOI 10.1145/3732772.3733559. Formalized version: arXiv 2502.12697 v2; numbering follows it. Copy of the paper: paper.pdf.

DOI · arXiv · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🛠️ Hard fix. All main results hold: Theorem 2 (for every fixed p ∈ (0,1), BFW elects a unique leader almost surely, and w.h.p. within O(D² log n) rounds), Theorem 3 (O(D log n) rounds for p = 1/(D+1)) and Section 3 (flow conservation, Ohm's law, and "there is always a leader"). The paper's anti-concentration proof of the O(D² log n) bound fails at Lemmas 14 and 17, and we replace it by a new martingale/Lyapunov proof. Lemma 19 (used for Theorem 3) is false as stated for small D (K₂, P₃), Theorem 3 still holds with a window of 2D+3 rounds, the "almost surely" claim needed a separate argument, and knowing D only up to a constant factor was checked only for upper bounds on D.

Mistakes in the paper

🔧 Lemma 19 (elimination within 2D+1 rounds, used for Theorem 3)

Lemma 19, read per configuration in round t as its proof does, is false for small D: on K₂ (D = 1) with both nodes in B• in round t = 1, neither node loses leadership before round t + 4 > t + 2D + 1, whatever the coins (lemma19_fails_K2, lemma19_fails_P3).

Impact. Theorem 3 is true: with windows of 2D+3 rounds it holds with explicit constants (elim_window, theorem3, theorem3_whp).

Details

On the path u–w–v (D = 2) the earliest elimination is in round t + 2D + 1, not before it. Both counterexamples hold for every coin field and are proved in Lean. The proof of Lemma 19 has two further gaps:

  • P(v does not beep by itself in [t, t+2D+2]) = (1−p)^{2(D+1)} is wrong if v is in B• in round t (the probability is then 0).

  • In the case N_t(u) = N_t(v), the "self-beep of u between t and t+D+1" may be the beep in round t itself. That beep is already counted, so u does not get ahead of v.

Repair

We shift the coin windows (coins of u in rounds t+2..t+D+2, all coins of v in rounds t..t+2D+2) and use windows of length 2D+3. Theorem 3 then holds with explicit constants: P(no single leader from round k(2D+3) on) ≤ n²(127/128)^k (elim_window, theorem3, theorem3_whp).

✏️ Slips

  • Lemma 12 as stated only gives a beep of v in some round s ≤ t + dis(u,v), but Lemma 19 needs it to happen after round t. Its proof does give s > t, and we prove that version (lemma12).

  • Lemma 14 is stated for the chain started in W, but its proof needs the stationary start. The lemma is applied (in Lemma 15) only to the stationary copy. Not formalized; our proof of Theorem 2 does not use it.

  • Lemma 21 should read P(Σ_{i≤n} W_i ≥ k) = P(Bin(k−1,p) ≤ n−1), not P(Bin(k,p) ≤ n): for n = 1, k = 2, p = 1/2 the two sides are 1/2 and 3/4. The form used in Lemma 14 is correct. Not formalized.

Typos

  • Proof of Lemma 19, case N_t(u) = N_t(v): "N_s^beep(u) ≥ 1" should read N_s(u) > N_s(v).

  • Proof of Lemma 17, last bound: the display ends with ≤ 1/n², while the lemma states n^{−4}, which is what the chosen A gives and what the union bound in Theorem 2 uses.

  • The Discussion (§5) cites "Theorem 1", which does not exist; Theorem 2 is meant.

Proof gaps in true statements (3)Lemma 14 · Lemma 17 · Theorem 2

🛠️ Lemma 14 (proof of the anti-concentration bound)

The proof of Lemma 14 claims P(Z ≥ EZ + k(2p+1)) ≥ 1/2 for a binomial Z, which is false.

Impact. This is the first step of the chain (Lemmas 14, 15, 17) behind Theorem 2's O(D² log n) bound. The statements are true. We prove that bound by a different route (see "The formalization") (theorem2_time, theorem2_whp).

Details

A binomial's median is within 1 of its mean, so the probability in Lemma 14's proof is < 1/2 (for p = 1/2, k = 1, t = 100 it is 0.333). Any smaller constant works instead; this repair is not in Lean.

🛠️ Lemma 17 (proof of P(σ_{u,v} > AD² log n) ≤ n^{−4})

The proof applies Lemma 15, which bounds P(|Δ| < d), where it needs a shifted interval |Δ + δ| ≤ D.

Impact. The gap is fixable and the statement is true, but that repair is not in Lean; the O(D² log n) bound is saved by the same different route (theorem2_time, theorem2_whp).

Details

The proof also conditions only on E_{k−1} instead of the whole past, and it uses Lemma 15's "d large enough" although D may be 1.

🔧 Theorem 2 ("almost surely")

The proof only bounds the probability of two leaders in round AD² log n by n^{−2}, and that does not give probability 1.

Impact. Statement true; proved by a separate argument (theorem2_ae, theorem2_tail).

Repair

Each window of 2D+3 rounds has a good event of positive probability (1−(1−p)^{D+1})(1−p)^{2D+3}, and that event eliminates one of any two given leaders. (theorem2_ae, theorem2_tail)

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Synchronous beeping model on a finite connected SimpleGraph; anonymous, uniform nodes. A listening node hears a beep iff some neighbour beeps.

  • BFW has 6 states: a phase W/B/F and a leader bit (Figure 1).

  • No failures, no adversary. The only randomness is the W• → B• coin. The execution is a deterministic function of a coin field c : ℕ → V → Bool.

  • Section 3 is proved for every coin field, i.e. for every rule deciding W• → B•, as the paper remarks.

  • Probabilistic results use i.i.d. Bernoulli(p) coins on ℕ × V (Measure.infinitePi). Events are tracked by the finite set of coins they depend on (Determined); events on disjoint coin sets are independent.

  • Initial configuration: Eq. (2), i.e. all nodes waiting and at least one leader. This is more general than the paper's all-W• start.

  • D is any upper bound on all distances (the diameter, or more).

  • Definition 1 is UniqueFrom T: a fixed node is the only leader in every round t ≥ T.

Remarks.lean

Our proof of the O(D² log n) bound is new. While u and v are both leaders, the triple (phase u, phase v, N(u) − N(v)) follows a 9-phase pair chain (pchain_couple). Write a = 1 + 2p. The quantity M = N(u) − N(v) + h(x_u) − h(x_v), with h(W) = 0, h(B) = −2p/a, h(F) = −p/a, is a martingale. Add a corrector k(x_u) + k(x_v) and put

g = ((D+2)² − M² + k(x_u) + k(x_v))·a³ / (2p(1−p)).

Then g has drift exactly −1 (lyap_drift), so T·P(|N(u) − N(v)| ≤ D for T steps) ≤ g ≤ G_max (alive_bound). By Lemma 11 the leaders u, v can coexist only while |N(u) − N(v)| ≤ D. Hence one of them is eliminated within T₂ = ⌈(D+2)²(1+2p)³/(p(1−p)) + 4⌉ rounds with probability ≥ 1/2. A union bound gives a unique leader from round ⌈6 ln n⌉·T₂ on with probability ≥ 1 − 1/n².

Not formalized. The anti-concentration machinery: Theorem 13, Theorem 20 (Berry–Esseen for mixing sequences) and Lemmas 14, 15, 17, 21. Our Theorem 2 proof does not need it. Also not formalized: the Discussion's tightness conjecture. The remark after Theorem 3 that the proof "can be easily generalized" to a constant-factor approximation of D is open: Lean covers upper bounds on D only, and approximations from below were not examined.

Trust. No sorry. All theorems in the table use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

  • Non-vacuity: theorem2_ae_example instantiates Theorem 2 on the triangle with p = 1/2 from the paper's initial configuration.

  • goodInit_initAll shows that the paper's start satisfies Eq. (2).

  • lyap_drift is an exact identity, checked case by case over the 9 phase pairs.

  • The probability space is Mathlib's Measure.infinitePi of Ber(true, false, p).

The model is self-contained in this folder. CSLib's stone-age model (Cslib.StoneAge) has deterministic transitions, so the randomized BFW protocol does not fit it; the deterministic protocols of the remark on randomness (DetProtocol) are stone-age algorithms run under the synchronous schedule (DetProtocol.toStoneAge, DetProtocol.run_succ).

The i.i.d. coin fields (coinMeasure), Determined events, independence of events on disjoint coin sets, and partition along fibres are shared code in CSLib, Cslib/Probability/CoinField.lean (namespace Cslib.Probability).
