import Papers.PODC2025.LanguageDecidability.Canonical /-! # The timed adversary `A_τ` and predictive decidability (Section 6) ## Model An algorithm `V` runs against the timed adversary `A_τ` (Figure 6). When process `p` sends `v` to `A_τ` (Line 03 of Figure 1, event `send`), `A_τ` announces `v` in `M[p]` (event `tw`, Lines 01–02 of Figure 6), the black box `A` answers `w`, and `A_τ` takes a snapshot of `M` (event `ts`, Lines 04–06); `p` then receives `(w, view)` (Line 04 of Figure 1, event `recv`). Each of these events is a separate step, so other processes can take steps in between. * Views: `View I O = Fin n → List (Sym I O)`, the announced invocations of each process in announcement order (the paper uses the set of announced invocations; lists carry at least as much information). * An algorithm against `A_τ` is an `Alg n I (TO I O) R`, whose responses are pairs `(w, view)`. * `x(E)`: the symbols of the `send`/`recv` events (`THasInput`). The *sketch* `x~(E)` (Appendix B): the history obtained by moving every invocation forward to its `tw` and every response backward to its `ts`, i.e. the symbols of the `tw`/`ts` events (`THasSketch`). The paper builds `x~(E)` from the views, up to the order of invocations (responses) with the same view; in the executions used below (`tcanon`) all these orders coincide. * Executions are fair and failure-free. ## Predictive decidability `PSDecides`/`PWDecides` are Definitions 6.1/6.2 **without** the conjunct "there is an execution `E' ≡ E` with `x(E') = x~(E)`" in the escape clause. Dropping it makes the definitions weaker, so the impossibility results proved for them (`lemma_6_2_*`) hold a fortiori for the paper's definitions; and the counterexample to Figure 9 violates them for a tight execution (`x~(E) = x(E)`), where that conjunct is irrelevant. `tcanon x` is the tight canonical execution (proof of Lemma 6.2: each process executes Lines 01–03 of `V` together with Lines 01–02 of `A_τ`, and Lines 04–06 together with Lines 04–07 of `A_τ`); its input and its sketch are both `x`, and it is causal. -/ namespace Podc2025.LanguageDecidability.Timed open
