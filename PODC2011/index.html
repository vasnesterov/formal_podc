159

Montresor, De Pellegrini, Miorandi — Distributed k-Core Decomposition

🛠️ Hard fix. Algorithms and round bounds hold; Theorem 3's proof needed a new argument, the worst-case family needs N − 2 rounds so the bound is not met exactly, and Corollary 2 misses 2M messages.

125

Kuhn, Moses, Oshman — Coordinated Consensus in Dynamic Networks

🛠️ Hard fix. All bounds survive: Theorem 6.8's sketch needed a new graph and walk, while Theorem 4.1, the diameter protocol's running time and Lemma 6.5 are false as printed but fixed locally.

108

Dinitz, Krauthgamer — Fault-Tolerant Spanners: Better and Simpler

🔧 Easy fix. The conversion theorem and O(log n) rounding hold up to slips; Lemma 3.8 is false as printed and fixed by one clause. The O(log Δ) bound and distributed theorems were not checked.

53

Göös, Suomela — Locally Checkable Proofs

✏️ Slip. Only slips: a typo in the gluing step and an imprecise honest proof. The Θ(log n), Θ(n²) and Θ(n) bounds hold; non-3-colourability was not checked.

36

Liang, Vaidya — Error-Free Multi-Valued Consensus with Byzantine Failures

🔧 Easy fix. As printed, an isolated faulty processor can trigger diagnosis forever, breaking the t(t+1) bound; ignoring its Detected bits restores the bound and the complexity. Consensus holds.

30

Korman, Sereni, Viennot — Toward More Localized Local Algorithms: Removing Assumptions Concerning Global Knowledge

⚠️ Weakened. Transformers (Theorems 1, 2, 4, deterministic 3) and uniform MIS and matching hold after local repairs; randomized Theorem 3 and the arboricity corollaries are not saved; matching needs a new encoding.

30

Delporte-Gallet, Fauconnier, Guerraoui, Kermarrec, Ruppert, Tran-The — Byzantine Agreement with Homonyms

🛠️ Hard fix. The whole characterization of Table 1 is proved; Theorem 19's necessity needed a new cover argument, and auxiliary Lemma 34 is unproved but unused.

28

Imbs, Rajsbaum, Raynal — The Universe of Symmetry Breaking Tasks

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 9 fails for the comparison-based algorithms Section 2.3 requires, and Lemma 1 misses a condition; both fixes are local. The task structure and the universality of perfect renaming hold.

19

Biely, Robinson, Schmid — Easy Impossibility Proofs for k-Set Agreement in Message Passing Systems

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 1 needs repaired conditions and Theorem 2 holds, but its bound is not the claimed tight border; Theorem 8's exact border for initial crashes stands. Section VII unchecked.

13

Afek, Morrison, Wertheim — From Bounded to Unbounded Concurrency Objects and Back

🛠️ Hard fix. All three results hold, but Lemma 15, the core of the iterator-stack impossibility, has a broken construction and needed a redesign. The composed claim of swap from 2-consensus objects was not checked.

9

Dobre, Guerraoui, Majuntke, Suri, Vukolić — The Complexity of Robust Atomic Storage

🔧 Easy fix. Both lower bounds hold in the round model of [1]; the scaling step works only when t_k divides t, and new block sizes repair it. Section 5's upper bounds unchecked.

7

Helmi, Higham, Pacheco, Woelfel — The Space Complexity of Long-Lived and One-Shot Timestamp Implementations

🛠️ Hard fix. All three bounds hold, but the proof of the one-shot lower bound (Theorem 1.2) breaks and needed new invariants; lower bounds are checked for deterministic implementations only.

7

Herlihy, Moses, Tuttle — Transforming Worst-case Optimal Solutions for Simultaneous Tasks into All-case Optimal Solutions

⚠️ Weakened. Transformation and optimality hold for t ≤ n−2 given cited lemmas; at t = n−1 Lemma 5 is false and Theorem 6 clashes with Theorem 3; Corollary 9 needs n ≥ t+k+1.

4

Jaggard, Schapira, Wright — Distributed computing with rules of thumb

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 4.1 holds for deterministic stationary and non-stationary 1-recall dynamics; the randomized and non-stationary k-recall claims are unproved, and Corollary 5.2 needs equilibria that are best-response fixed points.

4

Golab — A complexity separation between the cache-coherent and distributed shared memory models

🛠️ Hard fix. The separation and Theorem 6.2 hold, but the construction of Lemma 6.10 breaks regularity and needed a new invariant; Corollary 6.14 omits "terminating", and its CAS and LL/SC reduction was not checked.

4

Radeva, Lynch — Partial Reversal Acyclicity

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 5.5 (PR acyclic) holds by the paper's route; Lemma 5.3's proof mishandles an empty list and needs a changed case condition; "exactly one" in Invariant 3.2 fails for isolated nodes.

1

Wang, Welch, Lee — Time Bounds for Shared Objects in Partially Synchronous Systems

⚠️ Weakened. The d + min{ε, u, d/3} bounds hold; the (1 - 1/k)u bound is proved only under a stronger hypothesis, and the push + peek entry has no proof. The upper bounds were not checked.

1

Kentros, Kiayias — Solving the At-Most-Once Problem with Nearly Optimal Effectiveness

🔧 Easy fix. KKβ is safe, wait-free and has effectiveness n - 2m + 2; Theorem 4.4's equality holds only at f = m - 1, a local correction. Work complexity, IterativeKK and Write-All unchecked.
