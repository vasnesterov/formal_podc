import Papers.PODC2011.LocallyCheckableProofs.Basic import Cslib.Foundations.Data.List.BitEncoding /-! # The universal `O(n²)` upper bound (Section 6 of Göös–Suomela) "In connected graphs, any property of unlabeled graphs admits locally checkable proofs of size `O(n²)`. We can encode the structure of `G` and the unique node identifiers in `O(n²)` bits; the nodes can verify that their neighbors agree on the structure of `G` and that their local neighborhoods match those in `G`. Finally, the nodes can solve the problem by brute force." * `Code = List ℕ × List Bool`: a *graph code*, a list of identifiers `l` and a (row-major, `|l| × |l|`) adjacency matrix `a`. *Every* code decodes to an unlabelled instance `Code.toInst` (node set `l`, `x ~ y` iff `x ≠ y` and one of the two matrix entries is set). * `mat G s`: the adjacency matrix of `G` w.r.t. the identifier list `s`; for an enumeration `s` of the nodes, `(⟨s, mat I.G s⟩ : Code).toInst = I` (`toInst_mat`). * `encCode`: an injective bit encoding of codes (CSLib's self-delimiting `encList`, identifiers in binary, `Nat.bits`), with `|encCode (s, mat G s)| ≤ (4c + 10) n²` when the `n` identifiers are in `[1, n^c]`. * `universal_upper` (**Section 6**): for every `c` and **every** property `P` of unlabelled instances (not even closure under isomorphism is needed, the identifiers being visible), `P` admits locally checkable proofs of size `(4c + 10) n²` on the family of connected unlabelled graphs with identifiers in `[1, n^c]`. The verifier has local horizon `1`.
