`Nbr1Eq`: the data a radius-1 verifier at `v` may read (own membership, label and proof; the neighbours of `v`, which of these edges are marked, and their labels and proofs). `ViewEq.nbr1Eq`: equal views of radius `R ≥ 1` give `Nbr1Eq`; `isLocal_of_nbr1Eq`: a verifier determined by this data is local with horizon `1`. * `ViewEq.restrict_radius` / `IsLocal.of_le_radius`: a view of radius `R`
