We restrict `H₁`, `H₂` to *good* graphs: asymmetric (`Asymm`) and such that any two distinct vertices have at least three common neighbours (`ThreeCommon`). Then * `H ⊛ H` is symmetric (`isSymmetric_star_self`); * if `H₁`, `H₂` are good and non-isomorphic, `H₁ ⊛ H₂` is asymmetric (`not_isSymmetric_star`).
