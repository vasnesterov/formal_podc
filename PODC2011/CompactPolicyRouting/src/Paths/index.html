The weight of a walk is the `⊕`-composition of its edge weights, computed in the extended weights `A.Ext` (the empty walk has weight `1`). A preferred `s – t` path is a path of minimum weight among all `s – t` paths; `pw A wt s t` is its weight (the *preferred weight*; `φ` if `t` is unreachable). -/ namespace Podc.CompactPolicyRouting
