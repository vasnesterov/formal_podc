import Mathlib /-! # Routing algebras (Definitions 4.1–4.3 of Rétvári et al., *Compact policy routing*) We follow the numbering of Chapter 4 of G. Rétvári's D.Sc. dissertation (2020), which reproduces the PODC 2011 paper *Compact policy routing* (Rétvári, Gulyás, Heszberger, Csernai, Bíró) with proofs. A routing algebra `(W, φ, ⊕, ⪯)` is a totally ordered commutative semigroup with an absorbing maximal element `φ` (Definition 4.1). In Lean the order `⪯` is the ambient `LinearOrder` on `W` (smaller = preferred) and `⊕` is the field `op`. The *extended* weights `A.Ext = WithBot W` adjoin a neutral element `1 = ⊥` (the weight of the empty path); this makes `A.Ext` a commutative monoid, so path weights are list products and `w ^ k` is `Monoid.npow`. Several properties of Definition 4.3 are unsatisfiable when read literally because they quantify over `φ` too (`φ ≺ w ⊕ φ = φ` is false); we state them for `w ≠ φ` (`IsStrictlyMonotone`, `IsCancellative`, `IsCondensed`) and record the literal versions and their degeneracy (`not_isStrictlyMonotoneLit`, ...). -/ namespace Podc.CompactPolicyRouting universe u /-- Definition 4.1.
