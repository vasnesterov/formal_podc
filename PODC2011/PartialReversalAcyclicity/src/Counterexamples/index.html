For an isolated node both parts hold (`inv_3_2_exactlyOne_false`). 2. The proof of Lemma 5.3 (b), Case 2, claims that if `t.parity[w]` is odd and `w` is a sink then `s.list[w] = in-nbrs_w` "by Corollary 3.4", and simulates the step of `w` by a single `reverse(w)` of `NewPR` whenever `s.list[w] ≠ nbrs_w`. Both fail for a related pair of reachable states on three nodes: `G'init` has one edge `0 → 1`, the destination `2` is isolated; `s` is the initial state of `OneStepPR`; `t` is reached in `NewPR` by `reverse(1), reverse(0), reverse(0)` and has the initial orientation with `count = (2, 1, 0)`.
