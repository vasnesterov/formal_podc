"Left" and "right" refer to the paper's embedding of `G'init` in the plane with all edges directed from left to right; for two neighbours `u`, `v` it only matters which of them is to the left, i.e. whether `init u v` (`u` left of `v`) or `init v u`. Theorem 4.3 does not need a global embedding either: the cycle is shown to be a cycle of `G'init` or of its reverse. All invariants follow from one inductive invariant (`EdgeInv`): for an edge `{u,v}` with `u` to the left of `v`, writing `a = count[u]`, `b = count[v]`, either `a = b` and the edge points right iff `a` is even, or `a = b + 1` with `a` even and the edge points right, or `b = a + 1` with `a` even and the edge points left.
