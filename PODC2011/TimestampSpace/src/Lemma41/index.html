import Papers.PODC2011.TimestampSpace.Segments /-! # Lemma 4.1 of the paper Let `C` be reachable by a one-shot execution, let `B₀, B₁, B₂` be disjoint sets of processes each covering a set `R` of base objects, and let `U` be a list of at least two idle processes. Then there is a schedule `β σ β' σ'` where `{β, β'}` are the block writes by `B₀` and `B₁`, `σ` and `σ'` are concatenations of paused solo segments of distinct processes of `U` (every one of them is, right after its own segment, poised to write outside `R`), `|σ| + |σ'| = |U| - 1` and `|σ| ≥ ⌊|U|/2⌋`. We return the schedule as *moves* (process, labels), which is what the proof of Theorem 1.2 needs: `M₀` (the segments of `σ`) and `M₁` (those of `σ'`), with the covering property stated right after each segment. Since the processes of a segment never move again, the covering also holds at the end (part (b) of the lemma), see `SD.moved_U` in `OneShotStep.lean`. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false
