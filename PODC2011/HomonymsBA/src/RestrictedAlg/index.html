import Papers.PODC2011.HomonymsBA.Basic /-! # Proposition 18: the algorithm (Appendix A.3, Figures 6 and 7) Partially synchronous Byzantine agreement with numerate processes against restricted Byzantine processes, `n > 3t`, `ℓ > t`. **Authenticated broadcast with multiplicity (Figure 6).** Superround `k` consists of the rounds `2k` and `2k+1`. `Broadcast(i, m, k)` = put `m` into the `init` field of the round-`2k` message (the broadcaster's identifier `i` is the authenticated identifier of the pair). Every process keeps `a[h, m, k]` and sends its whole table in the `echo` field of every message (a function: at most one echo tuple per `(h, m, k)`, which is the paper's validity condition; entry `0` = no tuple). In round `R`: * if `R = 2k`, `a[h, m, k] := #received pairs tagged `h` whose `init` contains `m``; * `a[h, m, k] := max(a[h, m, k], α₁)`, `α₁` = the largest `α ≤ n` such that at least `n - 2t` received messages carry an echo `≥ α` for `(h, m, k)`; * in odd rounds, `Accept(h, α₂, m, k)` with `α₂` the largest `α ≤ n` such that at least `n - t` received messages carry an echo `≥ α`; the *witnesses* for `(m, k)` are `∑ h, α₂` (`witnesses`), stored in the state field `wit`. **Consensus (Figure 7).** Phase `ph` = superrounds `4ph … 4ph+3` = rounds `8ph … 8ph+7`; the leaders of phase `ph` are the processes with identifier `ph mod ℓ`. * round `8ph`: broadcast `propose v` for every `v ∈ proper` not conflicting with a lock; * round `8ph+2`: a leader with `n - t` witnesses (from round `8ph+1`) for some `propose v` sends `lock v`; * round `8ph+4`: broadcast `vote v` for a value `v` received in a `lock` message from the leader identifier (round `8ph+2`) with `n - t` witnesses for `propose v` (from round `8ph+3`); the choice depends only on the set of such values; * end of round `8ph+5`: if `n - t` witnesses for `vote v` (superround `4ph+2`), lock `(v, ph)` (removing other locks on `v`) and send `ack v` in round `8ph+6`; * round `8ph+6`: decide `v` if `n - t` witnesses for `propose v` (from round `8ph+5`) and `n - t` received `ack v` messages; * end of round `8ph+7`: remove a lock `(v₁, ph₁)` if there are `n - t` witnesses (current round) for some `vote v₂` of a phase `ph₂ > ph₁`, `v₂ ≠ v₁`. * every round: `proper` grows by the values in `t+1` received proper
