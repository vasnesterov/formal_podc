Identifiers `0, …, ℓ-1` (0-based) are split into `X = [0, t)`, `Y = [t, 2t)`, `Z = [2t, 3t)`, `W = [3t, ℓ)`; processes are `Fin n`. * `α`: process `k < ℓ` has identifier `k`, processes `k ≥ ℓ` have identifier `0` (a stack of `n - ℓ + 1` processes with identifier `0 ∈ X`); `Z` is Byzantine and silent; correct inputs `v₀`. * `β`: the same assignment; `Y` is Byzantine and silent; correct inputs `v₁`. * `γ`: `X` (one process per identifier) is Byzantine; *left* `= Y ∪ W` with input `v₀`; *right* `= Z ∪ W'` with input `v₁`, where `W'` are the processes `ℓ ≤ k < 2ℓ - 3t`, with identifier `k - ℓ + 3t ∈ W`; the remaining `n - 2ℓ + 3t` processes form an isolated stack with identifier `0`. Before round `T` all messages between different sides are lost and the Byzantine processes replay to the left what `X` sends in `α` and to the right what `X` sends in `β` (several copies from the stack: unrestricted); from round `T = gst` on everything is delivered and the Byzantine processes are silent.
