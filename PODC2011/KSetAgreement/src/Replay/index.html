import Papers.PODC2011.KSetAgreement.Model /-! # Replaying group-local executions (partitioning arguments) Generic facts about CSLib's asynchronous model (`Cslib.FLP.Algorithm`), used for the partitioning arguments of the paper: * `AgreeOn G s t`: the processes of `G` have the same local states in `s` and `t`, and every message in flight to `G` in `s` is also in flight in `t`. * `replay_mTr`: an execution in which only processes of `G` take steps can be replayed from any state that agrees on `G`, also for a different algorithm that behaves identically on `G`. * `agreeOn_mTr_outside`: steps of processes outside `G` preserve agreement on `G`. * `neverSteps_procFaulty`: a process that never takes a step is faulty (its wake-up message stays in flight). -/
