import Papers.PODC2011.AtMostOnceEffectiveness.Model /-! # Upper bound on effectiveness: `E_A(n, m, f) ≤ n − f` Theorem 2.1 of Kentros–Kiayias (PODC 2011), which is Corollary 1 of Kentros, Kiayias, Nicolaou, Shvartsman (2009): every algorithm that solves the at-most-once problem has effectiveness at most `n − f`. ## Model We use the model of `Model.lean`: deterministic processes, atomic registers, runs that may stutter, and crashes as fairness exemptions (`Run.Fair F`: the processes of `F` need not be scheduled). ## Proof 1. *Two pending `do`s* (`AtMostOnce.not_mem_log`, `AtMostOnce.pendingJob_injOn`): in a reachable configuration, if process `p` is about to perform job `j ∈ {1, …, n}`, then `j` has not been performed yet (else stepping `p` performs `j` twice), and no other process `q` is about to perform the same job (else step `p`, then `q`). Hence if all processes of `F` are about to perform jobs of `{1, …, n}`, then `Do + |F| ≤ n` (`do_add_card_le`). 2. *Adversary* (`advStep`, `advRun`): round-robin; at time `k` look at `p = k mod m`. If `p` is crashed, stutter. If `p` is about to perform a job of `{1, …, n}` and fewer than `f` processes have been crashed, crash `p` (stutter, add `p` to the crash set). Otherwise step `p` (stutter if `p` has terminated). 3. *Invariants* (`adv_inv`): crashed processes are frozen in front of a `do` of `{1, …, n}`; at most `f` processes are crashed; while fewer than `f` are crashed, no job has been performed. Hence `Do ≤ n − f` at all times. 4. *Fairness* (`advRun_fair`): with `F∞` = all processes that are ever crashed (`|F∞| ≤ f`), every process outside `F∞` is offered a step at the times `m·k + p` and either steps or has terminated. The hypotheses `0 < m` and `f ≤ n` of the paper are not needed (for `m = 0` the empty run works; for `f > n` the adversary never reaches `f` crashes and no job is ever performed). -/ set_option
