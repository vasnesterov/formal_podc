Otherwise some node `i` deviates from `c` (when it sees `c`) at infinitely many time steps; following the converging dynamics, letting `i` deviate at such a step and then activating `i` alone until a step at which it reproduces `c i` (such steps exist, because the converging dynamics activates `i` infinitely often), and repeating, gives a fair dynamics that changes infinitely often.
