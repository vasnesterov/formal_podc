import Papers.PODC2011.FaultTolerantSpanners.TwoSpanner.Defs /-! # Lemma 3.8 of Dinitz–Krauthgamer [DK11] is false as stated; a corrected version [DK11] M. Dinitz, R. Krauthgamer, *Fault-Tolerant Spanners: Better and Simpler*, PODC 2011 (arXiv 1101.5753), Section 3.5. For a partition of `V` into clusters (here: a labelling `cl : V → ι`, cluster `C = cl⁻¹ i`) the paper defines * `N(C)` — the vertices outside `C` adjacent (in either direction) to a vertex of `C`; * `δ(C)` — the arcs with exactly one endpoint in `C`; `E(C)` — the arcs with both endpoints in `C`; * `G(C)` — the subgraph of `G` induced by `C ∪ N(C)`; * `LP(C)` — LP (4) for `G(C)`, where the arcs of `δ(C)` get cost `0`. **Lemma 3.8** claims `∑_{C ∈ P} LP*(C) ≤ LP*`. This is false: an arc of `G(C)` with both endpoints in `N(C)` keeps its cost in `LP(C)`, but may lack (in `G(C)`) the length-2 paths that serve it in `G`. In the proof, the solution `⟨x^C, f^C⟩` puts `x^C_e = x_e` on `E(C)` and `x^C_e = 1` on `δ(C)`, but says nothing about such arcs. `lemma_3_8_counterexample` gives an explicit counterexample (`r = 0`, four vertices). `lemma_3_8_corrected` proves the statement for the modified program `LP'(C)` in which **every** arc of `G(C)` outside `E(C)` has cost `0` (this is what the analysis of Theorem 3.9 uses: "`x̃_e / 4` are the averages of the `LP(C)` values for the rounds in which `e` does not have cost `0`", i.e. the rounds in which `e ∈ E(C)`). Optimal values `LP*` are not formalised: the counterexample is stated as "there is a feasible solution of LP (4) of value `4`, while every family of feasible solutions of the `LP(C)` has total value `≥ M`", and the corrected lemma as "every feasible solution of LP (4) yields feasible solutions of the `LP'(C)` of no larger total value". -/ open Finset namespace DinitzKrauthgamer variable {V :
