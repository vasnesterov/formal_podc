Two configurations whose memories agree except on one iterator stack `s` (where they are related by some relation) and in which a process `r` has the same local state: a step of `r` keeps them related as long as the operation of `r` on `s` (if any) behaves the same in both (`step_off`). `write_read_next_false`: if after `α a b` only `u` and after `α b a` only `¬u` can be decided, `a` cannot be poised to write to an iterator stack while `b` is poised to read the iterator that this write initializes. In `α a b` that iterator is at position `2`, in `α b a` at position `1`; everything else is equal. A fresh process `R` cannot tell the two apart until it reads this iterator; stopping `R` just after this read in `α b a` and just before it in `α a b` gives two executions of opposite valencies with
