import Papers.PODC2011.UnboundedConcurrency.Common /-! # Valency with respect to all processes, and Lemma 13 (existence of a critical state) Afek–Morrison–Wertheim, PODC 2011, Section 5.3. In the unbounded concurrency model the valency of an execution is taken with respect to *all* extensions (by any of the infinitely many processes). A bivalent execution then need not have a critical extension in the usual sense (new processes may keep the execution bivalent forever, each taking one step), so the paper uses a weaker notion (Definition 2): `α` is critical if a step of `P` leads to a `v`-valent execution while a step of `Q` followed by a step of `P` leads to a `(¬v)`-valent one. ## Main definitions * `Uni A x α v`: no extension of `α` (by any processes) decides `¬v` (`α` is `v`-valent, or decides nothing at all, which wait-freedom excludes) ## Main statements * `Uni.append`, `false_of_out_both`, `false_of_agree`, `false_of_mem_eq`: executions of opposite valencies are distinguishable — by every process that outputs in both, by every process with the same local state in both, and by a fresh process if the memories are equal;
