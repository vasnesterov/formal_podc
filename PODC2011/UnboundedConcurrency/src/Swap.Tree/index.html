The **in-order** of the tree is the order of the dyadic rationals `key (d, k) = (2k+1) / 2^(d+1) ∈ (0, 1)`: the root is `1/2`, its left subtree is `(0, 1/2)`, its right subtree is `(1/2, 1)`, and so on. The right subtree of `v` is `rightSub v` (nodes strictly deeper than `v` whose key lies in `(key v, key v + 2^-(d+1))`). ## Main definitions * `Node`, `Node.key`, the linear order on nodes (in-order), `rightSub` * `next d S` (`NextUnaccessedNodeAtDepth`): the smallest node of depth `d` larger than every node of the snapshot `S`, if any; * `prev r m` (`GetPrevNodeMaxDepth`): the largest node of depth `≤ m` smaller than `r`, if any. ## Main statements * `Node.gap`: two keys of depth `≤ R` differ by at least `2^-(R+1)`; * `Node.between_left`: the nodes strictly between `leftNb v i` (the largest node of depth `i` smaller than `v`) and a node `w` of `v`'s right subtree are `v`, nodes of `v`'s right subtree, and nodes of `leftNb v i`'s right subtree (the tree fact behind Lemma 1);
