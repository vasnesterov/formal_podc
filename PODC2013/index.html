178

Lenzen — Optimal Deterministic Routing and Sorting on the Congested Clique

🛠️ Hard fix. Routing in 16 and sorting in 37 rounds hold for every n; the non-square proof of Theorem 3.7 fails and needed a new virtual-node reduction, the other gaps are local.

100

Vaidya, Garg — Byzantine Vector Consensus in Complete Graphs

⚠️ Weakened. Theorems 1–5 hold, but tightness for restricted algorithms is lost: Theorem 6's synchronous necessity has a counterexample at n = 4, d = 2, f = 1; only its sufficiency survives, with one constant fixed.

81

Lenzen, Peleg — Efficient distributed source detection with limited bandwidth

✏️ Slip. Pipelined Bellman–Ford solves (S, h, σ)-detection in σ + h − 1 rounds with one pair per message, with Lemmas 4.3–4.6, Corollaries 4.7 and 4.10 and the β-pairs remark; only Corollary 4.4's equality part fails when fewer than k sources are within h hops.

74

Dieudonné, Pelc, Villain — How to Meet Asynchronously at Polynomial Cost

🔧 Easy fix. Polynomial-cost rendezvous (Theorem 3.1) holds; Lemma 3.3's proof misses a case and the length estimates for Π are false, both fixed locally. Section 4 applications unchecked.

41

Raynal, Stainer — Synchrony Weakened by Message Adversaries vs Asynchrony Restricted by Failure Detectors

❌ Intent lost. The characterizations of Σ (QUORUM) and of Ω in read/write (SOURCE, TOUR) and the adversary hierarchy are refuted; SOURCE for Ω in AMP is unestablished. Theorem 1 and Theorem 6 stand.

26

Attiya, Gotsman, Hans, Rinetzky — A Programming Language Perspective on Transactional Memory Consistency

⚠️ Weakened. Opacity suffices for refinement after small definition fixes and is necessary for trace refinement, but Theorem 24's necessity for states has a broken proof and is open for the intended semantics.

21

Dolev, Lenzen — Early-Deciding Consensus is Expensive

🛠️ Hard fix. All checked bounds hold (t²f/44 Byzantine, f + 2 rounds, Ω(n²f) crash); the orderly-crash algorithm needed a new correctness proof, and three other proofs needed local repairs.

20

Lv, Moscibroda — Fair and Resilient Incentive Tree Mechanisms

🛠️ Hard fix. Impossibility, TDRM and CDRM all hold after slips (PO needs a strict inequality); TDRM's Sybil-proofness needed a new proof, since the appendix argument fails.

17

Kowalski, Mostéfaoui — Synchronous Byzantine Agreement with Nearly a Cubic Number of Communication Bits

❌ Intent lost. Two Byzantine processes break agreement at n = 7, t = 2, so the protocol is not Byzantine agreement; Theorem 2 and Lemma 5 are false. Validity, Lemmas 1-4 and the basic bit count hold.

12

Dubois, Guerraoui — Introducing speculation in self-stabilization: an application to mutual exclusion

⚠️ Weakened. SSME's self-stabilization and ⌈diam/2⌉ synchronous bound hold, Theorem 2 by a new proof; the optimality lower bound is false in general and holds only when privileged reads the own state.

12

Klasing, Kosowski, Pająk, Sauerwald — The Multi-Agent Rotor-Router on the Ring: A Deterministic Alternative to Parallel Random Walks

❌ Intent lost. Proofs of Theorems 3.2 and 3.5, the worst-case and best-case cover times of the abstract, are broken and unrepaired, though not refuted. Delayed deployments, corrected Lemma 3.1 and Theorem 3.7 are proved.

7

Castañeda, Gonczarowski, Moses — Brief announcement: Pareto optimal solutions to consensus and set consensus

🛠️ Hard fix. Unbeatability of Opt0, OptMaj, Opt_min-k and U-Opt0 holds, but Lemma 15 needed a new induction and Lemmas 6, 10 and Corollary 1 need added conditions. The uniform k-set protocol was not checked.

7

Attiya, Hendler, Levy — An O(1)-Barriers Optimal RMRs Mutual Exclusion Algorithm

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 1 (CAS version) holds under TSO, but Lemma 2 is false as stated and safety needs a new store-buffer invariant; the read/write version (Theorem 12) was not checked.
