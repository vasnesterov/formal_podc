We read "discovering a failure" as knowledge (full-information protocol): `i` knows at time `m` that `p` has crashed iff `⟨p, m⟩` is guaranteed crashed at `⟨i, m⟩` (`GuarCrashed r i m p m`); the rule **Corollary 1** (`corollary1`): `P0opt` is not unbeatable if `t ≥ 2` and `n ≥ 4` (the paper's example: one failure discovered in each of rounds 1 and 2, all initial nodes seen at time 2), and if `2 ≤
