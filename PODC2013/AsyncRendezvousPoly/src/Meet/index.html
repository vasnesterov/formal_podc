import Papers.PODC2013.AsyncRendezvousPoly.Model /-! # Forcing meetings: the two basic arguments All synchronization arguments of Section 3 reduce to two facts about the continuous model, both proved with the intermediate value theorem. * `meet_of_rep` (the core of Lemma 3.1, "catching"): if agent `a` follows a trajectory `T` (forward) while agent `b` keeps repeating `T T̄`, they meet. We map the position of `b` to a coordinate on `T` with a continuous triangle wave (`foldR`, written with `arccos ∘ cos`), and apply the IVT to the difference of the coordinates of `a` and `b`. * `meet_of_count` ("an integral trajectory forces progress"): if `b` follows `c` consecutive integral trajectories while `a` performs at most `c` edge traversals, they meet. Some integral trajectory of `b` is followed while `a` stays inside one closed edge `e`; `b` traverses `e` during it, and the IVT on `e` gives the meeting (`meet_of_edge`). **Remark (imprecision in the paper).** The paper's informal version of the second fact (§3, "if one agent follows an integral trajectory during some time interval, then it must either meet the other agent or this other agent must perform at least one complete edge traversal during this time interval") is false as stated for the continuous model: the other agent may cross a node in the middle of an edge-to-edge move without completing any edge traversal inside the interval (see `differencies.md`).
