import Papers.PODC2013.AsyncRendezvousPoly.Meet import Papers.PODC2013.AsyncRendezvousPoly.Bounds /-! # Synchronization lemmas (Lemmas 3.2–3.6) Dieudonné, Pelc, Villain, *How to meet asynchronously at polynomial cost*, PODC 2013 (arXiv:1301.7119v2), §3.2. Two agents `x`, `y` (`Agent`: modified label, start node, walk chosen by the adversary) execute Algorithm RV-asynch-poly in a connected graph of size `n`. All statements are of the form "if the agents have not met before, then by the time agent `y` completes part `Y` of its route, agent `x` has completed part `X` of its route", i.e. an inequality between completion times `τ`. The hypothesis "not met before" is `NoMeet E x y Ex Ey`: no meeting at a time `t` at which `x` has completed at most `Ex` and `y` at most `Ey` edge traversals. Instead of the paper's `n + l` we use any `N0 ≥ n` (the paper's choice is `N0 = n + l`); the synchronization lemmas do not use `l`.
