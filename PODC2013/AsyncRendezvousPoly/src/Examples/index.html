import Papers.PODC2013.AsyncRendezvousPoly.Main /-! # Non-vacuity and the paper's length estimates * `k2Explorer`: on the two-node graph `K₂`, `R(k, v) = (v, other node)` (one edge, `P ≡ 1`) is an exploration family; `idWalk` (`τ i = i`, unit speed) is a walk. So all hypotheses of `theorem_3_1_explicit` are satisfiable (`k2_meet`). * `paperYstar`, `paperAstar`: the paper's estimates `Y*_k = 2P(k)·Q*_k` and `A*_k = 2P(k)·Z*_k` (proof of Theorem 3.1) are not upper bounds on `|Y(k)|`, `|A(k)|` in general: for `P ≡ 1`, `|Y(1)| = 10 > 6 = Y*_1` and `|A(1)| = 42 > 12 = A*_1` (`paper_Ystar_wrong`, `paper_Astar_wrong`). The paper forgets that `Y'(k)` inserts `P(k) + 1` copies of `Q(k)` (one at each of the `P(k) + 1` nodes of `R(k)`) plus `P(k)` edges.
