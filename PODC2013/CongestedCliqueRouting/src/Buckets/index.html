For `x ∈ U`, the bucket index is `min (rk U x / q) (k-1)` (`bucketOf_delims`); if `|U| ≤ q k`, every bucket receives at most `q` elements of `U` (`card_fiber_bucketOf_delims`). * The segment lemma `card_filter_add_one_le`: the elements of `H` in a fibre of a monotone map form a contiguous block of positions, so a fibre with `m` elements of `H` contains at least `m / p` samples.
