import Papers.PODC2013.CongestedCliqueRouting.Primitives /-! # The routing algorithm on `s²` nodes (Algorithms 1 and 2 of the paper) Node set `V ≃ Fin s × Fin s` (groups `W` of size `s = √n`). Every node `v` initially holds the messages `inp d v` (at most `s²` of them), every node is the destination of at most `s²` messages. The algorithm takes 15 rounds: * **Phase A** (Step 2 of Algorithm 1 = Algorithm 2; rounds 0–5). Rounds 0–1: group announcement of the counts `c0 v j` (messages of `v` for group `j`) (Steps 1 and 3 of Algorithm 2, merged). Round 2: node `(a, j)` broadcasts the total `T a j` of messages of group `a` for group `j` (end of Step 1). Locally: König-split `T` into `s` parts `Tp x` with row/column sums `≤ s²` (Step 2: the `n` messages from `W` to `W''`), distribute the quota of each group among its members (`q`, the "deferred completion" of Step 2), split once more inside the group (`Ep`, Step 4). Rounds 3–4: Corollary 3.3 inside the groups (Step 5). Round 5: every node sends, for every target group, one message to each of its members (Step 6). * **Phase B** (Step 3 of Algorithm 1 = Corollary 3.5; rounds 6–9): group announcement of the counts per destination group, König split, Corollary 3.3. * **Phase C** (Step 4 of Algorithm 1; round 10): one message per edge to the destination group. * **Phase D** (Step 5 of Algorithm 1 = Corollary 3.4; rounds 11–14): group announcement of the counts per destination, Corollary 3.3 delivering every message at its destination. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false set_option linter.deprecated
