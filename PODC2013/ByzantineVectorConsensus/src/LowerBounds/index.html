The `3t + 1` part is reduced to the Fischer–Lynch–Merritt bound for weak consensus (CSLib, `weakValidity_not_solvesConsensusSync_of_ne`) by restricting to inputs on the diagonal of `ℝ^d`; the `(d + 1) t + 1` part is the direct argument below. * `theorem_4`: **Theorem 4** — approximate BVC in the partially synchronous (hence also in the asynchronous) model needs `n ≥ (d + 2) t + 1`. Instead of the paper's "`f = 1` first, then simulation of `f` processes by one", we partition the processes directly into `d + 1` (resp. `d + 2`) groups of size `≤ t` by the residue of their index (`card_mod_class_le`); group `l ∈ [0, d]` gets input `a + lbPoint d c l` (`0` and the scaled standard basis vectors), group `d + 1` is the *slow* group of Theorem 4. A process of group `m` cannot exclude that group `l ≠ m` is Byzantine but behaves correctly (synchrony: `Run.enlarge`; partial synchrony: `Run.extend`, where in addition the slow group is correct but its messages are delayed past the decision time), so its decision lies in the hull of the inputs of the groups `≠ l` for every such `l`; by `eq_add_lbPoint_of_mem_convexHull` it must decide its own input.
