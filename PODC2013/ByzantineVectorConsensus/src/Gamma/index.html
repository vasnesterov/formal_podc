import Mathlib /-! # The safe area `Γ` and Lemma 1 of Vaidya–Garg (PODC 2013) `Γ(Y) = ⋂_{T ⊆ Y, |T| = |Y| - f} H(T)` (equation (1) of the paper), where `H` is the convex hull. A multiset `Y` of points is represented by an indexed family `y : ι → E` restricted to a finite set of indices `C` (Appendix B of the paper defines multiset subsets exactly through index sets). * `GammaOn f C y` — `Γ` of the multiset `{y k | k ∈ C}`. * `GammaOn_nonempty` — **Lemma 1**: if `|C| ≥ (dim E + 1) f + 1` then `Γ ≠ ∅`. The paper derives it from Tverberg's theorem; we derive it from **Helly's theorem** (`Convex.helly_theorem'` in Mathlib) and a counting argument: any `dim E + 1` of the sets `T` miss together at most `(dim E + 1) f < |C|` indices, so they share a point of `Y`. * `GammaOn_subset_convexHull` — `Γ` is contained in the hull of every sub-multiset with at least `|C| - f` elements (used for validity). * `gammaPt` — a deterministic choice of a point of `Γ` that depends only on the multiset. * `lbPoint`, `eq_lbPoint_of_mem_convexHull` — the point configuration of the lower-bound proofs (Theorems 1 and 4) and the fact that it pins down every valid decision. * `gamma_empty_of_card_eq` — Lemma 1 is tight: `Γ` can be empty when `|Y| = (d + 1) f`. -/ open Finset Module open scoped Pointwise namespace ByzantineVectorConsensus section Gamma variable {ι E : Type*} [AddCommGroup E] [Module ℝ E] -- CSLib candidate (`GammaOn` and Lemma 1 via Helly) /-- `Γ` (equation (1) of the paper) of the multiset `{y k | k ∈ C}`: the intersection of the convex hulls of all sub-multisets of size `|C| -
