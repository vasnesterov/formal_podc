Every event satisfies `cost + pot' ≤ pot + [p is in the remainder section] · (19·h + 23)` (`pot_step`); summing over a passage gives Lemma 10 (`RMRMain.lean`). Outside the busy-waiting statements `pot` only depends on the program counter of `p` (a budget for the remaining program-order steps: every step costs at most one RMR for a read plus one for an issued write). For the two busy-waiting statements: * statement 25 (`phi25`): with `x = exits` in memory, while `x ≤ e + 1` the budget is `miss(exits) + (e + 2 − x) + F + 1 + [lock ≠ −1 ∧ miss(lock)]`, where `F = ⌊(2e + 4 − mu)/2⌋` counts the lock acquisitions (successful CAS or hand-over) that can still happen before `exits` reaches `e + 2` (`mu` = `2·exits + [Pend]`, `RMRInv.lean`). Once `x ≥ e + 2` the loop ends after at most one more read of `lock` and one of `exits`. At entry (`x = e`) this is at most `6` (`phi25_entry`). * statement 27 (`phi27`): `miss(signal[p]) + [signal[p] = false]` (at most `2`); only one write of another process (`signal[p] ← true`) can
