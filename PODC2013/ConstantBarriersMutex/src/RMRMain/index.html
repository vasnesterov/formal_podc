import Papers.PODC2013.ConstantBarriersMutex.RMRPassage import Papers.PODC2013.ConstantBarriersMutex.Invariant /-! # Lemma 10 of the paper: constant barriers and `O(log n)` RMRs per passage Attiya, Hendler, Levy, *An O(1)-Barriers Optimal RMRs Mutual Exclusion Algorithm*, PODC 2013, Lemma 10: "A process performs a constant number of barriers and incurs `O(log n)` RMRs during a passage." Model: Algorithm 1 under TSO (`Model.lean`), `n = 2^h` processes, CC cost model of Section 3 (`RMR.lean`). The bounds hold for **every** run (no fairness) and every process `p`, for every time interval `[t₁, t₂)` within one passage of `p` (`Run.WithinPassage`: `p` is not in the remainder section at any time strictly between `t₁` and `t₂`), in particular for a complete passage and for every prefix of an incomplete one. * `lemma10_rmr`: at most `19·h + 23 = 19 log n + 23` RMRs. * `lemma10_barriers_le`: at most `2` barriers complete; `lemma10_barriers`: a complete passage completes exactly `2` barriers (statements 22 and 50). * `stmt25_rmr_le`: the busy-wait of statement 25 costs at most `6` RMRs (the paper's proof says 4: "at most twice when it reads `exits` and at most twice when it reads `lock`"; `lock` can in fact be read remotely 4 times, see `README.md`); `stmt27_rmr_le`: statement 27 costs at most `2`. -/ namespace Podc.ConstantBarriersMutex variable {h : ℕ} /-- **Lemma 10 of [AHL13], RMR part.** In every run, the number of RMRs (CC model) incurred by a process `p` during one passage, or during any part of a passage, is at most `19·h + 23 = 19 log n + 23`. -/ theorem lemma10_rmr (r : Run h) (p : Proc h) {t₁ t₂ : ℕ} (hw : r.WithinPassage p t₁ t₂) : r.rmr p t₁ t₂ ≤ 19 * h + 23 := r.rmr_le_of_inv r.inv p hw /-- **Lemma 10 of [AHL13], barrier part (upper bound).** In every run, a process completes at most two barriers during one passage (or any part of it). -/ theorem lemma10_barriers_le (r : Run h) (p : Proc h) {t₁ t₂ : ℕ} (hw : r.WithinPassage p t₁ t₂) : r.barriers p t₁ t₂ ≤ 2 := r.barriers_le p hw /-- **Lemma 10 of [AHL13], barrier part.** A complete passage completes exactly two barriers (statements 22 and 50). -/ theorem lemma10_barriers (r : Run h) (p : Proc h) {t₁ t₂ : ℕ} (hP : r.Passage p t₁ t₂) : r.barriers p t₁ t₂ = 2 := r.barriers_passage p hP /-- **Statement 25 (proof of Lemma 10 of [AHL13]).** Busy-waiting at statement 25 costs at most `6` RMRs: if `p` executes statement 24 at time `t₀` and stays at statement 25 at all times in `(t₀, t₂)`, then `p` incurs at most `6` RMRs in the events at times `t₀ + 1, …, t₂ − 1`. (The paper claims at most `4`.) -/ theorem stmt25_rmr_le (r : Run h) (p : Proc h) {t₀ t₂ : ℕ} (h0 : (r.s t₀).pc p = .e24) (he : r.ev t₀ = .step p) (hin : ∀ t, t₀ < t → t < t₂ → ((r.s t).pc p).At25) : r.rmr p (t₀ + 1) t₂ ≤ 6 := r.stmt25_rmr_le_of_inv r.inv p h0 he hin /-- **Statement 27 (proof of Lemma 10 of [AHL13]).** Busy-waiting at statement 27 costs at most `2` RMRs. -/ theorem stmt27_rmr_le (r : Run h) (p : Proc h) {t₀ t₂ : ℕ} (hin : ∀ t, t₀ ≤ t → t < t₂ → (r.s t).pc p = .e27) : r.rmr p t₀ t₂ ≤ 2 := r.stmt27_rmr_le_of_inv r.inv p hin end Podc.ConstantBarriersMutex
