A mutual exclusion algorithm provides *k-bounded waiting* if, in every execution, no process enters the critical section more than `k` times while another process is waiting in its entry section. The *waiting section* starts when the CAS of statement 23 has been performed (Definition 1); in the model it consists of the program counters `PC.Waiting` (statements 24–27). * `lemma8` — while `p` is in its waiting section during the time interval `[t₁, t₂]`, any other process `q` enters the critical section at most `log n + 2 = h + 2` times at steps in `[t₁, t₂)` (any run, no fairness needed). * `lemma8_log_n_add_three` — the paper's statement, `(log n + 3)`-bounded waiting. * `lemma9` — the key lemma, in the form used by the proof (see `BW.lemma9`). The proof follows the paper.
