import Mathlib /-! # Chain functions of TDRM and their inequalities A participant with contribution `c` is represented by TDRM as a chain of `N = n + 1` nodes (`n = ⌈c/μ⌉ - 1`): a head of contribution `ε = c - n μ ∈ (0, μ]` followed by `n` nodes of contribution `μ`. For such a chain (with discount `a`): * `Gc c = ε aⁿ + μ g(n)`: the depth-weighted sum of the chain seen from its tail; * `Hc c = ε + a μ g(n)`: the depth-weighted sum of the chain seen from its head; * `Qc c = ε² + a μ ε g(n) + μ² s(n)`: the "inner" quadratic form `Σ_{w ≥ x} a^{d(w,x)} C(w) C(x)`; where `g(n) = Σ_{i<n} aⁱ` and `s(n) = Σ_{k<n} g(k+1)`. The inequalities below (`Qc_add`, `Qc_superadd`, `Hc_add_le`, `Gc_add_le`, `Gc_subadd`) are the whole content of the "optimal Sybil partition is an ε-chain" Lemmas 1–5 of the paper. -/ namespace
