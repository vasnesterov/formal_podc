import Papers.PODC2013.IncentiveTrees.Geometric import Papers.PODC2013.IncentiveTrees.Impossibility import Papers.PODC2013.IncentiveTrees.TDRMChain /-! # The TDRM mechanism (Algorithm 4) and its closed form TDRM with parameters `λ, μ, a, b, φ` transforms the referral tree `T` into the *reward computation tree* (RCT) `T'`: a participant `u` becomes a chain of `N_u = ⌈C(u)/μ⌉` nodes, the head with contribution `C(u) - (N_u - 1) μ`, the others with `μ`; the children of `u` hang below the tail of its chain. Every RCT node `w` gets `R'(w) = (λ/μ) C(w) Σ_{x ∈ T'_w} a^{dep_w(x)} b C(x) + φ C(w)`, and `R(u)` is the sum of `R'` over the chain of `u`. (For `C(u) = 0` we use a chain of one node of contribution `0`; the paper's `N_u = 0` would be an empty chain.) `tdrm_node` gives the closed form `R(u) = (λ b / μ) (Q(C(u)) + a G(C(u)) X) + φ C(u)`, with `X` the sum of the depth-weighted contributions of the RCTs of the children (`rctW`), and `Q, G, H` the chain functions of `TDRMChain.lean`. -/ namespace Podc.IncentiveTrees open Chain section
