The paper proves it by iterating Lemma 3.5, whose proof needs the algorithm to decide within `f' + 2` rounds in executions with `f' < f` faults (`theorem_3_6` in `Critical.lean` has this hypothesis). The statement holds without it. The repair: in the executions `E(W)` the Byzantine critical node keeps equivocating *forever* (it sends to the nodes of `W` what it sends in `E₀` and to the others what it sends in `E₁`, in every round from `r` on) instead of falling silent after round `r + 1`.
