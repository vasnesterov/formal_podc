import Papers.PODC2018.AsymptoticConsensus.Valency /-! # The lower-bound engine: greedy executions with slowly shrinking valency The proofs of Theorems 1, 2, 3 and 5 of FNS'18 share one argument: from the current configuration, the valency is covered by the valencies of the (finitely many) successors, any two successors are connected by a short chain of successors with intersecting valencies (Lemma 7 / Lemma 12 / Lemma 20), hence some successor keeps a `1/(D+1)` fraction of the valency diameter. We formalize it once, for *block moves*: a move `m ∈ K` applies the finite sequence of graphs `blk m 0, …, blk m (L-1)`. Network models are the case `L = 1` (Theorems 1, 2, 5); the `Ψ`-sequences of Theorem 3 use `L = n - 2`. Valencies are taken w.r.t. the set `BlockPat K blk L` of patterns that are concatenations of blocks (the paper's "properties", Section 6.1), which is shift-invariant at block boundaries, so no snapshots are needed.
