import Mathlib /-! # Asynchronous message passing on a graph, and leader election as a game (Sections 2, 7 of [YM18]) [YM18] A. Yifrach, Y. Mansour. *Fair Leader Election for Rational Agents in Asynchronous Rings and Networks.* PODC 2018 (arXiv:1805.04778v2). **Model** (Section 2, for an arbitrary communication graph). Processors are the vertices of a finite simple graph `G` on `V` (the ids, known to everybody). Neighbours communicate over reliable FIFO links (one per direction). Processors are deterministic and event driven; the randomness of processor `v` is an input `i ∈ I v` drawn uniformly from a finite set, independently of the other processors (the input space is the product `∀ v, I v`). A processor (`GProc`) is given by * `sent h w`: all messages sent to `w` after processing the history `h` (the list of received `(sender, message)` pairs, in order of reception — the processor sees the interleaving of its links); `sent [] w` are the messages sent at wake-up; sending is incremental; * `out h`: `some o` if after processing `h` it terminates with output `o : Option V` (`none` = `⊥`). A configuration is the vector of processed histories; a step on the directed link `(u, v)` delivers the next pending message from `u` to `v` if `u, v` are adjacent and `v` has not terminated (`gdeliver`). An oblivious schedule is a sequence of directed links `σ : ℕ → V × V` (`grun`), fair if every directed edge occurs infinitely often (`GFair`). The outcome (`goutcome`) is `some o` if every processor terminates with output `some o`, otherwise `FAIL = none`. **Game** (Definitions 2.1–2.3). `prob`, `expU`: probability and expected utility over the uniform inputs; `IsRational` (Def. 2.1), `Protocol.deviate` (Def. 2.2: the members of a coalition `C` run arbitrary strategies, which may depend on their own random input), `IsFLE` (every leader has probability exactly `1/n` for every fair schedule), `IsResilient` (Def. 2.3). Protocols are general (not necessarily symmetric), as in Appendix F; since a processor knows its id, this is no restriction. **Definition 7.1** (`IsKSimTree`). As written, Def. 7.1 asks for a graph homomorphism `f : G → T` into a tree with connected fibres of size `≤ k`; since a tree has no loops, an edge inside a fibre is impossible, so all fibres are singletons (`IsKSimTreeLiteral.card_fiber_le_one`) and the definition does not depend on `k`. The intended notion (stated in words after Def. 7.1 and used in Cor. F.4) is a partition of `V` into connected parts of size `≤ k` whose quotient graph is (a subgraph of) a tree: every edge of `G` lies inside a part or joins two parts adjacent in `T`. IsKSimTreeLiteral.card_fiber_le_one {k : ℕ} (h : IsKSimTreeLiteral G k) : IsKSimTreeLiteral G 1 := by obtain ⟨W, T, f, hT, -, hconn⟩ := h refine ⟨W, T, f, hT, fun w => ?_, hconn⟩ rw [Set.ncard_le_one] intro x hx y hy by_contra hxy obtain ⟨p⟩ := (hconn w).preconnected ⟨x, hx⟩ ⟨y, hy⟩ cases p with | nil => exact hxy rfl | @cons _ z _ hadj _ => have h1 : G.Adj x z.1 := hadj have hfz : f z.1 = w := z.2 have := f.map_rel h1 rw [show f x = w from hx, hfz] at this exact this.ne rfl end Podc.FairLeaderElection.Trees
