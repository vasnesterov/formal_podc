Processors `Fin n` sit on the asynchronous unidirectional ring of `Cslib.UniRing`. Each processor terminates with an output in `Option (Fin n)` (`none` is `⊥`, abort). A *protocol* is a family of strategy profiles `P ω` indexed by the joint randomness `ω : Ω` of the processors (a finite type with the uniform distribution). An *adversarial deviation* (Def. 2.2) of a coalition `C` replaces the strategies of the processors in `C` by arbitrary strategies `A`; we consider deterministic deviations (the adversaries' strategies do not depend on `ω`), which is all that is needed for the negative results of the paper and does not affect Lemma 2.4. * `outcomeUnder P σ`: the outcome of the execution of profile `P` under the oblivious schedule `σ` (`some v` if all processors terminate with output `some v`, else `FAIL = none`); by `outcomeUnder_eq` it does not depend on the fair schedule and equals `outcome P`. * `prob`, `expU`: probability of an outcome and expected utility over `ω`. * `IsRational`, `IsFLE`, `IsResilient` (Def. 2.3), `IsUnbiased`; Lemma 2.4 is `lemma_2_4`. -/ namespace Podc.FairLeaderElection open Cslib.UniRing variable {n : ℕ} [NeZero n] {M : Type*} /-- Profiles of strategies with outputs `Option (Fin n)` (`none` = `⊥`). -/ abbrev Profile (n : ℕ) (M : Type*) := Fin n → Proc M (Option (Fin n)) /-- The outcome of a profile: `some v` if every processor terminates with output `v`, otherwise `FAIL` (`none`).
