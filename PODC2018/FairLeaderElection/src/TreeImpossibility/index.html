1/2` and `1 ≤ k ≤ 2`. * `k2FLE`, `k2FLE_isFLE`: an FLE protocol on `K₂` (`0` draws the leader, sends it and outputs it, `1` outputs what it receives). * `thm_7_2_boundary_false`: the statement of
