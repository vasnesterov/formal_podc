(2), with only `ln 0 - ln 0 = 0` as convention). The direction "TV ⇒ multiplicative" fails already for the hardcore model, at distance `1`: pinning a neighbour `u` of `v` to *occupied* forces `μ^σ_v(occupied) = 0`, while pinning it to *unoccupied* gives `μ^τ_v(occupied) > 0`, so `err(μ^σ_v, μ^τ_v) = ∞` and no finite rate `δ_n(1)` can bound it. * `hardcore G λ`: the hardcore model (weighted independent sets) as a Gibbs distribution; `hardcore_locallyAdmissible`, `hardcore_gibbsLocal` (constraints of diameter `≤ 1`). * `hardcore_not_mssm`: on any graph with an edge, the hardcore model has **no** strong spatial mixing in multiplicative error with any real-valued rate. * `corollary_5_2_false`: the class of hardcore models (`λ = 1`) on perfect matchings `matchingGraph m` (`n = 2m` vertices, every `m ≥ 1`) is locally admissible, local, and has strong spatial mixing with exponential decay in TV distance (`δ_n(d) = 2 · (1/2)^d`), but has no multiplicative strong spatial mixing at all. The paper's own proof route (Theorem 5.1 for `err`) only bounds `err` at distances `> 2t + ℓ` where `t` is the locality of the boosted algorithm, which gives decay rate `√α` at large distances.
