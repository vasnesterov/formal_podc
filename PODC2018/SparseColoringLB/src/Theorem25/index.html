It is planar and triangle-free. The paper derives Theorem 2.5 from Observation 2.4 with the Klein bottle grid `G_{5,2ℓ+1}`, whose balls of radius `< ℓ` look like balls of `H_{2ℓ}`. Since `G_{5,2ℓ+1}` has *more* vertices than `H_{2ℓ}` (`10ℓ + 5 > 10ℓ`), Observation 2.4 does not literally apply, and it would only give `r ≤ ℓ - 2`. We use the Klein bottle grid `klein 5 k` with the *same* vertex set as `H_k`: `H_k` is `klein 5 k` with the twisted wrap-around edges deleted, and the column shift `kshift` (an automorphism of `klein 5 k`) moves any edge to the middle of `H_k`, where the deleted edges are invisible within radius `r` as soon as `2r + 2 ≤ k`.
