We use, as planar graphs, the triangulated cylinders `cutTorus w c` obtained from `torus w` by deleting the edges between two consecutive columns `c` and `c + 1` (a strip of `w + 1` nested triangles; it is planar, see `differencies.md`: planarity itself is not formalized). Seen from a node at distance `≥ r` from the cut (and `> r` on one side), `torus w` and `cutTorus w c` have the same `r`-ball.
