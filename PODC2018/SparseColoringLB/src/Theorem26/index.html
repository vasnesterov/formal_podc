The Klein bottle grid `klein m m` (`m` odd, `2r + 3 ≤ m ≤ N`) is not 3-colourable, and every edge of it is seen, within radius `r` from both endpoints, exactly as an edge in the middle of the grid: shift the edge to column `r` (Klein automorphism `kshift`), delete the twisted edges (cylinder `C_m □ P_m`), rotate the rows (cylinder automorphism) and delete the wrap-around rows (grid `P_m □ P_m`), and finally put the `m × m` grid in a corner of the `N × N` grid (the remaining nodes get the unused identifiers).
