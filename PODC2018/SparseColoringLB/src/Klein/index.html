Equivalently, `klein m n = C_m □ P_n` plus the twisted wrap-around edges. The paper cites Gallai: `G_{2k+1, 2ℓ+1}` is 4-chromatic. We prove the part that is used, in a more general form: for every odd `m ≥ 3` and every `n ≥ 2`, `klein m n` is not 3-colourable (`klein_not_colorable`). The proof is the classical winding-number argument: in a 3-colouring with colours in `ℤ/3`, every edge has a "slope" `±1`; around each 4-face the slopes sum to `0`; hence the total slope `W j` of the column cycle `j` does not depend on `j`, while the twisted gluing reverses the orientation of the column cycle, so `W 0 = -W 0`. But `W 0` is a sum of `m` odd numbers.
