Here a process `p` also reads `huh?` from `j` when `p ∈ nb j` and `p` got *no* round-2 message from `j` (an `ok` `j` would have sent `all[1]` to `p`). With this decoding the 1-bit protocol solves AC in `γ̃_f` (`solvesACMid`): the proof of `MidRound15D.lean` goes through, the only change being how a correct process `c` that knows `all[1]` learns that
