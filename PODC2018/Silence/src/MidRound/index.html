import Papers.PODC2018.Silence.LowerBounds /-! # Committing in mid-round (Appendix D of Goren–Moses): the model `γ̃_f` and lower bounds In `γ̃_f` (the model of Guerraoui–Wang) a process may decide *in the middle of round `m + 1`*: after its round-`m + 1` sending phase and before receiving. A process that performs a sending action in its crashing round is guaranteed that the message is delivered. We model this with the same runs as `γ_f` and a mid-round decision event `DecidesMid alg r p m d` ("decides at time `m + ½`"): `p` is alive at time `m`, all its round-`m + 1` messages are delivered (so it did not crash before completing its sending phase), and its decision function (applied to its state at time `m`) returns `d`. "Deciding before time 2" means `m ≤ 1`. Results: * `silent_choir_mid`, `chain_to_correct_mid`: the Silent Choir Theorem / Lemma 4 for mid-round deciders (proved at the run level: KoP does not apply verbatim to mid-round decisions, because whether the decision is performed depends on the crash pattern, not only on the local state); * `lemma16a`, `lemma16b` (Lemma 16), `corollary5`, `corollary6` (`n² + fn − n` messages). -/ namespace
