Vaidya. *Optimal Record and Replay under Causal Consistency.* PODC 2018; full version arXiv:1805.08804v2, Appendix C. Definition C.4 extends the strong causal order to a family of partial orders `U = {U_m}`: `(w₁, w₂) ∈ SCO(U)` iff both are writes and `(w₁, w₂) ∈ U_{proc w₂}`; this is `π.SCO U`. Lemma C.5: if every `U_m` is a strict partial order on `Obs m` containing `PO|Obs m` and `SCO(U)`, then `U` extends to strongly causal views. Our proof differs from the paper's (which orders pairs of writes of two processes for all processes simultaneously): each process is completed independently by the generic `exists_strictTotal_extension`, which never adds new pairs `(x, y)` with `x` a write and `y` a write of the process itself.
