Contents are never repeated (`W.Nodup`): this is the paper's "the content of `C` is unique". * `L`: a linearization of the crash-free projection `N(H)` of the history, maintained incrementally (`IsLinearization.push`). Successful `CAS` operations are linearized at their successful `cas`, `READ`s and `CAS`es that fail at line 3 at their read of `C`. A `CAS` of `q` that read the current content `c` with `val = old` and has not yet applied its `cas` is linearized (*speculatively*, as failed) right after the successful `cas` that overwrites `c`. A speculatively linearized operation whose process crashes before returning is retracted (`IsLinearization.erase_ro`: its entry is read-only at its position). `At L x o r`: the entry of `x` in `L`
