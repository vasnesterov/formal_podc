556

Herlihy — Atomic Cross-Chain Swaps

🔧 Easy fix. The printed protocol is unsafe and not live; a deadline guard and one extra Δ repair it, proved safe, live and atomic. The strong-connectivity characterization holds.

79

Barenboim, Elkin, Goldenberg — Locally-Iterative Distributed (Δ+1)-Coloring below Szegedy–Vishwanathan Barrier

🔧 Easy fix. Core O(Δ) + log* n algorithm holds. The intro's 2p + 1 bound fails, fixed by Section 3's larger q. Side variant 3AG never terminates on K₂ (unsaved). Applications not checked.

53

Kol, Oshman, Saxena — Interactive Distributed Proofs

⚠️ Weakened. Upper bounds hold after local fixes. Lemma 3.11 is false; the Ω(log log n) lower bound is repaired only for bounded challenges, so the claim with unbounded shared randomness stays unproved.

52

Attiya, Ben-Baruch, Hendler — Nesting-Safe Recoverable Linearizability: Modular Constructions for Non-Volatile Memory

⚠️ Weakened. Recoverable read/write and CAS and the test&set impossibility hold; the counter, the only example of nesting, is not NRL, because a nested crash loses READ's volatile sum, and no repair is proved.

35

Függer, Nowak, Schwarz — Tight Bounds for Asymptotic and Approximate Consensus

⚠️ Weakened. Tight contraction-rate and decision-time bounds hold, Theorem 11 by a new proof. Theorem 4's converse is false; the valency characterization of exact consensus needs continuous consensus functions.

27

Censor-Hillel, Dory — Distributed Spanner Approximation

🔧 Easy fix. The main CONGEST lower bounds for directed spanners hold, and Lemma 3.2's reduction needs one extra round. The algorithms and the weighted and LOCAL bounds were not checked.

18

Feng, Yin — On Local Distributed Sampling and Counting

🔧 Easy fix. Corollary 5.2 is false: TV strong spatial mixing does not imply multiplicative mixing, a minor by-product. The cores of the main reductions and of Theorem 5.1 hold; their composition and round counts are unchecked.

16

Aboulker, Bonamy, Bousquet, Esperet — Distributed Coloring in Sparse Graphs with Fewer Colors

🔧 Easy fix. Main 6-colouring algorithm unchecked. Of the lower bounds, Theorem 2.6 is false for odd k by one round; the Omega(sqrt n), Omega(n) bounds and Theorem 2.5's exact bound are proved.

11

Yifrach, Mansour — Fair Leader Election for Rational Agents in Asynchronous Rings and Networks

⚠️ Weakened. Attacks on A-LEADuni hold. Theorem 7.2 is proved only for n = 2, and Theorem 8.1's coin-toss step is wrong and unrepaired. Theorem 5.1 and PhaseAsyncLead are unchecked.

9

Goren, Moses — Silence

🛠️ Hard fix. Silent Choir, the lower bounds and STEALTH, D2, 1.5D hold, but the protocol proofs' black-box consensus fails and needed a concrete floodset and a new argument; the tradeoff needed a D = 1 bound.

4

Burman, Beauquier, Sohier — Brief Announcement: Space-Optimal Naming in Population Protocols

🛠️ Hard fix. The formalized impossibilities and protocols hold, but the proof of Theorem 11 is broken and was replaced by a new adversary argument; Table 1 omits n > 2. Propositions 16, 17 not checked.

3

Xiang, Vaidya — Brief Announcement: Partially Replicated Causally Consistent Shared Memory

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorems 1 and 3 (necessary and sufficient timestamp condition) hold with new proofs; Lemma 4 and footnote 2 are false; the timestamp-size lower bound was not checked.

1

Attiya, Castañeda, Hendler, Perrin — Separating Lock-Freedom from Wait-Freedom

✅ Correct. No mistakes found; the impossibility Theorem 11 is proved in a stronger form, but the lock-free algorithms of Theorems 4 and 7, the other half of the separation, were not formalized.

1

Wei, Huang, Lu — Specification and Implementation of Replicated List: The Jupiter Protocol Revisited

🛠️ Hard fix. Out-of-range positions make the printed protocols diverge, fixed by one line; the proofs of Section 5 are not real arguments, so Theorem 26 needed a new proof. Equivalence and the weak list specification hold.

0

Boppana, Halldórsson, Rawitz — Simple and Local Independent Set Approximation

🔧 Easy fix. All main results hold; Theorem 4's indistinguishability proof needed another pair of graphs, and Theorem 2's constant 1.657 is rounded the wrong way (the exact 4(√2−1) is correct).

0

Jones, Khan, Vaidya — Brief Announcement: Optimal Record and Replay under Causal Consistency

🛠️ Hard fix. All optimal-record theorems hold after fixing Definition 6.4 and the notion of online record; the proofs of Theorem 6.7 and Lemma C.1(b) are broken and needed new arguments.
