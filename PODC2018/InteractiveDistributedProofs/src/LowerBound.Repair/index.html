The repair replaces `μ_A(F)` by the **joint law** `μA' F` of the bridge challenges `(r_{x_A}, r_{x_B})` and the set `𝓜_A(F, r)`, a distribution on `(R × R) × Set M`. The key observation is a conditional independence: `𝓜_A(F, r)` depends only on the challenges of `V_A ∪ {x_A, x_B}` (`MA_congr`), `𝓜_B(F₁, r)` only on those of `V_B ∪ {x_A, x_B}` (`MB_congr`), so given the bridge challenges they are independent, and (`uprob_inter_nonempty_eq_sum`) `Pr_r[𝓜_A(F, r) ∩ 𝓜_B(F₁, r) ≠ ∅] = ∑_{(s, S)} μA' F (s, S) · g(s, S)`, where `g(s, S) ∈ [0, 1]` (`gB`) is the probability over the `V_B` challenges that `S` meets `𝓜_B(F₁, r)` when the bridge challenges are `s`; `g` does not depend on `F`. With `F = F₁` the left side is `> 2/3` and with `F = F₂ ≠ F₁` it is `< 1/3` (Corollary 3.10), so `1/3 < ∑ (μ₁ − μ₂) g ≤ ½ ‖μ₁ − μ₂‖₁`: the repaired Lemma 3.11 (`lemma_3_11_repaired`). The packing Lemma 3.12 then bounds `|𝓕| < 5^(|R|² · 2^|M|)` (`card_family_lt`), and with `|R|, |M| ≤ 2^L` and `|𝓕| ≥ 2^{c k²}` this gives `c k² < 3 · 2^{2^{L+1}}`, i.e. `L ≥ log log k − O(1)` (`theorem_1_4_repaired`). **The challenge length matters.** The domain of `μA'` has size `|R|² · 2^|M|`, so the bound needs the challenge alphabet `R` to be bounded (Definition 1 gives each node `ℓ`-bit challenges, and `InDAM` bounds `|R|`). For protocols with unbounded randomness, which the introduction claims are also covered, this argument does not apply. The paper's claim "`G(F_A, F_B)` is symmetric iff `F_A = F_B`" is taken as the hypothesis `hiff` (only its direction `F_A ≠ F_B → ¬ Sym G(F_A, F_B)` has content: `sym_dumbbell_self`). The paper's other requirement on `𝓕` (`FamilyOK`: asymmetric, pairwise non-isomorphic graphs) is only used through `hiff`, so it is not a hypothesis here. `cex_joint_laws_far` shows the hypotheses are
