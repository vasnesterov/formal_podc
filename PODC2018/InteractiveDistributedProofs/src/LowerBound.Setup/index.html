import Papers.PODC2018.InteractiveDistributedProofs.Model /-! # Setting of the lower bound (Section 3.4): dumbbell graphs, simple protocols, `𝓜_A`, `μ_A` * `𝓕` is a family of graphs on `Fin k` (the paper's `{1, …, n}`); the paper requires all of them to be asymmetric and pairwise non-isomorphic (`FamilyOK`). * `dumbbell FA FB vA` is the paper's `G(F_A, F_B)`: copies of `F_A` on `V_A = {a i}` and of `F_B` on `V_B = {b i}`, bridge nodes `x_A, x_B`, and the path `v_A – x_A – x_B – v_B` with `v_A = a vA`, `v_B = b vA` (corresponding vertices, as needed for `G(F, F)` to be symmetric). * `IsSimple` is Definition 6: the bridge nodes accept iff a predicate of their view of the challenges and of their own response holds and both bridge nodes got the same response. * `MA` is `𝓜_A(F, r)`: the responses `m` to `x_A` that extend to a response making all of `V_A ∪ {x_A}` accept on `G(F, F)`;
