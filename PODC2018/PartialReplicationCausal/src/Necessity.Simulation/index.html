It is preserved by corresponding writes and by closes (which apply *all* updates sent to the closing replica), and implies that the causal dependency graphs of every replica differ only in `u_B` (`diffOnly_of_logSim`). * `MdSim b w S s₁ s₂`: for the replicas of `S`, metadata and applied sets coincide, and pending updates carry the same metadata. It is preserved by writes, applies and closes of replicas of `S` that do not store the register `w` of `u_B` (the closes make the same choices). -/ namespace Podc.PartialReplicationCausal.Necessity
