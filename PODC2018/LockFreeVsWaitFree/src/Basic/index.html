Assuming these properties only on admissible executions makes the impossibility result stronger than the paper's. ## Schedules In an admissible execution every process has at most one possible next label: an idle process `q ≠ p₀` can only invoke `output()` of side `1`; a pending process can only take its (deterministic) next step `I.nextLabel C q`; `p₀` invokes if it has not invoked yet and has nothing to do once its operation returned. The paper's "`q(C)`" is `X.ext1 α q`, and `X.ext α σ` lets the processes of a schedule `σ : List P` take their next label in turn. ## Valency (Section 5) * `X.Potent α v`: some admissible extension of `α` completes the operation of `p₀` with output `v` (paper: `α` is `v`-potent); * `X.Univ α v`: `α` is `v`-potent and not `w`-potent for `w ≠ v` (paper: `v`-univalent); * `X.Biv α`: `α` is potent for two different values (paper: bivalent).
