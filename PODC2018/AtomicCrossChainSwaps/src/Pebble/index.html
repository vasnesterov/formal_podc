Firing = placing pebbles (contracts) on all leaving arcs. Lemma 4.1: everybody fires iff `L` is a feedback vertex set; Lemma 4.3: in a strongly connected digraph everybody fires by round `diam D - 1`. * The eager game from `z` is bounded reachability (`ReachIn`): Lemma 4.2/4.3. * Theorem 4.12 (core, "waits-for" argument): if followers publish on a leaving arc only after all their entering arcs carry contracts, no arc on a cycle of followers ever gets a contract. * Lemma 4.13 (single leader): with simple paths, `D(v, v̂) ≥ D(w, v̂) + 1` for every arc `(v, w)` leaving a follower `v`; with the paper's literal (lax) paths it fails. -/ namespace
