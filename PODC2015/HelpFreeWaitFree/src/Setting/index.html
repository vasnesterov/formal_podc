import Papers.PODC2015.HelpFreeWaitFree.Shape /-! # Setting of the proof of Theorem 4.3 and the core claim We fix (`Ctx`) an implementation `I` of a type `A` from arbitrary base objects, a linearization function `f` that is help-free in the weak sense `OwnerOnlyWith` (a step deciding `a` before `b` is taken by `a`'s owner), wait-freedom, three distinct processes `p₁ p₂ p₃`, and the data `op, W, R` of an exact order type. As in the paper, `p₁` runs `op₁ = (p₁, 0)` (operation `op`), `p₂` runs `W`, `p₃` runs `R`. `X.Std n h` is the situation of Claims 4.5.2 and 4.5.3: `p₂` has completed its first `n` operations (which are `W(n)`) and they are decided before `op₁`, `p₂` has invoked at most `W(n+1)`, `p₁` at most `op`, and `p₃` and all other processes nothing. `core` is the heart of Claims 4.5.2/4.5.3: if `op₁` or `op₂ = (p₂, n)` is decided before `op₃ = (p₃, 0)`, and then `p₃` runs and completes `R(m)`, then in every extension the results of `R(m)` are those of a sequence of `W(n+1) ∘ (R(m) + op?)` if `op₂ ≺ op₁` in the linearization, and of `W(n) ∘ op ∘ (R(m) + W_{n+1}?)` otherwise. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false set_option linter.style.longLine false namespace Podc.HelpFreeWaitFree open Cslib.SharedMemory variable {A : ObjSpec} {ι : Type} [DecidableEq ι] {B : ι → ObjSpec} {P L : Type} [DecidableEq P] /-! ### Generic helpers -/ section Helpers /-- Responses of a sequence with tags, read off a linearization. -/ lemma taggedResps_of_seqOK {α : List (Label P A)} (tg : OpId P × A.Op → Bool) : ∀ {q : A.State} {S : List (OpId P × A.Op)}, SeqOK α q S → (∀ e ∈ S, tg e = true → Completed α e.1) → taggedResps A q (S.map (fun e => (tg e, e.2))) = (S.filter tg).filterMap (fun e => respOf α e.1) | _, [], _, _ => rfl | q, (x, o) :: S, hS, hc => by obtain ⟨h1, h2⟩ := hS have ih := taggedResps_of_seqOK tg h2 (fun e he => hc e (List.mem_cons_of_mem _ he)) by_cases ht : tg (x, o) = true · obtain ⟨r, hr⟩ : ∃ r, respOf α x = some r := by have := hc (x, o) (by simp) ht rw [completed_iff_respOf] at this;
