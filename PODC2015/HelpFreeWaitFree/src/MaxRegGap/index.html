import Papers.PODC2015.HelpFreeWaitFree.MaxRegister /-! # A gap in the proof of Theorem 4.11 (Claim 4.7.1, second case) In the proof of Claim 4.7.1, the paper argues (second case for line 4): after `W₃ = WriteMax(3)` completed without being decided before the pending `R₁ = ReadMax`, "`R₁` cannot possibly return any value `≥ 3` … But if `R₁` cannot possibly return a value `≥ 3`, then `R₁` is decided before `W₃`." The last inference is not valid: whether a *pending* operation is decided before another one depends on the linearization function, which may leave a pending read out of the linearization (Definition of linearization in Section 4.3) until it returns, even though its response is already determined. We show this on the max register of Figure 4.5 with the linearization function `lin'`: the linearization points of Section 4.8.2, except that a `ReadMax` is only included once it has returned (`isLinFun_lin'`). In the history `gapHist` (process `0` invokes `ReadMax` and reads `0`; process `1` then runs `WriteMax(3)` to completion), every extension in which `R₁ = (0, 0)` returns gives it the response `0` (`gap_resp`), yet `R₁` is not decided before `W₃ = (1, 0)` (`gap_not_decided`). (`lin'` is also help-free by the reasoning of Claim 4.8.1, since a read is inserted when it returns, at its read step; we do not formalize this, it is not needed to refute the inference.)
