import Papers.PODC2015.StabilizingByzStorage.Regular /-! # Eventual regularity of Fig. 2 (Lemma 3) Every read of the register of Fig. 2 that is invoked after some write has completed returns a *regular* value: the value of the last write completed before the read was invoked, or of a write invoked before the read returns and not completed when the read was invoked. The proof is by an inductive invariant `Inv` over reachable states: * bookkeeping of the ghost tags (`SInv`, per server); * `Inv.sync`: after the `WRITE` of the current/last write `k - 1` ended (`k = kSync σ`), at least `n - 2t` correct servers have `last_val` set by a write `≥ k - 1` and only messages of writes `≥ k - 1` pending (`Ghost.Fresh`); * `Inv.read`: during a read, at least `n - 2t` correct servers are fresh w.r.t. `base - 1` and all their recorded replies (and their `helping_val` once they delivered the `READ(true)`) are regular. At the return step, `2t + 1` copies among `n - t` replies include one from such a server. Main results: `lemma3_regular` (on the ghost history) and `lemma3_regular_exec` (on the labels of a finite execution, with the regularity condition `RegularRead` of Section 2.2). No bound on `n`, `t` or `|B|` is needed: the counting only uses `|Q| = n - t` and the `n - 2t` synchronized correct servers of the guards. -/ namespace Podc.StabilizingByzStorage.Regular open
