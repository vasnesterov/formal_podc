Let `p ≠ w0` be idle in the final configurations `C₁` of a `p`-clean execution `α₁` and `C₂` of a `p`-dirty execution `α₂` (both standard). If `p` could not distinguish `C₁` and `C₂` (`Impl.Agree p C₁ C₂`), then a solo run of a further operation of `p` would return the same response from both (`soloOp_eq_of_agree`); it completes by obstruction-freedom (`soloOp_spec`). In every linearization of the extended executions, the response is determined by the abstract state just before the new operation, and real-time order (plus the standard-execution discipline `Std`) pins down this state enough to force different responses: * **ABA-detecting register** (`separates_aba`): a new `DRead()` of `p` returns flag `false` after the clean execution (the last write is linearized before `p`'s last clean read) and `true` after the dirty one (the write of the dirty round is linearized after all of `p`'s earlier reads). * **LL/SC** (`separates_llsc`): a new `SC(y)` of `p` succeeds after the clean execution (`p`'s link from its clean `LL()` is still valid: only the writer executes `SC`, and all its operations are linearized before that `LL()`) and fails after the dirty one (the writer's `LL(); SC(x)` round succeeds and invalidates all links, and `p` executes no `LL()` afterwards).
