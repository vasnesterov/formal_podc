Then from every reachable quiescent configuration `Q` and for every list `ps` of distinct readers, a `(ps ∪ {w0})`-only execution leads to a configuration `C` where (i) all processes outside `ps` are idle, (ii) some ordering of `ps` is a successful schedule for `C`, and (iii) for every object `R`, at most `t` processes of `ps` are poised to `Write` `R` and at most `t` are poised to `CAS` `R`.
