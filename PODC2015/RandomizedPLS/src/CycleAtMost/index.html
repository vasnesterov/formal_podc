Port numbers are consistently ordered: `(q,t)` has its successor `(q,t+1)` on port `0` and its predecessor on port `1`. * `cycleAtMost_G`: every simple cycle of `G` has at most `c` nodes. The joining edges are bridges, so a cycle never uses them and stays inside one `B_q`. * The copies are the edges `H_q = {(q,1), (q,2)}`, `q < m` (`r = m = n/c`). * Crossing `H_i` and `H_j` creates the cycle `(i,2) … (i,c-1) (i,0) (i,1) (j,2) … (j,0) (j,1)` with `2c` nodes (`not_cycleAtMost_cross`); the crossing stays connected. * `thm_5_6_det`, `thm_5_6_rand`: Theorem 5.6 (lower bounds). -/
