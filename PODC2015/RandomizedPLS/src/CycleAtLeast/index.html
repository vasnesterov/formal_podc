It contains the `c`-cycle (`cycleAtLeast_G`). The copies are the cycle edges `H_k = {v_{3k+1}, v_{3k+2}}`, `k < ⌊c/3⌋` (`Wheel.K`). **The paper's `H_1` is a typo.** The paper takes `H_1 = G_e{v₀, v₁}` and `H_i = G_e{v_{3i}, v_{3i+1}}` (`i ≥ 2`). Since `v₀` is adjacent to every node, `H_1` is not independent of any other `H_i` (Definition 4.1), see `printed_H1_not_independent`; the crossing would not even be a simple graph. With `H_1 = G_e{v₁, v₂}` (as in Theorem 5.2) the proof goes through.
