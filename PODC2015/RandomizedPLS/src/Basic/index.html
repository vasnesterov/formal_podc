import Mathlib import Cslib.Probability.Finite.Uniform /-! # Randomized proof-labeling schemes: the model (Section 2 of Baruch–Fraigniaud–Patt-Shamir) M. Baruch, P. Fraigniaud, B. Patt-Shamir. *Randomized Proof-Labeling Schemes.* PODC 2015. * `Config S`: a port-numbered configuration (Section 2.1). The node set `V` is any finite type; `nbrs v : List V` lists the neighbours of `v` by port number (port `i` is `(nbrs v)[i]`, ports are numbered from `0`); every node has a state in `S` (identities, inputs, weights, … are part of the state). Graphs are simple (`nodup`, `loopless`, `symm`); connectivity is a property (`Config.Connected`) that families may require. * `IsPLS ver prover F P` (deterministic proof-labeling scheme, Section 2.2): labels are bit strings; the verifier at `v` sees its state, its label and the labels of its neighbours ordered by port. * `RScheme` (randomized verifier, Section 2.2): node `v` draws private coins uniformly from a finite space `Ω (state) (label) (degree)`; from its coins it computes one certificate (a bit string) per port; node `v` then decides from its state, its label and the certificates received from its neighbours (ordered by port). Coins of distinct nodes are independent (product space `Coins`). `IsRPLS σ prover F P pacc prej`: completeness `Pr[accept] ≥ pacc` on legal configurations with the prover's labels, soundness `Pr[reject] ≥ prej` on illegal configurations for every labelling. One-sided: `pacc = 1, prej = 1/2`; two-sided: `pacc = prej = 2/3`. * `EIScheme` (Definition 4.5, edge-independent schemes): every port has its own independent coin. * Verification complexity: `LabelBound` (PLS) and `CertBound` (RPLS) bound the label / certificate lengths on legal configurations by a function `κ` of the configuration (e.g. of `n`). * `Copies c r`: `r` pairwise independent port-preserving copies `emb i : W → V` of a pattern graph `H` (Definition 4.1 and the hypotheses of Proposition 4.3); `Copies.cross i j` is the crossing of Definition 4.2 induced by `σ = emb j ∘ (emb
