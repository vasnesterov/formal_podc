Baruch, P. Fraigniaud, B. Patt-Shamir. *Randomized Proof-Labeling Schemes.* PODC 2015. Given a deterministic PLS `(ver, prover)` for `(F, P)` with labels of at most `κ` bits, the compiled scheme is exactly the paper's: * `compileProver prover`: the label of `v` is the list `ℓ(v), ℓ(w₁), …, ℓ(w_d)` (own label, then the labels of the neighbours ordered by port); * `compileScheme ver` (an edge-independent scheme): on every port `v` sends the fingerprint `fpSend ℓ(v) x` of its own label (Lemma 3.3), with an independent coin `x` per port; `v` rejects if its label is not of the form `ℓ₀ :: ℓ₁ … ℓ_d` with `d = deg v`, otherwise it runs the fingerprint decider for each port `i` against `ℓᵢ` and, if all pass, outputs `ver (state v) ℓ₀ [ℓ₁, …, ℓ_d]`. ## Main statements * `compile_isRPLS`: the compiled scheme accepts legal configurations with probability `1` and rejects illegal ones with probability `> 2/3` (`compile_sound`), whatever the labels. * `compile_certBound`: certificates have `2 · fpBits κ ≤ 2 log₂ κ + 6` bits. * `theorem_3_1`: Theorem 3.1 of [BFP15]; corollaries
