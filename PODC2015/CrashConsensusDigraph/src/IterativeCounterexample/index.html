Intuitively, the values alternate between "true" and "negated", so `¬max` simulates alternating Max and Min rounds. Proof: the values after 4 iterations only depend on the deliveries in iterations `0, …, 3`; there are finitely many delivery patterns with at most one crash, and all of them (with all inputs) are checked by evaluation (`check_ok`, by `decide`). (Bounded exhaustive search, outside Lean, also confirms `notMax`, `T = 4` for `f = 2`; for `f = 3` it fails at `T = 4`.) -/ open Finset set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace
