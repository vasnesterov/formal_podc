import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.ApproxConsensus.DigraphConditions import Mathlib /-! # Crash-tolerant node connectivity and the fault-tolerant diameter Graph-theoretic notions of Tseng–Vaidya, *Fault-Tolerant (Crash-Tolerant) Consensus in Directed Graphs* (PODC 2015), Section 2. We use the shared reach sets of `Cslib/Computability/Distributed/ApproxConsensus/DigraphConditions.lean`: `Reaches G F a b` says that `a ∉ F` and `b` is reachable from `a` in the reduced graph `G_F` (Definition 1: the subgraph induced by `V − F`; `Digraph.ReachableAvoiding`). * `CTConn G k` — Definition 2 (*`k` crash-tolerant node connectivity*): every reduced graph `G_F` with `|F| ≤ k` has a node (a *source* / root, Definitions 3–4) reaching all its nodes. * `ctConn_iff_oneReach` — this is the *1-reach* condition of later work by the same authors (every two nodes of `G_F` have a common ancestor in `G_F`). The proof of Theorem 1 uses this reformulation implicitly ("there is a pair `i, j` such that no node has paths to both"). * `ReachesWithin G F k a b` — `b` is reachable from `a` in `G_F` by a path with at most `k` edges (`a ∉ F` and a walk of length `≤ k` in `G.deleteVerts F`, CSLib's `Relation.RelatesWithinSteps`). * `FTDiamLE G f D` — `D` bounds the *fault-tolerant diameter* `d` of equation (1): every source `s` of every reduced graph `G_F` (`|F| ≤ f`) reaches all of `G_F` within `D` hops, i.e. `height(s, G_F) ≤ D`. `ftDiam G f` is the paper's `d` (the least such `D`), and `ftDiamLE_card`: `d ≤ n − 1`. -/ open Finset Relation set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace CrashConsensusDigraph open Cslib.ApproxConsensus variable {V : Type*} [DecidableEq V] {G :
