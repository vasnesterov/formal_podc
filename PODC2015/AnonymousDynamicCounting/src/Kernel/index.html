import Mathlib /-! # The linear system `m_r = M_r s_r` of `M(DBL₂)` and its kernel (Lemmas 2–4) Di Luna, Baldoni, *Investigating the Cost of Anonymity on Dynamic Networks* (PODC 2015 brief announcement; full version arXiv:1505.03509v1), Section 4.2. In a multigraph of `M(DBL₂)` every non-leader node is joined to the leader, in each round, by the edges of one of the label sets `{1}`, `{2}`, `{1,2}` (`Lab.one`, `Lab.two`, `Lab.both`). The history of a node over `t` rounds is a word of length `t` over `Lab` (`words t`). The leader observes, for every label `j` and every word `p` of length `i < t`, how many nodes had history `p` before round `i` and an edge labelled `j` in round `i`: the row `(j, p)` of the paper's matrix, i.e. `rowSum t s j p = ∑_{w : RowMem j p w} s w`. **Indexing.** The paper's matrix `M_r` describes the leader's knowledge at the end of round `r` (rounds `0, …, r`); its columns are the words of length `r + 1`. We index by the number of rounds `t = r + 1`, so the paper's `M_r`, `k_r` are our objects for `t = r + 1`. Main results: * `inKer_iff`: the integer (or any commutative-ring) solutions of `M_r d = 0` are exactly the multiples of `kvec`, where `kvec (x :: w) = sgn x * kvec w` with `sgn {1} = sgn {2} = 1`, `sgn {1,2} = -1` (Lemmas 2 and 3: `k_r = [k_{r-1}, k_{r-1}, -k_{r-1}]`). * `sum_kvec`, `card_kvec_neg`, `card_kvec_pos` (Lemma 4). * `Matrix` form over `ℚ`: `ker_mulVecLin_matrix`,
