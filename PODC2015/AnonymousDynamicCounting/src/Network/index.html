Each node broadcasts one message (unbounded size) per round and receives the *multiset* of its neighbours' messages of that round (anonymous broadcast, no labels, the degree is not known before receiving). Deterministic algorithms (`NAlgo`): the leader starts in its own initial state, all other nodes in a common one. **The networks of the reduction.** `chainNet k L M` turns `M ∈ M(DBL_k)` into a dynamic network: a static path `c_0 – c_1 – ⋯ – c_L` whose end `c_L` is the leader, `k` relay nodes `V₁` (pairwise adjacent, all adjacent to `c_0`) and the nodes `W` of `M`; in round `r`, relay `j` and `w ∈ W` are adjacent iff `j ∈ L r w`.
