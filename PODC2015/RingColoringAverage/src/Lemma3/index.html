We prove it for minimal **4-colouring** algorithms (the paper states it for minimal 3-colouring algorithms and reduces to 4-colouring with "it is sufficient to prove the result for 4-colouring, as it implies the result for 3-colouring"; this reduction is not valid, because a 3-colouring algorithm that is minimal among 3-colouring algorithms need not be minimal among 4-colouring algorithms, which is what Lemma 2 needs; see `differencies.md`). Theorem 1 only needs the 4-colouring version. We also only assume `r ≤ r(v)` (not `r = r(v)`), and that the ring has at least `2r + 1` nodes.
