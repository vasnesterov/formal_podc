import Papers.PODC2015.MSTImpromptuRepair.FindAny import Papers.PODC2015.MSTImpromptuRepair.PairwiseExtend /-! # Lemma 5 fails with the paper's hash range `r > ∑ deg` `FindAny` step 3a draws a pairwise independent `h : [1, maxEdgeNum(T)] → [r]` with `r` "a power of 2 `>` the sum of degrees of nodes in `T`", and step 3b uses the indices `i = 1, …, lg r`. Lemma 4, however, needs `|W| < 2^(ℓ-1) = r/2` (`W` = the leaving edges), and its index `j` may be `0`. Instance: the star `K_{1,3}` (center `0`, leaves `1, 2, 3`, IDs `1, …, 4`), `T = {0}`: the sum of degrees is `3`, so `r = 4` (`ℓ = 2`, indices `{1, 2}`) is allowed. The three edges are all leaving, with the paper's edge numbers `10, 11, 12` (ID concatenation, smallest first, 3-bit fields). We give a pairwise independent family on `[0, 12] ⊇ [1, maxEdgeNum(T)] = [1, 12]` (`hfam`, `hfam_pairwiseIndep`) for which, for **every** seed, the number of leaving edges hashing into `[2] = {0, 1}` is `0` or `2` (the high bits of the three hash values are `s₁, s₂, ¬(s₁ ⊕ s₂)`), so `min = 2`, `w(T) = 10 ⊕ 11 ⊕ 12 = 13`, which is the number of no edge: `Sum = 0` and the round fails (`step_eq_none`). Hence `FindAny-C` returns `∅` with probability 1 and `FindAny` never finds the leaving edges, whatever `HP-TestOut` does (`findAnyC_eq_none`, `findAny_eq_none`, `lemma5_findAnyC_false`). -/ namespace
