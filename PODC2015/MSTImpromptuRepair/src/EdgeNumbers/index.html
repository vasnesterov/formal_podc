import Papers.PODC2015.MSTImpromptuRepair.HPTestOut /-! # Edge numbers and `HP-TestOut` The paper defines the *edge number* of `{u, v}` as the concatenation of the endpoint IDs, smallest first (`edgeNumber`), and uses it in `𝒫(D)(z) = ∏_{e ∈ D} (z - edge_number(e))` for sets `D` of *ordered* pairs. Taken literally the number of `(u, v)` and of `(v, u)` coincide and `HP-TestOut` never detects anything (`hpTestOut_edgeNumber_never`). With the *ordered* concatenation (`dartNumber`) the numbers of distinct darts are distinct (`dartNumber_injective`), and all hypotheses of `hpTestOut_error_lt` hold (`hpTestOut_dartNumber_error_lt`).
