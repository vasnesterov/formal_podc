import Mathlib /-! # Lemma 12: disjoint tiles (Censor-Hillel et al., PODC 2015) Lemma 12 of [Censor-Hillel, Kaski, Korhonen, Lenzen, Paz, Suomela, *Algebraic Methods in the Congested Clique*, PODC 2015] (combinatorial part): if `∑_y deg(y)² < 2n²` then one can choose for every node `y` sets `A(y), B(y) ⊆ V` with `|A(y)| = |B(y)| ≥ deg(y)/8` such that the tiles `A(y) × B(y)` are pairwise disjoint. ## Construction * `tileSize d` is the side `f(y)` of the tile of a node of degree `d`: `d/4` rounded down to a power of two (as in the paper), but `1` for `1 ≤ d ≤ 3` (the paper's rule gives no positive integer there) and `0` for `d = 0`. We have `d ≤ 8 * tileSize d`. * Area: `16 f² ≤ d² + 15` for every `d`, so `∑ f² ≤ k²` where `k = 2 ^ Nat.log 2 n` is `n` rounded down to a power of two (for `n ≥ 3` from `∑ d² < 2n²` and `2k ≥ n + 1`; for `n ≤ 2` directly). * Packing (instead of the paper's iterative quadtree procedure we use the equivalent Z-order / Morton order): sort the nodes by decreasing tile size (ties by identifier); the offset of `y` is the total area of the tiles before it. As all sizes are powers of two and the earlier ones are at least as large, `f(y)²` divides the offset, so the Morton-order interval `[off(y), off(y) + f(y)²)` is exactly an aligned `f(y) × f(y)` square.
