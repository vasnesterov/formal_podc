import Papers.PODC2015.LoopFreeUpdates.PeacockSelect /-! # Peacock (§4): one double round and node merging Let `C` be a good selection (the nodes updated in an odd round). A node is *inside* `C` if it lies strictly inside the interval `(pos₁ c, pos₁ (out₂ c))` of some `c ∈ C`. After the odd round the walk from `s` visits exactly the nodes that are not inside `C` (`reach_iff_not_inside`), so the even round updates the nodes inside `C` that are not themselves in `C` (`prune C`, Lemma 3). *Node merging.* An updated node is merged into the target of its new rule. After the two rounds the merged network is again a line (Lemma 3): it is the instance `reduce` on the *kept* nodes (neither in `C` nor inside `C`), whose old and new routes are the restrictions of the old and new routes. The key facts are * `rlf_of_reduce`: a relaxed loop-free state `Y` of the merged instance gives the relaxed loop-free state `C ∪ prune C ∪ Y` of the original one (this justifies the merging); * `reduce_length` (Lemma 2): `3 N' + 1 ≤ 2 N` where `N`, `N'` are the numbers of nodes before and after the double round. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.LoopFreeUpdates open
