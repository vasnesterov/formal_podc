import Papers.PODC2015.SafetyLivenessExclusion.Counterexample /-! # The counterexample to Theorem 4.9 with `Lmax` = wait-freedom `Counterexample.lean` refutes Theorem 4.9 with an ad-hoc `Lmax`. Here `Lmax` is the paper's own example of a strongest liveness requirement, **wait-freedom** (`WaitFree`: every invocation of a process that does not crash eventually returns). With the same safety property `S` and the same implementation `Is`: * every fair history of `Is` is wait-free, except the finite histories `Y` in which `p₁` crashed (possibly after invoking), `p₀` invoked once and waits forever, and nobody received a response (`Is_fairH_subset`, a liveness analysis of `Is` under the paper's fairness); * every history of `Y` is fair for *every* valid implementation ensuring `S` (`Y_subset_fairH`): after it, the state is the same as after `crash₁ · inv₀` (the inputs of `p₁` only touch `p₁`'s local state, which the crash erases), and there no answer to `p₀` is allowed by `S`. Hence `WaitFree ∪ Y` is the strongest liveness property that does not exclude `S`, and it is not `WaitFree` (`theorem_4_9_false_waitFree`). -/ namespace Podc.SafetyLivenessExclusion.Counterexample
