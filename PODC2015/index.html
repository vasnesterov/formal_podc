Alistarh, Gelashvili, Vojnović — Fast and Exact Majority in Population Protocols

⚠️ Weakened. AVC is exact after a one-symbol fix; the four-state lower bound is proved only for unordered interactions; the convergence-time bounds, the main claim, were not checked.

78

Ludwig, Marcinkowski, Schmid — Scheduling Loop-free Network Updates: It's Good to Relax!

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 2's reduction is false, so NP-completeness for 3 rounds is unproved and we neither prove nor refute it; the Ω(n) lower bound and Peacock's O(log n) relaxed schedules hold.

78

Attiya, Ellen, Morrison — Limitations of Highly-Available Eventually-Consistent Data Stores

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 12's unbounded-message bound holds with a new decoding argument; the OCC limit, Theorem 6, is unestablished because two proof steps fail, though it is not refuted.

70

King, Kutten, Thorup — Construction and Impromptu Repair of an MST in a Distributed Network with o(m) Communication

🔧 Easy fix. HP-TestOut, FindMin and FindAny are wrong as printed, each saved by a local fix; a Markov step stays unrepaired, affecting only a constant. Message bounds and Build MST were not checked.

63

Censor-Hillel, Kaski, Korhonen, Lenzen, Paz, Suomela — Algebraic Methods in the Congested Clique

🔧 Easy fix. 4-cycle detection and semiring multiplication hold after a short repair of Lemma 12's proof and two slips; fast ring multiplication and its applications, the main point, were not checked.

50

Tseng, Vaidya — Fault-Tolerant Consensus in Directed Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. Tight conditions hold, synchronous and asynchronous. Algorithm WA can deadlock as printed, fixed by keeping early messages. Side result Theorem 5 holds only for eventual outputs.

25

Censor-Hillel, Petrank, Timnat — Help!

⚠️ Weakened. The stack is not an exact order type; the global view theorem misses increment; the read/write max register proof has a gap. Queue and snapshot impossibility and the definition of help stand.

23

Baruch, Fraigniaud, Patt-Shamir — Randomized Proof-Labeling Schemes

🔧 Easy fix. Compiler and universal bound hold. The crossing lower bounds fail for an illegal G but hold for a legal G, which every application has. MST bounds and upper bounds of Theorems 5.2-5.3 are unchecked.

20

Peluso, Palmieri, Romano, Ravindran, Quaglia — Disjoint-Access Parallelism: Impossibility, Possibility, and Cost of Transactional Memory Implementations

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 1 is false, so the proofs of Theorems 1 and 2 fail; both theorems are re-proved by a new argument. The WRTO algorithm and the lower bounds were not checked.

18

Berenbrink, Klasing, Kosowski, Mallmann-Trenn, Uznański — Improved Analysis of Deterministic Load-Balancing Schemes

🔧 Easy fix. Observation 3.2's constant is about twice too large; the lower bounds of Theorems 4.1-4.3 hold after local proof repairs. The upper bounds (Theorems 2.3, 3.3, Table 1) were not checked.

17

Bouzid, Mostéfaoui, Raynal — Minimal Synchrony for Byzantine Consensus

❌ Intent lost. Figure 3 can block forever, so EA-Termination and consensus termination are false, and the proof of Lemma 3 fails; whether a repair works is open. Safety, CB-broadcast and adopt-commit hold.

17

Bonomi, Dolev, Potop-Butucaru, Raynal — Stabilizing Server-Based Storage in Byzantine Asynchronous Message-Passing Systems

❌ Intent lost. The practically stabilizing atomic register (Fig. 3, Theorem 3), on which the SWMR/MWMR registers rest, has new/old inversions forever in a fair execution with n = 9, t = 1; the stabilizing regular register (Theorem 1) holds, even for n > 6t, after repairing the proofs of Lemmas 2–3.

15

Di Luna, Baldoni — Investigating the Cost of Anonymity on Dynamic Networks

🔧 Easy fix. The Ω(log n) counting lower bound on G(PD)₂ and the D + Ω(log n) bound for constant dynamic diameter hold for all algorithms. Lemmas 4 and 5 are each printed off by one, and the proofs of Lemma 2 and Corollary 1 have local gaps, which we closed.

14

Ostrovsky, Rosenbaum — Fast Distributed Almost Stable Matchings

🛠️ Hard fix. Deterministic ASM and its O(log^5 n) round count hold; the proof of Lemma 4.4 misses men who turn bad while inactive and needs a new charging argument; randomized variants unchecked.

13

Lenzen, Rybicki, Suomela — Towards Optimal Synchronous Counting

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 1 and main Theorem 3 hold, with constants of Section 4.3 corrected. Theorem 2 only with a weaker time constant, nothing depends on it. Section 5 was not checked.

10

Aghazadeh, Woelfel — On the Time and Space Complexity of ABA Prevention and Detection

🔧 Easy fix. The lower bounds hold for linearizable implementations, the register bound checked for deterministic ones only. The weak correctness condition is misworded and Lemma 2 misses a case; both are local fixes. Upper bounds unchecked.

9

Bushkov, Guerraoui — Safety-Liveness Exclusion in Distributed Computing

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 4.9 is false with idle steps but proved under the paper's reactive convention after a local proof repair; two false remarks corrected. Section 5's TM inputs unchecked.

8

Delporte-Gallet, Fauconnier, Kuznetsov, Ruppert — On the Space Complexity of Set Agreement

⚠️ Weakened. Figures 3 and 4 livelock. Figure 3 has a proved one-line repair; the repeated upper bound (Theorem 8) stays unproved. The lower bound n + m - k holds; anonymous results unchecked.

5

Dubois, Guerraoui, Kuznetsov, Petit, Sens — The Weakest Failure Detector for Eventual Consistency

🔧 Easy fix. Sufficiency of Ω and EC ⇔ ETOB hold; Algorithm 5 needs numbered promotions and EIC-Agreement must be among correct processes. The necessity of Ω (Lemma 1) was not formalized.

5

Golab, Li, López-Ortiz, Nishimura — Computing Weak Consistency in Polynomial Time

✅ Correct. No mistakes: correctness of GPO and CGS, normalization and the graph characterization hold as written. The running-time bounds were not formalized.

1

Feuilloley — Brief Announcement: Average Complexity for the LOCAL Model

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 1 and the largest-ID results hold; the unproved reduction to minimal algorithms needed a new Zorn and compactness argument, and Lemma 3 had to be restated for 4-colouring.
