import Papers.PODC2015.DeterministicLoadBalancing.Model import Podc.Foundations.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.OddGirth /-! # The ROTOR-ROUTER without self-loops Berenbrink, Klasing, Kosowski, Mallmann-Trenn, Uznański, PODC 2015 (arXiv:1404.4344v4), Section 1.1 and Theorem 4.3 (`d⁺ = d`: no self-loops). Every node `v` has a fixed cyclic order of its `d` edges (`ord v : Fin d ≃ N(v)`) and a rotor `ρ : Fin d` pointing to one of them. A node holding `x` tokens sends them one by one, starting at the edge the rotor points to and advancing the rotor after each token. Hence the edge at position `i` receives `⌊x/d⌋ + [ (i − ρ) mod d < x mod d ]` tokens, and the rotor advances by `x mod d`. This file defines the balancer (`rotorRouter`) and proves an abstract periodicity lemma (`rotorRouter_periodic`): every symmetric "two-phase" flow `g` (with `g v w + g w v = 2L` along every edge and the values `g v ·` within `1` of each other) is realised by the rotor-router for suitable edge orders and initial rotors, alternating between the loads `Σ_w g v w` and `Σ_w g w v`. -/ open Finset
