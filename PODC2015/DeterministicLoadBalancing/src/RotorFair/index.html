The key identity is `rotorShare_add`: sending `a` tokens and then `b` tokens (from the advanced rotor) puts on every edge exactly as many tokens as sending `a + b` tokens at once. Hence the cumulative flow over the edge at position `i` after steps `0, …, t` is `rotorShare (ρ₀ v) i (S v)`, where `S v` is the total number of tokens `v` has sent (`cumFlow_rotorRouter`), and any
