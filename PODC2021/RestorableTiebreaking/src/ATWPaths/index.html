Since `r` may be negative, "shortest path" could mean a minimum-weight *simple path* (`IsATWPath`) or a minimum-weight *walk* (`IsATW`, used in `theorem19`). The two readings agree (`isATW_iff_isATWPath`): the simple-path reading already forces every closed walk of `G*` to have positive weight (each closed walk `x → a ⇝ x` weighs at least `w(x,a) + w(a,x) = 2`, because the edge `a → x` is the unique lightest `a ⇝ x` path), so lightest walks are simple paths.
