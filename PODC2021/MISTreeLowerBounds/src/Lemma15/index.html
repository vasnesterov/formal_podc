Along an edge of colour `c` the algorithm fails if one endpoint outputs a non-configuration or the two labels on the edge are incompatible; the probability of this event is `failProb (Pi Δ a x) p c`. * `lemma_15`: for `Δ ≥ 2`, `x < Δ` and `a ≥ 1`, some edge fails with probability at least `1/Δ^8` (in fact `1/(36Δ²)`). * `lemma_15_false_delta_one`: the statement of the paper ("for all `x ≤ Δ - 1` and `a ≥ 1`") is false for `Δ = 1`: on `K₂` (the 1-regular tree) the distribution "`M` or `A`, each with probability `1/2`" fails with probability `1/2 < 1 = 1/Δ^8`.
