The direction bits of the `Δ` incident edges form a proper vertex colouring with `2^Δ` colours (the endpoints of the edge of colour `c` disagree on bit `c`); the greedy MIS by increasing colour rank (`inS`: a node joins iff no lower-ranked neighbour joins) only depends on decreasing chains of ranks, hence on the radius-`2^Δ` view. -/ namespace Podc.MISTreeLowerBounds open Cslib.ColourSystem
