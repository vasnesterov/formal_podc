Lower bounds for MIS and bounded-outdegree dominating sets in trees — PODC 2021

Alkida Balliu, Sebastian Brandt, Fabian Kuhn, Dennis Olivetti. Improved Distributed Lower Bounds for MIS and Bounded (Out-)Degree Dominating Sets in Trees. PODC 2021. DOI 10.1145/3465084.3467901. Formalized version: arXiv 2106.02440 v1; numbering follows it.

Verdict: ✏️ Slip. The whole deterministic port-numbering part (§3) is correct: the round-elimination step Π_Δ(a, x) → Π_Δ(⌊(a−2x−1)/2⌋, x+1) (Lemmas 6, 8, 9, Corollary 10), Lemmas 5, 11, 12 and the sequence of Lemma 13 (with an explicit condition in place of "Δ large enough") are all formalized and, with a Lean proof of the forward direction of round elimination (Theorem 3), give the Ω(log Δ) port-numbering lower bound for k-outdegree dominating sets, k ≤ Δ^ε, with ε = 1/6. Only Lemma 15 for Δ = 1 and one symbol in the definition of R̄ are wrong. The lifting to LOCAL (imported Theorem 14, hence Theorem 1 and Corollary 2) was not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

✏️ Slips

  • Lemma 15 for Δ = 1 (randomized 0-round failure probability): the lemma claims that for all x ≤ Δ − 1 and a ≥ 1, every randomized 0-round algorithm for Π_Δ(a, x) fails with probability at least 1/Δ⁸. This bound is 1 for Δ = 1, but on K₂ the algorithm that outputs M or A with probability 1/2 each fails only with probability 1/2 (lemma_15_false_delta_one): both M and A are node configurations of Π_1(1, 0), and the algorithm fails only on MM or AA. The proof gives a failure probability of at least 1/(3Δ)² and then uses 1/(3Δ)² ≥ 1/Δ⁸, which needs Δ ≥ 2. The paper only uses large Δ, so nothing downstream is affected. We prove the lemma for Δ ≥ 2 with the bound 1/(36Δ²), which also covers algorithms whose outputs are not always valid configurations (lemma_15).

  • §2.3, definition of R̄: Σ_{Π''} is "the sets that appear in the configurations of E_{Π''}", but E_{Π''} is defined from Σ_{Π''}. It should say N_{Π''} (Problem.alphRbar).

Typos

  • §2.3, definition of R̄: "Σ_{Π''} =⊆ …".

  • Lemma 8 says Π⁺_Δ(a, x) "has time complexity max{T − 1, 0}", but its proof gives only "at most", as Corollary 10 says; we prove this "at most max{T−1, 0}" form (lemma_8).

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Deterministic port-numbering model on the infinite Δ-regular tree. The tree is the Cayley graph of CSLib's free Coxeter group FreeCox Δ, so its edges come with a proper Δ-edge colouring. Port numbers equal colours at both endpoints, as in the graph family of Lemmas 12 and 15. The colours are known, so arbitrary port numbers would carry no extra information.

  • Input: an edge orientation, i.e. the paper's edge port numbering {1, 2}. Correctness is required for every orientation.

  • Algorithms are functions of the radius-t view (IsLocal). The view contains the orientation of every edge whose two endpoints are within distance t. So a 0-round algorithm sees no orientation, which is the paper's convention in Lemma 12 ("they do not even see the port numbering of the edges").

  • Problems and operators. Node-edge-checkable problems (Problem): node configurations as colour-indexed tuples, plus a symmetric edge relation. R and R̄ are defined exactly as in §2.3, including maximality and the derived alphabets.

  • 0-round reductions ("Π' is solvable in 0 rounds given a solution of Π, given the colouring", ZeroRoundReduction) are maps between colour-indexed configurations.

  • k-outdegree dominating set (SolvesODS): each node outputs membership in S and one direction bit per edge. S must be dominating, every edge inside S must be claimed by exactly one endpoint, and every node of S has outdegree ≤ k inside S.

  • Theorem 3, forward direction. The paper imports Brandt's round elimination theorem ([13], Thm 4.3). We only need "T rounds for Π ⇒ T−1 rounds for R̄(R(Π))", and we prove it in this model (RoundElim.lean). Being on the full infinite tree with free inputs, the proof needs no independence hypothesis. The orientation is used exactly once: both endpoints of an edge must extend their label pair to the same maximal edge configuration.

  • Randomized model (Lemma 15). A randomized 0-round algorithm is one output distribution, sampled independently at every node.

  • Strength of the result. This setting (tree, colouring, orientation) is the strongest one in which the paper proves its port-numbering bound. The paper also allows arbitrary port numbers; as argued above, this changes nothing.

Proof remarks. Lemmas 5 and 11 need k ≤ Δ and x ≤ Δ, since without them the configuration M^{Δ−x}X^x does not exist. This is the paper's convention 0 ≤ a, x ≤ Δ for the family. In the proof of Lemma 8, the step "each Yᵢ contains B" does not need the earlier fact Yᵢ ⊆ OUABPQ: a right-closed set without B already lies in PQ.
