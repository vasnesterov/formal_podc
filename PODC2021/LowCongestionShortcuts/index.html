Low-Congestion Shortcuts for Graphs Excluding Dense Minors — PODC 2021

Mohsen Ghaffari, Bernhard Haeupler. Low-Congestion Shortcuts for Graphs Excluding Dense Minors. PODC 2021, pp. 213–221. DOI 10.1145/3465084.3467935. Formalized version: full version arXiv:2008.03091v1 (numbering follows it). Copy of the paper: paper.pdf.

Verdict: ⚠️ Weakened. The main upper bound is proved: Theorem 3.1, and hence the existential Theorem 1.2, with 8δ + 1 blocks and after local repairs of Observations 2.6 and 2.7 and of case (II). The matching lower bound behind the paper's tightness claim is not saved: the construction in the proof of Lemma 3.2 does not have the claimed diameter, and no repair is proved. The distributed algorithms were not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

❌ Lemma 3.2 (lower bound: diameter of the construction)

The graph constructed in the proof of Lemma 3.2 violates the diameter bound: for δ' = 5, D' = 12 (so δ = 3, k = 2, D = 6, 174 vertices), the vertices v_{3,3} and v_{10,11} are at distance exactly 15 (LowerBound.paperGraph_dist_eq, LowerBound.paperGraph_not_diam_le).

Impact. The proof fails, so Lemma 3.2, and hence Lemma 1.3, is unestablished; the statement is plausibly true with different constants. The upper bounds are unaffected.

Details

The proof shows that every vertex is within 1.5D + 1 of the middle of the top path. That bounds the radius, but the proof concludes "diameter ≤ 1.5D + 1 ≤ D'"; the diameter can be nearly twice as large. Numerical experiments (not formal) suggest that k = ⌊(D' − 2)/(3δ)⌋ repairs the construction.

🔧 Theorem 3.1 (block count 8δ)

The proof of Theorem 3.1 only bounds the block count by 8δ + 1, and the literal 8δ is false on the one-vertex graph, where δ(G) = 0 but the part {v} always has one block (theorem_3_1_literal_false_singleton).

Impact. An off-by-one: we prove the theorem with 8δ + 1 blocks (theorem_3_1), so Theorem 1.2 gets dilation (8δ + 1)(2D + 1) instead of 8δ(2D + 1).

Details

In case (I), the blocks of P_i are counted by their topmost vertices. The proof counts only overcongested edges (e, P_i) ∈ B, but the topmost vertex of a block can also be the root, which has no parent edge and is never overcongested. On the one-vertex graph this makes 8δ = 0 blocks impossible.

🔧 Observation 2.6 (blocks far from the part)

Observation 2.6 is false as stated: on the path 0 – 1 – 2 rooted at 0, with part {2} and H = {01}, there are two blocks, but 2 is disconnected from 0, so the dilation is infinite (observation_2_6_counterexample).

Impact. It fails only for shortcuts with useless tree edges far from the part, and it is fixed by the evident condition that every block meets its part, which the shortcuts built in Theorem 3.1 satisfy (observation_2_6, blocksMeetPart_shortcut).

Details

Definition 2.3 lets a tree-restricted H_i contain tree edges far from P_i. A block of such an H_i need not meet P_i, so G[P_i] + H_i can be disconnected.

Repair

Require every block to contain a vertex of its part. We add this to Definitions 2.4/2.5. The shortcuts built in Theorem 3.1 ("all ancestor edges of P_i") satisfy it. (observation_2_6, blocksMeetPart_shortcut)

🔧 Observation 2.7 (number of halving rounds)

The proof's log₂ k halving rounds are not enough to cover k parts, which need ⌊log₂ k⌋ + 1 rounds (for k = 1 part one round is needed but log₂ 1 = 0), so the stated congestion c log₂ n is not established.

Impact. Harmless asymptotically: we prove congestion c(⌊log₂ n⌋ + 1) (observation_2_7).

✏️ Slips

  • Theorem 3.1, case (II): "at least half of the parts have degree at least 8δ" yields only ≥ where the proof needs <. Take the exact negation of case (I): more than half of the parts have degree strictly greater than 8δ (caseII_false).

  • Theorem 3.1, case (II): the claim that each edge-node is included with probability 1 − 1/(4D) is wrong, since an edge-node whose v_e lies in no part is always present. This is harmless for the arithmetic, which only uses the bound |O| ≤ k/c on the number of edge-nodes, but it means the strict inequality must come from the part-nodes.

Typos

  • Figure 3.2 uses δ' = 3, outside the lemma's range δ' ≥ 5.

Proof gaps in true statements (2)Theorem 3.1, case · Theorem 3.1, case

🔧 Theorem 3.1, case (II) (edges of B never present in B_{P'})

When the lower endpoint v_e of an overcongested edge lies in a part P_i, the pair (e, P_i) is never present in B_{P'}, so the bound E|E_{P'}| ≥ 3k/(16D) is unjustified.

Impact. Repaired with a sharper presence probability (exists_blocker, caseII_false, final_arith).

Details

The representative is then v_e, so the path "from v_e to r excluding r" is empty. Moreover, the edge-node e exists only if P_i ∉ P', so the pair (e, P_i) is never present.

Repair

Each edge-node loses at most one such pair, since parts are disjoint. We compensate with a sharper presence probability (1 − p)^{D−1} ≥ (3D+1)/(4D) instead of 3/4, together with δ ≥ 1/2 for any graph with an edge. The arithmetic then closes. (exists_blocker, caseII_false, final_arith)

🔧 Theorem 3.1, case (II) (representatives must be topmost)

"Some representative r ∈ P_i reachable from v_e via T \ O" does not give the claimed independence from the event P_i ∈ P', which needs the path from v_e to r to avoid P_i.

Impact. Repaired by choosing r as the first vertex of P_i on the way down (exists_rep).

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model. Purely graph-theoretic. No distributed model is formalized, because the CONGEST algorithms are cited from other work.

  • G is a finite simple graph. "Diameter D" means connected with all distances ≤ D. Rooted spanning trees are given by parent pointers with depths.

  • Parts are pairwise disjoint vertex sets, each inducing a connected subgraph. A shortcut is a family of edge sets of G.

    • Congestion bounds per-edge multiplicity.

    • Dilation bounds the diameter of G[P_i] + H_i.

    • Blocks are the components of (P_i ∪ V(H_i), H_i).

  • Minors are branch-set models. MinorDensityLE δ means every minor has |E| ≤ δ|V|. minorDensity is the (attained) supremum. Theorem 3.1 holds for every δ ≥ δ(G).

  • Overcongested edges: instead of running the greedy, we take a set O with the greedy's fixed-point property (exists_stable).

  • The connectivity and disjointness of the branch sets of B_{P'} are only asserted in the paper; we prove them.

  • The probabilistic method (independent Bernoulli(1/(4D)) parts) is an explicit weighted sum over subsets, without measure theory: bernWeight and its lemmas come from the shared finite probability library Cslib/Probability/Finite/Bernoulli.lean.
