import Papers.PODC2021.SoreLoserHedging.Premiums /-! # The hedged multi-party swap (§7) as a timed execution model **Time** is discrete, in units of `Δ`; the protocol starts at time `0`. Conforming parties act in lock-step: a reaction to an event that is on chain at time `t` takes effect at time `t + 1`. Deadlines are inclusive. **Timeouts.** The paper only says that "each step takes time at most `Δ`" and that "each following step's timeout increases by `Δ`", and refers to [Herlihy 2018] for the base protocol. We fix a consistent family (`Swap`): escrow premiums on arcs leaving `v` must be deposited by `dl1 v`; redemption premiums with path `q` by `t1 + |q|` (here `|q|` is the number of vertices of `q`); activation is checked at `t2`; assets on arcs leaving `v` must be escrowed by `dl3 v`; a hashkey with path `q`, and a redemption premium with path `q`, time out at `B + |q|`. The constraints (`dl1_*`, `t2_ge`, `dl3_*`) give every conforming party one `Δ` of slack per step, as in the paper. **Executions** are records (`Exec`) of the on-chain events: escrow premiums `ep u v`, redemption premiums `rp i u v` (for the hashkey of leader `i`, deposited by `v` on `(u, v)`, with their path), escrowed assets `es u v`, and the first accepted hashkey `ky i u v` for leader `i` on `(u, v)` (presented by `v`, with its path). `fp i v` / `fk i v` are the first redemption premium / hashkey for `i` that `v` observes on a leaving arc (ties broken by the scheduler). `Admissible S C X` says that `X` is an execution of the hedged protocol in which the parties outside the coalition `C` conform: * **contract rules** (everybody): deadlines, well-formed paths (a redemption premium path is a simple path from the depositor to the leader; a hashkey path is a path in the sense of [Herlihy 2018]: all vertices but the last distinct); * **unforgeability**: a path containing a conforming party `x` (not at the head) has, from `x` on, exactly the path that `x` itself signed, no later than the event (premiums and keys are public as soon as they are submitted; front-running is allowed); * **conforming behaviour**, phase by phase as in §7.1, including the truncated phases that the proofs of Lemmas 3–5 describe: a follower deposits escrow premiums only after all entering arcs carry one; a leader deposits its redemption premiums only if all entering escrow premiums arrived; parties escrow assets only on *activated* arcs; a leader that escrowed nothing releases its secret at once (Lemma 4's proof); otherwise it releases it iff all entering arcs carry assets (Lemma 3's proof). The coalition is arbitrary otherwise: it may deposit, escrow, or present keys whenever the contracts accept them. **Payoffs.** An activated escrow premium `E(u, v)` goes to `v` if the asset on `(u, v)` is not escrowed in time (else it is refunded). A redemption premium `Rᵢ(q, u)` on `(u, v)` goes to `u` unless `v` presents a hashkey for `i` on `(u, v)` by `B + |q|`. `net v` is the net premium profit of `v`. -/ namespace Podc.SoreLoserHedging
