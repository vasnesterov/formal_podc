import Mathlib /-! # Premium formulas of the hedged multi-party swap (§7.1, Equations (1) and (2)) A swap digraph `G` on a finite vertex type; an arc `G.Adj u v` is a proposed transfer of an asset from `u` to `v`. A *path* `(u₀, …, u_k)` follows the arcs, `(uᵢ, uᵢ₊₁)` (Figure 3b; the text of §7 writes `(uᵢ₊₁, uᵢ)`, which contradicts Figure 3b and every later use). * `R q v` — Equation (1): the redemption premium that the head `w` of the path `q` deposits on the arc `(v, w)`; `q` is the path along which the premium distribution reached `w` (from `w` to the leader). If `v ∥ q` is a path (`v ∉ q`), `R q v = p + ∑_{(u,v) ∈ G} R (v ∥ q) u`; otherwise (`v ∥ q` a cycle, or `v` occurs inside `q`) `R q v = p`. The paper gives the recursion for every `v ∥ q` that is not a cycle; for `v` inside `q` it does not terminate, and conforming parties never extend such a `q` anyway, so we take `p` there. * `Rlead L = ∑_{(u,L) ∈ G} R [L] u` — the leader's total redemption premium `R(Lᵢ)`. * `Ein v` — Equation (2): the escrow premium `E(u, v)` on every arc entering `v` (it does not depend on `u`): `R(v)` if `v` is a leader, `∑_{(v,w) ∈ G} E(v, w)` otherwise.
