import Papers.PODC2021.AllYouNeedIsDAG.Ordering /-! # DAG-Rider: the DAG construction layer (Algorithm 2) as an asynchronous execution Keidar, Kokoris-Kogias, Naor, Spiegelman, *All You Need is DAG*, PODC 2021, Sections 2 and 4. **Model.** `n = 3f + 1` processes `P`, a set `byz` of at most `f` Byzantine processes (static: the adversary's choice is part of the run). Reliable broadcast is abstract: a run gives for every process `p` and every step `t` the finite set `dl p t` of vertex identifiers `(r, k)` that `p` `r_deliver`s just before its `t`-th step. Non-equivocation (the consequence of RBC Agreement + Integrity) is built in: the contents of all delivered vertices are given by one global `Content` `R.C`. The remaining RBC guarantees are hypotheses of `Run.Valid`: * *Validity*: a vertex broadcast by a correct process is eventually delivered to every correct one; * *Agreement*: a vertex delivered to a correct process is eventually delivered to every correct one; * *Authenticity*: a correct process delivers `(r, q)` with `q` correct only if `q` broadcast it, and then the delivered contents are the ones `q` computed (`createdOK`). Correct processes run Algorithm 2: every step is one iteration of the `while True` loop (buffer the delivered vertices that pass the check of line 25, add buffered vertices of round `≤ r` whose predecessors are present (lines 6–9), and advance the round if `DAG[r]` has `2f + 1` vertices (line 10)). When advancing to round `r + 1` the process broadcasts its vertex `(r + 1, p)`: block `blk p (r + 1)` (the `(r+1)`-th `a_bcast` block), strong edges to all of `DAG[r]` and weak edges such that it has a path to every vertex of `DAG` of round `≤ r - 1` (`set_weak_edges` produces such a set; we allow any). `DAG[0]` is the hard-coded set of `2f + 1` genesis vertices `G0` (only their sources matter);
