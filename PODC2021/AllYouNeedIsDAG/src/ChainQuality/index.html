That is, for every prefix of ordered messages of size `(2f + 1) r`, at least `(f + 1) r` were broadcast by correct processes." No proof is given, and `order_vertices` delivers each causal history "in some deterministic order". * `chain_quality`: the claim holds whenever the deterministic order is **round-major** (lists vertices by non-decreasing round; the order inside a round is arbitrary, so the adversary may even have all Byzantine vertices of a round delivered first). Key fact (`fullBelow_orun`): in every prefix of the output, every round below the highest round of the prefix already has `2f + 1` vertices; there are at most `f` Byzantine vertices per round
