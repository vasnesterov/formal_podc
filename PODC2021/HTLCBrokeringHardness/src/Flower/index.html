In the flower graphs of the paper the broker starts with nothing (`flowerOwn`). * `Knapsack`: a knapsack instance `K = (X, V, W)` with items `(vᵢ, wᵢ)`; `Knapsack.Satisfiable`. * `flower K m c`: the flower graph `F` of Figure 2, steps (1)–(3.c), with `m` levels and `c` bitcoins on each contract `(yᵢ, b)`, `2 ≤ i ≤ m` (the paper takes `c = min_w(X) − 1` and `m = ⌈Σ_w(X)/min_w(X) − 1⌉ + 1`, see `paperFlower`). Level `k : Fin (m+1)` of the construction is the pair `y_{k+1}, z_{k+1}` of the paper; the paper's coin `C_i` is `Asset.C i`. -/ namespace Podc.HTLCBrokeringHardness open Finset namespace TxGraph
