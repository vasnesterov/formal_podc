import Papers.PODC2021.WakeUpMatching.Radio import Mathlib.Algebra.BigOperators.Ring.Finset import Mathlib.Basic.Real.Basic import Mathlib.Tactic.Positivity import Mathlib.Tactic.Linarith /-! # Lemma 5.2: the probability that a given edge joins the matching in one round Dani, Gupta, Hayes, Pettie, PODC 2021 (arXiv:2104.09096v2), Lemma 5.2. Conditioned on the matching `M(t−1)` (here: fixed partner variables `partner`), every node independently recruits with probability `r/2`, accepts with probability `r/2` and sleeps with probability `1 − r` (`roleLaw r`); the probability of an event of the role vector is `prob r E = ∑_{ρ ∈ E} ∏_u roleLaw r (ρ u)`. (The roles drawn by matched nodes are ignored by the protocol.) Lemma 5.2: for unmatched neighbours `v, w` whose residual degrees are at most `Δ` (in the paper `Δ = Δ(t−1)`, the maximum residual degree), `P(v and w get matched to each other in this round) ≥ r²/2 · (1 − r)^(Δ − 1)`. The proof is the paper's: the event contains the two disjoint "isolated handshake" events (`v` recruits, `w` accepts, the common unmatched neighbours sleep, the other unmatched neighbours of `v` do not accept and those of `w` do not recruit, and symmetrically), whose probability is `(r/2)² (1−r)^A (1−r/2)^B` with `2A + B = d(v) + d(w) − 2`. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace
