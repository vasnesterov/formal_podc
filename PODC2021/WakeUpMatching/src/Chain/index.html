import Mathlib.Algebra.BigOperators.Fin import Mathlib.Algebra.Order.BigOperators.Ring.Finset import Mathlib.Basic.Real.Basic /-! # Finite path spaces of time-inhomogeneous processes and the chain rule A random process runs for `T` steps; at step `t` an input `x ∈ X` is drawn from the law `p t`, independently of the other steps, and the state evolves by `s ↦ f t s x`. The path space is `Fin T → X`, with the product weight `∏ i, p i (ω i)`. The main result, `pathProb_forall_le`, is the "chain rule" bound used in the proof of Lemma 5.4 of Dani, Gupta, Hayes, Pettie, *Wake up and join me! An energy-efficient algorithm for maximal matching in radio networks* (PODC 2021): if at every step `t ∈ I`, *whatever the current state `s`*, the step event `Z t s` has probability at most `c t`, then the probability that the event happens at every step of `I` is at most `∏ t ∈ I, c t`. ## Main definitions * `pathExt`, `pathState`: the input at time `t` and the state after `t` steps of a path. * `pathWeight`, `pathProb`, `pathStepProb`: product weight, probability of an event of paths, probability of an event of one step. ## Main results * `pathProb_true`, `pathProb_nonneg`, `pathProb_le_one`, `pathProb_mono`, `pathProb_not`. * `pathProb_exists_le`: the union bound. * `pathProb_forall_le`: the chain-rule product bound. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false
