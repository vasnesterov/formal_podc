import Papers.PODC2021.RandomizedSymmetryTopology.ZeroOne /-! # Consistency projections, Definition 3.4 and Lemma 3.5 Fraigniaud, Gelles, Lotker, PODC 2021, Section 3.3–3.4. All complexes in the paper are chromatic and all maps are name-preserving. Restricted to a single facet `σ = {(i, vᵢ) : i ∈ [n]}`, a chromatic complex on the vertices of `σ` is determined by the sets of *names* of its simplices, and a name-preserving vertex map between two such complexes is unique (`(i, vᵢ) ↦ (i, wᵢ)`); it is simplicial iff every simplex of the source is a simplex of the target. We therefore represent: * the consistency projection `π(σ)` (Eq. (11)) of a facet `σ` with values `v : Fin n → V` by `consistencyComplex v : Set (Finset (Fin n))`, the nonempty sets of names with equal values; * `π̃(ρ)` (Eq. (18)) for a realization `ρ` at time `t` by `consistencyComplex (M.K ω t)`: the relation `i ∼ j` is `Kᵢ(t) = Kⱼ(t)` (Eq. (15)); * the existence of a name-preserving simplicial map `π̃(ρ) → π(τ)` by `consistencyComplex κ ⊆ consistencyComplex τ`. Main results: * `isFacet_consistencyComplex_iff`: the facets of `π̃(ρ)` are the classes of `∼` (used in Lemma 4.3: `dim γ + 1` is the size of a class); * `lemma_3_5`: Definition 3.1 and Definition 3.4 agree on every global state / realization, hence `Pr[P(t) solves O | α] = Pr[S(t) | α]` and the two notions of eventual solvability coincide. -/ open Finset Filter Topology namespace Podc.RandomizedSymmetryTopology variable {n : ℕ} /-- The consistency projection of a facet with values `v` (Eqs.
