Keidar, Kokoris-Kogias, Naor, Spiegelman — All You Need is DAG

🛠️ Hard fix. BAB properties hold; Lemma 1 fails for indirect commits, chain quality needs a round-major order, and Claim 6 needs a new robust-core argument; complexity claims not checked.

137

Abraham, Nayak, Ren, Xiang — Good-case Latency of Byzantine Broadcast: a Complete Categorization

🔧 Easy fix. All lower bounds hold; Theorem 10's executions needed a small repair, and for f = n − 1 the bound is only ⌊n/2⌋Δ, still Θ(n). Upper bounds mostly not checked.

55

Liu, Gupta, Vaidya — Approximate Byzantine Fault-Tolerance in Distributed Optimization

🛠️ Hard fix. Sufficiency holds after a local repair; necessity is false or vacuous as stated and holds on redundant inputs by a new proof, placing resilience in [eps', 2 eps']; gradient-descent results unchecked.

49

Kuznetsov, Tonkikh, Zhang — Revisiting Optimal Resilience of Fast Byzantine Consensus

🔧 Easy fix. The tight bound 3f + 2t − 1 (5f − 1 for t = f) holds in both directions; the safety proof needs one extra check on votes.

48

Xue, Herlihy — Hedging Against Sore Loser Attacks in Cross-Chain Transactions

🔧 Easy fix. Hedging of swaps and the auction lemmas hold after local changes to Lemmas 2–3, Equation (1) and one redemption rule; brokered commerce and general multi-party liveness were not checked.

29

Ghaffari, Haeupler — Low-Congestion Shortcuts for Graphs Excluding Dense Minors

⚠️ Weakened. The O(δD) shortcut theorem is proved after local repairs (8δ+1 blocks, one more halving round); the tightness claim is not saved, as the Lemma 3.2 construction exceeds its diameter bound.

28

Balliu, Brandt, Kuhn, Olivetti — Improved Distributed Lower Bounds for MIS and Bounded (Out-)Degree Dominating Sets in Trees

✏️ Slip. The Ω(log Δ) port-numbering lower bound by round elimination holds; Lemma 15 needs Δ ≥ 2 and one definition has a wrong symbol. The lifting to LOCAL was not checked.

24

Emek, Keren — A Thin Self-Stabilizing Asynchronous Unison Algorithm

⚠️ Weakened. Thin unison (Theorem 1.1) stands after local repairs. The synchronizer corollary is false for arbitrary tasks and holds for static ones such as LE and MIS. The LE and MIS theorems were not checked.

24

Schett, Danezis — Embedding a Deterministic BFT Protocol in a Block DAG

⚠️ Weakened. Equivocation breaks no duplication, so the main embedding theorem fails; it holds for properties that survive Byzantine duplicates. Block DAG, gossip and interpretation lemmas stand; transfer of arbitrary liveness properties unchecked.

23

Fitzi, Liu-Zhang, Loss — A New Way to Achieve Round-Efficient Byzantine Agreement

⚠️ Weakened. The t < n/3 protocol works with threshold t + 1, not n - 2t. The 3κ/2-round t < n/2 protocol fails against the claimed adaptive adversary: one iteration errs with probability at least 1/2.

20

Burman, Chen, Chen, Doty, Nowak, Severson, Xu — Time-Optimal Self-Stabilizing Leader Election in Population Protocols

🔧 Easy fix. Protocol 3 as printed never stabilizes, but with the intended guard it is proved correct; a by-product tail bound fails. State lower bounds hold; the time bounds and Section 5 were not checked.

11

Sela, Herlihy, Petrank — Linearizability: A Typo

✅ Correct. No mistakes. The original definition is neither local nor nonblocking; the amended one is both and equals linearization points; the inclusions of Figure 6 are strict.

10

Gilbert, Newport, Vaidya, Weaver — Contention Resolution with Predictions

🛠️ Hard fix. The entropy and perfect-advice lower bounds hold, but the proofs of Theorems 3.3 and 3.7 are wrong and were replaced by new arguments. Collision-detection entropy bound and randomized upper bounds unchecked.

9

Bodwin, Parter — Restorable Shortest Path Tiebreaking for Edge-Faulty Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. Restorable tiebreaking exists as claimed; Corollary 22's count and Theorem 23's proof need local repairs, and application sizes for f ≥ 1 and all running-time and CONGEST claims were not checked.

8

Hadzilacos, Hu, Toueg — On Register Linearizability and Termination

🛠️ Hard fix. All formalized theorems hold; the proof of Theorem 14 is wrong and needed a new construction, and the proof of Theorem 7 needs a conditional form of Lemma 19.

7

Altisen, Devismes, Durand, Johnen, Petit — On Implementing Stabilizing Leader Election with Weak Assumptions on Network Dynamics

🔧 Easy fix. Hierarchy, all impossibilities and Algorithm LE hold; false Lemma 7 needs one extra condition, and proofs of Lemmas 12, 16 and Theorem 1 need local repairs. The cited self-stabilizing algorithms are unchecked.

7

Czerner, Esparza — Lower Bounds on the State Complexity of Population Protocols

🔧 Easy fix. Leaderless bound η ≤ 2^((2n+2)!) and leader bound η < F(n) hold after local repairs; BB_L must fix the number of leaders. The F_ω level of the leader bound rests on an unchecked citation.

7

Brusse, Ellen — Reductions and Extension-Based Proofs

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 1's proof has two broken steps; repaired and proved only when R₂ needs no communication. The general case stays open; the extension-based game and transfer for local R₂ stand.

5

Chan, Lesani — Brief Announcement: Brokering with Hashed Timelock Contracts is NP-Hard

🔧 Easy fix. The printed Knapsack reduction fails for weight-1 items and through a misplaced parenthesis in m; doubling weights saves it, and its correctness is proved, though polynomial time is not formalized.

4

Ovens — The Space Complexity of Scannable Binary Objects

🔧 Easy fix. Lower bounds and the lock-free upper bound hold; two proof steps need local changes, the abstract drops the body's conditions on k and n, and Observation 3 fails only from configurations never used.

4

Ben-David, Nayak — Brief Announcement: Classifying Trusted Hardware via Unidirectional Communication

❌ Intent lost. The separation of trusted hardware into two power classes is false: reliable broadcast with sequencing implements unidirectionality for all n > 2f. The positive constructions hold after small repairs.

4

Elkin, Matar — Ultra-Sparse Near-Additive Emulators

🔧 Easy fix. The centralized emulator with n^{1+1/kappa} edges holds; a wrong index in Algorithm 1 and local gaps in Lemma 2.10 and Corollary 2.15 are repaired. CONGEST algorithm and spanners not checked.

3

Dani, Gupta, Hayes, Pettie — Brief Announcement: Wake Up and Join Me! An Energy Efficient Algorithm for Maximal Matching in Radio Networks

🛠️ Hard fix. Maximal matching w.h.p. holds; Theorem 6.3 needed a new proof, Theorem 6.5 has load k + 1 and a repaired coverage proof; the energy bound for Delta >= 2 was not checked.

2

Fraigniaud, Gelles, Lotker — The Topology of Randomized Symmetry-Breaking Distributed Computing

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 4.2's "if" direction is false for the printed Eq. (2) but holds once the sender's port is added; Lemma 3.2's zero–one step needs a few lines. The other checked results hold.

1

Czerner, Guttenberg, Helfrich, Esparza — Decision Power of Weak Asynchronous Models of Distributed Computing

🔧 Easy fix. All Section 3 limitations hold for every selection constraint; the proofs lean on [16] for exclusive selection and Lemma 3.5 needs a second constant. The characterizations of Sections 4–6 are unchecked.
