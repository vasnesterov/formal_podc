Time is discrete, `δ = 2e` (so `0.5δ = e`), and all delays are *effective* delays (see `Basic.lean`). Processes are labelled by `lab : P → ℕ`: `0 = g`, `1 = A`, `2 = B` (contains the broadcaster `s`), `3 = C`, `4 = h`. The mirror labelling is `lab' p = 4 - lab p` (swap `g ↔ h`, `A ↔ C`). * `W va lab` — the *world* of Execution 1: everybody correct and starting at `0`, the broadcaster has input `va`; delays: `C → g` is `Δ + e`, `C → A`, `g → C`, `A → C`, `h → A` are `Δ − e`, `A → h` is `Δ + e`, `g ↔ h`, `C → B`, `h → B` are `L` (very late), everything else `δ`. Execution 1 is `W a lab` with `C ∪ {h}` Byzantine (`Run.enlarge`); Execution 4 is the mirror. * `X lab lab' va vb` — the *physical execution* shared by Executions 2 and 3: `B` is Byzantine and replays, up to time `TB = Δ + e`, what it sends in `W va lab` towards `g, A` and what it sends in `W vb lab'` towards `C, h`; `C` starts at time `e`, everybody else at `0`; delays as in Execution 2 of the paper (`g ↔ A`: `δ`, `g ↔ C`: `Δ`, `C → A`: `Δ − δ`, `A → C`: `Δ`, `h → A`: `Δ − e`, `A → h`: `Δ + e`, `C → h`: `e`, `h → C`: `3e`, `B → C`: `3e`, `g ↔ h`: `L`, everything else `δ`). Execution 2 is `X lab lab' a b` with `B ∪ {h}` Byzantine; Execution 3 is the mirror `X lab' lab b a` with `B ∪ {g}` Byzantine.
