The state of a phase records, for every vertex `x`, a tag `stat x`: * `S` — `x ∈ S_i` (not yet considered); `N` — `x ∈ N_i` (buffer); * `U` — `x` was considered and found unpopular (its cluster joined `U_i`); * `T` — `x` was considered and found popular: a supercluster was formed around it (`x ∈ P_{i+1}`); * `J` — the cluster of `x` joined a supercluster (`x ∈ Γ(r)` for a popular `r`, or it was left in `N_i` at the end of the phase); * `out` — `x` is not a center of `P_i`; together with `par x`, the center of the supercluster that `x` joined (or, for `x ∈ N_i`, the one "that was formed when `x` joined `N_i`", line 23), and the emulator edges `E` built so far. **Order of consideration.** The paper removes "a cluster center" from `S_i` in an arbitrary order (line 6). We consider the vertices in the order of an arbitrary list `L` containing all centers, skipping those no longer in `S_i`. Every execution of the while loop is of this form (extend the sequence of considered centers by the remaining ones), and conversely, so quantifying over all lists is quantifying over all executions of Algorithm 1. **Line 18** reads `d_G(r_C, r_{C'}) ≤ 2δ_i`;
