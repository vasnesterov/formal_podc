Rounds are **0-indexed**: `G t` is the paper's `G_{t+1}` and `exec A G γ t` is the paper's configuration `γ_{t+1}`. `IsDG V G`: all edges of `G` are between distinct vertices of `V`. * Journeys: `Reach G p a b q` (for `a ≤ b`) holds iff `p = q` or there is a journey from `p` to `q` all of whose edges are taken in rounds `t ∈ [a, b)` (at strictly increasing times): the library notion `Cslib.DynNet.Journey G a p b q`. The temporal distance `tdist G i p q` is the paper's `d̂_{G_{i▷}}(p, q)` (`Cslib.DynNet.tdist`, `tdist_le_iff`). * Algorithms are deterministic full-information round algorithms (CSLib `Cslib.HeardOf.StepFn`): each process broadcasts its whole state; the receiver also learns who sent what. Each process has its own code (`step p`) and output `lid p : σ → ι`. This is at least as strong as the paper's local broadcast of (part of) the state, so impossibility results transfer. * An algorithm is designed for a fixed number `n` of processes (well-formedness: the code of a process may depend on its identifier, `n`, and class parameters such as `Δ`, but not on `V`). -/ namespace StabilizingLEDynamic open Cslib.HeardOf variable {ι σ : Type*} /-- A dynamic
