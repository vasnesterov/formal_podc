The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model. Purely combinatorial, Herlihy–Wing style, with no implementations.

  • A history is a finite list of events inv p x o / res p x r over several objects. Well-formedness, H|p, H|x, extensions and complete(H) follow the paper.

  • Operations are identified explicitly as (process, object, index). The paper identifies operations of H, H', complete(H') and S only implicitly, through equivalence.

  • Sequential specifications are arbitrary per-object sets of histories. They may be nondeterministic or partial, and are not required to be prefix-closed; hnil is assumed only where needed.

  • Definitions 2.2, 4.1 and 7.1 are stated literally (LinearizableHW, Linearizable, LinearizableLP). In 7.1, linearization points are gaps between events, which is equivalent to distinct real-valued points.

  • The Section 3 counterexamples use concrete instances: 0/1 register values and queue values 1, 2.

Implicit assumptions made explicit.

  • The "only if" direction of locality (Theorem 5.1) needs the empty history to be legal for every object. This follows from the paper's prefix-closed specifications, provided they are non-empty (left implicit in the paper).

  • "Total operation" (Section 3.3, Theorem 5.2) is read relative to the sequential specification: from every legal complete sequential history, some response makes the operation legal.

Proof remarks. We did not formalize the paper's own proof of Theorem 5.1 (the Herlihy–Wing minimal-cycle argument). Theorem 5.1 (locality) is proved by a different route:

  • Give each object's operations the running-maximum linearization points from Claim 1.

  • Points of different objects never coincide.

  • Merge all objects' operations by point. The result respects the real-time order of H.

Side result. CSLib's linearizability notion Cslib.SharedMemory.IsLinearization is exactly the amended Definition 4.1. This is proved as Cslib.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing.cslib_linearizable_iff and linearizable_iff_cslib, and cslib_rejects_H1 shows it rejects Figure 1's history. Other formalizations built on CSLib (e.g. PODC 2024 History-Independent Objects) therefore use the right notion.
