Randomization is thus by private seeds; the seed vector `ω : P → Ω` is drawn from an arbitrary probability measure `μ`. Decisions are `Option Bool`: `some b` decides `b`, `none` decides `⊥`. * **Contexts** (`Context`): initial preferences and a failure pattern (crash round and the set of agents still reached in the crashing round), exactly as `Cslib.SyncCrash.Run` without the inputs. * **Outcome** (`outcome`): consensus on `b` iff every nonfaulty agent's (first) decision is `b`. * **Utilities** (`Utility`): agents *care only about consensus*: `β₀ i > β₁ i > β₂ i` are `i`'s utilities for consensus on its own preference, consensus on the other value, and no consensus. * **Solution concepts.** `SolvesFairConsensus μ σ f` (agreement, termination, integrity, validity almost surely, plus fairness) and `IsEpsNash μ u σ ε f` (`ε`–`f`-Nash equilibrium: in every context with at most `f` faulty agents no agent gains more than `ε` by a unilateral deviation).
