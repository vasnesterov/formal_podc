import Papers.PODC2016.RationalConsensus.Records /-! # The strategy `σ_cons` (Section 3.2.2, Algorithm 1 of Halpern–Vilaça) Agents `Fin n`; at most `f` crashes; the protocol runs `f + 1` rounds. **Randomness.** Agent `i`'s seed (`Seed n f p`) consists, for every `t ∈ {0, …, f}`, of the secret `x_i[t] ∈ {0, …, n - t - 1}` and the slope `a_i[t] ∈ 𝔽_p` of the degree-1 polynomial `q_i^t(X) = x_i[t] + a_i[t] X` over `𝔽_p = ZMod p` (the paper leaves the field unspecified; we take a prime `p > n`). Agent `j`'s share of `q_i^t` is its value at the evaluation point `j + 1` (`evalPt`); the paper evaluates at `j` with identifiers `0, …, n - 1`, which hands agent `0` the secret itself (`q_i^t(0) = x_i[t]`). **Messages** (`Msg`). In every round `m ≤ f + 1` agent `i` sends to every `j` its status report (here: for every agent the earliest round in which `i` knows it crashed, `recTab`); in round 1 also its preference and `j`'s shares; in round `f + 1` also, for every `l ≠ j`, the share of `q_l` that `i` received from `l` in round 1. The flag `selfShare` adds, in round `f + 1`, `i`'s own share `q_i(i + 1)`. `selfShare = true` is line 20 of Algorithm 1 as written (`(y_li)_{l ≠ j}` ranges over `l = i` too; line 14 forgets to define `y_ii`); `selfShare = false` is the prose reading "the shares it received in round 1", under which Theorem 2 fails for `n = f + 2` and `f = 0`. **Decision** (`decideVal`, lines 46–56) at the end of round `f + 1`: compute `NC_m` from the records, the first round `m` that seems clean, `t = n - |NC_m|`, reconstruct `x_l[t]` for `l ∈ NC_m` by interpolating `l`'s share received in round 1 with one forwarded share, and decide the preference of the agent with the `(S + 1)`-st highest identifier in `NC_m`, where `S = Σ_{l ∈ NC_m} x_l[t] mod (n - t)`. Whenever something needed is missing, decide `⊥` (`none`). **Not modelled** (they never fire when nobody deviates, which is all Theorem 2 is about): the random "signatures" `z`, the reporter identities in status reports and the consistency checks 1–3 and 5–7 of the paper; check 4 is only kept as "a second share point exists", and check 8 only as `t ≤ f`.
