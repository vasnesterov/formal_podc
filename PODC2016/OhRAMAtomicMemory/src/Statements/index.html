import Papers.PODC2016.OhRAMAtomicMemory.Model /-! # Statements: atomicity and termination * `Atomic Pr`: every history of a reachable configuration has a linearization (CSLib `IsLinearization` for `register V v₀`); this is the paper's atomicity (A1–A3), extended to executions with pending operations in the standard way. * `Live Pr f`: in every fair execution (CSLib `Exec.Fair`: every enabled reception by a correct process eventually happens = reliable channels) in which at most `f` *servers* crash (any number of readers and writers may crash), every operation of a correct client returns. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.OhRAM open Cslib.SharedMemory Cslib.AsyncMP variable {W R S T V : Type} [DecidableEq W] [DecidableEq R] [DecidableEq S] [Fintype S] [LinearOrder T] [OrderBot T] [DecidableEq V] /-- At most `f` servers are crashed at any time of `e`. -/ def ServerResilient {L M Act : Type} {A : ObjSpec} [DecidableEq M] {Pr : Protocol (Node W R S) A L M Act} (e : Pr.Exec) (f : ℕ) : Prop := ∀ t, (Finset.univ.filter (fun s : S => Node.sv s ∈ (e.cfg t).crashed)).card ≤ f /-- Termination with at most `f` server crashes. -/ def Live {L M Act : Type} {A : ObjSpec} [DecidableEq M] (Pr : Protocol (Node W R S) A L M Act) (f : ℕ) : Prop := ∀ e : Pr.Exec, e.Fair → ServerResilient e f → e.OpsTerminate end Podc.OhRAM
