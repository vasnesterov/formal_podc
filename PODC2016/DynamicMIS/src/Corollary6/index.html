This is a SET-LOCAL algorithm on the new graph (`directAlg`, `run_directAlg`; model `Cslib/Computability/Distributed/Local/LocallyIterative.lean`, the state of a node is its rank, presence and MIS bit). We show (`directRun_eq_of_le`, `directRun_notMem_infl`) that the run reaches the new greedy MIS after at most `|S|` rounds and that only nodes of `S` ever change state, so by Theorem 1 the expected number of rounds and of adjustments are both at most one (`corollary6_rounds`, `expected_adjustments_le_one`).
