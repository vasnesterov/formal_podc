Proof structure (cf. the paper's Lemmas 2–3): 1. (`flip_data`) Let `H` be the graph without the changed edge and `A_c` the union of the old and new node sets. If the invariant breaks at `v*`, then the rule of `H[A_c]` holds at `v*` for the old states, i.e. `v* ∈ greedyMIS(H, A_c, π)`; the old states (and the new greedy MIS) satisfy the greedy rule of `H[A_c]` at every other node. 2. (`infl_eq`) Hence `S` is the three-valued influenced set of `v*` in `H[A_c]` (`DynamicMIS.influenced_iff`). 3. (`SimpleGraph.sum_card_influenced_le`) `E[|influenced(v*)| · 1{v* ∈ greedyMIS} · 1{v** before v*}] ≤ Pr[v** before v*]`; summing over the two possible orientations of an edge gives `≤ 1`. -/ open
