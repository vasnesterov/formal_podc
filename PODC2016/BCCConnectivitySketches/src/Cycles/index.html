Fix a graph `G`, `r > 0` and a total order of the edges, given by an injective weight `w : Sym2 V → ℕ`. For every cycle of length `≤ 2r` pick its `w`-maximal edge (`IsHeavy`) and delete all picked edges: this is `prunedGraph G r w` (the paper's `G̃`). * `proposition_1`: `G̃` has the same connected components as `G`; hence (`connected_iff`, `IsSpanningForest.of_prunedGraph`) `G` is connected iff `G̃` is, and every spanning forest of `G̃` is one of `G`. Injectivity of `w` (a *total* order) is essential: with ties, a triangle whose three edges have equal weight loses all three edges. * `prunedGraph_noShortCycles`: `G̃` has no cycle of length `≤ 2r`. * `proposition_2` (corrected): a graph with no cycle of length `≤ 2r` (girth `> 2r`) on `n ≤ s ^ r` vertices is `s`-degenerate, i.e. `O(n^{1/r})`-degenerate. The paper states girth `≥ 2r`, which is false: `proposition_2_false` (for `r = 2`, `K_{m,m}` has girth `4` and is not `(m-1)`-degenerate). * `ball`, `localView`, `isHeavy_localView_iff`: whether an
