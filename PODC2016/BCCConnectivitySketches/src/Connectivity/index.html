Protocol: `v` computes its row of `G̃` (delete the maximal edge, w.r.t. a fixed edge order, of every cycle of length `≤ 2r`); this is possible locally (`isHeavy_localView_iff`). `s`-degenerate (`proposition_2`), so the pruning reconstructs all of `G̃` (`reconstruct_degenerate`); any spanning forest of `G̃` is one of `G` (`proposition_1`). -/ namespace
