Construction (as in the paper): `p = sketchPrime n d` is the least prime `> (1+n)^{2d}·n` (by Bertrand's postulate `p ≤ 2(1+n)^{2d}·n`); `x = sketchPoint n d ∈ 𝔽_p` is a common non-root of all polynomials `P(S) - P(S')`, `P(S) = ∑_{i ∈ S} X^i`, for distinct `S, S' ⊆ [n]` of size `≤ d` (there are fewer than `(1+n)^{2d}` such pairs, each difference is a nonzero polynomial of degree `< n`, so fewer than `p` roots in total). Then `f(v) = ∑ v_i x^i`. Indices are `Fin n` (`0, …, n-1`; the paper's `x^{i-1}` for `i = 1, …, n`).
