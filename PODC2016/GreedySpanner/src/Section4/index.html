import Papers.PODC2016.GreedySpanner.Theorem5 /-! # Section 4: the greedy spanner in doubling metrics A finite metric space is a weight function `w : Sym2 V → ℝ≥0` on the complete graph `⊤` (`IsMetricW`: zero on the diagonal, positive off it, triangle inequality); the paper also views a metric space as a complete weighted graph. For a subgraph `H`, the *induced metric* `M_H` is the complete graph with weights `inducedW H w (xy) = δ_H(x, y)`. * `lemma5` — `H` the greedy `t`-spanner, `H'` any `t`-spanner of `M_H`: `w(H) ≤ w_{M_H}(H')`. * `lemma6` — for `1 ≤ t < 2` and positive weights: `|H| ≤ |H'|`. Both are proved for an arbitrary connected weighted graph `G` instead of a metric (the proofs do not use the triangle inequality of `G`), so they also hold verbatim for metrics (`G = ⊤`). * `IsDoubling w D` — every ball of radius `r` is covered by `≤ 2^D` balls of radius `r/2` (doubling dimension `≤ D`). `observation7` — if `H` is a `t`-spanner of a metric of doubling dimension `≤ D` and `t ≤ 2`, then `M_H` is a metric of doubling dimension `≤ 2D`. * `theorem6` — for a metric of doubling dimension `≤ D` and `1 ≤ t < 2`, the greedy `t`-spanner has at most `OPT_t^sparse(M(n, 2D))` edges and lightness at most `OPT_t^light(M(n, 2D))`. * `corollary3_of_forall_exists` — Corollary 3 with the cited Theorem 4 [Got15] as a hypothesis. -/ open Finset SimpleGraph open scoped ENNReal NNReal
