import Papers.PODC2016.GreedySpanner.Basic /-! # Lemma 3: the only `t`-spanner of the greedy `t`-spanner is itself The proof follows the paper: if `e ∈ H \ H'`, take a shortest path `P` in `H'` between the endpoints of `e` (weight `≤ t · w(e)`) and let `e'` be the edge of `P ∪ {e}` examined last by the greedy algorithm. All other edges of the cycle `P ∪ {e}` were added before `e'`, and they form a walk between the endpoints of `e'` of weight `≤ w(P) ≤ t · w(e) ≤ t · w(e')`, so `e'` would have been rejected.
