Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Low-Congestion Shortcuts without Embedding — PODC 2016.

Paper: Haeupler, Izumi, Zuzic, Low-Congestion Shortcuts without Embedding, PODC 2016; formalized from the journal version arXiv:1607.07553v3 (numbering follows it).

Summary: the deterministic results (Lemmas 1, 2, 3, 5, Theorem 2, Corollary 6, the deterministic version of Theorem 1 and the combinatorial part of Lemma 4) are proved. Lemma 1 needs a precise reading. Lemma 3's proof sketch has a gap, which we repaired. Lemma 2's broadcast is proved by a different argument (time reversal).

1. Errors and gaps in the paper

  1. Lemma 1, literal statement false. Definition 4 allows a T-restricted H_i to contain tree edges that are not connected (via H_i) to P_i. Definition 5 counts only components containing a node of P_i. Definition 2's dilation is the diameter of E_G[P_i] ∪ H_i. For the path 0 – 1 – 2 rooted at 0, P = {2}, H = {01}: b = 1, depth 2, but E_G[P] ∪ H is disconnected (lemma_1_literal_false; here the disconnection uses that the vertex set is P ∪ V(H), as in CSLib's DilationLE). The failure does not depend on that reading: on the path 0 – … – 4 rooted at 0, P = {4}, H = {01, 23} has b = 1 and the edge set {01, 23} itself is disconnected (lemma_1_literal_false_edgeSet). The paper explicitly allows such H_i (remark after Definition 5: components without nodes of P_i "need not be counted"). A degenerate, definitional case (✏️); the repair (drop the useless components) is immediate. We prove:

    • (a) lemma_1: any two nodes of P_i are joined in G[P_i] + H_i by a walk of length ≤ b(2D+1) − 1. This is what the paper's proof shows and what Theorem 2 uses.

    • (b) lemma_1_dilation: the full dilation bound b(2D+1) when every component of (P_i ∪ V(H_i), H_i) meets P_i (CSLib's BlocksMeetPart; then Definition 5 agrees with CSLib's numBlocks, blockParam_eq_numBlocks). All shortcuts built in the paper (CoreSlow, CoreFast, FindShortcut) satisfy this (blocksMeetPart_cutShortcut, theorem_1_deterministic).

    The same issue was found for Observation 2.6 of Ghaffari–Haeupler PODC'21 (Papers/PODC2021/LowCongestionShortcuts/).

  2. Lemma 3, proof gap (statement true). "Each leader r builds a BFS tree comprised of all the nodes that believe r is the leader": that set need not be connected to r (example in the README). Nodes outside every BFS tree are not covered by the failure-detection and counting argument. Repaired procedure (Verification.lean): BFS along edges whose endpoints share a leader, b₀ supersteps. Unreached nodes reject. A node raises a flag if it is unreached or has a neighbour that is unreached or has another leader. Each BFS tree convergecasts the OR of flags and its size, and broadcasts the result. verification_correct: in a connected supergraph, a node accepts iff the supergraph has ≤ b₀ nodes. The proof does not need the IDs to be distinct. Supersteps: 4b₀ + 1 (verificationSupersteps). Caveat: BFS and convergecast are given by their output specifications; group B (all reached nodes with B's leader) is one BFS tree only when each leader value has a unique source, i.e. when block IDs are distinct (as in the paper: block ID = smallest node ID in the block). With duplicate IDs the specification Accept is still correct but is not literally what a per-tree convergecast computes.

  3. Lemma 2, broadcast. The paper proves the convergecast and says the broadcast is symmetric. The induction does not transfer: it uses that a more urgent subtree containing a child edge of v contains v's edge (true upwards), and the per-edge rank is not monotone downwards (README, example). We prove the broadcast bound D − 1 + c by reversing time in the greedy convergecast schedule (IsConvergecastSchedule.reverse). The resulting algorithm first runs the convergecast with dummy messages, recording send rounds, and then replays them in reverse. This is a different (still simple, deterministic) algorithm from the paper's greedy broadcast. The greedy broadcast's D − 1 + c bound (true by a standard delay-sequence argument) is not formalized.

  4. Typos/slips. Algorithm 2 uses the threshold ≥ 4c·p; the text uses > 4·p. In the proof of Lemma 4 the second Chernoff bound is written Pr[S ≤ 2E[S]] instead of Pr[S ≥ 2E[S]]. The O(log N) iterations of FindShortcut are exactly ⌊log₂ N⌋ + 1 (numIter, remaining_numIter).

2. Statements changed or strengthened

  • Congestion. All hypotheses use CSLib's CongestionLE (each edge in at most c of the H_i). This is weaker than Definition 2 (PartCongestionLE: at most c of the sets E_G[P_i] ∪ H_i, implies CongestionLE by PartCongestionLE.congestionLE), so Lemmas 4, 5 and Theorem 1 are proved under a weaker hypothesis. Conclusions about the computed shortcut are also in the CongestionLE sense.

  • Lemma 5 / Lemma 4 blame argument. We blame part i for an unusable edge e when e sees P_i and e ∉ H_i. The paper also requires e ∉ E_G[P_i]; this is not needed. A missed edge is e ∈ U ∩ H_i (the paper says e ∈ U ∩ (E_G[P_i] ∪ H_i)). We prove #blocks(H'_i) ≤ #blocks(H_i) + #missed_i (blockParam_cutShortcut_le), #blamed_i ≤ #blocks(H_i) (card_blamed_le) and Σ missed ≤ Σ blamed (sum_missed_le_sum_blamed). Hence the parts with missed_i ≤ 2b (at least half) have ≤ 3b blocks (many_good_of_cut). The bound |U| ≤ Nb/c is not needed. The hypothesis is only "each unusable edge sees at least 2c parts", which covers both CoreSlow (> 2c) and CoreFast (≥ 2c w.h.p.).

  • "At least half". |P'| ≥ |P|/2 is card ι ≤ 2·|J|.

  • Lemma 2 height. The paper's height_T(v) is generalized to any h with h(w) + 1 ≤ h(parent w) (sent_of_le); the final bound uses h(v) = D − depth(v), giving D − 1 + c rounds. The priority is any injective key compatible with the tree (Compatible); the paper's (depth of the subtree's top, then ID) is one (compatible_topDepth, exists_schedules). Subtrees are given by their edges and top vertex (SubtreeFamily).

  • Theorem 1. Only the deterministic version is proved: FindShortcut with CoreSlow gives congestion 2c(⌊log₂ N⌋+1) and block parameter ≤ 3b. The paper's statement is the randomized one with CoreFast (congestion 8c per iteration w.h.p.); its deterministic analysis is lemma_4_combinatorial plus the same iteration. The verification step is identified with its output specification (goodOn: parts with ≤ 3b blocks), justified by corollary_6.

  • Theorem 2. "Convergecasting O(log n)-bit messages" is formalized for an arbitrary commutative monoid aggregate (theorem_2_convergecast). The broadcast from the leader down the BFS tree of the supergraph (b − 1 supersteps) is described but has no separate Lean statement (it is the trivial downward flooding along the tree). The total O(b(D+c)) is aggregationRounds_le: (4(b−1)+1) supersteps of ≤ 2(D−1+c)+1 rounds. This lemma is pure arithmetic; there is no end-to-end theorem about a CONGEST execution of Theorem 2 (see §3, Supersteps).

3. Modelling choices

  • Rounds. CONGEST is modelled through schedules: per tree edge and round at most one message (an O(log n)-bit ID or partial aggregate), plus causality. Message contents and local computation are not modelled. For Lemma 2 the greedy algorithm is an explicit round-by-round process (greedy), and its send times form a convergecast schedule.

  • Supersteps. Theorem 2, Lemma 3 and Corollary 6 are verified on the supergraph of a part: synchronous algorithms in which each node updates from its neighbours' values via min, +1-min or sums. One superstep's cost (superstepRounds D c = 2(D−1+c)+1) is justified by Lemma 2 applied to the family of all blocks of all parts (blockFamily, congestion ≤ c by blockFamily_congestionLE) plus one round over intra-part G-edges. The composition of the message-level schedules across supersteps is not formalized. In the BFS step we use CSLib's Connected.bfsTree (parents = neighbours with smaller label) after proving that the flooded labels are the distances (floodDist_spec). For Lemma 3 the multi-source BFS within regions and the per-tree convergecast are given by their specifications (Reached, group, Accept).

  • CoreSlow is modelled by its output (the unique fixed point coreSlowCut, existence by the bottom-up construction exists_cut, uniqueness cut_unique); its O(D·c) running time is reduced to the fact that every transmitted list has ≤ 2c IDs (card_coreSlow_list_le) over depth(T) phases.

  • Iteration. Parts that are already good or not under consideration are replaced by ∅ (restrictParts); empty parts are seen by no edge, so this is the same as running the subroutine on the remaining parts only.

  • Randomness. CoreFast (shared randomness, Chernoff bounds) is not modelled; Lemma 4 is proved conditionally on the two high-probability events (lemma_4_combinatorial).

  • Trees are rooted spanning trees with parent pointers and depths (CSLib RootedTree), depth ≤ D; the paper's "depth O(D)" is an explicit parameter D.

4. Not formalized

  • Chernoff bounds and the w.h.p. statements of Lemma 4 / randomized Theorem 1 (O(c log N) congestion, round count O(D log n log N + bD log N + bc log N)), shared randomness.

  • Corollaries 4–5 (genus-g graphs, MST via Boruvka), the cited existence theorem of [GH16], Section 1.4 survey results (Corollaries 1–3, [HHW18]).

  • The greedy broadcast's bound (see 1.3) and the message-level implementation of supersteps.
