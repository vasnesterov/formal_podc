Parts (`SimpleGraph.IsParts`), shortcuts (`ι → Finset (Sym2 V)`), rooted spanning trees (`SimpleGraph.RootedTree`), `T`-restricted shortcuts (`RootedTree.Restricts`, Definition 4) and the graph `G[P_i] + H_i` (`SimpleGraph.partGraph`) come from CSLib. Here: * `blockParam P H` (Definition 5): the number of *block components* of a part `P` with shortcut edges `H`, i.e. the connected components of `(V, H)` that contain a vertex of `P`. (CSLib's `numBlocks` counts the components of `(P ∪ V(H), H)`, including those that miss `P`; the two agree under `BlocksMeetPart`, see `blockParam_eq_numBlocks`.) * `PartCongestionLE` (Definition 2): every edge `e` lies in at most `c` of the sets `E_G[P_i] ∪ H_i`. This implies CSLib's `CongestionLE` (which only counts the `H_i`); all our hypotheses use the weaker `CongestionLE`. * `climb_image_ncard_le_blockParam`, `blockParam_le_climb_image_ncard`: for a `T`-restricted `H`, block components are in bijection with their tops (the end of the climb along `H`). -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace ShortcutsNoEmbedding open SimpleGraph
