import Papers.PODC2016.ShortcutsNoEmbedding.Defs import Papers.PODC2016.ShortcutsNoEmbedding.Supersteps import Podc.Computability.Distributed.Congest.SubtreeRouting /-! # Theorem 2 of [HIZ16]: routing on tree-restricted shortcuts All algorithms of Theorem 2 run on the *supergraph* of each part: its nodes are the block components of the part (`Blocks P H`), and two blocks are adjacent if a `G`-edge inside the part joins them (`superGraph P H`). One superstep consists of one round of communication over the `G`-edges between blocks and an intra-block convergecast + broadcast, which by Lemma 2 takes `≤ 2 (D - 1 + c) + 1` rounds for all parts simultaneously. * `blockFamily T H`: the block components of all parts as a family of subtrees of `T` (`(i, t)` = the block of part `i` whose top is `t`). `blockFamily_congestionLE`: every tree edge lies in at most `c` of them. `theorem_2_intraBlock`: by Lemma 2, a convergecast and a broadcast inside all block components take `≤ D - 1 + c` rounds each. * `superGraph_connected`, `card_blocks`: the supergraph is connected and has `blockParam P H ≤ b` nodes, so its diameter is `≤ b - 1`. * `theorem_2_leader` (Theorem 2.1): after `b - 1` supersteps of min-flooding (starting from the smallest ID in each block), every block knows the smallest ID of the part. * `theorem_2_convergecast` (Theorem 2.2): along the BFS tree of the supergraph rooted at the leader's block (computed by `floodDist` in `b - 1` supersteps), `b - 1` supersteps of tree convergecast deliver the aggregate `∑_{v ∈ P} x_v` (any commutative monoid) to the leader's block.
