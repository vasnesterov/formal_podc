Proof (as in §A.5): a cycle of `(WR ∪ WW) ; RW?` in `splice(G)` gives (Theorem 19's translation, `DepGraph.acyclic_si_iff`) a cycle of `splice(G)` without adjacent anti-dependencies, hence (Lemma 24) a simple one. Each of its edges comes from a conflict edge of `G` between transactions of the two sessions (equation (2), Lemma 17); joining consecutive representatives by session-order edges gives a simple cycle of `DCG(G)`. If one of the joining edges is a predecessor edge, the cycle is critical; otherwise it is a cycle of `G` without adjacent anti-dependencies, contradicting `G ∈ GraphSI`. -/ namespace Podc.AnalysingSI
