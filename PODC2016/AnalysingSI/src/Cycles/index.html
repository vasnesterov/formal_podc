import Papers.PODC2016.AnalysingSI.CycleDefs /-! # Cycles in edge-labelled graphs The dynamic criteria of Cerone–Gotsman (Theorems 16, 19, 22) talk about *cycles* of dependency graphs and chopping graphs and the labels of their edges ("two adjacent anti-dependency edges", "conflict, predecessor, conflict", …). We represent an edge-labelled multigraph on `α` by a family of relations `E : L → α → α → Prop` (`E l a b`: an `l`-labelled edge from `a` to `b`). * `Walk E a w b`: `w = [(l₁, v₁), …, (lₖ, vₖ)]` describes the walk `a —l₁→ v₁ —l₂→ ⋯ —lₖ→ vₖ = b`. * `IsCycle E w`: `w` is a nonempty closed walk (cycles may repeat vertices, as in §A.5 of the paper); its vertices are `w.map Prod.snd`, its labels `w.map Prod.fst`; it is *simple* (`IsSimple`) if no vertex repeats. * `CycAdj P ls`: two cyclically consecutive labels of the cyclic sequence `ls` satisfy `P`. Main facts: rotation invariance, Lemma 24 (a cycle without adjacent `P`-pairs contains a simple one), and the translation between cycles and the relations `(B ; A?)⁺`, `B⁺ ; A?`. -/ -- CSLib
