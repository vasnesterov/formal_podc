The protocol `cexProtocol` solves nonuniform 2-set consensus with `t = 1` and makes every low process decide immediately, yet process `0` decides `2`, not `0`, at time `1`: * a process with a low input decides it at time `0`; * at time `1`, a high process that did not hear from some process in round 1 and whose only low value is `0` decides its smallest high value; every other high process decides its smallest value. With `t = 1`, a process that misses a message knows the unique faulty process; then all other processes are correct, so `0` is decided (by its correct holders) and the only other value decided is the smallest high input of the correct processes.
