76

Filtser, Solomon — The Greedy Spanner is Existentially Optimal

✏️ Slip. Existential optimality and near-optimality in doubling metrics are proved, with only slips in proofs and definitions. Corollaries rest on cited bounds; the approximate-greedy construction of Section 5 was not checked.

68

Censor-Hillel, Haramaty, Karnin — Optimal Dynamic Distributed MIS

🔧 Easy fix. One adjustment and one round in expectation hold in the synchronous model after local repairs to the layers of S, Algorithm 2's rule 4 and several proofs; the asynchronous model was not checked.

62

Attiya, Burckhardt, Gotsman, Morrison, Yang, Zawirski — Specification and Complexity of Collaborative Text Editing

🔧 Easy fix. RGA as printed violates even the weak list specification, but a one-line index fix proves Theorem 1; the Ω(D) metadata lower bound holds after two local proof repairs.

57

Shibata, Mega, Ooshita, Kakugawa, Masuzawa — Uniform Deployment of Mobile Agents in Asynchronous Rings

✏️ Slip. Algorithm 1 (knowledge of k, Theorem 3), the impossibility without knowledge of k or n (Theorem 5), the Ω(kn) move bound and Lemmas 2–4 hold; Algorithm 2's loop guard and Algorithm 6's array bounds are slips. Theorems 4 and 6 (Algorithms 2–6) are only simulated, not proved.

46

Cerone, Gotsman — Analysing Snapshot Isolation

🔧 Easy fix. SI characterisation, chopping and robustness results hold; Lemma 26's proof misses a case, fixed by reordering it, and a typo in Definition 20 makes Theorem 21 false as printed.

42

Bar-Yehuda, Censor-Hillel, Schwartzman — A Distributed (2+ε)-Approximation for Vertex Cover in O(log Δ/ε log log Δ) Rounds

✅ Correct. No mistakes: the (2+ε)-approximation and its round bounds hold, also for CONGEST apart from message sizes. Of the contradiction with [KMW10] only the arithmetic was checked.

31

Jayanti, Tarjan — A Randomized Concurrent Algorithm for Disjoint Set Union

⚠️ Weakened. Linearizability and wait-freedom of all variants are proved; the efficiency results are unchecked, and Lemma 4.2's Chernoff step, behind the O(log n) bound and Theorem 5.1, is wrong, repaired only on paper.

27

Amiri, Schmid, Siebertz — A Local Constant Factor MDS Approximation for Bounded Genus Graphs

⚠️ Weakened. Local constant factor for bounded genus holds after easy repairs of Lemmas 7, 9, 11 and Corollary 8, but Theorem 13's O(g) factor is only proved as O(g²).

25

Ellen, Gelashvili, Shavit, Zhu — A Complexity-Based Hierarchy for Multiprocessor Synchronization

🔧 Easy fix. All formalized bounds and algorithms hold after local proof repairs; the only loss is the word 'anonymous' in Theorem 8.8, whose n − 1 location protocol is proved only with process identifiers.

24

Haeupler, Izumi, Zuzic — Low-Congestion Shortcuts without Embedding

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 3's verification and Lemma 2's broadcast needed local repairs; the deterministic core stands. The randomized construction and end-to-end round bounds were not checked.

23

Halpern, Vilaça — Rational Consensus

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 hold, the latter after a new counting argument replaces its wrong agreement proof; the rest are slips in Algorithm 1. The equilibrium results, Theorems 3 and 4, are unchecked.

20

Mostéfaoui, Raynal — Two-Bit Messages are Sufficient to Implement Atomic Read/Write Registers in Crash-prone Systems

🔧 Easy fix. The two-bit atomic register is correct, safety and liveness; the proofs of Lemmas 5 and 10 need small repairs, and code between waits must run atomically, as is standard.

18

Montealegre, Todinca — Brief Announcement: Deterministic Graph Connectivity in the Broadcast Congested Clique

🔧 Easy fix. The deterministic sparse sketch, one-round O(d log n) pruning and both connectivity protocols are proved; the proof of the ⌈1/ε⌉-round bound stops one round late and needs a sharper count, and Proposition 2's "girth at least 2r" must read "greater than 2r".

17

Cadambe, Wang, Lynch — Information-Theoretic Lower Bounds on the Storage Cost of Shared Memory Emulation

❌ Intent lost. With FIFO channels a constant-state relay refutes the bounds of Theorems 4.1, 5.1, B.1. The no-gossip bounds hold with a new assumption, ReaderQuiet. Theorem 6.5 and non-FIFO channels unchecked.

15

Spiegelman, Cassuto, Chockler, Keidar — Space Bounds for Reliable Storage: Fundamental Limits of Coding

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 1 is false, so the f + 1 lower bound holds only for algorithms not coding across writes. Algorithm 2 needs a local fix; the adaptive algorithm is unchecked.

15

Bonomi, Del Pozzo, Potop-Butucaru, Tixeuil — Optimal Mobile Byzantine Fault Tolerant Distributed Storage

❌ Intent lost. Both regular-register protocols (CAM, CUM) fail at the paper's own parameters and no repair is proved; only lower bounds 3 and 5 are re-proved, in a restricted model, so optimality is lost.

8

Castañeda, Gonczarowski, Moses — Unbeatable Set Consensus via Topological and Combinatorial Reasoning

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 1, the core of the combinatorial proof, is false, but one added hypothesis saves it; unbeatability and u-Pmin[k] hold, while the topological proof was not formalized.

4

Hadjistasi, Nicolaou, Schwarzmann — Brief Announcement: Oh-RAM! One and a Half Round Read/Write Atomic Memory

🔧 Easy fix. OhSam, OhMam, OhSam' and OhMam' are atomic and terminate with fewer than half of the servers crashed; only Table 1's read message complexity is false, because stale relays re-trigger readAcks, and nesting one line restores it.

2

Baruch, Ostrovsky, Rosenbaum — Brief Announcement: Space-Time Tradeoffs for Distributed Verification

⚠️ Weakened. Universal t-PLS and the optimal acyclicity tradeoff hold; the general crossing lower-bound tool is false with identifiers and true only in anonymous networks; slips in Algorithm 1 and Proposition 16.

2

Taubenfeld — Brief Announcement: Computing in the Presence of Weak Crash Failures

✏️ Slip. The register consensus algorithm tolerating one crash (n−1)-failure, the k-set consensus algorithm tolerating ℓ+k−2 crash (n−ℓ)-failures and the impossibility results all hold (Theorem 7 assuming the classical k-set impossibility for k ≥ 2); only degenerate cases fail (n = 1 for Fig. 1 and Corollary 2, n = 2 for the remark after Corollary 2), and the appendix's k-deadlock-freedom definition is carelessly worded.

1

Petersen, Rotbart, Simonsen, Wulff-Nilsen — Near-Optimal Adjacency Labeling Scheme for Power-Law Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. Upper and lower bounds hold with C = 1/ζ(α). Proofs of Propositions 3 and 4 and Theorem 3 need local repairs; Proposition 4 fails only for c ≥ n, Theorem 3 needs large n.

0

Hendler — On the Complexity of Reader-Writer Locks

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 1 is false but the lower bound holds via a new counting lemma; the algorithm breaks Mutual Exclusion until a one-line fix; its liveness and RMR bounds, hence tightness, were not checked.
