import Papers.PODC2016.RWLockRMRTradeoff.Alg.Safety /-! # Bounded Exit for the algorithms `A_f` (Lemma 10 of Hendler, PODC 2016) The exit code of a reader (lines 40–54) and of the writer (lines 25–26; `WL.Exit` is omitted in the single-writer model) contains no waiting, so a process that leaves the critical section returns to the remainder section within a bounded number of its own steps, whatever the other processes do. This holds for every variant (`ReadOrder`, `Line47`) and every group assignment. We measure the remaining exit steps by `RLocal.exitRank` / `WLocal.exitRank` (`none` outside the exit section and the CS) and show that each own step decreases the rank and other processes' steps do not change it. -/ namespace Podc.RWLockRMRTradeoff.Alg variable {n G : ℕ} /-- Remaining steps of a reader in the critical section or its exit section (at most 5: lines 40, 41, 51 (two reads), 52). -/
