import Papers.PODC2016.BoundedGenusMDS.Lemma12 /-! # Counterexamples to Lemma 7, Corollary 8 and Lemma 11 (as stated) All graphs here have a vertex cover `{p, q}` of size two, so every depth-1 minor `H` satisfies `|E(H)| ≤ 2|V(H)|` and no depth-1 minor is `K_{3,3}` (`SimpleGraph.depth1_of_cover_two`). Both graphs are planar, so they satisfy Assumption 6 with `c = 2`, `t = 3`. * `G7 K` (Lemma 7.2, Corollary 8): `m_i – c' – u_k – m_j` for `k < K`, i.e. `K_{2,K}` on `{c', m_j} × {u_k}` plus the pendant edge `m_i c'`. `M = {m_i, m_j}` is a minimum dominating set, `D = ∅`, `N_{E'}(m_i) = {c'}`, `N_{E'}(m_j) = {u_k}`, and every `u_k` has the neighbour `c'` in the other star: `K` vertices of `N_j` see `N_i` (Lemma 7.2 claims `≤ 2ct = 12`), and `|Y| ≥ K` (Corollary 8 claims `≤ 6c²t|M| = 144`). With the ordering `m_j, m_i` the same graph violates Lemma 7.3 (`≤ 4ct = 24`) for `K = 25`. * `G11 K` (Lemma 11): the book `K_{1,1,K}` on `m, x, u_k`. `M = {m}`, `D = ∅`, `C_m = {x}`, `dom ≡ x` is a valid dominator choice (ties between `m` and `x`), `Y = Z = ∅`, and all `K + 1` leaves of `m` have their dominator inside the star outside `Y ∪ Z` (Lemma 11 claims `≤ (2(t-1)+4)c|M| = 16`). -/ open
