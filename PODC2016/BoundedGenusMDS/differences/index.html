Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Local MDS Approximation on Bounded Genus Graphs — PODC 2016.

Paper: S. A. Amiri, S. Schmid, S. Siebertz, A Local Constant Factor MDS Approximation for Bounded Genus Graphs, PODC 2016. Text used: arXiv:1602.02991v2 (12 Feb 2016, the latest arXiv version). The ACM proceedings version could not be compared. All statements refer to arXiv v2.

Errors in the paper (statement level)

  1. Lemma 7 (items 2 and 3) is false (🔧).

    • Graph. G7 K has vertices m_i, c', m_j, u_1..u_K and edges m_i c', c' u_k, m_j u_k. It is planar, and every edge meets {c', m_j}. So every depth-1 minor has |E(H)| ≤ 2|V(H)| and K_{3,3} is not a depth-1 minor (SimpleGraph.depth1_of_cover_two): Assumption 6 holds with c = 2, t = 3.

    • Setup. M = {m_i, m_j} is a minimum dominating set (G7_min). D = ∅ (G7_phase1). C_{m_i} = C_{m_j} = {c'}.

    • Item 2 (order m_i, m_j). |{u ∈ N_j : u sees N_i}| = K > 2ct = 12 for K = 13 (lemma_7_2_false).

    • Item 3 (order m_j, m_i). K > 4ct = 24 for K = 25 (lemma_7_3_false).

    • The statements do not mention C, so the counterexamples do not depend on the choice of C_m.

    • Item 1 (m_i ∈ D) has the same proof gap but was not refuted formally.

    • Where the proof breaks. The covering vertex c ∈ C_{m_j} may itself lie in N_i. Then the branch sets "star of m_i without c", {c}, {m_j} do not see the u whose only N_i-neighbour is c.

    • Repair. Count only leaves outside C with a neighbour outside C (Setup.X a b). The bounds become 2c(t-1) (Setup.lemma_7_12) and 4c(t-1) (Setup.lemma_7_3).

  2. Corollary 8 is false (🔧). In G7 145 the paper's Y contains all 145 vertices u_k, but 6c²t|M| = 144 (corollary_8_false). This holds for every choice of C.

    • Repair. Use Y = leaves outside C of stars of M \ D with a neighbour outside C in another star, and Y_D = its part that sees stars of M ∩ D. Then |Y| + |Y_D| ≤ 6c²(t-1)|M| (Setup.corollary_8). The proof charges every vertex to an oriented edge of the depth-1 minor H, at most 2c(t-1) (forward) and 4c(t-1) (backward) per edge. It splits the ≤ 2c|M| oriented edges by the order of their endpoints.

  3. Lemma 11 is false (🔧).

    • Graph. The book K_{1,1,17} (spine m x, pages u_k; vertex cover {m, x}), with c = 2, t = 3.

    • Setup. M = {m}, D = ∅, C_m = {x}. Every admissible C_m (|C_m| ≤ 4, m ∉ C_m, covering 17 pages) must contain x.

    • Dominators. dom ≡ x is a legal choice: m and x both have residual degree |V| (S11_isDominator).

    • Violation. Y = Z = ∅, and all 18 leaves of m have dom(u) ∈ N_{E'}(m) \ (Y ∪ Z), while (2(t-1)+4)c|M| = 16 (lemma_11_false).

    • Where the proof breaks. It applies Lemma 9 to dom(u), which may lie in C_m.

    • Repair. Exclude C from the range as well, i.e. require dom(u) ∈ N_{E'}(m) \ (C ∪ Y ∪ Z), and count sources u ∉ N[D]. Then the paper's bound (2(t-1)+4)c|M| holds (Setup.lemma_11). Per star: #sources ≤ |N_{E'}(m) \ N[D]| ≤ deg(x) ≤ 4c + 2c(t-1). The middle inequality comes from d_{V-D}(m) ≤ d_{V-D}(x); it is not "m has at most … E'-neighbours", which fails when m's leaves are dominated by D.

  4. Lemma 9 is false for some admissible C_m (🔧; not formalized, informal counterexample).

    • Graph. m – v, both adjacent to 18 vertices forming 6 paths a_i – c_i – b_i. This is planar: draw K_{2,18} with the u's on a line and the path edges inside consecutive faces. So c = 3 and t = 3.

    • Setup. D = ∅. C_m = {c_1, …, c_6} is admissible and v ∉ C_m.

    • Violation. v has 18 neighbours in N_{E'}(m), but the lemma claims 2c(t-1) = 12.

    • Where the proof breaks. It covers only neighbours u ∈ N(c) for c ∈ C_m, not u ∈ C_m.

    • Repair. Count only neighbours outside C (Setup.lemma_9), which is all Lemma 11 needs.

  5. Theorem 13: the O(g) approximation factor is not established (⚠️).

    • What the paper uses. The proof uses Lemma 12 with t = 4g+3 and treats c as a constant ("c ≤ 3.01 … in sufficiently large graphs", §4).

    • Why c grows with g. Lemma 5 (applied to H̃) and Lemma 10 (applied to G[Z ∪ M ∪ C]) need the density bound for small depth-1 minors too. Small graphs of genus g have density up to Θ(√g) (K_k).

    • Lean. density_unbounded: for every c, some complete graph satisfies the genus-type hypotheses (Euler's bound for all depth-1 minors with some g, and no K_{4g+3,3}) but has a depth-1 minor of density > c.

    • What we prove. With c(g) = 3 + ⌊√(3g)⌋ + 1 (depth1DensityLE_of_euler) the factor is ≤ 1100(g+1)² (theorem_13, factor_le). Whether the algorithm is an O(g)-approximation remains open.

    • Rounds. "O(g) communication rounds" holds but is far from tight: 6 rounds suffice (output_isLocal).

Proof repairs (statements true)

  1. Lemma 5.

    • Gap. H̃ contracts the stars only for m ∈ M \ D. Vertices v ∈ N(D \ M) \ (D ∪ M) whose unique H-neighbour in M lies in M ∩ D remain as extra vertices of H̃, so |V(H̃)| = |D \ M| + |M| is false.

    • Repair. For every m ∈ M, contract m together with the vertices outside M ∪ D assigned to m (via any assignment a : V \ M → M to an M-neighbour). Keep {w} for w ∈ D \ M.

    • Double counting. With S = Σ_{w ∈ D\M} deg_{H̃}(w) ≥ (2c+1)|D\M| and inclusion–exclusion over ordered touching pairs, 2S ≤ 2c|V(H̃)| + 2c|D \ M|.

    • Generality. The lemma holds for every dominating set M, not only minimum ones.

  2. Lemma 12.

    • Gap. The paper's accounting relies on Corollary 8 (false) and Lemma 11 (false).

    • Repaired accounting. dom(R) ⊆ (M ∪ C) ∪ Z ∪ Y ∪ dom(Y_D) ∪ dom(R ∩ (M ∪ C)) ∪ dom(Src) (Setup.image_dom_subset). A dominator outside M ∪ C ∪ Y ∪ Z either lies in the source's own star (then it is counted by Lemma 11), or lies in a star of M ∩ D (then the source is in Y_D).

    • Constant. (c+1) + (2c+1) + (2c+1) + (2c+1) + 6c²(t-1) + (2(t-1)+4)c = 6c²t + (2t+5)c + 4 + (4c − 6c²) ≤ 6c²t + (2t+5)c + 4, for t ≥ 1. So the paper's constant is recovered exactly.

    • Degenerate case. t = 0: excluding K_{0,3} forces |V| ≤ 2, handled separately.

  3. Lemma 10. The claim |Z| < |M ∪ C| fails for the empty graph (both sides 0; degenerate, ✏️). We prove |Z| ≤ |M ∪ C| (Setup.lemma_10). The paper's "more than ½·4c|Z| edges" is also handled correctly when Z ∩ C ≠ ∅: we count ordered pairs.

Imprecisions and typos

  1. Lemma 12's hypothesis reads "edge density at most c", but the proof needs Assumption 6 (all depth-1 minors have density ≤ c). Lemma 12 lies in the scope of Assumption 6 ("for the rest of this section"), so we read it with Assumption 6 (✏️). The Lean statement assumes Depth1DensityLE G c.

  2. The last display of Lemma 12 drops the factor |M| on the right-hand side.

  3. Proof of Lemma 7:

    • "m_i ∉ C_{m_2}" should read C_{m_j}.

    • It is not implied "by definition of E'": C_{m_j} is arbitrary. However, m_i covers no vertex of N_j, so it can be ignored (we do so, lemma_7_12).

  4. The remark after Theorem 13 ("for planar graphs … a 199-approximation") is lemma_12 with c = 3, t = 3 (6·9·3 + 11·3 + 4 = 199), taking as hypotheses that depth-1 minors of planar graphs are planar (so |E(H)| ≤ 3|V(H)|) and that planar graphs exclude K_{3,3}. With these facts the claim holds; it is not stated as a separate Lean theorem.

  5. Algorithm 1 has a stray end if. The algorithm returns D ∪ D' with D' = dom(V \ N[D]), as modelled.

Modelling choices

  1. Dominator choice. Line 15 "choose any dom(v)" is formalized as a hypothesis IsDominator. lemma_12 holds for every such choice, adversarial ones included. For locality we fix the tie-break "smallest identifier among the maximizers" (domMin, Locality.lean).

  2. Approximation. IsApprox G f S requires |S| ≤ f·|M| for every dominating set M. This is equivalent to |S| ≤ f·γ(G) and avoids defining γ.

  3. Parameters. c is a natural number, as in the paper ("an integer c"). ε(H) ≤ c is stated as |E(H)| ≤ c·|V(H)| for finite depth-1 minors on vertex types in Type. t is any natural number; the paper assumes t ≥ 3, and our statements are stronger.

  4. Depth-1 minors. A depth-1 minor model is a family of pairwise disjoint vertex sets, each with a center adjacent to all its other vertices, and every edge of H is realized by a G-edge between the branch sets. This matches §2 ("pairwise disjoint stars G_v ⊆ G"). Bridge to CSLib minors: SimpleGraph.Depth1Model.toMinorModel, SimpleGraph.MinorDensityLE.depth1DensityLE.

  5. Ordering and E'. The paper defines E' inductively along an ordering of M with M ∩ D first. We use an injective rank ord on M (D-first). The star of v ∉ M is the ord-first M-neighbour S.star v, which gives exactly the paper's N_{E'}(m) (Setup.leaves). The covering sets C_m are part of the data (Setup.Cm, any admissible choice). The counterexamples exhibit specific admissible choices; the existence of a Setup for every dominating set is Setup.exists_of_dominating.

  6. Genus. Mathlib has no surfaces or genus, so Theorem 13 assumes the consequences of Lemmas 1, 2, 4:

    • Euler's bound |E(H)| ≤ 3|V(H)| + 6g for all depth-1 minors (EulerEdgeBound; weaker than 3|V| + 6g − 6, so it is implied for orientable genus g and for non-orientable genus g̃ with g = g̃);

    • exclusion of K_{4g+3,3} as a depth-1 minor.

    Lemma 3 and Section 4 are not formalized.

  7. LOCAL model. Locality is extensional (CSLib Cslib.Local.IsLocal): membership of v in the output depends only on the edges at nodes at distance < 6 from v. Identifiers are the elements of the linearly ordered vertex type.

Not formalized

  • Lemmas 1–4 (topological graph theory; used as hypotheses).

  • Lemma 14, Algorithm 2 and Theorem 15 (Section 4), and Section 5 (first-order definability).

  • The counterexample to Lemma 9 (informal, item 4).
