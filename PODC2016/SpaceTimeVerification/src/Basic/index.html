import Mathlib import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.Local.Locality /-! # `t`-round proof labeling schemes: the model Basic notions for *Space-Time Tradeoffs for Distributed Verification* (R. Ostrovsky, M. Perry, W. Rosenbaum; PODC 2016 brief announcement by M. Baruch, R. Ostrovsky, W. Rosenbaum; full version arXiv:1605.06814). * A *configuration* is a graph `G : SimpleGraph V` on a vertex type `V : Type` with states (inputs) `I : V → ι`. Configurations of all sizes live on different vertex types, so one scheme can be run on a graph and on a connected component of it (Theorem 7 needs this). * `BallIso t G x v G' x' w`: the labelled radius-`t` balls of `v` in `G` and of `w` in `G'` are isomorphic. Explicitly, a map `φ` sends `v` to `w`, is injective on the ball, preserves the labelling `x` on the nodes at distance `≤ t`, and is a bijection between the neighbourhoods of every node at distance `< t` and of its image. This is exactly the information a node gathers in `t` synchronous rounds when it may use identifiers but its output does not depend on them (the paper's rule, Section 2.1: "an algorithm has the same output for all legal assignments of unique IDs"): the nodes at distance `≤ t` with their states and labels, and the edges incident to nodes at distance `< t`. Edges between two nodes at distance exactly `t` are not seen. * `TPLS ι Λ F P t`: a `t`-PLS for the predicate `P` on the family `F`: a prover, a verifier whose output at `v` is invariant under `BallIso t`, completeness and soundness on all finite configurations of `F`. * The three ways to produce `BallIso` used in the lower bounds: `ballIso_of_gAgree` (same vertex type, the graphs agree around `v` in the sense of CSLib's `GAgree`), `ballIso_comap` (renaming the vertices by an equivalence) and `ballIso_induce` (restriction to a union of connected components). -/ namespace Podc.SpaceTimeVerification open SimpleGraph -- CSLib candidate /-- The labelled radius-`t` ball of `v` in `G` (labelling `x`) is isomorphic to that of `w` in `G'` (labelling `x'`), witnessed by `φ`: `φ v = w`, `φ` is injective on the ball, preserves labels on it, and maps the neighbourhood of every node at distance `< t` bijectively onto the neighbourhood of its image. -/ def BallIso {V W α : Type*} (t : ℕ) (G : SimpleGraph V) (x : V → α) (v : V) (G' : SimpleGraph W) (x' : W → α) (w : W) : Prop := ∃ φ : V → W, φ v = w ∧ (∀ a b, G.edist v a ≤ t → G.edist v b ≤ t → φ a = φ b → a = b) ∧ (∀ a, G.edist v a ≤ t → x' (φ a) = x a) ∧ (∀ a, G.edist v a < t → ∀ b, G.Adj a b → G'.Adj (φ a) (φ b)) ∧ (∀ a, G.edist v a < t → ∀ b', G'.Adj (φ a) b' → ∃ b, G.Adj a b ∧ φ b = b') /-- A property of configurations (graph and states) of every vertex type. -/ abbrev CProp (ι : Type) := ∀ V : Type, SimpleGraph V → (V → ι) → Prop -- CSLib candidate /-- A `t`-round proof labeling scheme (`t`-PLS, Section 2.2) for the predicate `P` on the family `F`, with labels in `Λ`. The verifier's output at `v` (`verify G I ℓ v`: "`v` accepts") depends only on the labelled radius-`t` ball of `v` up to isomorphism (`isLocal`). Completeness: on every legal configuration the prover's labels are accepted everywhere. Soundness: if every node accepts some labelling, the configuration is legal. -/ structure TPLS (ι Λ : Type) (F P : CProp ι) (t : ℕ) where /-- The prover (an oracle that sees the whole configuration).
