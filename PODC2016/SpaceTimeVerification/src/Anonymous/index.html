import Papers.PODC2016.SpaceTimeVerification.Crossing import Papers.PODC2016.SpaceTimeVerification.LowerBound /-! # Proposition 4 and Theorem 5 hold in the anonymous model `Crossing.lean` shows that Proposition 4 and Theorem 5 (the crossing tool) are false for `t ≥ 2` when a node sees its labelled radius-`t` ball. Here we show that they are true in the *anonymous* (identifier-free, broadcast) message-passing model, where what a node learns in `t` rounds is its depth-`t` *view* (the depth-`t` truncation of its universal cover, with the states and labels attached). * `View α k`, `view G x k v`: the depth-`k` view of `v` in `G` with node data `x : V → α`: `view G x 0 v = x v`, `view G x (k+1) v = (x v, multiset of the depth-k views of the neighbours)`. An anonymous `t`-round verifier is a function of `view G (fun u => (I u, ℓ u)) t v`. * `view_transport`: an isomorphism `σ` of the induced `t`-neighbourhoods `N_t(e) → N_t(e')` preserving data and endpoints preserves the depth-`m` view of every node `y` with `dist(y, endpoint) + m ≤ t`. * `proposition_4_view`: under the hypotheses of Proposition 4 (and data-preserving `σ`), every node of the crossing `C(e, e', G)` has the same depth-`k` views (`k ≤ t`) as in `G`; `proposition_4_accept` derives that every node of every union of connected components of the crossing accepts if every node of `G` accepts. * `proposition_4_anon`: the pigeonhole form of Proposition 4 (labels from `L`, `|L|^q < r`). * `theorem_5_anon`: Theorem 5 in the anonymous model: every anonymous `t`-PLS uses at least `r^{1/q}` distinct labels on `G_s`, i.e. `r ≤ |L|^q`; `theorem_5_anon_length`: labels of length `≤ m` force `r ≤ 2^{(m+1)q}`, i.e. label size `Ω(log r / q)`. * `triangles_view_eq`: on the counterexample of `Crossing.lean` (disjoint triangles, `t = 2`) the crossing does not change any depth-`2` view.
