Lists of vertices are written in the paper's order: an oriented path / block `[v₀, v₁, …, v_{k-1}]` goes *down* the orientation (`v_i` is the parent of `v_{i+1}`). ## Definitions * `IsPar G ℓ v u`: integer whose binary expansion is `d(v_{t-1}) ⋯ d(v₀)`. ## Results * `prop16`: Proposition 16 (corrected): for `t ≥ 2`, a finite graph is acyclic iff it admits a correct acyclicity labelling. *
