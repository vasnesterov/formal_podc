Ellen, R. Gelashvili, N. Shavit, L. Zhu, *A Complexity-Based Hierarchy for Multiprocessor Synchronization*, PODC 2016 (arXiv 1607.06139), Section 3. An `m`-component unbounded counter has components `c_0, …, c_{m-1} ∈ ℕ`, supports `inc v` (increment `c_v`) and `scan` (return all components). The *racing counters* algorithm solves obstruction-free `m`-valued consensus among `n` processes: a process promotes (increments) its input, then alternates between a `scan` and promoting a value with the largest count; when a scan shows some `c_v` at least `n` larger than every other component, it decides `v`. To reuse the algorithm for the single-location implementations of Theorem 3.3, it is generic over a *counter simulation* `CounterSim m P`: a base object `S.B` with an increment operation `S.incOp p v k` (which may depend on the process `p` and on the number `k` of previous increments of `c_v` by `p`, as in the `set-bit` implementation), a read-only scan operation `S.scanOp`, a decoding of scan responses into counts, and an abstraction function `S.cnt` from states to counts, correct under a representation invariant `S.Inv` relating the state with the per-process increment counts. The `m`-component counter object `counterObj m` is the trivial instance (`counterSim`). ## Main results * `racing_solves`: the algorithm satisfies agreement and validity in *every* execution (not only one-shot ones), provided `Fintype.card P ≤ n`. * `racing_obstructionFree`: it is obstruction-free (CSLib's `Impl.ObstructionFree`, from every reachable configuration, including ones where processes were re-invoked). * `lemma_3_1`: Lemma 3.1 of [EGSZ16] for the counter object. ## Modelling choices / deviations from the paper * Tie-breaking: the promoted value after a scan is `argmax c`, *some* index of a maximal count (chosen by `Classical.choose`; the paper breaks ties arbitrarily). * Decision rule: the process decides `argmax c` if it is at least `n` larger than every other component. For `n ≥ 1` this is exactly the paper's rule (a component `n` larger than all others is the unique maximum); the threshold `n` is a parameter and agreement needs `|P| ≤ n`. * A process that has decided and is invoked again returns its decision immediately (one-shot tasks only constrain one-shot executions; this makes the algorithm a total `Impl` and gives full `ObstructionFree`). * The response of `inc` is an uninformative acknowledgement (`0`). * Values are `Fin m` with `m ≥ 1` (`[NeZero m]`). -/ namespace Cslib.SharedMemory.ComplexityHierarchy open Cslib.SharedMemory /-! ### The
