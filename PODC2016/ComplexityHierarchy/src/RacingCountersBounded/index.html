Lemma 3.2: obstruction-free `m`-valued consensus among `n` processes can be solved with an `m`-component bounded counter with `K = 3n`. The algorithm modifies the racing counters algorithm of Lemma 3.1: to promote `v`, a process looks (in its last scan) at a largest component `c_u` among the *other* components; if `c_u < n` it increments `c_v`, otherwise it decrements `c_u`.
