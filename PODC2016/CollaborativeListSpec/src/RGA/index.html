Timestamps are `(x, i)` with `x` one more than the largest first component in `N` and `i` the replica id. **Correction.** The paper builds the new node "as described in the Insert procedure of Section 4.1", which takes the parent at position `k - 1` of `s(N)`, i.e. counting tombstones. Then condition (1c) fails (`RGALiteral.lean`). We take the parent at position `min k n - 1` of the *visible* list `s(N) \ T` (of length `n`), as in the original RGA and as the positions of `ins(a, k)` (Section 3) refer to the user-visible list. **Proof structure.** `Inv` (Lemmas 3 and 4): the tree (tombstones) of replica `r` after a prefix consists of the nodes (elements) of the inserts (deletes) that happen before or are an event of `r` in that prefix, and the send buffer holds those of `r`'s updates since its last send (`inv`, using causal broadcast through `known_succ_recv`). Lemma 5: the nodes of all inserts form a TI tree `Nall` (`isTITree_Nall`) and every replica tree is a sub-TI tree (`isTITree_st`).
