Paths are lists of edges (`IsPath E s π s'`), cycles are closed paths, a multicycle is a multiset of cycles; its Parikh image `parikh Θ` is the multiset of all its edges, its displacement `mdisp δ Θ` plus a multicycle of short cycles (length `≤ |S|`). * `lemma_7_1` (Euler lemma): a total multicycle of a strongly connected net is the Parikh image of one cycle, through any given control-state. *
