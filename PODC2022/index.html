Camenisch, Drijvers, Hanke, Pignolet, Shoup, Williams — Internet Computer Consensus

🔧 Easy fix. ICC0's safety and deadlock freeness hold; the liveness lemma's "finalized" must become "committed", which is proved. ICC1 and ICC2 were not checked.

44

Abraham, Ben-David, Yandamuri — Efficient and Adaptively Secure Asynchronous Binary Agreement via Binding Crusader Agreement

🔧 Easy fix. All four (G)BCA protocols hold; Lemmas 4.5 and 4.9 need local proof repairs and Algorithm 5 a one-word fix. Efficiency comparisons and probabilistic round bounds were not checked.

26

Ghinea, Liu-Zhang, Wattenhofer — Optimal Synchronous Approximate Agreement with Asynchronous Fallback

❌ Intent lost. Π_AA is broken: Π_rBC is inconsistent for tₛ ≥ n/3, so honest parties stay at 0 and 1 (n = 3). The impossibility bound stands; no working protocol is proved.

16

Lenzen, Loss — Optimal Clock Synchronization with Signatures

🔧 Easy fix. Main clock-synchronization results unchecked. In the approximate-agreement part all statements hold; only Lemma 8's induction step needs a short repair.

13

Lenzen, Sheikholeslami — A Recursive Early-Stopping Phase King Protocol

❌ Intent lost. Proof of Theorem 1.2, the bit-optimal recursive protocol, fails on terminated nodes; not refuted, no repair proved. Lemma 4.2 false, repaired. Theorems 1.1 and 2.9 hold.

9

El-Hayek, Henzinger, Schmid — Broadcasting Time in Dynamic Rooted Trees is Linear

🔧 Easy fix. The first linear upper bound holds as stated; the printed proof is one round short, and a recount of the same pigeonhole closes it.

8

Rajsbaum, Raventós-Pujol — A Distributed Combinatorial Topology Approach to Arrow's Impossibility Theorem

⚠️ Weakened. Topological proof of Arrow's theorem stands after local repairs; Theorem 4.2 is false, and the domain characterization holds only after a redesign and for subdomains keeping every pair free.

5

Leroux — State Complexity of Protocols with Leaders

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorem 4.3 and the Ω((log log n)^h) bound hold as printed; the broken proof of Lemma 7.3 needed a new argument with a weaker constant, and its printed constant stays open.

3

Dory, Ghaffari, Ilchi — Near-Optimal Distributed Dominating Set in Bounded Arboricity Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. Main approximation theorems hold; Theorem 1.3's arithmetic, Observation A.1 on K₂ and Lemma 4.7 need local repairs. The lower bound is checked only as a combinatorial reduction.

3

Delporte-Gallet, Fatourou, Fauconnier, Ruppert — When is Recoverable Consensus Harder Than Consensus?

🔧 Easy fix. The characterization and all of Section 3 hold; Corollary 20, Propositions 19 and 21 and Lemma 11 have proof gaps with local repairs. Section 4 was not checked.

3

Censor-Hillel, Cohen, Gelles, Sela — Distributed Computations in Fully-Defective Networks

✅ Correct. No mistakes found, but the main theorem is only partly checked: cycle simulator and impossibility proved; Robbins simulator safe on a given cycle; its liveness, the cycle's construction and complexity unchecked.

3

Tseng, Zhang — Brief Announcement: Computability and Anonymous Storage-Efficient Consensus with an Abstract MAC Layer

✏️ Slip. Theorem 1 (regular store-collect over the abstract MAC layer) holds; wait-freedom needs the implied assumption that handlers do not starve the main thread; the consensus algorithms were not checked.

2

Fraigniaud, Paz, Rajsbaum — A Speedup Theorem for Asynchronous Computation with Applications to Consensus and Approximate Agreement

⚠️ Weakened. The speedup theorem and all numbered results hold, but the claim that binary consensus does not speed up approximate agreement holds only for id-and-round inputs, with a bound sometimes one round short.

2

Cohen, Keidar, Spiegelman — Brief Announcement: Make Every Word Count: Adaptive Byzantine Agreement with Fewer Words

❌ Intent lost. The headline O(n(f+1))-word BB for n = 2t + 1 needs the adaptive weak BA of the full version. The authors withdrew the one the BA cited (v1), and the corrected v2 violates agreement (n = 5, f = 2; n = 3, f = 1). Only the BB→weak BA reduction (after a one-line fix) and the failure-free strong BA hold.

1

Ovens — The Space Complexity of Consensus from Swap

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 1 and all obstruction-free lower bounds hold; the proofs of Lemmas 13, 14, 16 and Theorem 10 need local repairs. Nondeterministic and randomized versions were not checked.

1

Attiya, Enea, Welch — Blunting an Adversary Against Randomized Concurrent Programs with Linearizable Implementations

⚠️ Weakened. Lemmas 4.3 and 4.4 are false and the proof of Theorem 4.2 collapses; its bound is proved for r = 1 only, while a weaker bound still gives convergence to atomic objects for every r.

1

Liu, Golab — Brief Announcement: Towards a Theory of Wear Leveling in Persistent Data Structures

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 4.4 is false for "any implemented object": test-and-set is a counterexample. It holds for FAI, so the correct, lock-free counter of Figure 1 is optimal, as the paper intended.

1

Bezerra, Kuznetsov — Brief Announcement: How to Tame Multiple Spending in Decentralized Cryptocurrencies

⚠️ Weakened. The lower bound k ≥ k_max and the optimality claim are false; the optimum for k-CB is max(1, λ_s), a smaller independence number. Algorithms 1–2 and the k_max upper bound stand.

0

Fraigniaud, Lambein-Monette, Rabie — Brief Announcement: Fault Tolerant Coloring of the Asynchronous Cycle

❌ Intent lost. Neither 5-coloring algorithm is wait-free: two processes can run forever on every cycle, so the O(log* n) bound fails. Palette and properness hold; the 6-color Algorithm 1 is correct.
