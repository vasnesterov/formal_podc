import Papers.PODC2022.AnonymousMACConsensus.Model /-! # MAC-SC: the inductive invariant The invariant `Inv` of reachable configurations of MAC-SC (with handler priority). It consists of * `HInv`: well-formedness of the history (node `i`'s operations are numbered `0, 1, …` and are sequential) and facts recorded when a collect read its view (`PastOK`: every store or collect completed before the collect's invocation is reflected in the view it returns); * knowledge facts (`Knows`): every completed operation's broadcast has reached every node that has not crashed (in its view or in its handler queue) — the "durable and timely" insight of the paper. -/ namespace Podc.AnonymousMACConsensus variable {ι V : Type*} /-! ## List positions -/ lemma lt_length_of_getElem?_eq_some {α : Type*} {l : List α} {p : ℕ} {a : α} (hp : l[p]?
