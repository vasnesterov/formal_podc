In each round, if all messages up to that round broadcast by honest parties have been delivered to all honest parties, and all notarization and proposal delay times have elapsed, then all honest parties will have finished the round with a notarized block." We read "will have finished" with the paper's "wait for" semantics: an honest party executes a clause as soon as its condition holds. So the hypothesis is a reachable state in which * every message of a round `≤ k` broadcast by an honest party is in every honest pool; * every honest party in a round `≤ k` that has started its round has its proposal and notarization delays elapsed (for all ranks); * no honest party in a round `≤ k` has an enabled clause of the Tree Building Subprotocol (`TBSettled`); and the conclusion is that every honest party is past round `k`. The proof: take an honest party in the smallest round `j ≤ k`. If it waits for the beacon, it has the `n - |F| ≥ t + 1` honest beacon shares. If some honest party already finished round `j`, its notarization (and the echoed chain below it) has been delivered. Otherwise every honest party is in round `j`, has proposed, and has cast a notarization share for the valid non-disqualified block of minimal rank in its pool; since disqualifications are echoed, all
