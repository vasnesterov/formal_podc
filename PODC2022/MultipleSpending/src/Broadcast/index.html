Delivery is the (single, persistent) decision `dec`. * `IsKCB T s k A`: Validity, `k`-Consistency and Integrity of `k`-CB. * `LocalProgress T s A` (the paper's assumption) and the per-value strengthening `LocalProgressAll T s A` (for *every* value the source may broadcast). * `trivialAlg s`: the source sends its value, everybody delivers the first value received from the source. `trivialAlg_isKCB`: it is a `k`-CB with `k = max 1 (max_{F ∈ F, s ∈ F} |Π − F|)`. * `theorem_5_1_false`: in the uniform model with `n = 3`, `q = 1`, `f = 1` the inconsistency number is `3`, but `trivialAlg` implements `2`-CB (with Local Progress, even per value), for every source.
