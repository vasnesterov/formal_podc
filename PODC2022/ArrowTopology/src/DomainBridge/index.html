Rajsbaum, A. Raventós-Pujol, *A Distributed Combinatorial Topology Approach to Arrow's Impossibility Theorem*, PODC 2022. For three alternatives (`T3 : ThreeAlts X`) and any domain `D` of profiles (any set of voters `ι`): * `admitsNonDictatorial_iff_sign`: `D` admits a unanimous, IIA, non-dictatorial aggregation map iff there is a sign function `s` on the vertices of `N_I` that is unanimous and simplicial on the triangles `T3.sig '' D` spanned by `D`, and is not a projection on them. * `AdmitsNonDictatorial.mono`: if `D ⊆ D'` and every pairwise pattern occurring in `D'` already occurs in `D`, then `D'` admitting a non-dictatorial map implies that `D` does (non-dictatoriality only depends on which "vertices" occur). * `PairFree`: the classical notion of a *free pair* (Le Breton–Weymark); a domain in which every pair is free contains all vertices of `N_I` (`forall_exists_sig_eq_of_pairFree`). -/ namespace
