Raventós-Pujol, *A Distributed Combinatorial Topology Approach to Arrow's Impossibility Theorem*, PODC 2022 (and Section 4.3 of the full version): two voters (`Fin 2`), alternatives `Alt = {A, B, C}`. A profile is written `prof o₁ o₂` with `o₁ o₂ : Ord3` among the six orders `ABC, …, CBA` (`BAC` = `B ≻ A ≻ C`). Domains are sets of profiles; the finite ones are given by lists of `Prof2 = Ord3 × Ord3` (`dom L`). It holds for disjoint blue edges (critical pairs): `thm42_admits_of_disjoint`, `thm42_maximal`. Its "moreover" part is false too: `thm42_moreover_false`. * `paper_triple_example_not_critical`: the full version's example critical triple `((BAC, ACB), (BCA, ABC), (BCA, ACB))` has both of its first
