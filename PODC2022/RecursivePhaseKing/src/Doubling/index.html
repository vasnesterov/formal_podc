For every iteration `j` the outputs of the three subroutine calls are arbitrary subject to their specifications, applied to the opinions of all correct nodes. Terminated nodes keep taking part with their final value, which is the paper's convention (3) ("they will assume that `i` sends `b` in all future rounds"). `Trace.validity`, `Trace.agreement`, `Trace.terminated_of_good`: the argument of Theorem 2.9 / Corollary 3.2 for an arbitrary sequence of elector sets. * `K4 n j`: the elector sets of Algorithm 4 on nodes `Fin n` (node `i` is the paper's `i + 1`). * `jStar f`: the least `J` with `2^(J+1) + J > f`, i.e. the least number of iterations that `f` faults cannot all spoil. * Lemma 3.1 as stated is **false** for `f = 2` (`lemma_3_1_false`, `n = 7`, faulty nodes `1, 2`): the first elector set `{1, 2, 3, 4}` may contain both faults.
