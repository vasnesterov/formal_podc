The KMW lower bound itself (an `Ω(log Δ / log log Δ)`-round bound for polylogarithmic MFVC approximation in LOCAL) is **not** formalized; we formalize the combinatorial content of the reduction: * the graph `H = lbGraph G K` (`K` copies of `G` with every edge subdivided by a "middle" node, plus one node `t_u` per vertex `u` of `G`, adjacent to all copies of `u`); * `lbGraph_arboricityLE_two`: `H` has arboricity at most `2`; * `lbGraph_degree_le`: every degree of `H` is at most `max K (max (Δ+1) 2)` (so `Δ_H = Δ²` for the paper's `K = Δ² ≥ Δ + 1`, `Δ ≥ 2`); * `exists_dominating_lbGraph`: `OPT_MDS(H) ≤ K · |C| + n` for every vertex cover `C` of `G`; * `fracCover_of_dominating`: every dominating set `S` of `H` yields a fractional vertex cover `y` of `G` (computed locally: `y_u` is the number of copies `i` such that the copy of `u` in `S` or a middle node of `S` was "moved" to `u`, divided by `K`) with `∑ y ≤ |S| / K`; * `host_adj`/`edist_host_le`: the simulation map `H → G` sends adjacent nodes to equal or adjacent nodes, so `R` rounds on `H` are simulated by `R` rounds on `G`; * `reduction`: if `S` is a `c`-approximate dominating set of `H` with `K = Δ²` and `G` has no isolated vertices, then `y` is a `2c(1 + 1/Δ)`-approximate fractional vertex cover of `G`.
