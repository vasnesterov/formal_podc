Schedule `schedN n`: all processes take a round at time `1`; from time `2` on, only processes `0` and `n - 1` (the global minimum and the global maximum of the identifiers, which are neighbours) take their rounds, simultaneously. The others crash after their first round (or are slow). The registers of `1` and `n - 2` stay frozen at the initial states, and `0`, `n - 1` alternate between two local states with period `2`; they never return.
