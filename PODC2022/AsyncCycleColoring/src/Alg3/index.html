We follow the proof of Lemma 4.10 (`r̂_{q₋ₖ₋₁}(t₀) = ⊥` is the case `k = r̂_p(t₀)`): a `⊥` value of `r` counts as `-1`, i.e. it blocks line 11. In lines 6–10 a `⊥` neighbour contributes nothing, as in Algorithm 2.
