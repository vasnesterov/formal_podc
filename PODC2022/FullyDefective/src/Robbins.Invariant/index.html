import Papers.PODC2022.FullyDefective.Robbins.Model /-! # Lemma 11 of [CCGS22] (safety part): the invariant of Algorithm 3 An inductive invariant of Algorithm 3 (`Robbins/Model.lean`). The paper proves Lemma 11 by "repeating the proof of Lemma 5" for the occurrences of the nodes; here the invariant is stated directly on positions of the Robbins cycle. ## The common part (`Common`) * `netEq` — channel accounting: on every channel `x → y` the pulses in transit are the `request` pulses sent by `x` and not yet counted by `y`, plus possibly the unique token/data/end pulse (the ghost `wv`). Request pulses are counted per epoch: `x` sends `mult x y` of them on `x → y` in each epoch (one per link `x → y` of the cycle) and `y` expects as many; * `reqLe` — `y` never counted more `request` pulses from `x` than `x` has sent; * `rot` — Challenge 1 of the paper: **every node's segment `0` contains the token**, i.e. its occurrence `0` is the first occurrence at or after the token position `τ`. ## The stages of an epoch `H = C τ` is the token holder (or the last node that held the token). * `TokStage` — every node completed all started epochs and is in its token phase; the token is either held by `H` (lines 1–3 of `H`) or travels counterclockwise on the link `τ → τ - 1`; * `DataStage pre d s` — epoch `|pre| + 1` with payload `d`: the data pulses travel clockwise one by one; `s < d·L` links have been traversed so far and the current pulse is on the link `τ + s → τ + s + 1`. Every non-holder `u` has received `cntF u τ s` data pulses (the number of its occurrences among `τ + 1, …, τ + s`) and waits at its next occurrence; * `EndStage pre d r` — the `end` pulse travels counterclockwise on the link `τ + r + 1 → τ + r`; non-holders whose occurrences are all at clockwise distance `≤ r` from `τ` are still in their data phase, those with all occurrences beyond `r` have completed the epoch, the others are in their `end` loop. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.FullyDefective.RobbinsSim variable {V : Type*} {L : ℕ} /-- Whether the node already
