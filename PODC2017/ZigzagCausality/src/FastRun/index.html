Fix a run `r` and a basic node `σ` of `r`. Given a timing `T` of the non-initial nodes of `past(r, σ)` (`FData`), we build a run `r' = FData.run` in which * every non-initial node `σ' ⇝_r σ` appears exactly at time `T σ'` (so `r' ∼_σ r`), and * every other node of process `j` is created no earlier than `ψ j` (`FData.psi`), the time of the auxiliary node `ψ_j` of the extended bounds graph `G_E(r, σ)` (Definition 16). `ψ j` is given in closed form: it is the longest path into `ψ_j` in `G_E(r, σ)` that starts at a boundary edge, `ψ j = max(1, max {T b + 1 − U(q)})` over non-initial past nodes `b` and channel paths `q` from `j` to the process of `b` (the edges `ψ_i → ψ_j` of weight `−U_ji` and `b → ψ_{b.1}` of weight `1`). Delivery in `r'` (Definition 24): 1. a message sent at a past node `u` and received in `r` at a past node `v` is delivered at `T v`; 2. a message whose sender time and channel are in the "slow" set `Sl` is delivered at its upper bound (used to delay the message chain of the first node of the precedence); 3. every other message is delivered at `max(t + L, ψ j)` (the earliest legal time after the auxiliary node). The conditions on `T` (`FData.Good`) are exactly the constraints of `G_E(r, σ)` that matter: `L`/`U` bounds of messages inside the past (`T_recv`), monotonicity along timelines (`T_mono`), and the `E'`–`E'''`–`E''` constraints of messages that leave the past (`T_esc`).
