It implies `Alg.ObstructionFree δ f Wr` for every `Wr`. Proof. Suppose a correct client `c` never returns. The potential `Ψ = cnt + 2|coverSet| + |q| + |rq| (+ 2|R c| while collecting for a write)` never increases while `c` is busy and bounds the number `cnt` of low-level operations triggered by `c`; hence `c` eventually stops triggering, then its pending operations eventually stop changing, so they are all on crashed servers (fairness). The next step of `c` then finds its collect complete (all registers of the correct servers responded) or `|wrSet| ≥ |R c| - f` (the registers of `R c` are on distinct servers), so it moves on — and since it cannot trigger anything, it can only return or enter the writing phase once, a contradiction. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.RegisterEmulationSpace.Alg2 open
