import Papers.PODC2017.MaxISLocalRatio.Basic /-! # Parallel weight reductions on an independent set (Section 2.1, Lemma 2.2) Let `U` be an independent set. Every `u ∈ U` reduces its weight `w(u)` from every vertex of its **closed** neighbourhood `N[u]` (including `u` itself, whose weight becomes `0`): * residual weights `w₂(v) = ∑_{u ∈ U ∩ N[v]} w(u)` (`red G U w`), * reduced weights `w₁ = w - w₂`. From a solution `x` for the reduced graph we get `x' = x ∪ {u ∈ U | N(u) ∩ x = ∅}` (`extend G U x`, equation (1) of the paper). Lemma 2.2: if `x` is `r`-approximate for `w₁` then `x'` is `r`-approximate for `w₁` and for `w₂`, hence (Theorem 2.1) for `w`. The approximation factor comes from a *local independence bound* (`LocalBound G U r`): every independent set meets every `N[u]`, `u ∈ U`, in at most `r` vertices. The paper uses `r = Δ` (`localBound_of_degree_le`, needs `Δ ≥ 1`) and, for matchings in Section 2.4, `r = 2` on the line graph (see `Matching.lean`). The paper writes `N(v)` for the closed neighbourhood here (`w₂[u] = w[u]` for `u ∈ U` and "at most `|N(u)| - 1`" only make sense for `N[u]`); see `differencies.md`. -/ set_option
