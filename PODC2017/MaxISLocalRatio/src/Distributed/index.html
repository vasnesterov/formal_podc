The `(Δ+1)`-colouring of line 1 is a black box: the colour is part of the local input (any proper colouring `c` with `c v < k`). A node state holds the identifier, the colour, a weight, the iteration at which the node became a *candidate* (it was a local colour maximum and sent `reduce(w(v))`), and the candidate's decision. A non-candidate with weight `≤ 0` is *removed* (`NotInIS`). * round `2i` ("select", line 18): a remaining node whose colour exceeds the colours of all its remaining neighbours becomes a candidate of level `i` and keeps its current weight as the amount it reduces (lines 19–21); * round `2i+1` ("reduce", lines 4–9): every non-candidate subtracts the amounts of its neighbours that became candidates of level `i`; * every round ("addition stage", lines 12–17): an undecided candidate of level `j` decides `NotInIS` if a neighbour that became a candidate later decided `InIS`, and `InIS` if all its neighbours are removed, earlier candidates, or later candidates that decided `NotInIS`.
