Theorem 4.1 claims `2^{x+1} Δ` colours for `t = Δ^{1/(x+1)}`. We check the arithmetic for the integer choice `t = ⌊Δ^{1/(x+1)}⌋` (any `t ≥ 1` with `t^{x+1} ≤ Δ`): `(2t-1)^x · (2⌈Δ/t^x⌉ - 1) ≤ 2^{x+1} Δ`, without the extra colour-reduction round the paper uses for `x = 1`.
