import Papers.PODC2017.DeltaEdgeColoring.Basic /-! # CD-Coloring (Algorithm 1): Theorems 2.4, 2.5, 2.6 and 3.2 Algorithm 1 (`CD-Coloring(G, D, S, t, x)`) recursively computes a connector, colours it with `D(t-1)+1` colours (black box [17]), and recurses on the colour classes in parallel; after `x` levels it colours the remaining classes directly with `D(⌈S/t^x⌉-1)+1` colours and returns the tuple of all colours. We unfold the recursion level by level: `stages … j` is the state after `j` recursion levels (the union of all current subgraphs, their clique cover, the clique-size bound, and the index of the current subgraph of every vertex, i.e. the tuple of connector colours so far, encoded in mixed radix). Running all recursive calls "in parallel" on the colour classes is the same as one call on the disjoint union `sameColor G φ` of the classes. The final colour of `v` is the pair `⟨index, ψ v⟩`, encoded as `index · B + ψ v`. The parameter `t` may depend on the recursion level and on the current clique size (`τ j S`); Algorithm 1 uses a constant `t` (`cdColoring`), the algorithms `𝒜_x` of the proof of Theorem 3.2 use `t = ⌊S^{1/(x+1)}⌋` at every level (`algA`). ## Main results * `theorem_2_4`: after `x` levels with parameter `t`, the subgraph indices form a `((D(t-1)+1)^x, ⌈S/t^x⌉)`-clique-decomposition (in the paper's form `((tD)^x, S/t^x + 2)`). * `theorem_2_5`: the resulting colouring is proper. * `theorem_2_6`: it uses fewer than `(D(t-1)+1)^x · (D(⌈S/t^x⌉-1)+1) ≤ (tD)^x (S/t^x + 2) D` colours. * `theorem_3_2`: for `D ≥ 2` and parameters `t` with `t² ≤ S` (in particular the paper's `t = ⌊S^{1/(x+1)}⌋`, `algA`), the colouring uses fewer than `D^{x+1} S` colours, without any colour reduction. * `paper_inductive_step_fails`: the inequality used in the paper's inductive step for `𝒜_2` (`(D(t-1)+1) · D^{i+1} ⌈S/t⌉ ≤ D^{i+2} S`) is false for `D = 2`, `S = 31`, `t = ⌊√31⌋ = 5`. -/ open Finset
