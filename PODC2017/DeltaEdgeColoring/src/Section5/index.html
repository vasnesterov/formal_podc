import Papers.PODC2017.DeltaEdgeColoring.EdgeSchedule /-! # Lemma 5.1 and Theorem 5.2: `(Δ + O(a))`-edge-colouring via an H-partition **Lemma 5.1.** `V = A ∪ B` (disjoint), vertices of `A` have degree `≤ d`, `G(A)` and `G(B)` are already properly edge-coloured. Every `a ∈ A` labels its edges to `B` injectively by `0, …, d-1`; in round `i` every `b ∈ B` colours its edges of label `i`, choosing distinct colours `< Δ + d - 1` that avoid the colours of all adjacent edges coloured so far. **Theorem 5.2.** Given an H-partition `H_0, …, H_{ℓ-1}` of degree `d` (every vertex has at most `d` neighbours in its own or later sets) and proper edge colourings of the sets `G(H_i)`, process the sets from `H_{ℓ-1}` back to `H_0`; in stage `i` colour the edges between `H_i` and later sets by the procedure of Lemma 5.1 (`A = H_i`). Globally, the edge `ab` with `h a < h b` is coloured in round `(ℓ - 1 - h a) · d + lab a b` by its endpoint `b`. ## Main results * `lemma_5_1`, `theorem_5_2`: every run (every colouring following the round rule) is a proper edge colouring with colours `< max k₀ (Δ + d - 1)`, and runs exist (the rule can always be followed). * `lemma_5_1_parallel`, `theorem_5_2_parallel`: two adjacent edges coloured in the same round share the endpoint that chooses their colours (the `B`-endpoint / the endpoint in the later H-set), so the choices made in one round by different vertices never conflict. * `lemma_5_1_rounds`, `theorem_5_2_rounds`: `d` rounds, resp. `ℓ · d` rounds. * `theorem_5_2_colors`: with the H-sets coloured greedily (`2d - 1` colours, `exists_hSet_coloring`) the result uses at most
