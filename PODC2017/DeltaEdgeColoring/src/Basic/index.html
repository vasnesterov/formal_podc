import Mathlib /-! # Clique covers and connectors (Section 2 of Barenboim–Elkin–Maimon, PODC 2017) The paper works with a graph `G` of *diversity* `D` (every vertex lies in at most `D` maximal cliques, or more generally in at most `D` cliques of a "consistent clique identification") and maximal clique size `S`. We model the identified cliques as a **clique cover** `𝒬`: a finite family of cliques of `G` covering every edge, every vertex lying in at most `D` members, every member of size at most `S` (`IsCliqueCover`). The family of all maximal cliques is one (`maximalCliques`). The **connector** `G'` (Section 2) splits every clique `Q ∈ 𝒬` into consecutive blocks of `t` vertices (by identifier) and joins two vertices iff they lie in the same block of some `Q`. ## Main results * `ncard_neighborSet_le_of_isCliqueCover`: a clique cover gives max degree `≤ D · (S - 1)`. * `connector_degree_le` (**Lemma 2.1**): the connector has max degree `≤ D · (t - 1)`. * `isCliqueCover_refine` (**Lemmas 2.2 and 2.3**): for a proper colouring `φ` of the connector, the colour-class subgraphs `G_i` (all together: `sameColor G φ`) have the clique cover `refine 𝒬 φ` with diversity `≤ D` and clique size `≤ ⌈S/t⌉`; hence max degree `≤ D · (⌈S/t⌉ - 1)` (`lemma_2_2`). * `diversity_sameColor_le` (**Lemma 2.3 (ii)** for maximal cliques): the diversity of an induced subgraph is at most the diversity of the graph. -/ open Finset namespace Podc.DeltaEdgeColoring set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false variable {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] /-!
