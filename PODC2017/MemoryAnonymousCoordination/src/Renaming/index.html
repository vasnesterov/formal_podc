The paper's proof of uniqueness (Theorem 5.2) is the same one-paragraph argument as for consensus: "each one of the other `n − 1` processes might write into one of the registers overwriting a value", ignoring non-atomic collects, stale pending writes, processes of other rounds and catch-up. It is repaired by a per-round lock (`RenamingLock.lean`) whose bad records are those of a round `≤ r` other than the winner's, and which records that every record of a later round names the round-`r` winner in its history; the ghost invariant for collects and the well-formedness of histories are in `RenamingSafety.lean`.
