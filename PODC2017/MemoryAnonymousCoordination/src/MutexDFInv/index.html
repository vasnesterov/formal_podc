`own1`, `own2`: a process in the remainder, waiting (lines 6–8) or returning from `unlock()` owns no register (no register holds its identifier); during the loops of lines 5 and 12 it owns no register at the positions already passed; * `c0`: during a collect, a register at a position already read holds the own identifier only if it was read as such (count version); * `z`: if both processes are *active* (lines 2–3), no register holding `0` has been *covered* by both (passed by the current scan of line 2, or by both scans if collecting); * `cc`: if both processes are collecting, `pot a + pot b ≥ m`, where `pot` is the number of own values read so far plus the number of own registers not read yet; * `phi`: if `a` is *passive* (lines 5–8), the registers owned by `a` or about to be erased by `a`, plus the `0`s already passed by a scanning `b`, are fewer than `⌈m/2⌉`; * `R`: if `a` is passive and `b` collects, the collect of `b` is guaranteed to see at least `⌈m/2⌉` own values: `b` cannot give up while `a` is passive; * `nbp`: the processes are never both passive. -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.MemoryAnonymousCoordination.Mutex open Cslib.SharedMemory
