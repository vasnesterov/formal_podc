Lemma 13 of the paper: `s(G₁) = s(G₂)` for any two `n × n` grids (identifier assignments) when `T(n) < n/4`. We prove it (`lemma13`) for an algorithm with `T` rounds that properly 3-colours every torus with identifiers in `[0, B)`, `B ≥ 3n²` (room for fresh identifiers), under `4(T + 2) ≤ n`: the label `ℓ(v)` reads the colours of the `3 × 3` block around `v`, so it depends on the identifiers within distance `T + 2`; the paper hides this additive constant in its convention that checking an LCL of radius `1` costs `O(1)` extra rounds. The proof is the paper's: replace the identifiers of the lower half of the torus by the same fresh identifiers in both instances; a row in the upper half sees no change, a row in the lower half sees
