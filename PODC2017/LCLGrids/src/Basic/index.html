import Mathlib /-! # 3-colourings of the toroidal grid and the auxiliary graph `H` Section 9 of Brandt et al., *LCL problems on grids* (PODC 2017). Nodes of the toroidal `n × n` grid are `Pos n = ZMod n × ZMod n`; `sh v a b` is the node at offset `(a, b)` from `v` (`a` to the east, `b` to the north). A colouring is `c : Pos n → Col` with the three colours `1, 2, 3` of the paper. Given a colouring, the paper builds a directed graph `H` on the colour-3 nodes: two colour-3 nodes `u, v` sharing two neighbours `w, w'` with `c w = 1`, `c w' = 2` are joined by an edge, directed so that the colour-1 common neighbour is on the *left* of the edge. On a torus with `n ≥ 5` two nodes share two neighbours iff they are diagonal neighbours, so the edges of `H` are diagonal steps `d : Diag`; the common neighbours of `u` and `u + d` are `u + d.left` (obtained from `d` by a 45° counter-clockwise rotation) and `u + d.right`. This gives `HEdge c u d`. The label `ell c v ∈ {-1, 0, 1}` of Lemma 14 is `1` if `v` is a northbound intersection (it has in- and out-degree `1` in `H`, the in-neighbour is on the row below and the out-neighbour on the row above), `-1` if it is southbound, and `0` otherwise. ## Main results * `indeg_eq_outdeg`: in a proper colouring every node has equal in- and out-degree in `H`; * `ell_eq`: `ell c v = inBelow c v - outBelow c v` (the in-edges from the row below minus the out-edges to the row below); * `ell_ne_zero_iff`: a colour-3 node has `ell ≠ 0` iff its west and east neighbours have different colours.
