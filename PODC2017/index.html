74

Brandt, Hirvonen, Korhonen, Lempiäinen, Östergård, Purcell, Rybicki, Suomela, Uznański — LCL Problems on Grids

✏️ Slip. Only slips: Theorem 22's third case wrongly says no solution exists ({0,3,4} is still global), Theorem 21 fails at n = 1. 3-colouring bound proved; classification and undecidability not checked.

74

Crooks, Pu, Alvisi, Clement — Seeing is Believing: A Client-Centric Specification of Database Isolation

✏️ Slip. State-based definitions match Adya's for SER, SI, RC, RU and PSI once the model's wording is read as committed-only; the comparisons of Theorems 5-9 were not checked.

39

Bar-Yehuda, Censor-Hillel, Ghaffari, Schwartzman — Distributed Approximation of Maximum Independent Set and Maximum Matching

✏️ Slip. Deterministic core holds with slips: line 11 of Algorithm 1 overwrites instead of summing, N(v) must be closed, Δ ≥ 1. Randomized results, the CONGEST line-graph simulation and Section 3 were not checked.

28

Taubenfeld — Coordination Without Prior Agreement

🛠️ Hard fix. All checked theorems hold, but the proofs of mutex deadlock-freedom and of renaming uniqueness are wrong and needed new arguments. Figure 2 breaks on input 0; a local change fixes it.

19

Barenboim, Elkin, Maimon — Deterministic Distributed (Δ + o(Δ))-Edge-Coloring, and Vertex-Coloring of Graphs with Bounded Diversity

⚠️ Weakened. Colour counts hold, including Δ + o(Δ) for a = o(Δ), after an easy repair of Theorem 3.2; Theorem 5.4's final step fails as cited, leaving the polylog claim unsupported. Running times unchecked.

17

Censor-Hillel, Haeupler, Hershkowitz, Zuzic — Broadcasting in Noisy Radio Networks

🛠️ Hard fix. All checked results hold, but the routing lower bound of Lemma 15 needed a new proof. The broadcast algorithms, the worst-case gap and Lemma 26 (noticed gap, undecided) were not checked.

13

Blondin, Esparza, Jaax, Meyer — Towards Efficient Verification of Population Protocols

🔧 Easy fix. WS³ soundness and the threshold protocol hold; Proposition 18's proof relies on a false monotonicity claim and misses cases, repaired by a lexicographic ranking. Complexity results and expressiveness were not checked.

8

Kulkarni, Vaidya — Effectiveness of Delaying Timestamp Computation

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 1 (inline timestamps of 2c + 2 integers) fails with non-FIFO channels because next[j] uses the first message sent, not the earliest receive; taking the least receive index fixes it. Lemmas 1–4, the n − 1 star algorithm and Theorem 2 hold; Lemma 2's proof needed a local repair.

7

Dan, Manohar, Moses — On Using Time Without Clocks via Zigzag Causality

⚠️ Weakened. The Visible Zigzag Theorem's only-if direction is false for general nodes; it holds with one endpoint a basic node of the past, which covers both coordination tasks. Theorems 1 and 2 stand.

5

Dolev, Erdmann, Lutz, Schapira, Zair — Stateless Computation

🔧 Easy fix. Theorem 3.1 and the label lower bounds hold after local proof repairs; the hardness of verifying stabilization and the L/poly and P/poly results were not checked.

4

Chockler, Spiegelman — Space Complexity of Fault-Tolerant Register Emulations

🔧 Easy fix. Register lower and upper bounds hold after local proof repairs; Theorem 4 needs a condition on the value domain, Theorem 8 a change of quantifiers, and Algorithm 2's mapping has an off-by-one.

2

Mohsen Lesani — Brief Announcement: Fence Insertion for Straight-line Programs is in P

✏️ Slip. Independence of fence types (Lemma 2.1), the reduction to chain multi-cut (Lemma 2.4) and optimality of the greedy Algorithm 1 all hold as proved; only the O(n log n) bound (really O(|T| log |T|)) and loop terminal pairs (v, v) are off; running time is not formalized.

1

Afek, Aspnes, Cohen, Vainstein — Brief Announcement: Object Oriented Consensus

⚠️ Weakened. The VAC + reconciliator template and Ben-Or's VAC hold after local fixes (decided processes must keep participating; probability-1 termination only in its deterministic step), but the Phase-King decomposition into adopt-commit + conciliator breaks agreement for n = 4, t = 1 (a Byzantine king makes the conciliator invalid) and is saved only by Phase-King's own rule of deciding after t + 1 kings; Raft not checked.
