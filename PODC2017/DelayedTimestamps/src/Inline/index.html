processes in `C` increment their own entry, processes outside `C` only merge); * an event `e` of a process `p_i ∉ C` additionally gets `id = p_i`, `index = index(e)` and a vector `next` indexed by by the query time `t`, and `∞` otherwise (Sections 4.1–4.2: the query waits until this index is known). `query ex t e` is `Q_t(e)`; `InlineTS.lt` is the order `<` of Section 4.4 (cases (i)–(iv)). * `theorem_1`: Theorem 1 holds for **FIFO** channels. * `theorem_1_false`: without FIFO channels (the paper only assumes reliable channels) Theorem 1 fails: `p_1 ∉ C` sends two messages to `p_0 ∈ C`
