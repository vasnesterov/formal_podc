Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Towards Efficient Verification of Population Protocols — PODC 2017.

Paper: Blondin, Esparza, Jaax, Meyer, Towards Efficient Verification of Population Protocols, PODC 2017 (arXiv 1703.04367v3).

Model and definitions

  1. Configurations. The paper uses Pop(Q), multisets with at least 2 agents. We use all of Multiset Q. The bound |C| ≥ 2 is kept for inputs (MProtocol.IsInitial, WellSpecified: 2 ≤ card X). In Termination, Silence and Definition 4, configurations with fewer than 2 agents are terminal and start no execution, so the definitions are equivalent.

  2. Transitions are a Set (Transition Q) of quadruples (p, q, p', q'), with totality as a field of MProtocol. Transition.Silent is pre = post, as in the paper. P[S] (MProtocol.restrict) adds the identity transitions (p, q, p, q).

  3. Executions and fairness. An execution is a CSLib TransitionSystem.Run of the step relation. Fairness is the paper's edge fairness (Run.EdgeFair). "Silent execution" is Run.Silent (eventually constant).

  4. Ordered partitions are lists [T₁, …, Tₙ] (IsOrderedPartition: nonempty parts, pairwise disjoint, union T). T₁ ∪ ⋯ ∪ Tᵢ₋₁ is layersBelow L i.

  5. Potential reachability (Def. 12). x : Transition Q → ℕ must be supported on T. The flow equations are over ℤ. Traps and siphons are Finsets of states. "U-trap" is IsUTrap U R (R• ∩ U ⊆ •R), "U-siphon" is IsUSiphon U R. The constraints are stated, as in Def. 12, for U = ⟦x⟧.

  6. Termination, Consensus, StrongConsensus are transcribed literally. The consensus output is IsConsensus O C b (∀ q ∈ C, O q = b).

Statements

  1. Proposition 19 is proved as the paper's stronger form. (a) is MProtocol.silent_iff_termination, (b) is MProtocol.wellSpecified_iff_consensus. The paper's "(a ⇒) follows immediately" needs the existence of fair executions. For edge fairness this is exists_edgeFair: CSLib's round-robin globally fair run, applied to the system whose states are the transitions.

  2. Proposition 20: as stated.

  3. StrongConsensus ⇒ Consensus, WS³ ⊆ WS² ⊆ WS: as stated (§4.3). Observation 11 is proved for firing sequences (FiresSeq.trap, FiresSeq.siphon).

  4. Lemma 21. The paper's statement indexes x by states ("x(q) ≥ 0 for every q ∈ Q, x(q) > 0 for some q"); it must be by transitions. We take x : Transition Q → ℚ with support in the non-silent transitions of T, x ≥ 0, x ≠ 0, and ∑ₜ x(t) · (post(t)(q) − pre(t)(q)) = 0 for all q. lemma_21_set holds for any set of transitions (no totality needed); lemma_21 is the protocol version.

  5. Lemma 22 is stated for arbitrary sets S (the steps) and U (the dead transitions) as ¬ PreservesDead S U ↔ ∃ s ∈ S, ∃ u ∈ U non-silent, ∀ non-silent u' ∈ U, pre(u') ≰ pre(s) + (pre(u) ⊖ post(s)). The paper fixes S = T \ U, but Prop. 7 applies it to P[Tᵢ]. The polynomial-time claim is not formalized.

  6. P_thr.

    • The paper says T contains the transitions of leader initiators and does not specify the other pairs, although the model requires totality. We add the identity transitions for pairs whose first state is a non-leader (as in Example 1). They are silent, so reachability, potential reachability, terminal configurations and (up to stuttering) fair executions are unchanged.

    • We prove everything for any bound v ≥ |c| + 1 with all |aᵢ| ≤ v (Threshold.protocol). The paper's vmax = max(|a₁|, …, |a_k|, |c| + 1) is Threshold.Pthr (Finset.fold max).

    • The input alphabet is any type Λ (a Fintype for vmax), not {x₁, …, x_k} with k ≥ 1.

    • Leader, opinion are Bools; values are Set.Icc (-v) v.

  7. Proposition 23: as stated (flowEq_val), for x supported on the transitions of P_thr.

  8. Proposition 24 is proved in the stronger form consensus_of_terminal: a terminal configuration with a leader and at least 2 agents is a consensus whose output is val(C) < c. The paper's two claims follow. The proof uses a second agent (to force the leader's opinion to match its value); in the paper this is |C| ≥ 2 from C ∈ Pop(Q). Since our configurations are arbitrary multisets, we add 2 ≤ card C as a hypothesis and discharge it in Prop. 17 by FlowEq.card_eq (the flow equations preserve the population size).

  9. Proposition 17: as stated (proposition_17, and proposition_17_Pthr for vmax). The explicit output val(I(X)) < c is consensus_of_potReach. In the trap argument for "C' contains a leader", the case where no transition of ⟦x⟧ moves an agent into the leaders is handled by the flow equations (exists_leader_of_potReachVia). The paper does not cover this case; for its literal P_thr (every listed transition moves an agent into the leaders) it is only x = 0, for ours also x supported on the added silent non-leader transitions.

  10. Proposition 18.

    • The paper gives the partition T₁ = {t : pre(t) ≠ ⦃q, r⦄, q ∈ L₀, r ∈ N₁}, T₂ = T \ T₁ for c > 0, and a symmetric one for c ≤ 0. We prove that the first partition works for every c (proposition_18_with).

    • Bounded model checking (v ≤ 3, up to 3 agents) indicates that the c ≤ 0 partition also works for every c. It is not formalized.

    • Silence of P_thr[T₁] uses a new proof: a lexicographic ranking (number of leaders, ∑ |values| of non-leaders, number of leaders with inconsistent opinion, number of "flippable" non-leaders). The paper's argument has a false step (a₀ ≥ a₁ ≥ ⋯, refuted by nonleaderAbs_not_antitone) and misses the saturated transitions (saturated_flip).

    • Silence of P_thr[T₂] (t2_silent) and T₁-deadness of P_thr[T₂] (t2_dead) follow the paper.

  11. Bonus: pthr_ws3 (P_thr ∈ WS³) and pthr_computes: every fair execution of P_thr from I(X) stabilizes to ∑ a(i) X(i) < c, i.e. P_thr computes the threshold predicate. The paper only claims membership in WS³.

Not formalized

  1. The complexity results: Prop. 3 (EXPSPACE-hardness), Props. 6, 7 (polynomial-time checks), Cor. 8, Prop. 15 (coNP), Thm. 16 (DP). The polynomial-time claims of Lemma 22 and the maximal trap/siphon remark of §4.2.

  2. The remainder protocol (Props. 25, 26), closure under negation and conjunction (Props. 27–29), and hence "WS³ is as expressive as WS". The experiments (§6).

Typos (no formal consequence)

  1. Proof of Prop. 18: "every execution of P_thr[T₁] is fair" means "silent". "such transitions set the opinion of non leaders to 0" should be "to 1".

  2. Observation 11: "if C(S) = 0" should read "C(P) = 0".
