Lemma 6: two strictly increasing sequences `z, z'` with `∀ x ∈ X, ∃ n, z_n = x` and `∀ x ∈ X, ∃ n', z'_{n'} = x` are equal. Read literally (sequences indexed by `ℕ`; only `X ⊆ range z` required), both are false: * Lemma 5 fails for every finite nonempty `X` (a strictly increasing `ℕ`-indexed sequence of elements of `X` would be injective) — `lemma5_literal_false`; * Lemma 6 fails because nothing forces `z` and `z'` to stay inside `X` — `lemma6_literal_false`.
