Unlike the state-broadcast form of `KingConsensus.lean`, in every round a correct node `u` computes, from its local state (inputs and all vectors received so far), a message `m u w : Option Msg` *for every receiver `w`*; `none` means that nothing is sent. A correct receiver `v` sees a vector `r v : Fin n → Option Msg` with `r v u = m u v` for correct `u` (`Consistent`), and arbitrary entries for faulty `u` (faulty nodes may send anything, including `nack`/`runGC` where a value is expected, or nothing). "Received `k` times `x`" counts distinct senders. An execution (`WGARun`, `GKCRun`, `KC8Run`) is the inputs of all nodes together with the received vectors of all rounds, subject to consistency for every round; the outputs of correct nodes are explicit functions of their inputs and received vectors. Messages are `Msg.val x` (a value), `Msg.nack` (Algorithm 2, line 3) and `Msg.runGC` (Algorithm 4, line 2). "Break ties arbitrarily" (Algorithm 2 line 5, Algorithm 3 line 8) is resolved by taking the value of the smallest sender whose value was received more than `t` times (`bigVal`). In Algorithm 2 "messages different from `x_v`" are received messages (not `⊥`) other than `val x_v`; a `nack` counts as different. In Algorithm 4 line 8, "received `z_{ℓ_v}` from `ℓ_v`" means receiving a value message from `ℓ_v`. ## Main results * `wga_silent`, `WGARun.spec` — Lemma 6.2 (`WGASpec` = Definition 6.1). * `GKCRun.spec`, `GKCRun.sparse` — Lemma 6.4 (`GKCSpec` = Definition 6.3, communication). * `KC8Run.spec`, `KC8Run.sparse`, `KC8Run.card_sent_le` — Theorem 6.5 (`KCSpec` = Definition 2.6, communication: under the hypothesis of the theorem every message of a correct node in each of the 8 rounds is sent by `ℓ`, to `ℓ`, or to a faulty node, hence at most `n (|F| + 2)` messages per round).
