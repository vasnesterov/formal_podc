It re-pairs its ports as `(b₀, a₁), (b₁, a₂), …, (b_k, a₀)` (`Pairing.repair`), leaving all other pairs (at `v` and elsewhere) unchanged. `lemma4` shows: in the new pairing two darts are on the same circuit iff they were on the same circuit before, or both were on one of `C₀, …, C_k`. So `C₀, …, C_k` are merged into a single circuit (whose edge set is their union, `lemma4_edges`), and every other circuit is unchanged. Only node `v` changes its pairing (`repair_pair_of_fst_ne`). The re-pairing itself only needs the `2(k+1)` darts to be distinct (`Pairing.repair`); the distinctness of the circuits is used only for the merging statement.
