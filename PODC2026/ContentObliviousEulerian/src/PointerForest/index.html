We have a finite set `α` of *circuits* (vertices of a contracted graph), a symmetric irreflexive adjacency `adj` in which every vertex has a neighbour, and injective IDs `id`. Every vertex `x` points to its neighbour `ptr x` of maximal ID. A vertex `x` is a *root* if `x` and `ptr x` point to each other and `x` is the larger of the two. Every non-root merges along the link `x — ptr x`.
