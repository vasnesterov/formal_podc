import Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.Dimension.Constructions import Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.Dimension.Finite import Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.FiniteDimensional.Defs import Mathlib.Basic.Real.Basic /-! # Circulations on the fractional entries of a matrix with integer line sums Let `α : ι → κ → ℝ` be a real matrix (indexed by finite types) all of whose row sums and column sums are integers, and suppose some entry of `α` is not an integer. Then: * `exists_circulation`: there is a nonzero matrix `Δ` with zero row and column sums whose support consists of non-integral entries of `α`; * `exists_cycle_direction`: `Δ` can moreover be chosen with at most two nonzero entries in every row and every column (so `Δ` is supported on a cycle of the bipartite row/column graph, with alternating signs `±d` along each row and column). These are the "cycle" steps of dependent rounding (Gandhi–Khuller–Parthasarathy–Srinivasan, JACM 2006) and of the proof that the Birkhoff polytope is integral. The proof is by linear algebra instead of explicit alternating walks: a nonempty set `T` of entries with `#T ≥ #rows(T) + #cols(T)` carries a nonzero matrix with zero line sums (`exists_zeroLineSums_of_card`, a dimension count), and a nonzero such matrix of minimal support has at most two nonzero entries per line. -/ -- CSLib candidate (the whole file) namespace
