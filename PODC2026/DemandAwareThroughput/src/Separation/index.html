The proof follows the paper. With `ε = 5/8 - κ` (the paper writes `ε = κ - 5/8 > 0`, a sign typo) and `b = (n-1)·M`, the fractional parts `b' = b - ⌊b⌋` have integer row and column sums and are rounded by dependent rounding (Theorem 3, the hypothesis `DependentRounding`). The graph has `⌊b_ij⌋ + β_ij + 1` arcs from `i` to `j` (`β` the rounding). Stage 1 sends `b_ij` directly; the excess capacity `η_ij = (1 - b'_ij) + b'_ij β_ij` has expected row/column sums `≥ 3n/4`, and by Theorem 4 (proved in `Chernoff.lean`) and a union bound some rounding has all row and column excess sums `> (3/4 - ε)n`. Stage 2 routes the remaining demand `σ_ij` over two-hop paths `i → h → j` proportionally to `min(η_ih, η_hj)`.
