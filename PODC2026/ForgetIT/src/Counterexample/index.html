import Papers.PODC2026.ForgetIT.Protocol /-! # Robust good-case latency is not `3δ` (Theorem 3, first item) The paper defines (Section 3) the *robust good-case latency* `T_rgc` as the worst-case decision latency, measured from GST, over all executions in which **GST coincides with a correct leader entering its view**, and claims `T_rgc = 3δ` for Forget-IT (Theorem 1, Theorem 3). We exhibit such an execution in which the correct processes decide only `14` ticks after GST, where one message delay after GST is one tick (`δ = 1` in the paper's time) and `Δ = 2` ticks. Setting: `n = 4`, `f = 1`, processes `0, 1, 2, 3`, round-robin leaders `leader(V) = V - 1 mod 4`; process `0` (leader of view 1) is Byzantine, process `1` (leader of view 2) is correct. 1. Before GST every message to process `1` from another process is delayed until GST. Process `0` never sends a `prepare`; it sends `echo-1/2/3` for view 1 with value `SAFE` to processes `2` and `3` (and nothing else, ever). 2. Processes `1, 2, 3` time out in view 1 and broadcast `vote-2`; processes `2`, `3` complete the view change of view 1 with the Byzantine echoes and enter view 2 (tick 11). 3. In view 2 the leader `1` is absent, so processes `2` and `3` time out: at tick `19` they have started the view change of view 2 (`vc[2] = true`), after which they never send `vote-1` in view 2 (line 37). 4. At GST = 21 process `1` receives the delayed messages, among them `f + 1` `echo-3` messages for view 1, and enters view 2 (line 80) — GST coincides with a correct leader entering its view. Its proposal cannot gather `2f + 1` `vote-1` messages, so view 2 decides nothing; the view change of view 2 waits for process `1`'s own timeout, and the processes decide only in view 3, at tick 35 = GST + 14. The run is a valid run of CSLib's partially synchronous model (`Run.Valid 1 0`: correct-to-correct messages are delivered by `max(τ, GST)`, i.e. one tick per hop in the paper's time, since a message produced in the step at `τ` is sent at `τ + 1`). All facts are checked by evaluating the run with `decide +kernel` through CSLib's memoised simulator.
