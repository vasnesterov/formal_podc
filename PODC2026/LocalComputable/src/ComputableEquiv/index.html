Proof (simpler than the paper's): for a fixed `N`, there are finitely many pairs (view, radius) that can be consulted in legal networks before the deadline `T(n)`. A *table* assigning outputs to such pairs is a finite object, and whether a table solves the problem on all legal networks is decidable. The restriction of the given algorithm `A_N` to these pairs is such a table, so an unbounded search over (codes of) tables terminates. The computable algorithm computes the first good table for its `N` and looks up its own view in it. This avoids the paper's maximally safe neighbourhoods altogether (cf. `differencies.md`). -/ namespace LocalComputable open Computable Encodable variable {Net View Out : Type*} /-- Lists: `findSome?`
