`orderStat_step` (**Lemma 4.2**, distributional form): `P[X'_(i) = x] = f_i 1[X_(i-1) = x] + g_i 1[X_(i) = x] + h_i 1[X_(i+1) = x]`; * `orderStat_ancestry` (**Lemma 4.4**): `P[X^t_(i) = x] = ∑_j (Pᵗ)_{j,i} 1[X^0_(j) = x]`, where `(Pᵗ)_{j,i} = ancestry f h t i j` is the `t`-step kernel of the birth–death chain on ranks (`i → i-1` w.p. `f_i`, `i → i+1` w.p. `h_i`); for distinct initial opinions
