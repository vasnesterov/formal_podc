Both hold for *every* movement model in which the adversary may let a robot reach its target, in particular for rigid movement and for non-rigid movement with any minimum distance `δ`. Proof of Theorem 4 (much simpler than the sketch in the paper). Gathering must end with a lone robot jumping onto the point of the other two; normalizing its view, some view `{0, w}` is answered by jumping onto the other point (`exists_fixed_view`), and the adversary can make every two-point view look like `{0, w}`. Also, since robots in a distinct configuration must eventually collide, some robot `x` in some distinct configuration `C` jumps onto another robot `y` (`exists_merge`).
