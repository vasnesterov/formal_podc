import Papers.PODC2026.RankingOpinions.TieShare /-! # Circles_TieSharing: modulo ranges, sectors and arcs Geometry used in the proof of Theorem 4.2. Fix a set `S` of colors with at least two elements (`S` is the set `M` of colors in relative majority, or, for the unique-majority case, the smallest greedy independent set with at least two elements). The colors are split into *sectors* `[μ, nxt S μ]_k` for `μ ∈ S`. A braket `⟨g|h⟩` is an `S`-arc if `g ≠ h` and no element of `S` lies strictly inside `[g, h]_k` (Claim 2 of the paper: after stabilization every braket is such an arc). An arc lies in the sector of `secOf S g` (the element of `S` closest to `g` counterclockwise), at offsets `off ≤ en ≤ secLen` (`arc_en_le`).
