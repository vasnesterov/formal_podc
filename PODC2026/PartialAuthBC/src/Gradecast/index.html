A message is `(kind, chain)` where `kind` is the phase in which it is sent (`0` = Extension, `1`/`2`/`3` = Echo1/2/3), and a chain is the list of its signers, sender first. The signature tokens of a message are the nonempty prefixes of its chain whose last signer is registered (prefix `τ` is the signature of `τ.getLast` on the chain before it). A chain `c` is a *valid `k`-chain for `i`* if it has length `k`, starts with the sender, has distinct signers, all registered, and does not contain `i` (Definition 3.1). **Reading of Phase 2.** For an unregistered party receiving a valid Extension we use the *sequential* reading of the two bullets of Figure 1 ("if `v = 0` set `v := 1`; if (now) `v = 1` and `g = 0` set `g := 1`"), i.e. the party ends with `(v, g) = (1, 1)` and echoes the chain if anything changed.
