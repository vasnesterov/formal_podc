Byzantine timestamps are unconstrained. * `supp ts θ tx tx' = I_θ(tx, tx') = {i | ts i tx + θ ≤ ts i tx'}` and `Prec ts θ φ tx tx'` (`tx ≺_{θ,φ} tx'`) iff `|I_θ(tx, tx')| ≥ φ n`. * The `(θ, φ)`-AOF graph is the relation `Prec ts θ φ`; it has a directed cycle iff `HasCycle`.
