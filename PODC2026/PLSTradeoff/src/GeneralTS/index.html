Every node then runs `FindMyCluster` (`fmc`) in its localized view, reconstructs the clusters near it, and checks for its own cluster the weak diameter, the cost ratio `1/t` and two separation at itself. `tsCertGeneral` is the resulting `(40K + 32)`-PLS certifying and outputting a `(16K + 12, 1/t)`-TS partition on connected graphs, with `K = t⌈log₂ n⌉`, of cost `2 cS + 7` where `cS` is the cost of the sharing scheme. Deviation: the reconstructed cluster of `u` only contains nodes within distance `2ρ` of `u` (`ρ` the largest radius); this makes the verifier local without changing the clusters. -/ namespace
