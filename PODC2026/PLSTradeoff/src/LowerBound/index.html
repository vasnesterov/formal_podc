Fischer, PODC 2026): there are graphs in which every `t`-ball contains a constant fraction of all nodes, a predicate with a `1`-PLS of cost `O(m)`, but every `t`-PLS for it has cost `Ω(m / t)`. ## The construction `G t m` is the layered graph `G_{t,m}` of the paper: `2t + 3` layers, the odd (paper) layers `1, 3, …, 2t + 3` consist of a single node, the even layers of `m` nodes, and consecutive layers are joined by complete bipartite graphs. Vertices are `LV t m = Fin (t + 2) ⊕ (Fin (t + 1) × Fin m)`: `Sum.inl k` is the single node of paper layer `2k + 1`, `Sum.inr (k, i)` is node `i` of paper layer `2k + 2`. The function `lay` is the paper layer minus one; two nodes are adjacent iff their layers differ by exactly one. Inputs are `m × m` bit matrices (`Inp m = Fin m → Fin m → Bool`, the paper's `{0,1}^{m²}`), and the predicate `P t m` says that the two end nodes `vfirst = v¹` and `vlast = v^{2t+3}` have equal inputs (inputs of all other nodes are ignored).
