import Papers.PODC2026.PLSTradeoff.Tradeoff /-! # Certifying and outputting a TS partition with `O(log n)` bits (Lemma 4.5) The honest prover gives every node the identifier of its cluster (`⌈log₂ n⌉` bits, fixed width) and one bit telling whether it lies in `X`. A node `v` sees radius `3t+2`; it reconstructs `C̃_u = {w : d(u, w) ≤ 2t, CompID(w) = CompID(u)}` for the nodes `u` near it, outputs these sets and the marked nodes, and checks for its own cluster `C̃_v` the weak diameter `≤ t`, the cost ratio `≤ ε`, and two separation at `v` (no unmarked node of another cluster within distance 2 of an unmarked `v`). Compared with the paper (Appendix A): the paper searches clusters within radius `r = 3t + 2` and claims an `r`-PLS, but a node then needs to see `B_r(u)` for `u` at distance up to `r + 2`, i.e. radius about `2r + 2`. Searching within radius `2t` suffices (`Ĉ_u = Ĉ_v` for `u ∈ Ĉ_v` only needs the two diameter checks), and then radius `3t + 2` is indeed enough. -/ namespace PLSTradeoff open Cslib.BitEncoding open
