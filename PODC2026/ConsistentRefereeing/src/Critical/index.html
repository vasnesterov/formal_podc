The simple cases of the proof of Theorem 5: * `Crit.invisible` — a step that does not change the memory (an invocation, a response, a read, or any primitive leaving its object unchanged): `α·i·j` and `α·j` agree on everybody but `i`; * `Crit.distinct` — steps on distinct objects commute; * `Crit.interfering` — steps on the same interfering object commute or one overwrites the other. -/ set_option
