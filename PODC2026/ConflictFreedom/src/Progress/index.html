We work in CSLib's asynchronous shared-memory model (`Cslib.SharedMemory.Impl`: deterministic processes, atomic base objects, executions as label sequences, any number of crashes) and state the progress conditions of the paper over its infinite executions (`Impl.InfExec`): * `ObjSpec.Conflicting A a b`: `a ≍ b`, the operations do not commute in some state; * `Correct e p`: `p` takes infinitely many steps or eventually has no pending operation; * `PendingFrom e p n`: the operation of `p` pending at time `n` never completes; * `SoloFrom e p τ`: from time `τ` on only `p` takes steps (step-contention-freedom); * `EventuallyConflictFree e`: from some time on no two simultaneously pending operations of distinct processes conflict (two operation instances are concurrent iff they are pending at a common time); * `WaitFreeP`, `LockFreeP`, `ObstructionFreeP`, `ConflictFree` (Definition 3.1), `WeakConflictFree` (Definition 3.2): the paper's definitions, verbatim. Main results: Proposition 3.3 (`waitFree_conflictFree`, `conflictFree_weakConflictFree`, `weakConflictFree_obstructionFree`, `progress_hierarchy`) and the parts of Proposition 3.4 that hold without further assumptions (`conflictFree_iff_of_total`, `weakConflictFree_iff_of_total`, `conflictFree_iff_of_empty`, `WeakConflictFree.lockFreeP_of_empty`).
