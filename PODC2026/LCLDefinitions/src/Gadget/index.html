The vertices of the chain of `e` are `gad e k`, `k < 6 * len e`; the `i`-th gadget consists of positions `6i, …, 6i+5`. We number the six vertices of an A-gadget so that the chain is a path `k — k+1` plus the chords `6i — 6i+2`, `6i+1 — 6i+4`, `6i+3 — 6i+5`. In the paper's numbering `1, …, 6` of Figure 1a the positions `0, …, 5` are the paper's vertices `1, 2, 3, 5, 4, 6` (paper edges `{1,2}, {1,3}, {2,3}, {2,4}, {3,5}, {4,5}, {4,6}, {5,6}` become `01, 02, 12, 14, 23, 34, 45, 35`). With this numbering the reflection of an A-gadget is `j ↦ 5 - j`, so reading a chain backwards is `k ↦ 6 * len e - 1 - k`. * Gadgeted distance: the paper's weight `w` is `1/2` on edges at an original vertex and `0` otherwise. We use the doubled weight `wt ∈ {0, 1}`, so `gd2 u v = 2 * gdist(u, v)` and `gball r v = {u | gd2 v u ≤ 2 r}` is the paper's `r`-gball. * The hypotheses of Theorem 5.1: `IsDist2Coloring` (condition
