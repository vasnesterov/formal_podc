In `G' = G - a₀b₀ + a₁b₁` (`a₁ ∈ A`, `b₁ ∉ A`) the distance from `a₁` to `y` is at least `dist(a₁, y)` for `y ∈ A` and at least `1 + dist(b₁, y)` for `y ∉ A`. * `not_reachable_cut`: if both endpoints of the new edge lie in `A`, then `A` is cut off. These are the only facts about the modified graphs used in the analysis of swaps and additions in trees. -/ -- CSLib candidate (the whole file is general graph theory) namespace
