The map `π` of the paper is `π(C) = render C.reg (fun X => X^{C(X)}_none)`. `clean_fires` describes all transitions enabled in a clean configuration (no `⟨elect⟩` transition is enabled, every transition changes the agents of at most two pointers, or one pointer and moves a register agent), and `clean_fire_pp`, `clean_fire_pr` construct steps between clean configurations. -/ set_option
