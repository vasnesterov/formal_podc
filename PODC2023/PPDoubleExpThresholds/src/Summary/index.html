import Papers.PODC2023.PPDoubleExpThresholds.Program /-! # Procedure-level semantics of population programs The paper analyses procedures through the outcomes of a call (Section 4, "Notation"): `C, f → C', b` (`f` may return `b` with registers `C'`), `C, f → restart`, `C, f → ⊥` (`f` may hang or not terminate), and `post(C, f)` is the set of these outcomes. We define them from the instruction-level semantics `Program.Step₀` (steps other than `restart`): * `Returns p C OF C' OF' b`: from the call of `p` (stack `[(p, 0)]`), a `return b` of `p` with registers `C'` and flag `OF'` is reachable; * `Restarts p C OF`: a `restart` instruction is reachable during the call; * `Terminates p C OF`: from every configuration reachable during the call, a `return` of `p` or a `restart` is reachable. Under (global) fairness this is exactly "the call cannot hang and cannot run forever", so `⊥ ∈ post(C, f)` iff `¬ Terminates`. The *abstract step* `AStep p` executes one instruction of `p` and treats calls as single steps using `Returns` of the callee. `reach_cases` decomposes every configuration reachable during a call of `p` into an abstract configuration of `p`, possibly inside a call of a callee. The lemmas `returns_iff`, `restarts_iff`, `terminates_of` and `agef` reduce the analysis of a procedure to its abstract steps. -/ namespace Podc.PPDoubleExpThresholds open Cslib.TransitionSystem
