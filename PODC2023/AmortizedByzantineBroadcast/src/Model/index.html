import Mathlib /-! # Algorithm 4 of [WXMRS23]: linear-communication multi-shot Byzantine broadcast [WXMRS23] J. Wan, Z. Xiang, A. Momose, L. Ren, E. Shi. *On the Amortized Communication Complexity of Byzantine Broadcast.* PODC 2023. This file is an executable lock-step model of Algorithm 4 (honest majority, `f ≤ (1/2 - ε) n`). * `n` nodes `Fin n`, a static set `F` of Byzantine nodes; lock-step rounds: a message sent in round `r` is available to its recipient from round `r + 1` on. Messages to oneself are delivered as well (a multicast includes the sender). * Slot `k ≥ 1` has `f + 2` epochs `0 ≤ i ≤ f + 1` of 11 rounds; epoch `i` of slot `k` starts at round `11((k-1)(f+2) + i)` (`roundOf`). The leader of epoch `0` is the slot's sender, the leader of epoch `i ≥ 1` is "node `i`", i.e. `Fin` index `i - 1` (`leader`). * Signatures and threshold signatures are ideal. Items are *symbolic*: `Item.cert k i m` is the threshold signature `C_{k,i}(m) = thsig(vote,k,i,m)`, `Item.cproof k i m` the commit-proof `thsig(C_{k,i}(m))`, `Item.cpf v` the corrupt-proof `thsig(accuse, v)`, and the signed atoms carry their signer. Unforgeability is a validity condition on the adversary (`Scenario.AdvValid`, `availAt`): a Byzantine node may send a signed atom only if its signer is Byzantine or an honest node has sent that atom (in this or an earlier round: rushing, eavesdropping adversary), and a threshold signature only if `n - f` of its shares are available in that sense. * Honest nodes run `act`, a transcription of the 11 steps and the four `(★)` rules of Algorithm 4. The states (`St`: everything received and everything sent, with round tags) are computed from the adversary's messages (`exec`). The paper says that node `u` runs the 11 steps "if it has neither 1) committed in slot `k`, nor 2) received the corrupt-proof `thsig(accuse, L_i)`", without saying *when* this is checked. Read at every step, a node holding a commit-proof has committed and never answers queries, which defeats rounds 8–11; the intended reading is presumably that the conditions are evaluated at the start of the epoch. We keep the three readings as a parameter `Skip` (`epochStart`, `everyStep`, `mixed`); the consistency proof works for all three, the counterexamples are checked for the relevant ones. -/ namespace
