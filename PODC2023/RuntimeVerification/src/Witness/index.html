With input `A` given as an abstract implementation `𝒜` (a set of histories), an execution is admissible if its history of `A` belongs to `𝒜`. A history `h` (responses `(y, λ)`) is a *history of `A*`* if it is the history of `A*` in such an execution. `sketch_isAStarHist`: for every execution `E` of `V_O` running `A*` (with `A`'s history in a prefix-closed `𝒜`) and every time `s`, the sketch `X(τ)` of the contents `τ` of `M` at time `s` is a history of `A*`.
