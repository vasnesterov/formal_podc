import Mathlib import Papers.PODC2023.RuntimeVerification.Similarity /-! # Timed histories A *timed history* describes a finite history by its operations and the times of their invocations and responses: process `p` invoked the operations `0, …, m p - 1` (arguments `op p k`), of which `0, …, c p - 1` completed (responses `res p k`), at times `ti p k` / `tr p k` in a linear order `T`. The list history `toList h` lists the events in increasing order of time. Timed histories are the bridge between executions (whose events have times) and the list histories on which linearizability / `GenLin` are defined: all real-time questions about `toList h` reduce to comparisons of times (`precedes_toList`). -- CSLib candidate: the whole file (timed histories ↔ Herlihy–Wing histories). -/ namespace Podc2023.RuntimeVerification open Cslib.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing
