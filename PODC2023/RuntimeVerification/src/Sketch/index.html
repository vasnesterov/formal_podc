We define `X(τ)` as a timed history `XTH τ`: an invocation gets the time `(|σ|, 0, rank)` where `σ` is the smallest view containing it, a response gets `(|λ|, 1, rank)`; since views form a chain, `|σ|` determines `σ`. The within-block order (`rank`, from the process index and the operation index) is one of the arbitrary orders the paper allows. Under the structural properties `Valid τ` (self-inclusion, containment comparability and process sequentiality of Remark 7.2, plus consistency), `XTH τ` is a well-formed timed history whose real-time order is: the response of `a` precedes the invocation of `b` iff `b ∉ λ_a` (`tr_lt_ti_iff`). -/ namespace Podc2023.RuntimeVerification open Cslib.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing set_option
