import Papers.PODC2023.TreeExplorationBFDN.Main /-! # Proposition 4.2: adversarial break-downs Cosson, Massoulié, Viennot, PODC 2023, Section 4.2, Proposition 4.2 (full version arXiv:2301.13307, Section 4.2, Proposition 6). At every round `t` an adversary chooses the set `act t` of robots allowed to move (`M^i_t = 1` iff `i ∈ act t`); blocked robots neither take part in the decisions of the round nor move. `A(M) = (1/k) ∑_t ∑_i M^i_t ∈ ℕ ∪ {∞}` is the average number of allowed moves. If `A(M) ≥ 2n/k + D² (log k + 2)`, all edges are visited (equivalently, since edges are identified with their lower endpoints, all nodes are visited). -/ set_option linter.unusedSectionVars false namespace Podc.TreeExplorationBFDN open Finset SimpleGraph open scoped ENNReal variable {V : Type*} {G : SimpleGraph V} {T : G.RootedTree} {k : ℕ} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] /-- Before the tree is explored, each round contributes at most its moves, except one round (the round in which the last dangling edges at the root are taken) where up to `k` allowed robots may stay idle. -/ theorem sum_card_act_le {act : ℕ → Finset (Fin k)} {e : ℕ → Config V k} (he : IsExec T act e) (t₁ : ℕ) (hnv : ∀ t < t₁, (e t).vis ≠ univ) : ∑ t ∈ range t₁, (act t).card ≤ k + ∑ t ∈ range t₁, (movedSet e t).card := by classical set SB := (range t₁).filter fun t => IsOpen T (e t) T.root ∧ ¬ IsOpen T (e (t + 1)) T.root have hSB : SB.card ≤ 1 := by rw [card_le_one] intro a ha b hb simp only [SB, mem_filter, mem_range] at ha hb by_contra hab rcases lt_or_gt_of_ne hab with h | h · exact he.not_open_mono h (he.inv _).root_vis ha.2.2 hb.2.1 · exact he.not_open_mono h (he.inv _).root_vis hb.2.2 ha.2.1 have hround : ∀ t ∈ range t₁, (act t).card ≤ k * (if t ∈ SB then 1 else 0) + (movedSet e t).card := by intro t ht split_ifs with hs · rw [mul_one];
