Abstract version of one depth-`d` phase of BFDN, phrased with round-indexed data: * `O t` — the "urns" alive at the start of round `t` (in BFDN: the open nodes at depth `d`); from round `t₀` on, urns only disappear; * `c t w` — the load of urn `w` at the start of round `t` (in BFDN: the number of robots anchored at `w`); the load of an alive urn only grows during a round, and `A t` is the total growth (the number of anchor assignments at depth `d` in round `t`); * loads of alive urns are balanced (differ by at most one) at the end of every round; * capacity: `∑_{w ∈ O t} (c (t+1) w + 1) ≤ k` (each alive urn also hosts its discoverer robot); * window: once all alive urns have load `+ 1 ≥ Δ`, the number of assignments in all later rounds is bounded by the number of robots that are not bound to alive urns, plus the robots bound to urns that die in this very round.
