Civit, Gilbert, Guerraoui, Komatovic, Vidigueira — On the Validity of Consensus

🔧 Easy fix. Classification and the Ω(t²) bound hold; Lemma 3's proof needs a local repair for n < 2t. Universal's O(n²) messages, and sufficiency with clock drift and sleeping processes, are unchecked.

13

Wan, Xiang, Momose, Ren, Shi — On the Amortized Communication Complexity of Byzantine Broadcast

❌ Intent lost. The honest-majority protocol behind the amortized linear claim violates Validity and Lemma 3; no repair is proved. Its Consistency holds, and the dishonest-majority protocol holds after a local fix to TrustCast's invocation.

8

Castañeda, Rodríguez — Asynchronous Wait-Free Runtime Verification and Enforcement of Linearizability

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 7.4 and Claim 7.2 are false as stated, but local changes save their uses; impossibility, predictive verification and self-enforcement all hold, with progress claims unchecked.

7

Kutten, Robinson, Tan, Zhu — Improved Tradeoffs for Leader Election

⚠️ Weakened. The abstract's tradeoff, the Omega(n log n) bound and both deterministic algorithms hold; Theorem 3.8 is one round too strong, so its k-round message bound holds only as Omega(n^{1+1/k}). Other sections unchecked.

5

Alpturer, Halpern, van der Meyden — Optimal Eventual Byzantine Agreement Protocols with Omission Failures

⚠️ Weakened. Correctness and the concrete protocols stand, but safe-implies-optimal holds only with an extra hypothesis, against one-shot competitors; the 0-chain definition needs a one-clause fix; the open-problem result of Section 7 is unchecked.

5

Friedrich, Gawendowicz, Lenzner, Zahn — The Impact of Cooperation in Bilateral Network Creation

🔧 Easy fix. All checked statements hold: proofs of Lemmas 2.4 and 3.5 need local repairs and the log-alpha tree bounds stand; the constant PoA for 3-BSE and the general-network results were not checked.

5

Naderibeni, Ruppert — A Wait-free Queue with Polylogarithmic Step Complexity

🔧 Easy fix. Lines 13 and 78 of Fig. 4 break linearizability as printed; one-line repairs restore Theorem 18 and wait-freedom. The dequeue bound is checked only in parts, the bounded-space version not at all.

4

Déprés, Mostéfaoui, Perrin, Raynal — Brief Announcement: The MBroadcast Abstraction

✏️ Slip. The abstract's paraphrase of mutual ordering is too weak if read literally; under the wording of Section 3, Property 1, Theorems 1, 2 and the construction from registers all hold.

3

Bashari, Jamadi, Woelfel — Efficient Bounded Timestamping from Standard Synchronization Primitives

❌ Intent lost. Figures 1–2 are not linearizable (Lean counterexample, 3 processes) and a separate-link repair is unproved; Observation 13 is also false. The constant step bound and Section 6 hold.

2

Cosson, Massoulié, Viennot — Brief Announcement: Efficient Collaborative Tree Exploration with Breadth-First Depth-Next

🛠️ Hard fix. Runtime of BFDN and the urn game hold; the reduction behind Lemma 2 fails and needed a new proof; Corollary 3.2 and Claim 1 are false as printed, with local fixes.

2

Czerner — Brief Announcement: Population Protocols Decide Double-exponential Thresholds

🔧 Easy fix. All main theorems hold; Lemma 11(c) is false for j = i, repaired by a short replacement step in Lemma 12(c), and Appendix B needs local fixes.

1

Jayanti, Jayanti, Jayanti — Brief Announcement: Efficient Recoverable Writable-CAS

🛠️ Hard fix. Durable linearizability, detectability and constant time hold under a per-process recovery rule, not the stated per-object one; the proof needed a new invariant and a refinement. Space bound not formalized.
