Process `p₀` writes `1` (both its messages are mdelivered only by itself) and then `p₁` reads `0` (its messages are mdelivered only by itself). The MBroadcast trace satisfies validity, no-duplication and the literal mutual ordering, and the execution is compatible with Global CS-termination if `p₀` crashes after its write. This is why we read "`p` mdelivers `m` before `m'`" as "when `p` mdelivers `m`, it has not mdelivered `m'`" (`DelivBefore`). * `alg3_nonvacuous`: an execution satisfying the (strong) MBroadcast safety
