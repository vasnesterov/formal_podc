Proof of mutual ordering. Suppose `p ≠ p'`, `p` mdelivers its own `m` (position `i`) before having mdelivered `m'`, and `p'` mdelivers its own `m'` (position `i'`) before having mdelivered `m`. The quorums `{p} ∪ A` and `{p'} ∪ A'` of the acknowledgements received by `p` and `p'` have `≥ n - t` elements each, so they intersect (`2(n - t) > n`) in some `k`. * `k = p ∈ A'` (or symmetrically `k = p' ∈ A`): `p` sent `ack(m')` (id `y'`) to `p'` and `init(m)` (id `x`). If `x < y'`, then `x ∈ dep(y')`, so by causal delivery `p'` received `init(m)` (and mdelivered `m`) before receiving `y'`, hence before `i'`. If `y' < x`, `p` mdelivered `m'` right after sending `y'`, before `x < i`. * `k ∈ A ∩ A'`: `k` sent `ack(m)` (id `y`) and `ack(m')` (id `y'`), each right after receiving the corresponding `init`.
