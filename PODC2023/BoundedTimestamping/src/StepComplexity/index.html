Lemma 12 of [BJW23]: `updateTS()` and `isEarlier()` have constant step complexity. We prove this for the encoding `alg n κ` of Figures 1–2 (`Alg.lean`) with the explicit bound `stepBound κ = 2κ + 107`, independent of `n` (`κ` is the constant of line 23). It is computed from the call graph, counting one step per shared-memory access: * `helpUpdateTS`: at most 3 iterations of lines 9, 10, 12, 13, i.e. `≤ 12` steps; * `helpActiveCluster`: `conditionalReset` makes at most 2 iterations of lines 26, 29, 30, 33 (`≤ 8` steps), followed by exactly `κ` steps of the `helpMoveTS` coroutine: `≤ 8 + κ`; * `isEarlier(x, y)`: lines 35–36 (2), possibly `helpUpdateTS(x)` (12), lines 37–38 (2), possibly `helpUpdateTS(y)` (12), `helpActiveCluster` (`8 + κ`), lines 40–42 (3), then at most 6 iterations of lines 44, 45, 46, 50, 51 (30) and lines 55–56 (2), or exit via line 48: `≤ 71 + κ` steps (`opCost κ (.ie x y)`); * `helpSystem`: line 16, possibly a nested `isEarlier` (`71 + κ`) and line 19, then `helpActiveCluster` (`8 + κ`): `≤ 81 + 2κ` steps; * `updateTS()`: lines 2–3 (2), two `helpUpdateTS` calls (24), `helpSystem` (`81 + 2κ`): `≤ 107 + 2κ` steps (`opCost κ (.upd p)`). The proof is a potential `pot κ : Loc → ℕ` (an upper bound on the remaining number of accesses, determined by the program counter, the loop counters `hit`, `rit`, `eit` and the return addresses `hret`, `cret`, `enested`; it does not depend on the `helpMoveTS` coroutine state `mv`, since `PC.K s` performs exactly `s` coroutine steps whatever the coroutine does) that strictly decreases at every base-object access (`pot_lt`).
