import Papers.PODC2023.BoundedTimestamping.Machine /-! # Observation 13 and Lemma 2 are false Observation 13 claims that during one `updateTS()` call a process increments `Counter` at most six times, and Lemma 2 that a process incrementing `Counter` `i` times completes at least `⌈i/6⌉ - 1` `helpActiveCluster()` calls in that interval (via Lemma 14: at most 6 increments between consecutive `helpActiveCluster()` calls). The proof of Observation 13 overlooks that `helpSystem()` (line 5) may call `isEarlier()` (line 18), which calls `helpUpdateTS()` twice (lines 36, 38) *before* its own `helpActiveCluster()` (line 39). So before its first `helpActiveCluster()` an `updateTS()` may run four `helpUpdateTS()` calls — up to 12 increments. Concrete execution (`n = 4`, `κ = 3`, `obsLabels`, 177 labels): process `0` invokes `isEarlier(2, 2)` and stops after announcing it (line 41); process `2` starts an `updateTS()`; process `1` runs `updateTS()`: one increment for itself, then in `helpSystem()` (`helpID = 0`) it finds the announcement and calls `isEarlier(2, 2)`, whose two `helpUpdateTS(2)` calls each increment `Counter` three times, because after each of process `1`'s `FAI()`s process `2` completes its `updateTS()` and starts a new one. Process `1` performs 7 increments within one `updateTS()` call, without having started any `helpActiveCluster()` call. This is a gap in the proofs of Lemmas 21 and 33 (which use Lemma 2 with the constants `ζ = 6n + 2` and `μ = 6n³ + 6n² + 2`), not by itself a counterexample to the algorithm. -/ set_option
