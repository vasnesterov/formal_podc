The enqueue sequence `E(B)` and the dequeue sequence `D(B)` of (3.1) are its enqueues and dequeues (`enqs`, `deqs`): filtering commutes with concatenation, so this is exactly (3.1). An operation is identified by its CSLib instance `(p, j)` (the `j`-th operation of `p`); it is represented by the leaf block `leaf(p).blocks[j + 1]`.
