The graph `G_k` (Anshelevich et al.): vertices `x`, `z`, `y_1, …, y_{k-1}`; edges `(x, y_i)` of cost `1/i`, `(x, z)` of cost `1 + ε`, and `(z, y_i)` of cost `0`.
