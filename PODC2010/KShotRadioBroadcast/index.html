k-Shot Broadcasting in Radio Networks — PODC 2010

Paraschos Koutris, Aris Pagourtzis. Brief Announcement: k-Shot Distributed Broadcasting in Radio Networks. PODC 2010. DOI 10.1145/1835698.1835717. Formalized version: the full version, Karmakar, Koutris, Pagourtzis, Sakavalas, k-shot Broadcasting in Ad Hoc Radio Networks, arXiv 1603.08393 v1; numbering follows it. We found no open copy of the 2-page brief announcement. Copy of the paper: paper.pdf.

Version caveat. All mistakes below are those of the 2016 arXiv text. The brief announcement (2 pages, no public abstract) presumably states the oblivious bounds (Θ(n²/k) for k ≤ √n, O(n^{3/2}) above) with at most proof sketches, which may differ from the full proofs checked here. The adaptive k-shot lower bound of Section 5 is a later result: the arXiv text says its conference version (CATS 2011) studied adaptive protocols only for k = 1 (Appendix A, not formalized). The verdict rests on Section 4 (Lemma 4.2), which concerns the oblivious upper bound.

DOI · arXiv · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🛠️ Hard fix. All three results hold, and we proved them in Lean with explicit constants: the Ω(n²/k) lower bound for oblivious k-shot protocols, the O(n²/k) upper bound of Oblivious k-Shot (k ≤ √n), and the adaptive Ω(n^{1+1/k}) lower bound. Two proofs do not work as written and needed new arguments: the proof of Lemma 4.2 (the progress lemma behind the upper bound) and the proof of the adaptive Theorem 3, which adds tree heights measured in absolute rounds. The rest are slips and a missing case k ∈ {1, 2}.

Mistakes in the paper

🔧 Section 4 (Oblivious k-Shot, k ∈ {1, 2})

The algorithm uses K = ⌈p/(k−2)⌉ Round-Robin steps per Line-Transmit step, which is undefined for k ≤ 2, yet the paper claims the O(n²/k) bound for every k ≤ √n.

Impact. Local fix: for k ∈ {1, 2} plain Round-Robin broadcasts in ≤ 4n² steps (roundRobin_complete), so the claimed bound holds for all k ≤ √n.

✏️ Slips

  • Theorem 3.3: the sum 1 + ∑_{j=1}^{⌊n/k⌋} (n − jk) overcounts. Lemma 3.2 gives a gain of n − |S| − k with |S| ≤ 1 + (j−1)k, so stage j gains n − 1 − jk (already T₁ ≥ 1 + n − k should be T₁ ≥ n − k). Also the last stages may use up every node, so that no node is left to be informed after the chain. With m = ⌊(n−2)/k⌋ stages the bound is ∑_{j=1}^{m} (n − 1 − jk) ≥ ((n−1)² − k(n−1))/(2k), the same order (theorem_3_3, theorem_3_3').

  • Lemma 3.1 is false for |Q| = 1 when V(S) ∪ Q = V: the node of Q then need never transmit, so t_{≤k}(w,T) is undefined. Lemma 3.2 needs it only for |Q| ≥ 2; we state it so (lemma_3_1).

  • Theorem 3 (§5), final estimate: "half of these terms are lower than (n/2)^{1/k}" should say "at least", and "2^{1/k} ≥ 2" should say "≤ 2".

Typos

  • Lemma 4.1: "p(k−2)⌈p/(k−2)⌉ > p²" should be ≥ (equality at k = 3). The chain of inequalities is still strict.

  • Theorem 3.3: "a3" for a2, and "until we have constructed a sequence of length k or less". Lemma 3.2: |s| for |S|. Its last step names the unused nodes of W as w_{j+1}, …, w_k; any unused node works.

  • Lemma 4.2 says v transmits "during the odd-numbered steps"; that holds only when K = 1.

Proof gaps in true statements (4)Theorem 3 · Lemma 4.2 · Theorem 2 · Lemma 3.1

🛠️ Theorem 3 (§5, adaptive Ω(n^{(1+k)/k}) lower bound)

The proof adds up the heights of the transmission trees of all layers, but Definition 1 builds a tree over absolute rounds, starting at round 1. So Theorem 1 bounds only the absolute round of the relay from L_{i+1} to L_{i+2}, not its delay after L_{i+1} receives the message.

Impact. Statement true; repaired with a relative-delay argument (exists_slow_network, exists_slow_network_rpow).

Details

Adaptive protocols may transmit before they hold the message. With one spontaneous shot per node, the ids are separated before the message arrives: every absolute tree of every layer has height at most B (onceProtocol_heightLE). Theorems 1 + 2 then only give relay rounds ≥ max_i height(T_i), not the sum. Theorems 1 and 2 themselves are correct (exists_extension_of_not_heightLE, card_le_sum_choose_of_kShotTree).

Repair

Measure from the round τ_i before which layer i+1 cannot be informed. In the window (τ_i, τ_i + d] each unused id has a 0/1 transmission pattern with at most k ones, so there are at most ∑_{r≤k} C(d, r) ≤ (d+1)^k patterns. If there are more unused ids than that, two ids share their pattern. Placing them in L_{i+1} keeps L_{i+2} uninformed for d more rounds (exists_extension_quiet). These delays add up over ⌊n/4⌋ layers with (d+1)^k ≤ n/2: some network is not informed after n^{1+1/k}/32 rounds when n ≥ 2·4^k.

🛠️ Lemma 4.2 (progress per stage) and Theorem 4.3

The proof's count is impossible: it claims v transmits at least k − k/r times during the r|F| stages of T, but v transmits at most once per stage. With |F| = 1, r = 2, k > 4 it would need more than 2 transmissions in 2 stages.

Impact. Statement true (with r = 2, and even without |F| ≤ k/2); repaired with a new counting argument (progress_add_le), giving Theorem 4.3 with explicit constants (theorem_4_3).

Details

The proof also splits on "v transmits alone in T" instead of "v is complete at the end of T". It ignores that the uninformed neighbour w may be informed during T. And it counts co-transmitters "outside F" as progress, although complete nodes that still have shots may also transmit. In Theorem 4.3, "Round-Robin guarantees progress k/2 over O(n) steps" needs that a Round-Robin appearance lies within the node's first k appearances, which is Lemma 4.1 with a time bound.

Repair

Let f = |F| and look at the next 2f stages. If some v ∈ F is not complete at their end, fix an out-neighbour w of v that is still uninformed then. Before that, v never transmitted alone, and by Lemma 4.1 it still has shots left. So v transmitted in each of the 2f stages, on a line, colliding at w. Each collider is an in-neighbour of w, hence active and in F or newly informed. The colliders are pairwise distinct, since two nodes share a line in at most one of p + 1 consecutive stages (eq_of_onLine_two_dirs). This forces progress ≥ f + 1. Otherwise every node of F completes, and progress is ≥ f. Hence broadcasting completes within 2(2n−1) stages of p(K+1) steps (obliviousKShot_complete). For long windows (2f > p+1), the time bound of Lemma 4.1 (exists_lone_transmission) completes every active node within p + 1 stages.

🔧 Theorem 2 (§5, height of k-shot transmission trees)

The induction on k reasons with O/ω about a single tree, as in "|P| ≠ O(a^{(i−1)/i}) ⇔ |P| = ω(a^{(i−1)/i})", which is not rigorous.

Impact. Statement true. A direct count proves it: a ≤ ∑_{i≤k} C(h, i) ≤ (h+1)^k, so height ≥ a^{1/k} − 1 (card_le_sum_choose_of_kShotTree, rpow_sub_one_le_of_kShotTree).

🔧 Lemma 3.1 (degree-1 peeling)

After removing a step vertex and its neighbour, the proof says the remaining graph G' is not conflicting, hence some step vertex has degree 1 in G'. But G' may fail to be conflicting only because of step vertices of degree 0.

Impact. Statement true (for |Q| ≥ 2): one should restrict to A' ∪ N(A'). We use the selective-family bound instead (lemma_3_1 via Cslib.card_le_of_forall_exists_card_inter_eq_one).

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Synchronous radio network on a finite id set V, n = |V|, directed links E (u transmits to v), one source, no collision detection. A node receives iff exactly one in-neighbour transmits. Deterministic protocols, no failures.

  • Oblivious protocols (Model.lean): a schedule σ : ℕ → Finset V. A node informed at step r transmits at its first k appearances after r, with no spontaneous transmissions. The execution is recvTime.

  • Lower bound (§3): proved for undirected networks. Correctness is assumed only on connected undirected networks (CorrectOnUndirected), and every constructed network is a connected SimpleGraph; this is the stronger form, as the paper remarks. The statement does not assume correctness: if the protocol fails somewhere, that network is the witness.

  • Upper bound (§4): every directed network in which all nodes are reachable from the source, ids Fin n with 0-based labels, p the least prime with n ≤ p².

  • Adaptive protocols (§5, Adaptive.lean): general deterministic state machines (state, action transmit m / listen, update on the received message), with spontaneous transmissions allowed. This contains the paper's full-information views. "Informed" is tracked semantically (hearing an informed node). k-shot is assumed only on the paper's layered family (KShotOnFamily), which is weaker than assuming it on all networks.

  • "Running time Ω(f)" is stated as "some network is not fully informed after f steps", with explicit constants.
