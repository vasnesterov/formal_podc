import Mathlib /-! # Adaptive `k`-shot broadcasting: the lower bound of Section 5 Karmakar, Koutris, Pagourtzis, Sakavalas. *k-shot Broadcasting in Ad Hoc Radio Networks*, arXiv:1603.08393v1, Section 5 (Definitions 1–2, Theorems 1–3). **Model.** Directed radio network `G : V → V → Prop` on a finite id set `V`, source `s`, synchronous rounds `0, 1, 2, …` (the paper's round `t` is our round `t - 1`), no collision detection: a node that does not transmit hears a message iff exactly one in-neighbour transmits; a transmitter hears nothing (`sender`). Deterministic adaptive protocols are arbitrary state machines (`Protocol`): initial state from the id and the source flag, action (transmit a message or listen) and update depending on the id, the round and the state; nodes may transmit before they know the source message. This contains the paper's full-information protocols `π(v, t, H_{t-1}(v))`. Being informed is tracked semantically (`Protocol.informed`): a node becomes informed when it hears a node that was informed at the start of the round. `k`-shot (`KShotOnFamily`): in every network of the family every node transmits in at most `k` rounds. Correctness of the protocol is *not* assumed by the lower bounds. **Family `G`.** A listing `e : V ≃ Fin n` with `e s = 0`; position `p` is in layer `⌊(p + 1)/2⌋` (`layerOf`), and the edges go from every node of a layer to every node of the next one (`familyGraph`). Layer `i` here is the paper's `L_{i+1}`. **Transmission trees (Definitions 1, 2).** Encoded by the transmission patterns `p : α → ℕ → Bool` of the ids: the tree node at depth `t` on the branch of `x` is `cell A p t x` (the ids acting like `x` in rounds `< t`); its left/right children are the listeners/transmitters of round `t`. `HeightLE A p h`: all depth-`h` nodes are singleton leaves. `KShotTree A p k`: every root–leaf branch has at most `k` right edges. **Main results.** * `card_le_sum_choose_of_kShotTree`, `card_le_pow_of_kShotTree`, `rpow_sub_one_le_of_kShotTree`: Theorem 2 (`a ≤ ∑_{i ≤ k} C(h, i) ≤ (h + 1)^k`, i.e. `height ≥ a^{1/k} - 1`). * `exists_extension_of_not_heightLE`: Theorem 1 as stated (absolute rounds). * `exists_extension_of_same_window`, `exists_extension_quiet`: the corrected (relative) form of Theorems 1 + 2: measured from the round `D₀` before which layer `i + 1` cannot be informed, the relay to layer `i + 2` can be delayed by `d` more rounds whenever `∑_{r ≤ k} C(d, r)` is less than the number of unused ids. * `exists_slow_network`, `exists_slow_network_uniform`, `exists_slow_network_rpow`: Theorem 3 (some network of `G` is not informed after `n^{1 + 1/k} / 32` rounds when `n ≥ 2 · 4^k`). * `onceProtocol_kShot`, `onceProtocol_heightLE`: non-vacuity of the `k`-shot hypothesis, and an illustration of why the paper's summation of absolute tree heights is not justified. -/ namespace Podc.KShotRadioBroadcast.Adaptive open Finset /-!
