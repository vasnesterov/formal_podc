Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of k-Shot Broadcasting in Radio Networks — PODC 2010.

Paper: Koutris, Pagourtzis, Brief Announcement: k-Shot Distributed Broadcasting in Radio Networks, PODC 2010. Formalized version: Karmakar, Koutris, Pagourtzis, Sakavalas, k-shot Broadcasting in Ad Hoc Radio Networks, arXiv:1603.08393v1 (the full version; numbering follows it). No copy of the brief announcement was available: all differences are relative to the arXiv text. The general adaptive k-shot lower bound (Section 5) is not in the BA (the arXiv text says its CATS 2011 conference version treated adaptive protocols only for k = 1).

Model (Section 2)

  • Nodes are a finite type V (labels), n = Fintype.card V. A network is a directed relation E u v ("u transmits to v") with a source s; undirected networks are E = G.Adj for a SimpleGraph G.

  • Oblivious protocol = schedule σ : ℕ → Finset V (step t ≥ 1). The k-shot rule (TransmitsAt): a node informed at step r transmits at its first k appearances at steps > r. No spontaneous transmissions, no collision detection; an uninformed node is informed at step t iff exactly one in-neighbour transmits at t (recvTime).

  • "Running time = worst case over networks" is formalized as: for every schedule there is a network on which broadcasting is not complete before the bound. The lower bound does not assume the protocol is correct: if it fails on some connected network, that network is the witness (theorem_3_3 splits on CorrectOnUndirected).

  • The paper's t_i(v,T), t_{≤i}(v,T) with shots(v,T) = 0 are undefined; we never use them and express "t_{≤k}(w,T) ≥ X" as "w, informed at T, transmits at some step ≥ X".

  • First occurrence t(R,T) is occ σ R T : Option ℕ (none = never occurs, i.e. the chain is blocked forever, which only strengthens lower bounds).

Section 3 (oblivious lower bound)

  • Lower bound for undirected networks. All constructed networks are connected SimpleGraphs and correctness is only assumed on those (CorrectOnUndirected), as the paper remarks the bound holds in symmetric networks. This is stronger than the directed statement.

  • Chain execution (Chain.lean). The paper identifies, without proof, the step at which a chain S ∘ … delivers the message with the first occurrence of S. We prove it (chain_outside_none, chain_last_informs) for any network that "starts with the chain" (StartsWithChain). Requires k ≥ 1 (for k = 0 nothing is ever transmitted).

  • Lemma 3.1. Stated for |Q| ≥ 2 (✏️ degenerate case: for |Q| = 1 and V(S) ∪ Q = V the single node of Q need not transmit at all, so t_{≤k}(w,T) is undefined). Lemma 3.2 only uses it with |Q| ≥ 2 once |Q| > k. The proof is by the selective-family bound Cslib.card_le_of_forall_exists_card_inter_eq_one (parity argument) instead of the paper's peeling of degree-1 step vertices. (The paper's peeling step says "G' is not conflicting, hence some u' ∈ B has degree 1 in G'", but G' may be non-conflicting only because of degree-0 step vertices; one has to restrict to A' ∪ N(A'). Minor, the statement is true.) The collision network (exists_blocked_network) attaches all nodes outside S ∪ P ∪ {v_t} to v_t so that it is connected; the paper leaves them unspecified.

  • Lemma 3.2. Proved as stated (t(R,T) ≥ T + n − |S| − k, written T + (n − ℓ) ≤ T' + k in ℕ), for any |Q| (trivial when |Q| ≤ k). The construction of R is reorganized as an induction (exists_seq_exhausted): pick the node of W with the latest next appearance and recurse; this is the paper's greedy M_i construction. The paper's final step ("any node in {w_{j+1},…,w_k} does not occur after …") misindexes the unused nodes of W; harmless.

  • Theorem 3.3. ✏️ The paper's sum 1 + ∑_{j=1}^{⌊n/k⌋} (n − jk) is off: Lemma 3.2 gives T_j ≥ T_{j−1} + n − |S_{j−1}| − k with |S_{j−1}| ≤ 1 + (j−1)k, so each stage gains n − 1 − jk, not n − jk (already T_1 ≥ 1 + n − k should be ≥ n − k); and the last stages may use up all nodes, so that no node is left to be informed after the chain. We stop at m = ⌊(n−2)/k⌋ stages and prove: broadcasting is not complete at any step t ≤ ∑_{j=1}^{m} (n − 1 − jk) (theorem_3_3), hence not at any t with 2kt + k(n−1) < (n−1)² (theorem_3_3'). Same Θ(n²/k) order. Also typos: "a3" for a2, "until we have constructed a sequence of length k or less", |s| for |S|.

  • Assumptions added: k ≥ 1, n ≥ 2.

Section 4 (upper bound)

  • Ids are Fin n with 0-based labels (label i ↦ point ⟨i div p, i mod p⟩); p is the least prime with n ≤ p² (smallestPrime), i.e. the least prime ≥ ⌈√n⌉; by Bertrand p ≤ 2⌊√n⌋. The paper's 1-based labels also work (coordinates are read mod p).

  • The schedule (obliviousKShot): step t ≥ 1 is a Line-Transmit step iff (t − 1) mod (K+1) = 0; stages of p(K+1) steps cycle through the directions 0, …, p; Round-Robin slots cycle through 0, …, p² − 1 (empty slots for labels ≥ n). K = ⌈p/(k−2)⌉ = (p + k − 3)/(k − 2).

  • Networks: directed, every node reachable from the source (Relation.ReflTransGen E s v).

  • Lemma 4.1 proved as stated for k ≥ 3, plus a time bound (the lone transmission happens by the end of stage ⌊r/S⌋ + p), needed by the progress argument (exists_lone_transmission). Typo: > should be ≥ in p(k−2)⌈p/(k−2)⌉ > p².

  • Lemma 4.2 🛠️ (proof gap): the proof's claim "v transmits during T at least k − k/r times" is impossible (T has r|F| stages, one line transmission per stage); also mixes up collisions at w with arbitrary simultaneous transmissions, ignores that w may get informed during T, and "odd-numbered steps" holds only for K = 1. Proved instead (progress_add_le): progress (= #informed + #complete) grows by ≥ |F| within 2|F| stages, for every |F| (no |F| ≤ k/2 needed), using the colliders-at-w argument and the time bound of Lemma 4.1.

  • Theorem 4.3 proved with explicit constants: completion within 2(2n−1) stages (obliviousKShot_complete); k·T ≤ 80 n² for 3 ≤ k ≤ √n (theorem_4_3, hypothesis k·k ≤ n); T ≤ 32 n ⌊√n⌋ for k > √n (theorem_4_3_large_k; the paper's detour through running the algorithm with √n shots is unnecessary). The paper's case split |F| > k/2 / |F| ≤ k/2 is replaced by the uniform Lemma 4.2. Gap 🔧 (merged with the Lemma 4.2 item in the README): "Round-Robin guarantees progress k/2 in O(n) steps" needs Lemma 4.1's time bound.

  • 🔧 k ∈ {1, 2}: the algorithm is undefined (K = ⌈p/(k−2)⌉); plain Round-Robin gives ≤ 4n² steps (roundRobin_complete).

Section 5 (adaptive lower bound)

  • Protocols are general deterministic state machines Protocol V σ M (init, act, δ); the paper's full-information protocol π(v, t, H_{t−1}(v)) is the special case where the state is the view. Spontaneous transmissions allowed, as in the paper. Rounds are numbered from 0 (paper's round t = our round t − 1).

  • "Informed" is semantic (Protocol.informed): a node is informed when it hears a node informed at the start of the round — the most generous notion, so lower bounds are at least as strong.

  • k-shot is assumed only on the networks of the family G (KShotOnFamily), weaker than on all networks. Correctness of the protocol is not assumed.

  • Family G: a listing e : V ≃ Fin n with the source at position 0; position p is in layer ⌊(p+1)/2⌋ (our layer i = paper's L_{i+1}), complete bipartite edges between consecutive layers.

  • Definitions 1–2: transmission trees are encoded by the transmission patterns of the ids (cell A p t x = the depth-t tree node containing x); HeightLE, KShotTree. The paper's "leaves are singletons" (justified by correctness) is the hypothesis HeightLE.

  • Theorem 2 🔧 (proof gap): the paper's induction over k with O/ω reasoning on a single tree ("|P| ≠ O(a^{(i−1)/i}) ⇔ |P| = ω(a^{(i−1)/i})") is not rigorous. Proved by counting: a ≤ ∑_{i≤k} C(h, i) ≤ (h+1)^k, so h ≥ a^{1/k} − 1 (card_le_sum_choose_of_kShotTree, rpow_sub_one_le_of_kShotTree); for k = 1 this is the paper's a − 1.

  • Theorem 1 proved as stated, in absolute rounds (exists_extension_of_not_heightLE).

  • Theorem 3 🛠️ (proof gap): summing the absolute heights over layers is unjustified (onceProtocol_heightLE: one spontaneous shot per node makes all absolute trees of all layers of height ≤ B). Proved by relative windows (exists_extension_of_same_window, exists_extension_quiet, exists_slow_network): for delays d_j with ∑_{r≤k} C(d_j, r) + 2(j+1) ≤ n, some network is not informed after ∑_j d_j rounds; uniform form ⌊n/4⌋·d rounds when (d+1)^k ≤ ⌊n/2⌋; and n^{1+1/k}/32 rounds when k ≥ 1, n ≥ 2·4^k (exists_slow_network_rpow). The paper's sum (n−1)^{1/k} + (n−3)^{1/k} + … is replaced by the uniform bound over ⌊n/4⌋ layers; the constant differs, the order n^{1+1/k} is the same.

  • ✏️ Arithmetic of Theorem 3: "half of these terms are lower than (n/2)^{1/k}" should be "at least"; "2^{1/k} ≥ 2" should be "≤ 2".

  • Minor: the two nodes of a layer need not receive the same incoming view (a transmitter hears nothing); harmless, behaviour is still determined by the earlier layers (state_eq_solo).

  • Appendix A (alternative 1-shot proof) is not formalized.
