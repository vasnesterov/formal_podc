import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing.Locality import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing.Nonblocking import Cslib.Computability.Distributed.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing.DetSpec /-! # Weak consistency, t-permutations and t-linearizations (Section 3.2) We formalize the history-level notions of Serafini, Dobre, Majuntke, Bokor and Suri, *Eventually Linearizable Shared Objects* (PODC 2010), on top of the Herlihy–Wing history framework of CSLib (`Cslib.SharedMemory.HerlihyWing`): histories are lists of invocation and response events `inv p x o` / `res p x r`, and an operation instance is identified by its `OpId` `(p, x, k)` (the `k`-th operation of process `p` on object `x`), which is invariant under restriction to an object. * `IdSeq`: a sequential history whose operations carry explicit identities (an operation of `H` together with the record `⟨p, x, o, r⟩` it has in the sequential history). The results `r` need not be the results of `H`. Unlike the CSLib list characterizations (`idsOf`), explicit identities do not force a process's operations to appear in program order: the paper's `τ(i, o)` and `t`-permutations need not respect program order of other processes (or of operations invoked before `t`). * `IsSeqOf spec H L`: `L` is a legal sequential history of distinct operations of `H`. * `WeaklyConsistent spec H`: for every operation `o` completed by `p_i` there is a legal sequential history `τ(i, o)` (we index it by the operation, whose identity contains its process) with (i) last operation `o`, with its result in `H`; (ii) only operations invoked in `H` before `o` completed; (iii) `τ(i, o') ⊆ τ(i, o)` for the operations `o'` of `p_i` invoked before `o`. * `after invTime t`: the operations invoked after time `t` (for a global clock `invTime`). All definitions below are stated for an arbitrary set `A` of "late" operations (the operations invoked after `t`); `A = after invTime t` gives the paper's notions. * `IsTPerm spec H A L`: `L` is a `t`-permutation: (P1) the operations of `L` are exactly those invoked in `H`; (P2) `<_{H,t} ⊆ <_L`, where `o₁ <_{H,t} o₂` iff `o₂` is late and the response of `o₁` precedes the invocation of `o₂`. * `IsTLin spec H A L`: `L` is a `t`-linearization: a `t`-permutation in which completed late operations have their results in `H`. `TLinearizable spec H A`. -/ -- CSLib candidate: weak consistency and
