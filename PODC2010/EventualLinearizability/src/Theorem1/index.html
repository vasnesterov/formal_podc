The two lemmas choose the response of the pending operation differently, and the choices can be incompatible. Counterexample (`theorem1_nonblocking_false`). One swap register (`read`, `swap v` writes `v` and returns the old value), initially `0`, three processes, `t = 3` (global time = position of the event): ``` 1 p1: inv swap(1) (i1, pending forever) 2 p0: inv read (o') 3 p0: res 1 4 p0: inv swap(2) (o, pending) 5 p2: inv read (r2) 6 p2: res 2 7 p2: inv read (r3) 8 p2: res 1 ``` `H` is weakly consistent and `3`-linearizable (`o', o, r2, i1, r3`). swap precedes `o`. * weak consistency forces `o` to return `1`: `τ(p0, o')` must end with `o'` returning `1`, so it contains `i1`; by (iii) `τ(p0, o)` contains `i1` before `o`, and `i1` is the only
