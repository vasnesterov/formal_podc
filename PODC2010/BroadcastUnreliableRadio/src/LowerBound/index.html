The execution is built in stages (`Stage k`): after stage `k` we have a placement `π` of processes on the nodes of layers `≤ k` (extended arbitrarily), a delivery rule `D`, and a time `t ≥ k (m-1)` at which exactly the processes of layers `≤ k` may be informed and one of them is about to send alone. A stage extends the execution by the pair of `exists_pair` (Claim 13), placed on layer `k + 1`, until one of the two sends alone (at least `L + 1 = m - 1` rounds later). If at some point the next isolation never happens, broadcast fails outright.
