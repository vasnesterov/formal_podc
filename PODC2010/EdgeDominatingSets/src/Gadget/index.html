Such a graph covers the one-node multigraph with loops `(·, 2j) ↔ (·, 2j+1)`. We give the 2-factorisation in closed form. Parameters: `m`, a shift `s : ℤ/m`, and `k` with `2k ≤ m`. Nodes: `a_x` for `x ∈ ℤ/m` and `b_y` for `y ∈ ℤ/m`, `y ≠ 0`. Writing `t^1` for `t xor 1`: * port `t` of `a_x` leads to port `t^1` of `b_{x+s+t}` if `x + s + t ≠ 0`; otherwise to port `t^1` of `a_{x-1}` (`t` even) or `a_{x+1}` (`t` odd); * port `t` of `b_y` leads to port `t^1` of `a_{y-s-t^1}`. Factor `j` is the bipartite circulant with differences `{s + 2j, s + 2j + 1}` with the node `b_0` removed and its two neighbours joined. For `s = 0`, `m = 2k` this is `K_{2k,2k-1}` plus a perfect matching on the `a`-side (the graph of Theorem 1); for `s = 1`, `m = 2k+1` it is the component `H(ℓ)` of Theorem 2 (with `c = a_0`). -/ namespace Podc.EdgeDominatingSets open Cslib.PortNumbering Finset namespace Gadget /-- `fl t = t xor 1`: port `2j` is paired with port `2j + 1`. -/ def fl (t : ℕ) : ℕ := if t % 2 = 0 then t + 1 else t - 1 theorem fl_fl (t : ℕ) : fl (fl t) = t := by unfold fl;
