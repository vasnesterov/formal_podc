import Papers.PODC2010.EdgeDominatingSets.Phases /-! # The three phases of the algorithm of Theorem 5 (§7.1), as global processes * Phase I (`matchStep`, `mphase1`): greedy matching along the matchings `M_G(i, j)`. Invariant: `M` is a matching (`isMatching_mphase1`); property (b) of §7.3 (`odd_cover_mphase1`). * Phase II (`propStep`, iterated over pairs `(i, q)` with `i ≤ Δ`, `q < Δ`): in step `(i, q)` every *black* node (degree `i`, unmatched) proposes along its port `q` to a *white* neighbour (degree `< i`, unmatched); every white node accepts the proposal arriving at its smallest port. This is a variant of the proposal algorithm of §7.1 (a black node proposes along port `q` even if the neighbour there is not white; the step is then wasted); it preserves maximality. Invariant: matching (`isMatching_propStep`). Result (`maximal_mphase2`): afterwards no edge joins two unmatched nodes of different degrees, which gives property (c). * Phase III (`phase3`): the 2-matching `P` of §7.1 (a variant of the paper's proposal algorithm that preserves its properties), a proposal algorithm on the subgraph `H` of edges with both endpoints unmatched: in step `q` every node that has no accepted proposal yet proposes along its port `q`; every node that has not accepted a proposal yet accepts the one arriving at its smallest port. `pin u = some p`: `u` accepted the proposal arriving at port `p`; `pout v = some q`: the proposal of `v` along port `q` was accepted.
