Perelman, Fan, Keidar — On Maintaining Multiple Versions in STM

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorems 1 and 2 hold with repaired proofs; Theorem 3 is false for Definition 4 as printed but holds for Section 7's potential-reader UP GC. The UP-MV algorithm was not checked.

81

O'Hearn, Rinetzky, Vechev, Yahav, Yorsh — Verifying Linearizability with Hindsight

✏️ Slip. Linearizability, memory safety and the Hindsight Lemma all hold; the Backbone Lemma as printed is false, but the weak chain that its proof gives and Theorem 7.5 uses is what was meant.

67

Barenboim, Elkin — Deterministic Distributed Vertex Coloring in Polylogarithmic Time

🔧 Easy fix. Sections 3–4 hold up to Corollary 4.4, whose case split needed a corrected threshold. The polylogarithmic corollaries answering Linial's question (Theorem 4.5, Corollaries 4.6, 4.7) were not checked.

60

Kuhn, Lynch, Newport, Oshman, Richa — Broadcasting in Unreliable Radio Networks

🔧 Easy fix. Theorems 2, 4, 10, 12 hold: Ω(n log n) and Ω(n) lower bounds and strong select. Proofs of Theorems 4, 10, 12 need local repairs; randomized O(n log² n) algorithm not checked.

26

Imbs, Raynal, Taubenfeld — On Asymmetric Progress Conditions

🛠️ Hard fix. All main results hold for n ≥ 2, but Theorem 2, the (n,x)-liveness hierarchy, needed a new model of blocking base objects; Lemma 6 and the arbiter specification have easy fixes.

23

Serafini, Dobre, Majuntke, Bokor, Suri — Eventually Linearizable Shared Objects

❌ Intent lost. Algorithm 1 is not weakly consistent, so Eventual Consensus is not shown sufficient; nonblocking and Lemma 5 are false. Locality and Theorem 2 hold; Theorem 4 and Aurora are unchecked.

21

Bhatt, Huang — Group Mutual Exclusion in O(log n) RMR

❌ Intent lost. The final algorithm (Figure 3) deadlocks with two processes and two sessions, so starvation freedom fails, and with four processes a single attempt incurs unboundedly many RMRs in the CC model. Mutual exclusion (via a new inductive invariant) and bounded exit hold; relaxed FCFS and pulling were not checked.

14

Suomela — Distributed Algorithms for Edge Dominating Sets

✏️ Slip. All algorithms and matching lower bounds hold as stated; the only flaw is a division by zero on edgeless graphs in the ratio computation of Section 7.8.

10

Alon, Emek, Feldman, Tennenholtz — Bayesian Ignorance

🔧 Easy fix. The checked bounds and "ignorance is bliss" hold. Rosenthal's potential fails with infinite costs but works on connecting profiles; several proofs need local repairs. Lemma 3.4 and the online bound behind Lemma 3.5 unchecked.

10

Aspnes — A Modular Approach to Shared-Memory Consensus, with Applications to the Probabilistic-Write Model

✏️ Slip. Two slips only; the composition, adopt-commit and conciliator results hold, but the end-to-end consensus bound was checked only conditionally on a per-stage failure hypothesis.

4

Danek — The k-Bakery: Local-Spin k-Exclusion Using Non-atomic Reads and Writes

❌ Intent lost. Figure 3, the non-atomic algorithm of the title, violates k-exclusion with 3 processes and k = 1; both atomic algorithms (Theorems 8, 9) are fully proved.

2

Koutris, Pagourtzis — Brief Announcement: k-Shot Distributed Broadcasting in Radio Networks

🛠️ Hard fix. Checked on the arXiv full version: the oblivious Θ(n²/k) bounds and the adaptive Ω(n^{1+1/k}) lower bound all hold, but the progress Lemma 4.2 behind the upper bound and the layer-summation proof of the adaptive bound needed new arguments.

1

Floréen, Kaski, Polishchuk, Suomela — Brief Announcement: Almost Stable Matchings in Constant Time

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 6 fails for Δ = 1 at i = 1, and the proofs of Theorems 1 and 2 inherit this; requiring i ≥ 2 fixes it. All three theorems hold with their constants.

1

Bar-Noy, Cheilaris, Feng, Levin — Finding mobile data under delay constraints with searching costs

🛠️ Hard fix. FRO's tight 8/7 (arbitrary tokens) and (4+√7)/6 (typical tokens) two-round ratios hold for every N, but the paper's proof via the reduction to N = 3, 4 (Lemma 4) has a false step and Lemmas 5–6 are omitted; we re-proved both theorems by a direct bracketing argument.
