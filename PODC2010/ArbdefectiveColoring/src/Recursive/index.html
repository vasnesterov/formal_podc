Afterwards all subgraphs are coloured legally in parallel, with disjoint palettes (lines 17–20). We keep the subgraph index of `v` as a label `lab i G v < p^i`; the current subgraphs are the classes of `classGraph G (lab i G)`. The parameters are bundled in `LCParams`: for an arboricity bound `α`, the H-partition uses degree parameter `A α` and `L α` rounds, the defective colouring black box `D α` (a `d α`-defective colouring with colours `< K α` in `R α` rounds), and the final legal colouring black box `B α`. The arboricity bounds follow line 15: `α_{i+1} = d α_i + ⌊A α_i / p⌋` (Corollary 3.6). * `lab_spec` (**Lemma 4.2(1)**): after `i` iterations, every subgraph has arboricity `≤ α_i`, and there are at most `p^i` subgraphs. * `legalColoring_correct` (correctness part of **Theorem 4.3**): the result is a legal colouring with fewer than `p^I · (A α_I + 1)` colours. * `legalColoringProc_isLocal`: the
