Differences between the formalization and the paper

Rendered from differencies.md of Group Mutual Exclusion in O(log n) RMR — PODC 2010.

Paper: Bhatt, Huang. Group Mutual Exclusion in O(log n) RMR. PODC 2010 (authors' copy, 10 pages).

Mistakes (with labels)

  • ❌ Theorem 1 (P2) / Lemma 3 — starvation freedom is false: a fair execution with 2 processes and 2 sessions deadlocks (starvationFree_false, Deadlock.lean). The proof of Claim 4 overlooks a line-10 SC(Ex,false) of a fellow process between q[16.3] and q[16.6] (a fellow's successful SC(Ex,false) at line 16.2 in that window has the same effect; in deadSched both happen).

  • ❌ Lemma 2 (P4) as stated, Claim 3 — false in the paper's formulation "no conflicting process active at time t" (concurrentEnteringPaper_false, claim3_false). In the strict reading (no conflicting process during the whole attempt) P4 is unchecked (exhaustive model checking with 2 processes found no violation), but its proof via Claim 3 is broken either way.

  • ❌ Invariant 8 — false (invariant8_false).

  • ❌ Theorem 1 / Lemma 5 (RMR) — the RMRs of one attempt are unbounded for n = 4 (rmr_unbounded, not_bounded_rmr, RMR.lean); line 7 rewrites Out[s] repeatedly while a process of the other side spins on it.

  • ✏️ Invariant 1 — false for processes at lines 1.2–1.3 (counted, no side); replaced by the exact count Inv.gC.

  • ✏️ Invariant 6 — the winner may be at line 16.14 (range 16.10–16.13 too small); our LI.win.

  • ✏️ Invariant 7 — false for processes in LE but not in LS (S may have been switched by Promote-Try); correct with p ∈ LS (LI.e1, LI.e2).

  • 🛠️ Theorem 1 (P1) — "follows easily from Invariant 5", but the invariants are unproved; proved with a new inductive invariant Inv (mutual_exclusion).

Modelling choices

  • Processes Fin n, sessions an arbitrary type J with decidable equality (the paper's bound m on the number of sessions is not needed for P1/P3; counterexamples use J = Fin 2).

  • One atomic step per numbered line; line 1.8 (return d) merged with line 1.7; the CS is one step cs.

  • G, C[s,d], Q are atomic objects; the f-array implementations (Theorems 2–3) are not modelled. C[s,d] ∈ ℕ with truncated decrement (never truncates in reachable states, by Inv.gC).

  • Q is one slot per process holding (s, d, tm); Min(Q) is computed by a scan keeping the element with strictly smaller time-stamp, so ties go to the smaller process index, as in the paper's order (tm, p).

  • LL/SC: per-process link bits set by LL and cleared for all processes by every successful SC — equivalent to the paper's definition. Line 16.12 is a plain read of S. Lines 9 and 11 are LL(Ex) (as written).

  • Ex = false is none; Exclear[false] is never read (line 12 is only reached with se ≠ false).

  • Waiting (lines 8, 12) is a read that leaves the configuration unchanged when the condition fails.

  • A process in the remainder section that takes a step starts an attempt with an adversary-chosen session.

  • Initial S = (s₀, 0, s₀) for a parameter s₀.

  • Fairness (for P2): every process takes infinitely many steps or stays in the remainder section from some time on (Sched.Fair). Starvation freedom is stated for fair executions in which no process stays in the CS forever.

  • Lemma 2 is formalized in the paper's wording (a process in the trying section, no active conflicting process, takes at most b steps); we refute it with a solo run.

  • RMRs: write-invalidate cache-coherent model restricted to reads of Out at line 8 by one process and to writes of Out (lines 5.5, 7, 16.11, 16.13); this is a lower bound on the process's RMRs. The counter is reset when the process starts an attempt, so it counts the RMRs of the current attempt.

  • P3 is stated as: 17 own steps of a process in the exit section bring it back to the remainder section, whatever the other processes do.

Not formalized

  • P5' (relaxed FCFS) — the paper gives no proof.

  • P6 (pulling, Lemma 4, Claims 9–10) — unchecked; random simulation (3–4 processes, ~2.5M premise instances) found no violation.

  • Concurrent entering in the strict sense (no conflicting process during the whole attempt) — unchecked; exhaustive model checking with 2 processes found no violation.

  • Invariants 2 and 4 are not stated separately; they follow from clauses of Inv (a3, e2, e3, g7; p5, p11, and for line 16.13 the weaker p13). The model checker confirms Invariants 2–5 and Lemma 1 on all reachable configurations with 3 processes and 2 sessions.

  • The f-array implementations of Theorems 2–3 and their RMR bounds.

  • The first two "attempts" (Figures 1–2) and their failure scenarios.
