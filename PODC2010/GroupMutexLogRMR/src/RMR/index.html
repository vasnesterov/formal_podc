import Papers.PODC2010.GroupMutexLogRMR.Model /-! # The RMR bound of Theorem 1 / Lemma 5 fails: unboundedly many RMRs at line 8 Lemma 5 of the paper claims that the busy-waiting loops at lines 8 and 12 cost O(1) RMRs ("By the Claims 6 and 8 … RMR associated with these loops is indeed just 2"). In the cache-coherent model every write to `Out[s]` by another process invalidates the cached copy of a process spinning on `Out[s]`, so its next read is remote, even if the written value is the same. Line 7 (`Out[s] ← d`) is executed by every process that finds `S = (s, d, s)` at line 6, and such processes may keep coming while a fellow process of the *other* side waits at line 8. Four processes, two sessions (`S = (0,0,0)` initially, `In[0] = 1`): * `r = 0` (session 0) joins side 0 (lines 1.5–1.6, since `S = (0, 0̄̄, −)`), takes time-stamp 1 at line 3 and pauses before enqueuing; * `q = 1` (session 1, side 1, time-stamp 2) enqueues; it cannot promote (`C[0,0] = 1` because of `r`) and waits at line 8; * `p = 2` (session 0) reads `In[0] = 1`, finds `Min(Q) = (1,1)` at line 1.4 and keeps side 1 (time-stamp 3); it waits at line 8 for `Out[0] = 1`; * `r` enqueues (it is now the smallest element), finds `S = (0,0,0)` at line 6, writes `Out[0] ← 0` and enters the CS, where it stays; * round: `f = 3` (session 0) finds `Min(Q) = (0,0)` (= `r`) at line 1.4 and `S = (0,0,−)` at line 1.5, joins side 0, writes `Out[0] ← 0` at line 7, passes the CS and leaves (no flip at line 14, since `Min(Q) = (0,−)`; no promotion, since `C[0,0] = 1`). Then `p` re-reads `Out[0]` at line 8: a remote reference. The round leaves the configuration unchanged except for `G`. After `K` rounds, `p` has made `K` remote reads in a single attempt, while `n = 4` and the point contention is at most 4. So no bound of the form O(min(log n, k)) — indeed no bound depending only on `n` — holds for the number of RMRs per attempt. ## Cost model `Cost.next` is the standard write-invalidate cache-coherent model restricted to the `Out` variables and to the reads of one process `p` at line 8 (a lower bound on `p`'s RMRs): a read of `Out[s]` at line 8 by `p` is remote iff `p` has no valid cached copy; afterwards it has one; a write to `Out[x]` (lines 5.5, 7, 16.11, 16.13) by another process invalidates `p`'s copy of `Out[x]`.
