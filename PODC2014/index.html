Drucker, Kuhn, Oshman — On the Power of the Congested Clique Model

⚠️ Weakened. Circuit simulation and clique and cycle lower bounds hold after local repairs; the K_{ℓ,m} gadget is broken for ℓ ≠ m, and the degeneracy upper bound is proved only for connected H.

160

Mostéfaoui, Moumen, Raynal — Signature-Free Asynchronous Byzantine Consensus with t < n/3 and O(n²) Messages

❌ Intent lost. Figure 2 never terminates against the paper's own adversary (n = 4, t = 1), so Lemma 5 and Theorems 2 and 3 fail; BV-broadcast and safety are proved.

34

Emek, Pfister, Seidel, Wattenhofer — Anonymous Networks: Randomization = 2-Hop Coloring

🔧 Easy fix. Theorems 1 and 2 hold; the proof of Lemma 9 has a view-depth off-by-one for the printed A*, repaired by using one more view level at the same round cost.

32

Aghazadeh, Golab, Woelfel — Making Objects Writable

🛠️ Hard fix. Linearizability of the transformation holds, but Lemma 4.5's proof is broken and needed a new linearization argument; the wait-freedom and step-complexity claims were not checked.

24

Newport — Consensus with an Abstract MAC Layer

🛠️ Hard fix. Figure 1's networks fail for D = 4, so Theorem 3.3 needed a new gadget. All lower bounds and the single-hop algorithm hold after small repairs. wPAXOS was not checked.

24

Hilke, Lenzen, Suomela — Brief Announcement: Local Approximability of Minimum Dominating Set on Planar Graphs

✏️ Slip. The (7 − ε) lower bound holds as stated; Lemma 1 only needs the implied assumption m ≥ 2T + 3.

23

Laurinharju, Suomela — Brief Announcement: Linial's Lower Bound Made Easy

✏️ Slip. Linial's bound T ≥ log*(n)/2 − 1, the reduction to colouring functions and Lemmas 1–2 hold for every n; Lemma 2 is stated for every k but fails in the unused case k = 1.

21

Ghaffari, Kantor, Lynch, Newport — Multi-Message Broadcast with Abstract MAC Layers and Unreliable Links

⚠️ Weakened. BMMB's upper bounds hold after local repairs. The grey zone lower bound's schedule breaks for two or more messages per broadcast; its one-message case and Section 4 are unchecked.

15

Michail, Spirakis — Simple and Efficient Local Codes for Distributed Stable Network Construction

🛠️ Hard fix. Global-Ring (Theorem 10) is false for n = 6; 2RC still gives the ring, the other checked protocols hold, kRC needs a new proof; running times and universality unchecked.

14

Göös, Hirvonen, Suomela — Linear-in-Δ Lower Bounds in the LOCAL Model

🔧 Easy fix. Omega(Delta) lower bound proved for deterministic algorithms; one step of the unfold-and-mix proof needs a short nested induction. The randomized extension was not checked.

8

Miller, Pelc — Time Versus Cost Tradeoffs for Deterministic Rendezvous in Networks

🔧 Easy fix. Both lower bounds and all algorithms hold; Proposition 2.3's cost bound needs the constant 8w+6, and 10 sectors instead of 6 avoid the false Fact 3.11 in Theorem 3.2's proof.

8

Schwarz, Winkler, Schmid, Biely, Robinson — Brief Announcement: Gracefully Degrading Consensus and k-Set Agreement under Dynamic Link Failures

❌ Intent lost. The gracefully degrading algorithm (Alg. 4) violates agreement already for consensus under VSRC(n, 3D + H) + MAJINF(1), so Theorem 11 is false; the impossibilities (Theorems 4, 7, 8, 9) and Theorem 10 hold, partly with new proofs, but the sharper VSRC'(H − 1) claim is lost.

5

Guerraoui, Ruppert — A Paradox of Eventual Linearizability in Shared Memory

🔧 Easy fix. Lemma 17's proof has a wrong step, repaired locally, and partial operations must be excluded. Theorem 12 and Proposition 18 hold; Proposition 15 (no consensus boost) is unchecked.

2

Hendler, Khait — Complexity Tradeoffs for Read and Update Operations

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemma 2 and Claim 1 are false as defined and Lemmas 4, 6 and Theorem 5's proof break; all tradeoffs survive a redesign, except Theorem 4 for huge M with slow ReadMax.

0

Raynal, Stainer, Taubenfeld — Brief Announcement: Distributed Universality: Contention-Awareness; Wait-Freedom; Object Progress, and Other Properties

⚠️ Weakened. Figure 2 is not contention-aware, a property in the title, and no repair is proved; linearizability, wait-freedom, progress of l objects and the necessity and sufficiency of (k,l)-SC hold, some with new proofs.
