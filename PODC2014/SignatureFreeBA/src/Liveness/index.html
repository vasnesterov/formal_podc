Its proof has two parts: 1. no correct process blocks forever in a round (the waits of lines 03 and 05 terminate); 2. once all correct processes have the same estimate `v`, they all decide in the first later round whose coin is `v`. Both are correct and proved here for every weakly fair execution and every coin sequence: `Exec.no_blocking` and `Exec.decide_of_unanimous`. In particular, with unanimous inputs every correct process decides in the first round whose coin is the common input (`Exec.decide_of_unanimous_inputs`).
