import Papers.PODC2014.SignatureFreeBA.Safety /-! # Theorem 1: the BV-broadcast algorithm of Figure 1 Mostéfaoui, Moumen, Raynal, PODC 2014, Section 3. The BV-broadcast instance of round `r` consists of the messages `B_VAL[r](·)`. A process *BV-broadcasts* `v` in round `r` when it broadcasts its own estimate `bval r v (some _)` (Figure 1, line 01), and `v ∈ bin_values[r]` at `q` is `BinVal t (inbox q) r v`. * `bv_justification` (safety, every reachable state). * `Exec.bv_obligation`, `Exec.bv_uniformity`, `Exec.bv_termination` (liveness, every weakly fair execution). * `theorem_1` collects the four properties. -/ namespace
