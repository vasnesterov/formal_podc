import Papers.PODC2014.EventualLinParadox.Basic /-! # Lemma 17: limits of `t`-linearizable fetch&increment histories Guerraoui, Ruppert. *A Paradox of Eventual Linearizability in Shared Memory.* PODC 2014. **Lemma 17.** If a history `H` of a fetch&increment object is `t₀`-linearizable for some `t₀` (e.g. a history of an eventually linearizable implementation) and every finite prefix of `H` is `t`-linearizable, then `H` is `t`-linearizable. We follow the paper's proof: operations with a response at a position `≥ t` ("late" operations) are put at the slot equal to their response value; the remaining slots `E` are filled first by the operations with a response before `t` (`A1`) and then by the pending operations (`A4`) in the order of their invocations. The paper's argument for the real-time order between a late operation `op₁` and a pending operation `op₂` contains a gap (a late operation that is still pending in the prefix considered by the paper may occupy a slot of `E`); we repair it by considering a prefix that contains the responses of all late operations invoked before `op₁`'s response (`count_E_le`). -/ namespace Podc.EventualLinParadox
