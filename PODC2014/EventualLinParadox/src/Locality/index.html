import Papers.PODC2014.EventualLinParadox.Basic /-! # Locality (Section 3.2: Lemmas 7, 8, Proposition 9) Guerraoui, Ruppert. *A Paradox of Eventual Linearizability in Shared Memory.* PODC 2014. * `weaklyConsistent_iff_restrict` — **Lemma 8**: `H` is weakly consistent iff every `H|x` is. The paper's "if" proof inserts the process's earlier operations on other objects "with responses as dictated by the objects' sequential specifications", which needs total operations. We avoid totality: for every other object `y` we take the weak-consistency witness (in `H|y`) of the process's *last* operation on `y` before `op`; it contains all earlier operations of the process on `y`. These finitely many witnesses (on pairwise distinct objects) are concatenated, followed by the witness of `op` in `H|x`. * `TLinearizable.restrict`, `exists_tLinearizable_of_restrict` — **Lemma 7** (from Serafini et al.), for finitely many objects and for finite or infinite histories. The "if" direction needs well-formedness (a response without invocation can never be linearized but is invisible in every `H|x`). The merge of the per-object linearizations orders an entry `e` of `S_x` by `key(e)`, the largest "late" operation (invoked at a position `≥ t`) at or before `e` in `S_x`. * `eventuallyLinearizable_iff_restrict` — **Proposition 9** (finitely many objects). * `prop9_false_infinite_objects` — the paper's counterexample with infinitely many registers. -/ namespace
