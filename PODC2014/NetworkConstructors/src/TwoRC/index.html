Hence the closed set `S` of kRC transports to 2RC, and Theorem 12 for `k = 2` gives an almost 2-regular network: all agents but at most one have degree 2 and the remaining one has degree at most 1. By the handshake lemma that agent would have even degree, hence degree 0, which is impossible in a connected network on `n ≥ 3` agents.
