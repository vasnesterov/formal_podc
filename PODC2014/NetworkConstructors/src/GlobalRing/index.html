import Papers.PODC2014.NetworkConstructors.Problems /-! # Protocol 6 (Global-Ring) does not construct a spanning ring (Theorem 10 is false) Theorem 10 of Michail–Spirakis claims that Protocol Global-Ring constructs a spanning ring. The closing rule `(l, q₁, 0) → (l', q₁', 1)` is meant to join the two endpoints of one line, but an `l`-endpoint and a `q₁`-endpoint of *different* lines may interact as well; then the two lines are joined with two blocked nodes in the middle. A blocked `q₁''` node that ends up as an internal node of degree 2 can never be unblocked (its only unblocking rule needs an active edge to an `l'`/`l''` node), and with `n = 6` agents the scheduler can reach a closed set of 8 configurations in which the active network alternates forever between a spanning line and a spanning ring. By global fairness, some fair execution visits all of them infinitely often, so its output never stabilizes (`globalRing_not_constructs`). -/ namespace Podc.NetworkConstructors.GlobalRing open Cslib
