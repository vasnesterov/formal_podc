The output states are `{q₂}` (the paper leaves `Q_out` implicit; with `Q_out = Q` the two wasted agents would be part of the output, which then is not a cycle cover). Every effective rule activates an inactive edge between two agents of degree `< 2`, so the configurations in which the agents of degree `< 2` are pairwise adjacent are silent and are reached.
