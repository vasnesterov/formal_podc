Invariant (`Inv`), as in the paper: the active graph is a forest of paths (acyclic, the degree of an agent is determined by its state: `q₀ ↦ 0`, `q₁, l ↦ 1`, `q₂, w ↦ 2`), and every non-trivial component contains exactly one *leader* (`l` or `w`). The final configurations (no `q₀`, a unique leader) have a spanning line as active graph and only the leader moves (`final_step_edge`). From every reachable configuration a final one is reachable: move a `w` leader along its line to an endpoint (`move_w`), let a leader absorb an isolated agent, or merge two lines. -/ namespace
