The random bits are independent fair coins, one per node and round: `bitMeasure V` is the product measure on `V × ℕ → Bool`. `SolvesAS` is this definition; `Solves` (`Model.lean`) is the combinatorial version used in the proofs. `SolvesAS.solves`: the probabilistic definition implies the combinatorial one (every cylinder of finitely many fixed bits has positive probability). Hence `GRAN_AS.gran`: membership in GRAN in the paper's (probabilistic) sense implies our `GRAN`, so Theorems 1 and 2 hold for the paper's definition.
