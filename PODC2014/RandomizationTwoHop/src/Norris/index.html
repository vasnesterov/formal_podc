We prove it by colour refinement: the partitions of the nodes by their depth-`k` views get finer with `k`; once two consecutive partitions agree they agree forever (`TEq` is defined by a uniform refinement step from tree depth `0`, i.e. from `L_1`), and a strictly finer partition has more classes. With at most `n` classes the partition is stable from tree depth `n - 1` on, i.e. from `L_n` on (`teq_stable`). The same argument on the disjoint union gives a two-graph version (`teq_stable_two`): views of nodes of graphs with `m` and `n` nodes that agree to tree depth `m + n - 1` agree forever.
