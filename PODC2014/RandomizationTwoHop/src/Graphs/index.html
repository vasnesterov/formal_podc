import Mathlib -- CSLib candidate (paper-local for now; see README) /-! # Labelled graphs, 2-hop colourings, factors and prime graphs Emek, Pfister, Seidel, Wattenhofer, *Anonymous Networks: Randomization = 2-Hop Coloring*, PODC 2014, §1.1 and §2.3.1. * `LGraph L`: a labelled graph `G = (V, E, ℓ)`: finite, connected, simple, labels in `L`. * `IsKHopColoring G k c`: `c u ≠ c v` whenever `u ≠ v` are joined by a walk of length `≤ k`; `IsTwoHopColoring` is the case `k = 2` (`isTwoHopColoring_iff`: adjacent nodes and nodes with a common neighbour get different colours). * `IsLocalIso`: a label-preserving map whose restriction to every neighbourhood `Γ(v)` is a bijection onto `Γ(f v)` (a covering map). `IsFactorMap X Y f`: a surjective local isomorphism; then `Y` is a *factor* of `X` (paper: `Y ≤_f X`). * `Isomorphic X Y`, `Prime X` (every factor of `X` is isomorphic to `X`). -/ namespace Podc.RandomizationTwoHop open SimpleGraph /-- A labelled graph (paper §1.1): a finite connected simple graph with a labelling `lab`. -/ structure LGraph (L : Type) where /-- The nodes. -/ V : Type [finite : Finite V] /-- The (simple, undirected) graph. -/ G : SimpleGraph V /-- Only connected graphs are considered. -/ conn : G.Connected /-- The labelling function. -/ lab : V → L attribute [instance] LGraph.finite namespace LGraph variable {L L' : Type} /-- The same graph with label-preserving local isomorphism from `(G, lG)` to `(H, lH)`: for every node `v`, the restriction of `f` to `Γ(v)` is a bijection onto `Γ(f v)` (property 3 of a factorizing map, §2.3.1), and labels Boldi–Vigna) if all its factors are isomorphic to it. -/ def Prime (X : LGraph L) : Prop := ∀ (Y : LGraph L) (f : X.V → Y.V), IsFactorMap X Y f → Isomorphic X Y section variable {V W U : Type} {G : SimpleGraph V} {H : SimpleGraph W} {K : SimpleGraph U} {lG : V → L} {lH : W → L} {lK : U → L} theorem IsLocalIso.adj {f : V → W} (hf : IsLocalIso G lG H lH f) {u v : V} (h : G.Adj u v) : H.Adj (f u) (f v) := (hf.bij u).mapsTo h theorem IsLocalIso.comp {f : V → W} {g : W → U} (hf : IsLocalIso G lG H lH f) (hg : IsLocalIso H lH K lK g) : IsLocalIso G lG K lK (g ∘ f) where lab v := by simp [hg.lab, hf.lab] bij v := (hg.bij (f v)).comp (hf.bij v) theorem IsLocalIso.id : IsLocalIso G lG G lG id where lab _ := rfl bij _ := Set.bijOn_id _ /-- A bijective local isomorphism reflects adjacency. -/ theorem IsLocalIso.adj_iff_of_injective {f : V → W} (hf : IsLocalIso G lG H lH f) (hi : Function.Injective f) {u v : V} : H.Adj (f u) (f v) ↔ G.Adj u v := by refine ⟨fun h => ?_, hf.adj⟩ obtain ⟨x, hx, hxv⟩ := (hf.bij u).surjOn h rw [hi hxv] at hx exact hx /-- The image of a 2-hop coloured graph (with colours read off the labels) under a surjective local isomorphism is 2-hop coloured.
