import Papers.PODC2014.RandomizationTwoHop.Theorem2 /-! # Walk languages: relabelling and truncation locality Algorithm `A*` augments the labels by bit strings `b(v)` that are themselves functions of the nodes' views. On walk languages this is `relabel f T`: every letter of a word is paired with `f` of the sub-language at that position (`relabelWord`). For a 2-hop coloured graph, relabelling the language of `v` by `f` gives the language of `v` in the graph relabelled by `v ↦ (ℓ(v), f(L∞(v)))` (`lang_withLab_relabel`). Truncation locality (`trunc_relabel_congr`): if `f` only looks at depth `d`, then the depth-`k` truncation of `relabel f T` only depends on the depth-`d + k` truncation of `T`. -/ namespace
