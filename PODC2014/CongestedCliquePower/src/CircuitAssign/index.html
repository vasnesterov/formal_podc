import Papers.PODC2014.CongestedCliquePower.Circuit /-! # Assigning the gates of a circuit to the players (proof of Theorem 2 of [DKO14]) The weight of a gate is `w(G) = |in(G)| + |out(G)| + [G is an input gate]`. The total weight is at most `2N + |X| ≤ n² (2s + 1) = n M` for `N ≤ n² s` wires, `|X| ≤ n²` inputs and `M = n (2s + 1)`. A gate is heavy iff `w(G) ≥ M`; hence there are at most `n` heavy gates, and they can be given to distinct players. The light gates are assigned greedily so that the total weight of the light gates of every player is at most `2M`. The paper uses `w(G) = |in(G)| + |out(G)|` and the threshold `n s`; its counting forgets that every wire is counted twice in the total weight (see `heavy_count_counterexample`). The weight `+1` for input gates is needed to bound the number of input gates a player owns. ## Main statements * `exists_greedy_assign`: greedy load balancing (`-- CSLib candidate`). *
