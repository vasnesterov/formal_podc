The paper's proof uses that `ex(n, H)` is non-decreasing in `n` and that `ex(2n, H) ≥ 2 ex(n, H)` (two disjoint copies of an extremal graph). Both facts need assumptions on `H`: for `H = K₂ ∪ K₁` (an edge plus an isolated vertex) `ex(2, H) = 1 > 0 = ex(3, H) = ex(4, H)` (`extremalNumber_edge_plus_point`). We prove Claim 6 for **connected** `H` with at least one edge (`claim_6`), which covers all graphs for which the paper uses it (cycles, cliques, complete bipartite graphs, trees). -/ namespace
