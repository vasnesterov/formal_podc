Oshman, *On the Power of the Congested Clique Model*, PODC 2014. * `LowerBoundGraph H F G'` — Definition 10: two vertex-disjoint copies `F_A = φA(F)`, `F_B = φB(F)` of `F` inside `G'` such that (I) every edge `e` of `F` lies on a copy of `H` whose edges include `φA(e)` and `φB(e)` and whose vertices in `V_A ∪ V_B` are exactly the four endpoints of `φA(e)`, `φB(e)`, and (II) every copy of `H` in `G'` is of this form. * `LowerBoundGraph.observation_11` — Observation 11: a subgraph `G ≤ G'` containing all edges of `G'` outside `E_A ∪ E_B` contains `H` iff `φA(e), φB(e) ∈ E(G)` for some edge `e` of `F`. * `LowerBoundGraph.IsSparse` — Definition 12 (`δ`-sparse). Copies of `H` are Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.Copy` (injective homomorphisms), i.e. not necessarily induced subgraphs isomorphic to `H`, as in the paper ("`G'` contains a subgraph `H'` isomorphic to `H`").
