Oshman, *On the Power of the Congested Clique Model*, PODC 2014, Section 3.5. Lemma 21 states that a `(K_{ℓ,m}, F)`-lower bound graph exists; its proof claims that for a bipartite `C₄`-free graph `F = (L ∪ R, E_F)` and all `ℓ, m ≥ 2` the following graph `G'` is one: two copies `V_A = {u_i}`, `V_B = {v_i}` of `F` (`φA i = u_i`, `φB i = v_i`), the matching `u_i v_i`, and sets `W_L` (`ℓ - 2` nodes) and `W_R` (`m - 2` nodes), with `W_L` complete to `φA(R) ∪ φB(L) ∪ W_R` and `W_R` complete to `φA(L) ∪ φB(R) ∪ W_L` (`lemma21Graph`). The proof claims that each side of a `K_{ℓ,m}` contains at least two nodes of `V_A ∪ V_B` "as `|W_L| = ℓ - 2`, `|W_R| = m - 2`"; but nodes of `W_R` may sit on the `ℓ`-side. We show: * `lemma21_bad_copy_succ` — for `m = ℓ + 1` (in particular `ℓ = 2`, `m = 3`) and any `F` having a vertex `k ∈ R` with two neighbours `i ≠ i'`, the copy `(W_R ∪ {u_k}) × (W_L ∪ {u_i, u_{i'}, v_k})` of `K_{ℓ,m}` uses no edge of `F_B`; `lemma21_bad_copy_succ'` is the symmetric case `ℓ = m + 1`. * `lemma21_bad_copy_free` — for `m ≥ ℓ + 2` and `|V_F| ≥ m - ℓ + 2` there is a copy of `K_{ℓ,m}` using no edge of `F_A` or `F_B` at all. * Consequences (`LBData.not_fits_of_noEB`, `LBData.isContained_inputGraph_of_noEB`): condition II of Definition 10 fails, and the conclusion of Observation 11 fails — the input graph `G(X, Y)` of the reduction with disjoint inputs `X = E_F`, `Y = ∅` (resp. `X = Y = ∅`) contains `K_{ℓ,m}`. * `lemma_21_counterexample` — the concrete instance `ℓ = 2`, `m = 3`, `F = K_{2,1}` (the path `i – k – i'`, `N = 3`), which satisfies all hypotheses of the paper (`ex(3, C₄) = 3`, `extremalNumber_three_cycleGraph_four`).
