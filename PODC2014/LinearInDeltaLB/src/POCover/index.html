import Papers.PODC2014.LinearInDeltaLB.FreeTree import Papers.PODC2014.LinearInDeltaLB.Defs /-! # The PO-view of an EC-graph (Section 5.1) The simulation EC ⇝ PO of Section 5.1 turns every edge `{u, v}` of colour `c` of an EC-graph into the two directed edges `(u, v)` and `(v, u)` of colour `c`; a loop becomes a directed loop. The universal cover of the resulting PO-graph, rooted at `a`, is the set `cover G a ⊆ F_k` of reduced words that can be followed letter by letter from `a` (letters `c` and `c⁻¹` both follow the `c`-edge). We prove that `poWalk` is a partial right action of `F_k` (`poWalk_mul`), so the cover re-rooted at `w` is the cover of the endpoint of `w` (`inv_smul_cover`), that covers of loopy graphs are loopy (`isLoopyF_cover`), that the cover only depends on the EC-view (`cover_tGraph_view`), and that its radius-`t` ball only depends on the radius-`t` ball of the view (`cover_view_inter_ballF`). -/
