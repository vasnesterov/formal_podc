Suomela, *Linear-in-Δ lower bounds in the LOCAL model*, PODC 2014]. We build, for `i = 0, …, k - 2`, pairs of good configurations (finite trees with loops, every node with at least `k - 1 - i` loops) whose roots have equal radius-`i` views, carry a loop of a common colour `c`, and receive different weights on it (invariant `Inv`, properties (P1)–(P3)). * Base case (Section 4.2): `exists_inv_zero`. The paper takes `c₀` to be the colour of the removed loop `e`; but `e` is absent from `H₀`, so we take a colour `c₀ ≠ e` on which the outputs differ (it exists because both nodes are saturated and `y(e) ≠ 0`). * Inductive step (Section 4.3), unfold and mix: `Inv.step`. The propagation principle (Fact 3) is `propagation`; the propagation walk is replaced by choosing a node with an "incoming disagreement" that is farthest from the root. View agreement (equation (3)) is proved by a mutual induction on the radius over the two halves of the mixed graph. * Main results: `ECAlg.exists_pair` and `ECAlg.le_runTime`. -/ namespace
