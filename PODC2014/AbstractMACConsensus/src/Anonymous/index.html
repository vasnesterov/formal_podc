In `A`, hold `q`'s broadcast for `t` rounds, where `t` is such that in the synchronous executions of `B` with constant inputs everyone has decided (Lemma 3.5). During these rounds, each node of copy `b` behaves like its preimages in `B` with all inputs `b` (Lemma 3.6, an instance of `syncLoc_comp`), so copy `0` decides `0` and copy `1` decides `1`.
