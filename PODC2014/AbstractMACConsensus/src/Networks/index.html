import Mathlib /-! # The networks of Theorem 3.3 (Figure 1) Newport, *Consensus with an abstract MAC layer*, PODC 2014, Section 3.2. The lower bound of Theorem 3.3 (no consensus without knowledge of the network size, for every diameter `D` and size) needs, for every even `D ≥ 4` and many sizes `n`, two connected networks `A` and `B` of the same size `n` and the same diameter `D` such that `A` contains two copies of a gadget joined through a bridge node `q`, and `B` *covers* each copy: the projection `p b` maps the neighbours of each node of `B` bijectively onto the neighbours (other than `q`) of its image. The paper's own network (Figure 1) does not have the claimed diameter (`claim_3_4_false_D4` below), so we use a different gadget `Γ`: * nodes `c, t, s, r, u`, pads `pad j` (`j ≤ m`) and path nodes `y 0, …, y (L-1)`; * edges `c – t, s, r, u, pad j`; `t – s`; `u – r`; `r – s`; `pad j – u`; `t – y 0`; `y i – y (i+1)`. `B` is the 3-fold voltage cover of `Γ` (voltage `1` on `u → r` and `r → s`), `A` is two copies of `Γ` joined by `q` at `c`, plus a star of `|Γ| - 1` leaves at `q`. Both have diameter `2L + 4`. * `Nets`: the structure the proof of Theorem 3.3 needs. * `exists_nets`: such networks exist for every `D = 2d + 2 ≥ 4` and `n = 3 (m + d + 5)`. * `claim_3_4_false_D4`:
