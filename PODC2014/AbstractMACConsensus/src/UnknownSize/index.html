The network `K_D` (Figure 2): two lines `L¹_D`, `L²_D` of `D + 1` nodes and a line `L_{D-1}` of `D` nodes, with every node of `L¹_D` and `L²_D` adjacent to the endpoint `e` of `L_{D-1}`; it has diameter `D`, like the line `L_D` itself. In the *semi-synchronous* execution the broadcast of `e` is held for `t` rounds: during them, `L¹_D` (inputs `0`) and `L²_D` (inputs `1`) behave exactly as the line `L_D` alone with inputs `0`, resp. `1` (`syncLoc_comp`), where all nodes decided by round `t` (Lemma 3.8). So agreement fails in `K_D`.
