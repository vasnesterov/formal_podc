55

Gupta, Vaidya — Fault-Tolerance in Distributed Optimization: The Case of Redundancy

⚠️ Weakened. Theorem 2 (CGC convergence) holds; Theorem 1 is vacuous literally, so '2f-redundancy is necessary' survives only as per-execution indistinguishability with exactly f faults and a common minimum point.

35

Brandt, Olivetti — Truly Tight-in-Δ Bounds for Bipartite Maximal Matching and Variants

🔧 Easy fix. Exact bound T_Δ(x, y), so 2Δ − 1 for maximal matching, holds in the port-numbering model after local fixes to §5's output rule and Lemma 4.3. LOCAL lifting was not checked.

31

Dinitz, Robelle — Efficient and Simple Algorithms for Fault-Tolerant Spanners

🔧 Easy fix. Correctness and the size bound of the modified greedy spanner hold; Lemma 7's proof needs kf = o(n) instead of f = o(n). Running times and distributed results were not checked.

18

Chen, Chen — Self-Stabilizing Leader Election in Regular Graphs

⚠️ Weakened. SS-Tree, the O(k^12) protocol, is refuted: all-root configurations keep every node a leader forever. SS-EulerCycle fails as printed but a one-line repair works, proved for even degree assuming the ring protocol.

16

Ben-Baruch, Hendler, Rusanovsky — Upper and Lower Bounds on the Space Complexity of Detectable Objects

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm 2 is not linearizable on Cas(v, v) and Algorithm 1's initialization is inconsistent; local fixes save both, and the Omega(N) and auxiliary-state lower bounds are proved.

14

Chan, Woelfel — Recoverable Mutual Exclusion with Constant Amortized RMR Complexity from Standard Primitives

🔧 Easy fix. Algorithm correct; O(1) amortized RMRs proved in DSM once a crash also starts a passage, a one-clause change to the definition. The CC bound was not checked.

12

Sakavalas, Tseng, Vaidya — Asynchronous Byzantine Approximate Consensus in Directed Networks

🔧 Easy fix. 3-reach is tight and Algorithm BW works after local fixes to message sets, covers, FIFO-Receive and several proof steps. End-to-end sufficiency is proved only for FIFO links; non-FIFO links were not checked.

11

Michail, Skretas, Spirakis — Distributed Computation and Reconfiguration in Actively Dynamic Networks

⚠️ Weakened. GraphToStar fails on a 4-node line, no repair proved; its time analysis and the distributed Ω(n log n) bound are unestablished. Centralized lower bounds and subroutines hold; Sections 4–5 are unchecked.

10

Madsen, Debois — On the Subject of Non-Equivocation: Defining Non-Equivocation in Synchronous Agreement Systems

🔧 Easy fix. The round and resilience bounds hold. Theorem 6.1 is false for n ≤ 3, Lemmas 4.7 and 4.8 fail for ⊥, and the authenticated model drops crash runs; all are fixed by local conditions.

9

Bar-Yehuda, Censor-Hillel, Maus, Pai, Pemmaraju — Distributed Approximation on Power Graphs

🔧 Easy fix. All checked statements hold; Corollary 17's proof misapplies Theorem 12 but its 5/3 bound is saved by a new accounting. Round complexities and the MDS half unchecked.

9

Balliu, Brandt, Efron, Hirvonen, Maus, Olivetti, Suomela — Brief Announcement: Classification of Distributed Binary Labeling Problems

🔧 Easy fix. All checked statements hold; proofs of Theorems 8.1 and 8.2 need short local repairs. The Θ(n) families and round-elimination fixed points are proved; the complete classification itself is unchecked.

7

Dufoulon, Burman, Beauquier — Can Uncoordinated Beeps tell Stories?

✏️ Slip. USI codes from Sidon sets hold with length 8h²(k+1)²; only a mod-l typo in Lemma 4 and degenerate cases are wrong. The beeping algorithms of Sections 4–6 were not checked.

7

Pacut, Parham, Schmid — Brief Announcement: Deterministic Lower Bound for Dynamic Balanced Graph Partitioning

🔧 Easy fix. The Ω(kℓ) lower bound holds with a slightly better constant, but its adversary needs two local repairs; O(kℓ) for PPL is proved. Theorem 3.2 (O(ℓ) for k = 3) was not checked.

7

Efron, Grossman, Khoury — Beyond Alice and Bob: Improved Inapproximability for Maximum Independent Set in CONGEST

🛠️ Hard fix. Theorems 1 and 2 hold, but the proofs miscount the cut, giving only log⁴ denominators; a constant-alphabet code saves and improves both bounds. The t-party framework stands.

5

Dolan — The Only Undoable CRDTs are Counters

✏️ Slip. The main theorem holds: every undoable CRDT is a tuple of counters; two Section 3 consequences miss operations that apply in no state.

5

Khanchandani, Wattenhofer — Byzantine Agreement with Unknown Participants and Failures

🛠️ Hard fix. Rotor-coordinator lemma and theorem are false as written and the consensus fill rule breaks agreement; a new stopping rule and good-round argument save both algorithms with n > 3f and their round bounds.

5

Godard, Imbs, Raynal, Taubenfeld — From Bezout's Identity to Space-Optimal Election in Anonymous Memory Systems

🛠️ Hard fix. All theorems hold, including the gcd characterizations and one-register election; the termination proofs of both algorithms and the safety proof of Algorithm 2 needed new arguments.

5

Baïg, Hendler, Milani, Travers — Long-Lived Snapshots with Polylogarithmic Amortized Step Complexity

🛠️ Hard fix. Lemmas 3.3 and 3.4 are false. Linearizability of the max array needed a new proof; wait-freedom and the snapshot hold after local repairs. The amortized bounds are checked only in their counting cores.

4

Huang, Huang, Wei — Fine-grained Analysis on Fast Implementations of Distributed Multi-writer Atomic Registers

⚠️ Weakened. The W2R1 algorithm is not atomic, so the fast-read condition loses its sufficiency half; Theorem 1, the fast-write impossibility, is true and proved with a new construction replacing §4.

4

Perrin, Mostéfaoui, Bonin — Extending the Wait-free Hierarchy to Multi-Threaded Systems

⚠️ Weakened. Proposition 4's proof ignores run-time allocation and holds only for static memory; other formalized results hold, while the universal construction, the placements themselves and Section 8 are unchecked.

4

Alistarh, Aspnes, Ellen, Gelashvili, Zhu — Brief Announcement: Why Extension-Based Proofs Fail

🛠️ Hard fix. The limitation result stands, but the adversary needs a new output-query rule and Lemma 4.13 a new proof; provers interleaving an infinite chain with others are not covered in Lean.

3

Shimi, Castañeda — K-set agreement bounds in round-based models through combinatorial topology

❌ Intent lost. General lower bounds (Theorems 5.4, 6.11) are false on a single star and the tight bound for stars loses its proof; all upper bounds and the single-graph lower bounds hold.

2

Rinberg, Keidar — Brief Announcement: Intermediate Value Linearizability: A Quantitative Correctness Criterion

🔧 Easy fix. All results hold (Omega(n) bound relative to the cited Israeli-Shirazi bound); the locality proof, Example 2's witness and two proof descriptions needed short local repairs.

1

Attiya, Kumari, Somani, Welch — Brief Announcement: Collect in the Presence of Continuous Churn with Application to Snapshots and Lattice Agreement

❌ Intent lost. CCC is not regular: a 5-node run without churn or crashes refutes Theorem 6 and Corollary 7, and no repair is proved. The snapshot over any regular store-collect and the churn arithmetic hold.

0

Moses, Zamir — Probably Approximately Knowing

🔧 Easy fix. All numbered theorems hold; a §7 claim about every protocol of Example 1 is false for mixed actions and holds only for the belief that Bob fires; the abstract omits a hypothesis.

0

Goldweber, Zhang, Kapritsos — Brief Announcement: On the Significance of Consecutive Ballots in Paxos

🔧 Easy fix. Consecutive Quorums keep Paxos safe; Definition 6 as printed makes Lemmas 4.2′ and 4.3′ false, the Lemma 4.3′ proof misses a case, and the printed TLA+ invariant is vacuous; all fixed locally.

0

Goren, Moses — Brief Announcement: On Using Null Messages in a Byzantine Setting

✅ Correct. Theorem 1.2 on silent validation rounds and Theorem 2.1 on SmallCouncil hold; the paper gives no proofs, so we reconstructed them.
