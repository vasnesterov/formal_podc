round-based models through combinatorial topology*, PODC 2020 (full version arXiv:2003.02869), Sections 2, 3, 5 and 6. ## Model * `n = |α|` processes; a *communication graph* is a `Cslib.HeardOf.Graph α` (`G j i`: `i` receives the message of `j`); self-loops are implicit (`Cslib.HeardOf.Hears`), as the paper assumes. * `Out_G(P)` (`out G P`): the processes hearing some member of `P` in `G`. * A *closed-above model* generated by `S` is `(⋃_{G ∈ S} ↑G)^ω`; since it is oblivious, the adversary picks any graph of `closedAbove S` in every round independently. * A one-round full-information algorithm is a decision map `δ p view` applied to the vector of received inputs (`Cslib.HeardOf.msgs`, `none` = not heard). * `k`-set agreement (`KSetAgreement`): every decision is the input of some process (validity) and at most `k` values are decided (agreement). * `r`-round *oblivious* algorithms (Definition 2.5): every process floods all `(process, input)` pairs it knows; after `r` rounds it applies a decision map to the set of known pairs (`known Gs x p`). ## Combinatorial numbers `domNum` (γ, Definition 3.1), `eqDomNum`/`eqDomNumSet` (γ^eq, Definition 3.3), `cov`/`covSet` (Definition
