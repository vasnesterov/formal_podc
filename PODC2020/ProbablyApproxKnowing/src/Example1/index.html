import Papers.PODC2020.ProbablyApproxKnowing.Basic /-! # Example 1 (relaxed firing squad) and the claim of §7 about it Example 1 of [Moses–Zamir]: Alice and Bob, synchronous rounds, every message is lost independently with probability `0.1`; Alice's initial bit `go` is `0` or `1` with probability `1/2` each. * `FS`: the protocol FS. If `go = 1` Alice sends two messages in round 1 and fires at time 2. Bob replies 'Yes' in round 2 and fires at time 2 if he received a message in round 1, and replies 'No' (and never fires) otherwise. We check the numbers of §1 and §8: `μ(both fire | Alice fires) = 0.99` (`FS.cprob_both`); Alice's belief that both fire when she fires is `1`, `0` or `0.99` after receiving 'Yes', 'No' or nothing (`FS.beliefAt_eq`); the threshold `0.95` is met with probability `0.991` (`FS.cprob_threshold`); and in the variant where Alice does not fire after a 'No', `μ(both fire | Alice fires) = 990/991 ≈ 0.99899` (`FS.cprob_both_noNo`). * `Mixed`: a protocol in the same setting in which Alice, when `go = 1`, fires at time 1 only with probability `1/2` (Bob fires at time 1 iff he received one of her two round-1 messages). It satisfies the constraint `μ(φ_both@fire_A | fire_A) ≥ 0.99` of §7, but Alice's belief in `φ_both` when she fires is `0.495` at every firing point, so the claim of §7 ("in every protocol that satisfies this constraint, … Alice's degree of belief that both are firing … meets or exceeds `0.9` with probability at least `0.9`") fails (`Mixed.section7_claim_false`). With the fact "Bob is firing" instead of `φ_both` the claim holds (`Mixed.belief_fireB`; in general `JointProtocol.section7_claim_fireB` in `Protocol.lean`). The runs only record the random outcomes that matter (whether Bob received at least one of Alice's messages, whether Bob's reply was delivered, Alice's coin); runs of the full tree that differ only in outcomes nobody's relevant local state depends on are merged, which does not change any probability or belief about the facts considered here. -/ namespace Podc.ProbablyApproxKnowing.Example1 open Finset PPS /-- The two agents. -/ inductive Ag | A | B deriving DecidableEq /-!
