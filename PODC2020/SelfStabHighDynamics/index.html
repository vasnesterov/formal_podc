Self-stabilizing Systems in Spite of High Dynamics — PODC 2020

Karine Altisen, Stéphane Devismes, Anaïs Durand, Colette Johnen, Franck Petit. Brief Announcement: Self-stabilizing Systems in Spite of High Dynamics. PODC 2020. DOI 10.1145/3382734.3404502. Numbering follows the full version.

Verdict: 🔧 Easy fix. Every result is true, and we proved all of them. This covers the impossibilities (no closed legitimate set for size-ambiguous algorithms in TC_B(Δ), the need for exact knowledge of n in TC_Q(Δ) and TC_R, unbounded stabilization time) and the three algorithms with their bounds 3Δ, 2Δ and Δ + 1. Corollary 1 is stated more strongly than what Theorem 1 gives, and Theorem 6 relies on two implicit model assumptions. The proofs of Lemma 6 (Algorithm 2) and Lemma 11 (Algorithm 3) each miss a counting step, which needs a short local repair.

Mistakes in the paper

✏️ Slips

  • Corollary 1 says that a closed set of legitimate configurations for one V means A is not size-ambiguous at all. It is meant as the contrapositive of Theorem 1, "A is not (k, V)-ambiguous", and the printed form is false. Algorithm 1 is size-ambiguous, yet for a single process it has a closed set of legitimate configurations (corollary_1_as_stated_false). The contrapositive holds (corollary_1), and Theorem 3 only uses that.

  • Theorem 6, Corollary 8, Lemma 13, Corollary 9 rely on two implied assumptions. The proof swaps the leader for a fresh process v ∉ V and keeps the other processes' states. So IDSET must have more than n identifiers: with IDSET = Fin 2, an algorithm that knows n elects 0 when n = 2 and is correct from round 0 (theorem_6_needs_large_IDSET). Also, the possible local states must depend on V only through n (SizeOnly): an algorithm whose state is V elects min V at once (theorem_6_needs_sizeOnly). Both counterexamples are pseudo-stabilizing (knowsN2_pseudoStab, knowsV_pseudoStab). The paper's algorithms know at most n and Δ and satisfy both assumptions (alg2_sizeOnly, alg3_sizeOnly).

  • Section 3.3 says Algorithm 1's legitimate configurations cannot be closed. That holds only for systems of at least two processes (alg1_not_closed, alg1_singleton_closed).

Typos

  • Algorithm 3, line 7: insert(p, ⟨id(0), 0⟩) should be insert(p, ⟨id(p), 0⟩).

  • Lemma 11, proof case 2: "edge (r, p)" should be "edge (r, q)".

Proof gaps in true statements (2)Lemma 6 · Lemma 11

🔧 Lemma 6 (Algorithm 2, identifiers inserted after round Δ are never evicted)

The proof claims that after id is pushed, evicting it requires inserting n new distinct identifiers. This is false: re-inserting identifiers already in the queue (lines i2–i3) moves id towards the head (Alg2.lemma_6_counting_claim_false: with n = 3, one new identifier evicts 5 from [1, 2, 5]).

Impact. Statement true; repaired (Alg2.lemma_6), so Lemma 7, Lemma 9 and Theorems 5 and 7 stand.

Repair

From round Δ + 1 on, the longest suffix of the queue with real identifiers only grows, and its elements are never evicted. If its first element is the head, the queue holds n distinct real identifiers, i.e. all of V, so no new identifier can arrive (Alg2.lemma_6_suffix).

🔧 Lemma 11 (Algorithm 3, id(p) reaches members(q) along journeys)

"id(p) cannot be removed by Lemma 10" and "this pair is not rejected" need a counting step that the proof does not give.

Impact. Statement true; repaired (Alg3.lemma_11), so Theorems 8 and 9 stand.

Repair

An entry with timestamp ≤ i − 2 is evicted at line i6 only if it has the maximal timestamp. Then all entries are ≤ i − 2, so by Lemma 10 all keys are real, and the full map is exactly V. So no absent identifier can arrive. This argument applies at every step of the loop, for every processing order and tie-break, and also at line 7, which the proof does not discuss (Alg3.mem_keys_of_full_lt).

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Synchronous rounds over dynamic graphs: G t is the snapshot of Round t + 1, edges join distinct processes of V. The classes TC_B(Δ), TC_Q(Δ), TC_R are defined through temporal distance (journeys with strictly increasing times). They are the all-pairs classes of Papers/PODC2021/StabilizingLEDynamic (same authors).

  • Processes are their identifiers, taken from a linear order. There are no crashes, the algorithms are deterministic, and the initial configuration is arbitrary. The adversary is the choice of the graph within the class.

  • An algorithm (SAlg) is defined for every set of processes. The code of a process does not depend on V, and knowledge (for instance n) is an input in the state. So it shows up as the sets of possible states S V p, which is the paper's S_A^V(p) used in Definition 3.

  • Communication is full-information local broadcast: each process receives the whole states of its current in-neighbours. This is stronger than sending part of the state, so the impossibilities are not weakened. The algorithms of Sections 4–6 use only the part the paper sends.

  • Algorithms 2 and 3 process the received pairs in an arbitrary order, and every round lemma holds for every order and every tie-break of max(p).

  • Definitions 1, 2 and 6 are stated for recurring classes, as in Remarks 1 and 2.

Remarks:

  • Theorem 1 is proved with a simpler TVG than the paper's: the complete graph on a k-subset without the leader in even rounds, and the complete graph on V in odd rounds. No rotating window is needed.

  • Theorem 3 is proved directly from the core of Theorem 1 and the set of the proof of Theorem 2. So, unlike Theorem 2, it does not need executions to stay in the configurations of A for V.

  • Theorem 6 holds for every algorithm whose states depend on V only through n, pseudo-stabilizing or not.

Not formalized. The memory and message-size claims of Corollary 6 and Theorem 5, and Remarks 4 and 5 (other problems).

Trust. No sorry; the listed theorems use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. Non-vacuity: the three algorithms satisfy the hypotheses of the general theorems (self-stabilizing; Algorithm 1 is size-ambiguous and Algorithms 2 and 3 are not, consistent with Theorems 1 and 3; all three are SizeOnly, and Algorithm 2 satisfies the Invariant hypothesis of Theorem 2). The static complete graph is in all three classes (K_mem_JssB). The model reuses Papers/PODC2021/StabilizingLEDynamic/Model.lean and CSLib DynamicNetworks/{HeardOf,Journey}.
