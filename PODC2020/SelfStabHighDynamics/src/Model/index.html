import Papers.PODC2021.StabilizingLEDynamic.Model /-! # Model: algorithms for every set of processes, self-stabilization, closure, size-ambiguity Altisen, Devismes, Durand, Johnen, Petit, *Self-stabilizing Systems in Spite of High Dynamics* (PODC 2020 brief announcement; full version: Theoretical Computer Science 2023, HAL hal-04109662), Sections 2–3. We reuse the dynamic-graph model of `Papers/PODC2021/StabilizingLEDynamic/Model.lean` (same authors, same classes): * processes are identified with their identifiers (`ι` = `IDSET`, `id(p) = p`), a system is a finite set `V : Finset ι`; * a TVG is a dynamic graph `G : ℕ → Graph ι` with `IsDG V G` (edges between distinct processes of `V`); rounds are **0-indexed**: `G t` is the snapshot of the paper's Round `t + 1`, and `exec A G γ t` is the paper's `γ_t` (the configuration at the end of Round `t`, i.e. at the beginning of Round `t + 1`); * `TC_B(Δ) = JssB Δ V`, `TC_Q(Δ) = JssQ Δ V`, `TC_R = Jss V` (temporal distance `tdist`, a journey of temporal length `≤ Δ` from time `i` uses the rounds `i, …, i + Δ - 1`); * algorithms are deterministic full-information round algorithms (`Cslib.HeardOf.StepFn`): each process receives the whole state of each current in-neighbour, which is at least as strong as the paper's `SEND` of part of the state. New here (Section 3): an algorithm `SAlg` is defined for **every** set of processes `V`. The code of a process `p` (`step p`, `lid p`) does not depend on `V`; knowledge about the system (such as `n`) is an input stored in the local state, so it is captured by the sets of possible local states `S V p` (the paper's `S_A^V(p)`), exactly as in the paper's discussion before Definition 3. Configurations of `A` for `V` are the `γ` with `γ p ∈ S V p` for `p ∈ V` (the states of processes outside `V` are irrelevant). Since all classes considered are recurring (`StabilizingLEDynamic.J_shift`), Definitions 1 and 2 are stated in the simplified form of Remarks 1 and 2 (executions from time `0` of every graph of the
