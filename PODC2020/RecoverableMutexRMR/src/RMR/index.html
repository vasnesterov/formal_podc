We put `W[p]` and `Exit[p]` in the segment of `p` (as the busy-wait of line 31 on `W[p]` requires), and charge *every* access to `C`, `D`, `Token` and `A[·]` as an RMR: this is an upper bound valid for any placement of these variables. Invocations of `Enter()`/`Exit()` and crashes cost nothing. **Result.** In every execution, the total number of RMRs is at most `25 · (number of Enter() invocations) + 76 · (number of crashes)`. Since every crash ends a passage and the recovery starts a new one, this is `O(m)` for `m` passages (Theorem 1/2 of the paper). See `README.md` for the literal reading of "passage".
