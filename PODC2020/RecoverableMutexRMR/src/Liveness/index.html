Consequently, after its last crash, a process executing `Enter()` enters the critical section (`enter_reaches_cs`), a process executing `Exit()` returns to the remainder section (`exit_reaches_rem`), and a process executing `Recover()` leaves it (`recover_terminates`). The proof (files `Liveness*.lean`): 1. `LivenessTrace.exists_no_new_skip`: after the last crash, eventually no ticket gets skipped (at most `2 · #crashes` tickets are ever skipped, potential `Ψ`). 2. `LivenessTrace.stuck`: a process avoiding the remainder and critical sections is eventually stuck at line 31 forever (lexicographic measure of `LivenessMeasure.lean`; back edges of the program are paid for by skipped tickets and by tickets beyond `Token`). 3. Its ticket `w` bounds `Token < w`, so `Token` becomes constant, nobody is in the critical section any more (`no_cs`, via `progress`), and calls of `Move_Token()` stop (`calls_stop`). 4.
