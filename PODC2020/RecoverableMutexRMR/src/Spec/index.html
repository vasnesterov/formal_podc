import Papers.PODC2020.RecoverableMutexRMR.Model /-! # Properties of recoverable mutual exclusion (Section 2 of the paper) Executions are schedules `e : ℕ → Ev P` (see `Model.lean`). * A schedule has *finitely many crashes* if after some time no crash event occurs. * A schedule is *fair* if every process that is outside the remainder section at some time takes a step at that time or later ("all processes that have pending method calls or that are in the critical section keep taking steps"). A step of a process in the critical section is its call of `Exit()`; processes in the remainder section need not take steps (if they do, they call `Enter()`). -/ namespace Podc.RecoverableMutexRMR variable {P : Type} [DecidableEq P] /-- Only finitely many crash events. -/ def FinCrashes (e : ℕ → Ev P) : Prop := ∃ T₀, ∀ t, T₀ ≤ t → ∀ p, e t ≠ .crash p /-- Fairness: processes outside the remainder section keep taking steps. -/ def Fair (e : ℕ → Ev P) : Prop := ∀ p t, (exec e t).pc p ≠ .rem → ∃ t', t ≤ t' ∧ e t' = .step p /-- Non-vacuity of the liveness hypotheses: the crash-free round-robin schedule of two processes is fair. -/ example : Fair (fun t => Ev.step (decide (t % 2 = 0))) ∧ FinCrashes (fun t => Ev.step (decide (t % 2 = 0))) := by refine ⟨fun p t _ => ?_, ⟨0, fun t _ q h => by cases h⟩⟩ by_cases h : decide (t % 2 = 0) = p · exact ⟨t, le_rfl, by simp only [h]⟩ · refine ⟨t + 1, by omega, ?_⟩ cases p <;> simp_all <;> omega end Podc.RecoverableMutexRMR
