Node `i` moves in rounds `0, …, kk i - 1` (jumping from `i - 2^s` to `i - 2^(s+1)` in round `s`) and then stays, where `kk i = #{s ≥ 0 : 3·2^s ≤ i + 1}` (`le_kk_iff`). So after `r` rounds its parent is `Q r i = i - 2^min(r, kk i)`, and its final parent is `F i = i - 2^(kk i)`. The final tree is the complete binary tree in level order: node `i` has depth `lev i = ⌊log₂ (i+1)⌋` (`lev_F`), and `g` has the children `g + 2^(lev g)` and `g + 2^(lev g + 1)` (`F_c1`, `F_c2`).
