import Papers.PODC2020.ActivelyDynamicNetworks.PointerJumping import Cslib.Foundations.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.RootedTree import Mathlib.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.Hasse /-! # TreeToStar (Proposition 2.1 / A.1) Michail–Skretas–Spirakis, PODC 2020, §2.3: starting from a rooted tree `T` (nodes know their parent), in every round each node whose parent is not the root activates the edge to its grandparent and deactivates the edge to its parent. This is synchronous pointer jumping: after `t` rounds the parent of `v` is `parent^[2^t] v` (`ptr T t`). * `exec T t`: the snapshot after `t` rounds; `exec_zero` (it starts from `T`), `exec_succ` (one round = the activations/deactivations of the rule), `isExec` (valid execution of the model), `isTree` (every snapshot is a spanning tree). * `star_iff`: the snapshot after `t` rounds is the spanning star centred at the root **iff** `⌈log₂ d⌉ ≤ t`, where `d` is the depth of `T`. So TreeToStar takes exactly `⌈log₂ d⌉` rounds. * `activeEdges_le`: at most `(n-1) + (n-2) = 2n-3` edges are active during a round (the tree edges plus the activated ones); every snapshot has exactly `n - 1` edges (`ncard_edgeSet`). * `numActivated_le`, `totalActivations_le`: at most `n - 2` activations per round and `(n-2)⌈log₂ d⌉ = O(n log n)` in total. * `ceil_log_depth_le_log_false`: the paper's `⌈log d⌉ ≤ log n` fails for the line on `6` nodes (`⌈log₂ 5⌉ = 3 > log₂ 6`), where TreeToStar indeed needs 3 rounds (`line6_not_star_two`). -/ namespace Podc.ActivelyDynamicNetworks open
