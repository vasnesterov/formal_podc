Values of the register `REG` and of pending writes are called the *values* of a configuration (`InVals`). The invariant `Inv` is the conjunction of * `Basic`: identifiers in `discarded` sets are identifiers of losers; a pending write of an identifier is `⟨id_p, discarded_p, phase_p⟩` of its non-loser writer `p`; there is always a non-loser, and the identifier in `REG` (if any) is the one of a non-loser; bookkeeping of the program counters; * either `NoLead` (no value `⟨id, D, ·⟩` with `|D| = n - 1` has ever been written: everything is electing and nobody returns), or `Lead L` for the *leader* `L`: all other processes are losers forever, every identifier in a value is `id_L`, every returned value is computed from `id_L`, plus the phase-specific facts `ElPart` (while `L` is electing) and `CoPart` (once `L` is committing) needed for termination. When the first value `⟨id_L, D, electing⟩` with `|D| = n - 1` is decided, `D` consists of identifiers of losers (`Basic`), so all processes but `L` are losers, and `Lead L` starts. -/ set_option
