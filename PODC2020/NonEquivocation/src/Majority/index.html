import Papers.PODC2020.NonEquivocation.Exec /-! # Consistent majorities (Definition 4.3) and Lemmas 4.4–4.8 Madsen–Debois, PODC 2020, Section 4.2. `τ` is the local view of a process `p` (`τ w = σ (p :: w)`, see `loc`). `Maj t τ p q Q v` (Definition 4.3, written `maj^{q,v}_{p,Q}(t, σ_p, P)` in the paper): `|Q| ≥ t + 1` and every route `p|w|q` with `w ∈ Q*`, `|w| ≤ t` (and no consecutive duplicates) carries the value `v` to `p`. In the paper `v` ranges over the values *and* `⊥` (Lemma 4.7 claims that no consistent majority for `⊥` exists). With `v : V` (a genuine value) `Maj` excludes `⊥` by typing; `MajOpt` is the paper's version with `v : Option V`, and `lemma_4_7_false` shows that Lemma 4.7 fails for it: the decider `p` itself may be the only non-faulty member of `Q` (its own "report" `σ(p|p|q) = σ(p|q)` is the `⊥` it received).
