Comparing `a ≠ MR` reads `MR` entry by entry (a *comparison collect*), `a := MR` is an *assignment collect*. * Recovery functions re-invoke the operation; no auxiliary state (`aux = id`). We prove that it is durably linearizable and detectable (`alg3_durLinDet`) and obstruction-free (`alg3_obstructionFree`), so the hypothesis "doubly-perturbing" of Theorem 2 cannot be dropped (a max register is not doubly-perturbing, Lemma 4). Linearization: a write that changes `MR[p]` at its write; a write that does not, at its read of `MR[p]` (unless it is already linearized, which happens when it is re-executed after a crash); a read speculatively at the first read of a comparison collect if at that moment `MR = a` (then `max a` is the current value), retracted if the collect fails or at a crash. -/ set_option
