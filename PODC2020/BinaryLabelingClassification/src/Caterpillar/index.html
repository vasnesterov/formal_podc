import Papers.PODC2020.BinaryLabelingClassification.Transport import Podc.Foundations.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.ParentGraph /-! # The caterpillar instances of §8 and their mirrored versions The lower bounds of Theorems 8.1 and 8.2 use a long "caterpillar" tree: a spine `w₀ – b₀ – w₁ – b₁ – ⋯ – w_M – b_M` of alternating white (`w_k`) and black (`b_k`) nodes, where every white spine node has `a` black leaves ("hairs") and every black spine node has `b` white hairs, so that the inner spine nodes have degrees `d = a + 2` and `δ = b + 2`. The remaining `r` nodes are white leaves attached to `b_M` (padding, so that every number of nodes can be realised). The *mirror* `mirror p q` reverses the part `w_p – b_p – ⋯ – w_q` of the spine (with the hairs). Relabelling the caterpillar along the mirror gives another tree (an isomorphic copy) which agrees with the caterpillar at every node except the four boundary nodes `b_{p-1}, w_p, w_q, b_q` (`adj_mirror`). Nodes far from the boundary therefore see the same view in both trees, while the spine order is reversed inside the mirrored part. -/ namespace Podc.BinaryLabeling.Caterpillar open SimpleGraph /-- The
