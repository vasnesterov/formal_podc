Starting from `G_{x,y}` (`CKP17.lean`): * every edge incident to a bit-gadget vertex is replaced by a path gadget: `PE.rb s ℓ i h` for the row–bit edge `row s ℓ i – bit s ℓ h (i h)`, `PE.cyc ℓ h s s'` for the 4-cycle edge `bit s ℓ h 1 – bit s' ℓ h 0`; * the clique edges are kept (`core`); * for every row `a^i_1` (resp. `b^i_1`) a shared gadget `PE.sh s i` is joined to `a^i_1` and to every `a^j_2` with `x(i, j) = 0` (resp. `y`). A gadget is a path of `m` new vertices whose first vertex is joined to the endpoints (`attach`, `PathGadget.lean`): `m = 1` is the weight-`0` vertex `p_e` of Section 5.2, `m = 3` the dangling path `DP_e[1..3]` of Section 5.3. `H x y m` is the resulting graph on `HV L m = GV L ⊕ PE L × Fin m`. The number of gadgets is `g = 2k + 4k log k + 8 log k` (`card_PE`). Partition (proofs of Theorems 20 and 22): `V'_A` = Alice's vertices of `G_{x,y}`, the gadgets both of whose endpoints are in `V_A`, and the shared gadgets `p^i_a`.
