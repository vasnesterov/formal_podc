import Mathlib /-! # The MVC lower-bound graphs `G_{x,y}` of Censor-Hillel, Khoury, Paz (DISC 2017) These graphs (Figure 1 of Bar-Yehuda, Censor-Hillel, Maus, Pai, Pemmaraju, *Distributed Approximation on Power Graphs*, PODC 2020, and Section 5 of [CKP17]) are the starting point of the `G²`-vertex-cover lower bounds (Theorems 20 and 22). `k = 2 ^ L`; a row index `i ∈ [k]` is a bit string `i : Fin L → Bool`. * Rows: `row s ℓ i` is `a^i_1` (`s = false`, `ℓ = false`), `a^i_2` (`s = false`, `ℓ = true`), `b^i_1`, `b^i_2` (`s = true`). The rows with fixed `(s, ℓ)` form a clique of size `k`. * Bit gadgets: `bit s ℓ h b` is `t^h_S` (`b = true`) or `f^h_S` (`b = false`) for `S = A_ℓ` (`s = false`) or `S = B_ℓ` (`s = true`). For fixed `(ℓ, h)` the four vertices form the 4-cycle `t_A – f_A – t_B – f_B – t_A`, i.e. `bit s ℓ h b ~ bit s' ℓ h b'` iff `b ≠ b'`. (The paper does not spell out the order of the cycle; this choice makes `{t_A, t_B}` and `{f_A, f_B}` the two independent pairs, which is what the argument of [CKP17] needs.) * `row s ℓ i ~ bit s ℓ h (i h)`: `a^i` is joined to `t^h` if bit `h` of `i` is `1`, else to `f^h`. * Input edges: `a^i_1 – a^j_2` iff `x(i, j) = 0`, `b^i_1 – b^j_2` iff `y(i, j) = 0`. `n = 4k + 8 log k` (`card_GV`).
