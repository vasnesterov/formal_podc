Maus, S. Pai, S. V. Pemmaraju, *Distributed Approximation on Power Graphs*, PODC 2020 (arXiv 2006.03746), Section 3.1. We formalize the *sequential semantics* of Algorithm 1 with `ε = 1/l` (the paper reduces to this case by `ε' = 1/⌈1/ε⌉`): Phase I repeatedly picks a centre `c ∈ C` with more than `l` `G`-neighbours in `R`, moves `N(c) ∩ R` to the cover `S = V \ R` and removes `c` from `C`; Phase II adds an optimal vertex cover `R*` of `G²[U]`, `U = V \ S`.
