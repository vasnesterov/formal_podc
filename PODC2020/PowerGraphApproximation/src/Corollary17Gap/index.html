import Papers.PODC2020.PowerGraphApproximation.Corollary17 /-! # The proof of Corollary 17 misapplies Theorem 12 The proof of Corollary 17 says that the leader "locally uses Theorem 12 to compute a 5/3-approximation for the remaining graph" `G²[U]`. Theorem 12 is about squares `G²`, and the remaining graph is only an induced subgraph of one. Here is a run where Algorithm 2 on the remaining graph is only a 2-approximation. `G` has vertices `aᵢ, bⱼ` (`i, j < 4`) and a midpoint `wᵢⱼ` adjacent to `aᵢ` and `bⱼ`. Phase I of Algorithm 1 with `ε = 1/2` picks the centres `a₀, a₁, a₂, a₃` and moves all `wᵢⱼ` into `S`; it ends with `U = {aᵢ} ∪ {bⱼ}` and `G²[U] = K₄,₄`. This graph is triangle-free and 4-regular, so parts 1 and 2 of Algorithm 2 do nothing and part 3 may take all 8 vertices (both endpoints of the perfect matching `aᵢbᵢ`, a valid 2-approximation). But `{aᵢ}` (4 vertices) covers `G²[U]`.
