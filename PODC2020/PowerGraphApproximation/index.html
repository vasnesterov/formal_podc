Distributed Approximation on Power Graphs — PODC 2020

Reuven Bar-Yehuda, Keren Censor-Hillel, Yannic Maus, Shreyas Pai, Sriram V. Pemmaraju. Distributed Approximation on Power Graphs. PODC 2020. DOI 10.1145/3382734.3405750. Formalized version: arXiv 2006.03746 v1; numbering follows it. Copy of the paper: paper.pdf.

DOI · arXiv · PDF · All differences · Lean source · GitHub

Verdict: 🔧 Easy fix. Every checked statement is true: the centralized 5/3-approximation for G²-MVC (Theorem 12, Lemmas 13–16), Lemma 6, the combinatorial core of the (1+ε)-approximation (Theorem 1, Lemmas 2–5), Corollary 17, and the Ω̃(n²) CONGEST lower bounds for exact G²-MWVC and G²-MVC (Theorems 20 and 22, Lemmas 21, 23, 24, with the [CKP17] family lemma proved from scratch). The one serious flaw sits in a side result that nothing depends on: the proof of Corollary 17 applies Theorem 12 to a graph that is not a square, where Algorithm 2 really is only a 2-approximation, and a new accounting saves the 5/3 bound. The CONGEST round complexities and the G²-MDS half of the paper were not checked.

Mistakes in the paper

✏️ Slips

  • Proof of Lemma 6: the claim |I| < n/α fails for the path 0–1–2–3 with r = 2, where I = {0, 3} is independent in G² and |I| = 2 = n/α (lemma_6_strict_false). The non-strict bound |I| ≤ n/α follows from the paper's own argument and is all the lemma needs (card_mul_le_of_indep, lemma_6).

  • Lemma 6's second sentence (the (1 + 1/⌊r/2⌋)-approximation) needs r ≥ 2; for r = 1 it divides by 0.

  • Theorem 12, degree-3 case: the three disjoint edges are {y₁,z₁}, {y₂,z₂}, {x,y₃}, not {x,y₂}.

  • The distributed description of Phase I calls vertices with "at least 1/ε" neighbours in R candidates; the pseudocode, Lemma 2 and Lemma 5 need "more than 1/ε".

  • Theorem 1, case ε > 1: "a G²-MIS has at most n/2 vertices" fails for n = 1.

  • Theorems 20 and 22: V_B = V(G) \ V_B should be \ V_A.

Typos

  • Lemma 5 writes Sᵢ = N(vᵢ) ∩ R for N(cᵢ) ∩ R.

Proof gaps in true statements (1)Corollary 17

🛠️ Corollary 17 (the proof misapplies Theorem 12)

The proof runs Phase I of Algorithm 1 with ε = 1/2 and then applies Theorem 12 to the remaining graph G²[U], which is not a square, and Algorithm 2 really is only a 2-approximation there (corollary_17_proof_gap).

Impact. The corollary is true: the overall 5/3 bound holds with a different accounting (induced_bound, corollary_17).

Details

The proof says the leader "locally uses Theorem 12 to compute a 5/3-approximation for the remaining graph" G²[U]. Theorem 12 only covers squares G². G²[U] is just an induced subgraph of a square, and every graph is one. The proof of Theorem 12 also breaks there: Lemma 13 (3) needs the G-midpoint of every blue edge to lie in V1, but now the midpoint can lie in S. Concrete run: take vertices a₀..a₃, b₀..b₃ and midpoints wᵢⱼ ~ aᵢ, bⱼ. Phase I picks a₀, …, a₃ and ends with U = {aᵢ, bⱼ} and G²[U] = K₄,₄. That graph is triangle-free and 4-regular, so Algorithm 2 goes straight to part 3. Part 3 may output all 8 vertices (both ends of a perfect matching), while 4 vertices suffice (corollary_17_proof_gap).

Repair

A midpoint in S has at most two neighbours in the triangle-free remainder, so it pays for at most one blue edge, just like a triangle vertex. Lemma 5 charges S at ratio 3/2, the same as part 1 charges triangles. So Lemma 14 (4) becomes s₁ + |S| ≥ (3/2)|V_{R'}| and the 5/3 computation goes through (induced_bound, corollary_17).

The formalizationmodel, Lean names of the results, trust

Model.

  • Graphs are finite SimpleGraphs. G.power r joins distinct vertices at G-distance ≤ r (Podc/Foundations/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Power.lean). Vertex covers are Mathlib's IsVertexCover or a Finset covering G²[X].

  • Centralized algorithms (Algorithms 1 and 2) are nondeterministic transition systems (Step). Each choice the pseudocode leaves open is a nondeterministic choice, and the theorems hold for every complete run. Algorithm 2 stores only V', since E' = G²[V'] throughout, as the paper proves. Degree-0 vertices may be removed at any time in parts 1–2 (the paper removes them eagerly). Part 3 is "any 2-approximate cover". Existence of runs comes from exists_step/exists_run: the existence claims of lines 9–10 hold, and part 3 can use a maximal matching. "Polynomial time" is formalized as an n + 3 bound on the number of steps, each step being a polynomial-time check.

  • Theorem 1 (CONGEST, O(n/ε) rounds) is formalized only in its sequential semantics with ε = 1/l: correctness and approximation, Lemma 3, and the facts behind the round count. These are: at most l neighbours in U per vertex, hence |F| ≤ ln, and at most n/(l+1) Phase-I iterations. BFS pipelining and ID symmetry breaking are not modelled.

  • Lower bounds use CSLib's deterministic synchronous CONGEST model with B-bit messages and families of lower-bound graphs (Cslib/Computability/Distributed/Congest/). Nodes know their IDs and neighbourhoods, plus their weight for Theorem 20. "Computes MVC of G²" is formalized, as in [CKP17]/Theorem 19, as deciding the threshold predicate "H² has a cover of size (weight) ≤ W'". The deterministic bound is unconditional, because CSLib proves CC(DISJ_K) ≥ K. The randomized versions assume CC^R(DISJ_K) ≥ κK (real κ > 0, private coins from nonempty coin spaces) as a hypothesis. There are also versions that need correctness only on connected networks (H_{x,y} is disconnected only for x = y = 1).

The namespaces sit under Podc.PowerGraphApproximation. theorem_2x gives k² ≤ 8TB log k + 2 for n = 10k + 12k log k + 32 log k (Theorem 22) and 6k + 4k log k + 16 log k (Theorem 20). The _n versions say n² ≤ c · log² n · (8TB log n + 2). The generic gadget machinery is in PathGadget.lean: attaching paths to a core graph so that the square, restricted to the core, is the core.

Remarks. Two routine details the paper leaves implicit. In the degree-2 case of Theorem 12, the edges {z,y₁} and {x,y₂} are disjoint only because z ≠ y₂, which holds because the graph is triangle-free; the paper does not say so. The order of the 4-cycle in the [CKP17] bit gadgets is not specified; we use t_A – f_A – t_B – f_B.

Not formalized. The CONGEST round complexities of Theorems 1, 7 and 11 and of Corollary 17. Theorem 7 and Lemma 8 (weighted). Theorem 11 and Corollary 10 (Congested Clique), and so the CONGESTED CLIQUE half of Corollary 17 beyond Centralized.induced_bound. Lemma 25, Theorem 26 and Corollary 27. Section 6 (G²-MDS algorithm). Section 7 (MDS lower bounds). Section 8 (centralized hardness).

Trust. No sorry; the listed theorems use only the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. Several checks show the hypotheses are satisfiable: Algorithm 2 always reaches an output (exists_run), Phase I always terminates (exists_final), and the "decides on connected members" hypotheses of Theorems 20/22 are met by CSLib's gathering algorithm (theorem_20_hyp_satisfiable, theorem_22_hyp_satisfiable). The Corollary 17 counterexample is checked by decide.
