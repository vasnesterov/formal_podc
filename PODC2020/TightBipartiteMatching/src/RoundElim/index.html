import Papers.PODC2020.TightBipartiteMatching.Views /-! # Bipartite round elimination (§2.2) * `Problem L`: a white constraint `W` and a black constraint `B` (sets of configurations, i.e. multisets of `Δ` labels). * `Problem.WhiteIn d Pr t` / `Problem.BlackIn d Pr t`: `Pr` has a white (black) `t`-round algorithm on the 2-coloured `(d+1)`-regular tree, i.e. white (black) complexity `≤ t`. * `univC` / `exC`: the universal and existential quantifier over configurations of sets of labels. `RB Pr` has the black constraint `univC Pr.B` and the white constraint `exC Pr.W`; `RW Pr` is dual. We use only **nonempty** sets of labels in the universal step (see `differencies.md`), and we do not perform the maximality simplification: both only make `R_B(Pr)` harder, so the round elimination theorem below is (slightly) stronger than the paper's Theorem 2.1, and every relaxation of it is in particular a relaxation of the paper's `R_B(Pr)` restricted to nonempty sets. * `theorem_2_1`: the round elimination theorem (forward direction, the one used for lower bounds): white complexity `t + 1` for `Pr` implies black complexity `t` for `R_B(Pr)`. The paper states "black complexity `min{0, T − 1}`", which is a typo for `max{0, T − 1}`. The forward direction needs no edge symmetry breaking (cf. `Papers/PODC2019/AutomaticSpeedup`, where the *converse* fails). * `Relax`: relaxations by a 0-round relabelling of the active side, and `relax_of_meaning`, the form in which the relaxations of Lemmas 4.1–4.3 are proved (each new label stands for a set of old labels; the universal configuration is enlarged label-wise). -/ -- CSLib
