The converse direction of `phi_W_counts` / `phi_B_counts`: sufficient conditions, in terms of the number of occurrences of each label, for a configuration to be allowed by the white or the black constraint of `Phi d x y`. * `phiW_matched`: `1 ≤ #M ≤ y`, no `Z`, no `P` (pattern `[MOX]^{y-1}[M][OX]^{Δ-y}`); * `phiW_unmatched`: only `P` and `X`, at least `Δ - x` labels `P` (pattern `[PX]^x[P]^{Δ-x}`); * `phiB_matched`: `1 ≤ #M ≤ y`, no `Z` (pattern `[MPOX]^{y-1}[M][POX]^{Δ-y}`); * `phiB_unmatched`: only `O` and `X`, at most `x` labels `X` (pattern `[OX]^x[O]^{Δ-x}`). -/
