The algorithm of §5, written as a synchronous local process (`Algorithm.ofLocal`) on a `Δ`-regular, properly 2-coloured port-numbered graph. Every node keeps sets of ports (`St`): `M` (matched), `S` (proposals sent), `R` (proposals received) and `B` (the batch of proposals sent in the node's most recent proposing round; this is implicit in the paper and is used only to bound the number of `M` edges by `y`). In round `r` the nodes of colour `act a0 r` are active (`a0` is the colour active in round `0`; the roles alternate): * an active node with `M = ∅` accepts the smallest port of `R` if `R ≠ ∅` (`St.acc`), and otherwise proposes over the `min(y, #free)` smallest ports not in `S` (`St.prop`, `batch`); * a passive node with `M = ∅` records the acceptances it receives in `M` and the proposals it receives in `R`; * nodes with `M ≠ ∅` do nothing. `Inv` collects the invariants (consistency of `M` across edges, `|M| ≤ y`, "every proposal is answered within one round", and the count of sent proposals); `inv_cfg` proves them for every round. -/ namespace TightBipartiteMatching namespace UpperBound open Finset Cslib.PortNumbering Cslib.PortNumbering.Algorithm /-- The local state of a node:
