Model: finite arrival (`finArrival`: in every infinite execution only finitely many processes take steps), finitely many shared objects allocated at set-up (`Fintype ιc`, `Fintype ιw`): binary consensus objects and window registers of arbitrary (fixed) sizes. Multi-valued consensus (process `p` proposes `p`) with agreement, validity and wait-freedom is impossible. Dynamic (finite) allocation of further objects at run time is *not* modelled (see `differencies.md`).
