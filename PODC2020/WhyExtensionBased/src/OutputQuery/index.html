import Papers.PODC2020.WhyExtensionBased.Actions /-! # The adversary's answers to output queries in phase 1 An output query `(C, Q, y)` asks for a `Q`-only schedule from `C` after which some process of `Q` has output `y`. Our (repaired) adversary answers: * **(S)** if some `Q`-descendant of `C` has already terminated with `y`: a schedule to it (restricted to its causal past, so that only decided states are passed); * **(U)** otherwise, if some undecided `Q`-descendant `x` with at least `t` rounds is *unprotected* (it has seen `y` and is not adjacent to a vertex that terminated with another value): let the process of `x` run alone until it is ahead of everybody, subdivide, and terminate its next state `s₁` (which has seen only its own previous state) with `y` — `s₁` is safe (`safe_solo`); * **(N)** otherwise `none`, recording the query: all its descendants are protected (`RecOK`). The paper's cases 1 and 2 are instances of (U) (with `x` of exactly `t` rounds); its case 3 may answer `none` although deeper descendants are unprotected, see `differencies.md`. -/ namespace
