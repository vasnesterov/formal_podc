import Papers.PODC2020.LongLivedSnapshotsPolylog.Counterexample /-! # Lemma 3.4 is false as stated Lemma 3.4: in a `b`-bounded-increment execution, if `K ≥ 2` unsuccessful loop iterations are performed by a `CompScan` instance `S`, then at least `⌈(K - 2) m / b⌉` instances of `MaxUpdate` *start* during the execution interval of `S`. The proof counts the `WriteMax` operations that take effect during `S` and then claims that each belongs to a `MaxUpdate` that started during `S`. But up to `n - 1` `MaxUpdate`s may have started before `S` and write during `S`. Counterexample with `n = 4`, `m = 16 = n²`, `b = 4` (the paper's parameters), `K = 3` (and helping period `X = 4`): processes `A = 0`, `B = 1`, `C = 2` have invoked `MaxUpdate(left, 16)`, `MaxUpdate(right, 16)`, `MaxUpdate(left, 20)` before `S = 3` invokes `MaxScan` (with `MR_left = MR_right = 15`); then 1. `A` writes `16` to `MR_left` during the first iteration of `S` (line 18 fails); 2. `B` writes `16` to `MR_right` during the second iteration (line 18 fails); 3. `C` writes `20`, completes, and runs `MaxUpdate(left, 24)`, `(left, 28)`, `(left, 32)` during the third iteration (line 18 fails). `S` has performed `3` unsuccessful iterations (its counter `c` is `3`), only `3` `MaxUpdate`s were invoked during `S`, but `⌈(3 - 2)·16/4⌉ = 4` (`lemma_3_4_false`). Lemma 3.5 (wait-freedom) survives: its proof only needs the number of `WriteMax` steps during the iterations, see `WaitFree.lemma_3_4` and `WaitFree.iterations_le`. -/ namespace
