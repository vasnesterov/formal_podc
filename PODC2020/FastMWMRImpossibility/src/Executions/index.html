The server logs are then `wlog x s`. * **Read phase** (`reach_reads`). Both readers invoke `read()`; every server receives the first-round message `q₁` of `r₁`, then the one `q₂` of `r₂`; `r₁` collects the replies of all servers and `r₂` the replies of all servers except `j` (if `j = some s`, the reply of `s` is delayed until the end of the execution: the request *was* delivered, which is the point where our proof deviates from the paper's informal "skip").
