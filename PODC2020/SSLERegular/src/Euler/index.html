Proof: take a trail of maximal length (`exists_isTrail_forall_isTrail_length_le_length`). It is closed, since otherwise its first vertex has odd trail-degree, hence an unused incident edge, and prepending it gives a longer trail. Every edge incident to its support is used, since otherwise rotating the circuit to that vertex and prepending the edge gives a longer trail.
