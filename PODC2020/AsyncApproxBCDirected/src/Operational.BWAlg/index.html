Paths end at the sender; the receiver checks it. * RedundantFlood (Appendix E): a node forwards the *first* `val` message received on a path `p` to every out-neighbour `w` such that `p ‖ v ‖ w` is redundant. * FIFO-Flood / FIFO-Receive (Appendix F): `COMPLETE` messages carry the counter `k` of their initiator (number of earlier `COMPLETE`s of that round) and are forwarded along simple paths; a message is FIFO-received along a path once all smaller counters were received along it. * The protocol logic (Maximal-Consistency, FIFO-Receive-All, Verify, Filter-and-Average) is the same as in `RoundModel.lean`, evaluated on the history (all conditions are re-evaluated after every reception; "for the first time" = first prefix of the history). -/ open Finset set_option
